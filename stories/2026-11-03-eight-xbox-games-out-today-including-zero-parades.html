Eight games launched on Xbox today, Tuesday, November 3 — and the headliner is a big one for RPG fans. ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut, an espionage RPG from the creators of Disco Elysium, leads the day's slate.

The headliner: ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut

ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut is the name at the top of today's Xbox releases. It comes from the creators of Disco Elysium, and it puts players in the world of espionage — spies, secrets, and high-stakes decisions — as the expanded Director's Cut edition of the game.

For anyone who followed Disco Elysium, the pedigree alone makes this the day's must-watch release. An espionage RPG from that team, arriving as a Director's Cut, is the kind of launch that anchors a whole release day.

Also out today: Pit of Goblin on Game Pass

The other named arrival is Pit of Goblin, which is out on Game Pass for console and PC today. A day-one Game Pass launch means subscribers can jump in at no extra cost on either console or PC — the kind of drop that makes a release day feel bigger than the headline game alone.

The full slate

Today's Xbox slate runs eight games deep in total. ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut leads it, Pit of Goblin lands day one on Game Pass, and the rest of the day's launches round out a busy Tuesday for Xbox players.
