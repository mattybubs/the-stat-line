Space Marine 2's Decimation update is live — Weekly Clash PvP, a Vanguard rework, and six new Heroic weapons

By Matt

Patch 16 is Space Marine 2's last big update of 2026, and it's a PvP-first one: a rotating Weekly Clash mode, a new Chaos Siege sector, a full Vanguard rework, and a half-dozen Heroic weapons to chase.

Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2's Decimation Update is live now — Patch 16, free on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC, and the last major update Saber Interactive has planned for 2026. After a year of operations, sieges and class reworks, this one is aimed squarely at the PvP crowd, with a new mode, a reworked class, and a fresh pile of gear to unlock.

Weekly Clash shakes up PvP

The headline addition is Weekly Clash, a new mode for Eternal War that rotates the rules every week. One week a match might be restricted to a single class, the next to a single weapon — and some variants hand out near-unlimited grenades. Saber says more modifiers are coming, and private lobbies can already craft their own rule sets.

It's a direct answer to the problem every live PvP mode eventually hits: the meta goes stale. Weekly Clash doesn't add maps or modes so much as it changes what the existing ones feel like, week to week.

Siege gets harder, Vanguard gets better

Chaos Siege, the wave-survival mode introduced in the Anniversary Update, gets a third sector that kicks in from wave 11 onward — plus a new enemy, the Loyalist Tactical Space Marine, whose Auspex Scan increases the damage you take. Surviving the late waves just got meaner.

The Vanguard class gets the rework treatment this time around. The Grapnel Launcher can now be used at close range, a batch of perks has been reworked and repositioned, and there's a new Revenge status effect: hitting an enemy afflicted with Revenge also damages nearby enemies. The Heavy is next in line for a rework, in Patch 17.

The loot

Six new Heroic Weapons join the Armoury — the Omnissian Axe, Bolt Rifle, Las Fusil, Plasma Incinerator, Heavy Bolt Pistol and Infernus Heavy Bolter — alongside four new helmets to unlock with Accolades, including the Space Wolves Iron Priest helm and Mk VI and Mk VII Hybrid variants. Season Pass III owners also get two new cosmetic packs today: the Deathwatch Champion Pack and the Mortifactors Cosmetic Pack.

There's a community event running alongside the patch, too. From November 26 through December 8, players can collectively recover 300,000 Gene Seeds to earn the Victrix Honour Guard Greave.

What's next

Saber has already sketched the road ahead: Patch 17 lands in the first quarter of 2027 with the Apothecary class and the Heavy rework, and the Year Three battle plan runs through Patch 19. For now, Decimation closes out 2026 with the game's PvP loop refreshed and its hardest mode made harder — which, for a game about being a giant in power armor, feels about right.
