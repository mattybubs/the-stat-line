TSL Bets: Warriors +6.5 (-110) at Rockets — eight straight wins and you're giving me points?

By Matt

The Warriors have won eight straight after an 0-3 start, Steph Curry is averaging 30.6 a game, and Houston is laying 6.5 with a banged-up frontcourt. Take the points with the hottest team in the league.

The game

Golden State Warriors (8-3) at Houston Rockets (8-3) — 9:30 PM ET, Toyota Center, ESPN

Rockets -6.5 (-110), moneyline Rockets -237, total 222.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Golden State: Jimmy Butler is out with a knee injury, Moses Moody is out with a knee injury, Gary Payton II is out with a knee injury, and Brandon Williams is out with a hamstring injury. Will Richard is questionable with a thumb injury (UCL tear). Kristaps Porzingis is away from the team. The Warriors are shorthanded — but they have been shorthanded all through this win streak.

Injuries — Houston: Clint Capela is out with an ankle injury and Steven Adams is out with a calf injury, leaving Alperen Sengun as the only healthy center. Oscar Tshiebwe is questionable with an illness. The Rockets' expected starters are Fred VanVleet, Amen Thompson, Kevin Durant, Jabari Smith Jr. and Sengun.

Storylines: The Warriors started 0-3 and have won eight straight since — the first team in NBA history to open a season with three losses and then win eight in a row. Steph Curry is averaging 30.6 points a game on 45.7% from three, and 32.3 over his last five. This is also a Durant-versus-Curry night — the former teammates who built a dynasty together, now on opposite sides of a matchup between two 8-3 teams. Both teams played Monday and had one day of rest. Houston's frontcourt depth is the question: with Capela and Adams out, Sengun is doing the center job alone against a Warriors team that pushes the pace.

The bet

Warriors +6.5 (-110). Six and a half points for a team that has not lost in eight games, with the best shooter alive playing the best basketball of his season. The Warriors are banged up, yes — but they have been banged up through the entire streak, and they keep winning anyway. Houston is good, and the Rockets are favored for a reason, but 6.5 is a lot of points against a team that has covered at a high rate during this run. Take the points.

The honest caveat

The Warriors are genuinely shorthanded — Butler, Moody and Payton are all out, and Richard is questionable. If Curry has an off night, there is not much behind him to carry the scoring. And the Rockets are 8-3 with Durant, VanVleet and Thompson, at home, with the better frontcourt. Houston could win this game by 10 and nobody would be surprised. The +6.5 is a bet on the streak and the shooter, not on the matchup — and streaks end. But the number is the number, and it is generous for a team that has not lost in a month.

The pick

Warriors +6.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $19.09. Eight straight wins, Steph Curry at 30.6 a game, and you're giving me six and a half points.
