Kith Winter 2026 Drops Today in Stores and Online

By Matt

Kith's Winter 2026 collection lands Friday, October 30, in flagship stores and online at 11:00 AM local time, fronted by Kit Harington in a British-countryside campaign.

Kith's Winter 2026 collection is out today — Friday, October 30, 2026 — in flagship stores and online at 11:00 AM local time.

The drop

The collection releases in Kith flagship stores and online at 11:00 AM local time. That local-time rollout means the drop lands at 11am wherever you are, not on a single global clock — set your alarm accordingly if you're going for the pieces that will move first.

The campaign

Kit Harington fronts the Winter 2026 campaign, shot in the British countryside. The imagery leans into the setting: layered tailoring and cold-weather outerwear, modelled against a rural backdrop rather than a studio.

What to expect from the collection

The throughline of the collection is layering done properly — tailored pieces built to stack, and outerwear built for actual cold. It's a winter collection in the literal sense: the kind of pieces you buy now and wear through the worst of the season, not a capsule that only works on a mild day.
