Kim Jones Is Putting His Own Name on the Door Again

By Matt

After Dior, Fendi and Louis Vuitton, the British designer launches KIM JONES for Fall/Winter 2027 — made in Japan, leather from Italy, first store in Hong Kong.

After two decades making other people's names famous, Kim Jones is putting his own back on the label.

The British designer — formerly of Louis Vuitton menswear, Dior Men and Fendi — announced his eponymous label KIM JONES on October 12, debuting with a Fall/Winter 2027 collection.

The plan

The collection bridges high fashion, streetwear and utilitarian design, built around a wardrobe "unrestricted by traditional categories." Apparel will be predominantly made in Japan; leather goods come from Italy. The price point is described as "considered" — luxury, but not entry-level.

It launches online in mid-2027 exclusively at kimjones.com, followed by the first flagship at Central Yards in Hong Kong that October, with more Asia stores and selected global stockists after. Longtime deputy Lucy Beeden joins him, and Jones is reportedly holding a majority stake alongside an undisclosed investor.

Why "again" matters

This isn't his first label — Jones ran an eponymous line from 2003 to 2008 after graduating from Central Saint Martins, closing it when he became creative director at Dunhill. It did especially well in Japan, which helps explain the manufacturing choice.

"After many years working with incredible houses and collaborators, it feels like the right moment to do something under my own name again," Jones said. On the Asia-first strategy, he's blunt: "Asia's got the pulse at the moment."

Meanwhile, the industry keeps churning — Seán McGirr exited Alexander McQueen after three years as Kering pivots the brand, and Veronica Leoni left Calvin Klein Collection.
