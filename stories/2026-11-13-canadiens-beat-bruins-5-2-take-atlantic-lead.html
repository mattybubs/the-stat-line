Canadiens bury the Bruins 5-2 in Boston and take over first in the Atlantic

By Matt

Montreal scored five straight, chased Jeremy Swayman, and leapfrogged Boston for the top spot in the Atlantic. The Canadiens have points in 12 straight games. The Bruins' seven-game home win streak is over.

The night

The Montreal Canadiens walked into TD Garden, scored five straight goals, and walked out in first place. Montreal beat the Boston Bruins 5-2 on Thursday night in the first of four meetings between the Original Six rivals, leapfrogging Boston for the top spot in the Atlantic Division.

It was over early, even if the clock said otherwise. Kirby Dach opened the scoring 2:34 in, Josh Anderson made it 2-0 before the first period ended, and then the second period turned into a rout: Jake Evans scored the winner at 4:24, Cole Caufield made it 4-0 at 8:14 — and Jeremy Swayman was pulled after allowing four goals on 17 shots — and Phillip Danault added a fifth at 14:15. The Bruins were down 5-0 before the middle frame was over. Casey Mittelstadt scored both Boston goals, one late in the second and one 53 seconds into the third, but by then Montreal was in cruise control.

Jacob Fowler stopped 30 of 32 in the Montreal net for his second career win in Boston, the city where he played his college hockey. Anderson and Evans each finished with a goal and an assist.

The streak

The win runs Montreal's point streak to 12 games — 9-0-3 in that stretch — and the Canadiens have not lost in regulation in a month. They are 10-0-2 when leading after two periods, which is another way of saying that once this team gets in front, the game is basically done. Boston, meanwhile, saw its seven-game winning streak on home ice snapped, and dropped to 13-5-1 despite outshooting Montreal 32-30.

The standings flip is the headline, but the way it happened is the story: a 5-0 lead on the road in a rivalry building, against a team that had not lost at home in seven games. Montreal did not sneak past Boston. It buried them.

What's next

The Canadiens host Colorado on Saturday night. The Bruins stay home for Minnesota the same night, looking to answer the first real beating of their season.
