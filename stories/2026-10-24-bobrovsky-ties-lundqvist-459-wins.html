Bobrovsky Ties Lundqvist at 459 Wins — One More Makes Him the Winningest Non-North American Goalie Ever

By Matt

Sergei Bobrovsky stopped 21 shots in Toronto's 5-4 win over the Rangers on Thursday, tying Henrik Lundqvist's 459 career wins — the most ever by a non-North American goalie.

The milestone came with a dagger. Troy Stecher scored with 26.3 seconds left in regulation — on a delayed penalty, off a feed from Morgan Rielly — to lift the Toronto Maple Leafs past the New York Rangers 5-4 on Thursday night at Scotiabank Arena, Toronto's third straight win. And in goal for the win was Sergei Bobrovsky, who stopped 21 shots to tie Henrik Lundqvist for the most wins ever by a non-North American goaltender: 459.

The number

Bobrovsky's 459th win ties Lundqvist, the former Rangers star, for sixth-most in NHL history — and the most ever by a goalie born outside North America. Next on the all-time list is Ed Belfour at 484. The very next win gives Bobrovsky sole possession of both marks: sixth all-time, and the outright non-North American record.

The game around it

It wasn't a quiet night to get there. Brandon Duhaime had a goal and two assists for Toronto, and Adam Fox answered with four assists for the Rangers — but Stecher's late winner settled it, and Bobrovsky's 21 saves held up. The Leafs improved to 6-4-2; the Rangers fell to 7-4-1.

What's next

One more win, and the record is his alone. No ceremony needed — just a goalie, a crease, and a number nobody born outside North America has ever reached before.
