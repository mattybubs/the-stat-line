Luka Doncic Over 35.5 Points (+102): Six straight games of 37 or more

By Matt

Doncic is averaging a league-leading 38.5 points, has cleared 35.5 in six straight games, and dropped 41 on Portland nine days ago. The alt line pays plus money.

Game slip

Portland Trail Blazers (6-1) at Los Angeles Lakers (7-2)

Friday, November 6, 2026 — 10:00 PM ET — Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles, CA — Prime Video, Spectrum SportsNet

Spread: Lakers -2.5

Total: 232.5

Moneyline: Lakers -156 / Trail Blazers +132

Odds source: FanDuel Sportsbook, Nov. 6

### Injury report

Los Angeles: F Sandro Mamukelashvili (illness) — questionable. G Bronny James and F Cameron Carr — out, G League assignment. F Adou Thiero — cleared, off the injury report, available for his season debut. Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves do not appear on the report.

Portland: G Ja Morant (left hamstring strain, Grade 2) — out until late November. G/F Shaedon Sharpe (right meniscus surgery) — out. F Vit Krejci (left ankle fracture) — out. Portland's three most recent wins have all come without Morant.

### Storylines

• Revenge game. Portland beat the Lakers 136-129 in this same building on October 27 — one of LA's only two losses. The Blazers ran them off their own floor.

• Luka Doncic is on a historic scoring run: a league-leading 38.5 points per game, 42.3 over his last three, shooting 49.5% from the field and 43.1% from deep. He has scored 37 or more in six straight games.

• Portland is 5-0 on the road and first in the West at 6-1. Damian Lillard is finding his rhythm — 25 points Wednesday in Phoenix, his most field goals in a game since March 2025 — and Deni Avdija is averaging 23.8 points with Donovan Clingan grabbing 10.5 boards a night.

• The first meeting totaled 265 points. These teams combine for 243.7 points per game; the total tonight is 232.5.

The bet

Luka Doncic Over 35.5 points (+102, Novig via Wise Guy Desk)

The standard line is 34.5 (-115 at Fanatics). The alt line at 35.5 pays plus money, and Doncic has cleared it in six straight games — 52, 41, 37, 45, 42, and 40. He put 41 on this same Portland team nine days ago, in a game where the Blazers still won. Portland is down its starting point guard and one of its best athletes, and the Lakers' entire offense runs through Doncic when it matters: 8.1 rebounds and 8.0 assists a night on top of the scoring, with Austin Reaves (23.6 ppg) as the only other reliable creator.

Why

The math is simple and it is on our side. Doncic's season average (38.5) sits three points above this line. His recent form sits further above it — 42.3 over the last three. The matchup cooperates: Portland plays fast, gives up points in bunches (132 to the Lakers' pace in the first meeting), and has no Morant to slow the game down or control tempo. If this stays close — and Portland is 5-0 on the road for a reason — Doncic plays 38-plus minutes and takes 25-plus shots. That is the recipe for 36-plus points.

The honest caveat

The market is not unanimous here. TalkSport's prop column likes the under 35.5 (-120), and there is a real case for it: Portland already beat LA while Doncic scored 41, so the Blazers have shown they can survive his scoring night. The blowout risk cuts both ways — if the Lakers run away at home, Doncic sits the fourth quarter, and 35.5 becomes a sweat. And his 5.0 turnovers a game are a reminder that high-usage nights can go sideways. But six straight games of 37-plus, at plus money, against a shorthanded defense he just torched, is the side with the edge.

The pick

Luka Doncic Over 35.5 points (+102). The best scorer in the league, in a revenge spot, at plus money. That is the bet.
