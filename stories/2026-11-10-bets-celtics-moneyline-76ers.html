TSL Bets: Celtics moneyline (-108) at 76ers — the healthier team at a coin-flip price

By Matt

The Celtics are 8-2, on a three-game win streak, and healthier than a 9-2 76ers team missing three rotation players. Boston moneyline at -108 is the play in Philadelphia tonight.

The game

Boston Celtics (8-2) at Philadelphia 76ers (9-2) — 8:00 PM ET, Xfinity Mobile Arena, NBC

76ers -1.5, total 217.5, moneyline Celtics -108 (DraftKings via ESPN). The 76ers sit first in the East. The Celtics have won three straight, winning by an average of 10.7 points over their last ten.

Injuries — Boston: No one on the main report. Devin Carter is out long-term with mononucleosis, but the rotation is intact.

Injuries — Philadelphia: Dominick Barlow is out with a back injury, Adem Bona is out with a cervical spine sprain, and Ariel Hukporti is out for the season with an Achilles injury. Joel Embiid is cleared and not on the injury report — he played Sunday, putting up 18 points and 10 rebounds against Chicago.

Storylines: This one has a subplot worth the price of admission: Jaylen Brown faces his former team for the first time since the July trade that sent him to Boston in the Brown-George swap. LeBron James signed with Philadelphia as a free agent in the offseason, and the 76ers have built a four-man attack around him, Embiid, Tyrese Maxey and the rest. Tatum is averaging 27.1 points a game; Maxey is at 26.9. Both teams are rolling — the question is whose depth holds up over 48 minutes.

The bet

Celtics moneyline (-108). This is a near coin-flip price on the better, healthier, deeper team. Boston's rotation is intact, Philadelphia is down three rotation players, and the Celtics have been winning by double digits. The 76ers are 9-2 and first in the East for a reason, and their home record is real — but at -108, you're getting the better team at a price that says the game is a toss-up. Take the better team at a toss-up price.

The honest caveat

Philadelphia is 9-2 at home with a four-man attack that can score from anywhere, and Embiid is healthy and back in the lineup. If LeBron and Maxey both go off, Boston's depth edge disappears fast. And this is a rivalry game with a revenge subplot — Brown facing his old team cuts both ways. The Celtics are the pick, but this is a one-possession game on paper, and the price says so.

The pick

Celtics moneyline (-108). A $10 ticket returns about $19.26. The healthier team, the deeper team, at a coin-flip price.
