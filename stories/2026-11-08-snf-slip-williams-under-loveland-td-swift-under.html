TSL Bets: SNF slip — Williams under 250.5, Loveland TD, Swift under 65.5 (about +790)

By Matt

The Sunday night slip is a game-script bet: Caleb Williams under 250.5 passing yards (-125) against the league's ninth-ranked pass defense, Colston Loveland to score (+165), and D'Andre Swift under 65.5 rushing yards (-115). Three legs, about +790.

The game

Tampa Bay Buccaneers (3-5) at Chicago Bears (6-2) — 8:20 PM ET, Soldier Field, NBC

Bears -6, total 47.5, moneyline Bears -270 / Buccaneers +220 (optimal-bet, DraftKings). The Bears have won three straight since Caleb Williams returned from injury — the last two against last season's Super Bowl teams, including a 34-17 win over the defending champions in Seattle on Monday night. Chicago is 6-2 against the spread and averages 387.3 total yards per game. Tampa Bay averages 300.0 total yards per game and relies on the ground game, with 168.6 rushing yards per contest against just 147.5 passing yards.

Injuries: Tampa Bay's Emeka Egbuka is good to go after practicing all week, and Bucky Irving is healthy — he ran for 170 yards last week against Atlanta. Baker Mayfield is having a miserable year by his standards: a career-worst 5.9 adjusted yards per attempt and just three touchdown passes across five games. On the Chicago side, Williams, Swift, and Loveland are all active with no injury designation reported this week — Williams missed three games earlier this season with a hamstring injury but has been hot since returning.

Storylines: Williams is 70.9% completions with six touchdowns and one interception, averaging 253.4 yards per game and 8.5 adjusted yards per attempt — legitimate MVP buzz when he is on the field. But Tampa Bay's pass defense ranks ninth in the league at 205.9 yards allowed per game, and the Bucs lead the league in time of possession with a run-heavy approach (53.5% handoffs over the last four games). The Bears are 6-point favorites at home on Sunday night, and the market expects Chicago to win — the question is how.

The legs

Leg 1 — Caleb Williams under 250.5 passing yards (-125)

Shop for the 250.5 — the consensus line is 245.5 at DraftKings and FanDuel, 246.5 at bet365, and 248.5 at BetMGM, but 250.5 is available at some books. Williams is hot: 305, 308, and 247 yards in his last three games, and he has thrown for 247 or more in each of his last six night games against NFC opponents. That is the honest caveat, and it is a real one. But the game script points under: Tampa Bay's pass defense is ninth in the league, the Bucs chew clock with the run and lead the league in time of possession, and the Bears are 6-point favorites who can win this game on the ground. OPTIMAL projects Williams at 245.7 yards. This is a game-script bet, not a talent bet — Williams is great, and the conditions still point under.

Leg 2 — Colston Loveland anytime touchdown (+165, BetMGM)

Loveland at plus money is the value leg. He is Williams' red-zone security blanket, and the Bucs' defense has been vulnerable to tight ends in the red zone all season. At +165, this is the leg that turns a solid slip into a +790 slip. If the Bears score through the air, Loveland is the most likely name on the scoresheet after the receivers — and the price says the market has not caught up to his role yet.

Leg 3 — D'Andre Swift under 65.5 rushing yards (-115, BetMGM)

Swift has 545 rushing yards and 10 rushing touchdowns in eight games, but the volume and efficiency tell a different story than the touchdown total. His game log: 124, 45, 84, 58, 43, 44, 78, 69 — under 65.5 in five of eight games. He is averaging 3.8 yards per carry, and last week against Seattle he needed 27 carries to get 69 yards. The Bears are favorites who will run the ball, but Swift's share of that work is not as big as the rushing total suggests, and the Bucs' run defense has been stout enough to hold him under this number.

Why this slip

Three legs, one game, one story: the Bears win a game that stays under the number. Williams throws for fewer than 251 yards because Tampa Bay controls the clock and the Bears lean on the run. Swift stays under 65.5 because his efficiency is poor and the workload is shared. Loveland scores because the Bears still need to finish drives, and he is the red-zone target. The legs fit together — if the game goes the way the slip needs it to, all three hit.

The honest caveat

Williams is the risk. He has thrown for 247 or more in six straight night games against NFC opponents, he is coming off 305 and 308, and he is playing at an MVP level. If he gets hot early, the under is dead by halftime. Loveland at +165 is plus money for a reason — tight end touchdowns are the most volatile prop on the board. And Swift has 10 rushing touchdowns in eight games; if he finds the end zone early, the Bears may feed him the ball. The slip pays about +790 because the Williams under is a bet against a quarterback who is playing the best football of his life.

The pick

3-leg SNF slip, about +790: Caleb Williams under 250.5 passing yards (-125) + Colston Loveland anytime TD (+165) + D'Andre Swift under 65.5 rushing yards (-115). A $10 ticket returns about $89.20. A game-script bet on Sunday night: the Bears win, the clock runs, and the numbers stay under. That is the bet.
