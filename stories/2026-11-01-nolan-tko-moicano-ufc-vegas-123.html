Tom Nolan Stops Renato Moicano in Round 3 to Headline UFC Vegas 123

By Matt

Tom Nolan extended his UFC win streak to six with a third-round TKO of Renato Moicano in the UFC Vegas 123 main event on Halloween night, earning a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus.

Tom Nolan's rise is no longer a quiet one. The Australian lightweight — "Big Train" — stopped veteran Renato Moicano by TKO at 1:28 of round three in the main event of UFC Vegas 123 on Saturday night at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, extending his UFC win streak to six and pocketing a $100,000 Performance of the Night bonus.

The finish came after Nolan battered Moicano late in round two. The referee stood the fighters up, Nolan took control on the feet, and a storm of unanswered strikes early in the third forced the stoppage. Moicano, a longtime lightweight staple, had no answer once Nolan found his range.

What it means

Six straight wins in the UFC's deepest division gets you noticed. Nolan entered the night as a rising prospect; he leaves it as a genuine lightweight problem — a big, aggressive finisher who has now stopped a ranked veteran on a main-event stage. The UFC's Halloween card was built as a showcase for exactly this kind of statement, and Nolan delivered the loudest one on it.

In the co-main event, undefeated welterweight Jacobe Smith kept his perfect record intact with a dominant unanimous decision over Randy Brown (30-26, 30-26, 30-26), bloodying the veteran with elbows across all three rounds. Smith's win keeps his own hype train rolling, but the night belonged to the main event.

The rest of the card

The undercard delivered finishes of its own: Jean-Paul Lebosnoyani stopped Farman Hasanov by doctor's stoppage in round three, Rodrigo Sezinando did the same to Theodor Berggren in round two, Lucia Szabova scored a violent third-round TKO over Tainara Lisboa in her official UFC debut, and Joe Kropschot submitted Nick Klein with an inverted triangle armbar in round two. All four finishers took home bonuses — Nolan, Szabova and Kropschot earned $100,000 Performance of the Night checks, with Lebosnoyani and Sezinando collecting $25,000 finish bonuses.

For Moicano, the loss is a hard reset. For Nolan, it's an arrival. The lightweight division has a new name to reckon with, and he just announced himself on Halloween night.
