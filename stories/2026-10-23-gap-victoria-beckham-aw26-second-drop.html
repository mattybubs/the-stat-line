Gap x Victoria Beckham's Second Collection (AW26) Drops Today

By Matt

The 37-piece Fall/Winter 2026 chapter of the multi-season partnership is live at 9 a.m. ET — knit-first this time, with cashmere and merino pullovers, CashSoft knits, oversized parkas and Chesterfield coats, $28–$368.

Gap x Victoria Beckham is back. The 37-piece AW26 collection — the planned second chapter of the multi-season partnership that debuted in April 2026 and reportedly sold out fast — is live today at 9 a.m. ET / 2 p.m. BST on gap.co.uk and select stores globally.

The Drop

• $28–$368, knit-first this time: cashmere and merino pullovers, Gap's CashSoft knits, oversized parkas, wool-blend Chesterfield coats

• Plus low-slung baggy denim with drawstring details

• Palette drawn from the Doris and Donald Fisher collection at SFMOMA — charcoal, deep greens, warm browns, vampy reds

• Campaign stars model Mona Tougaard, shot by Glen Luchford

The first drop sold out fast. Chapter two goes knit-heavy into fall — if it's anything like April, the parkas won't sit.
