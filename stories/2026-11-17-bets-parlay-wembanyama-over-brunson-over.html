TSL Bets parlay: Wembanyama OVER 27.5 points + Brunson OVER 25.5 points (~+254)

By Matt

Two props, two scoring stars, one parlay: Victor Wembanyama OVER 27.5 points (-117) against a Pistons frontcourt missing Jalen Duren, plus Jalen Brunson OVER 25.5 points (-110) at Toronto — about +254 combined.

The game slip — Pistons at Spurs

Detroit Pistons (8-4) at San Antonio Spurs (12-2)

Tuesday, 8:00 PM ET — Frost Bank Center, San Antonio — NBC

• Spread: Spurs -9.5

• Total: 221.5 (FanDuel) / 222.5 (DraftKings)

• Odds source: LineSmart / DraftKings, November 17

Injury report — Pistons: C Jalen Duren OUT (hamstring, 10th straight game). F Duncan Robinson OUT (quad). F Ron Holland II OUT (knee). F Taurean Prince OUT (knee). G Isaiah Joe doubtful (hip).

Injury report — Spurs: F Tobias Harris OUT (calf). C Jayden Quaintance OUT (knee). G De'Aaron Fox available (off the injury report).

Storylines: The Spurs are 12-2 on a 12-game winning streak. Wembanyama averages 28.6 points, 11.6 rebounds and a league-best 4.3 blocks per game, and his last five outings against a 28.5-point line read 29, 40, 32, 12 and 26. Detroit's frontcourt is gutted without Duren, its starting center and best rim protector.

New York Knicks (7-6) at Toronto Raptors (5-8)

Tuesday, 7:30 PM ET — Scotiabank Arena, Toronto

• Spread: Knicks -2.5 / -3.5

• Total: 216.5

Injury report — Knicks: Nobody on the injury report.

Injury report — Raptors: F Kawhi Leonard OUT (knee). C Nate Bittle OUT (foot). F Scottie Barnes available.

Storylines: Brunson is projected for 26.4 points against a 25.5 line, has gone over 26.5 in 4 of his last 5 and over 25.5 in 6 of his last 10, and scored 28 on his last visit to Toronto. The Knicks come in fully healthy against a Raptors side missing its best two-way player.

The bet

Victor Wembanyama OVER 27.5 points (-117, DraftKings) + Jalen Brunson OVER 25.5 points (-110, BetRivers) — about +254 combined

Why these legs

Wembanyama first. He averages 28.6 points a night against a 27.5 line, and the matchup tilts his way: Jalen Duren — Detroit's starting center — is out for a 10th straight game with a hamstring injury, leaving the Pistons without their rim protection against a 7-foot-4 scorer who is also the league's blocks leader. Three of his last five games cleared the line by real margin (29, 40, 32).

Brunson second. The projection sits at 26.4 against a 25.5 line, he has cleared 26.5 in 4 of his last 5, and he put up 28 the last time he played in Toronto. The Knicks have a clean injury report while the Raptors are down Kawhi Leonard, and Brunson is the entire engine of New York's half-court offense.

Both legs clear on their own: a scoring star against a depleted frontcourt, and a hot guard against a shorthanded defense. Neither leg is a heavy favorite, and the pair pays about +254 — gambling-ish, not chalky.

The honest caveats

Wembanyama's last five include a 12-point night — the variance is real, and a Spurs blowout (they're laying 9.5) could trim his minutes the same way it could help the over. Brunson's line has moved between 25.5 and 26.5 across books, so the number matters — take it at 25.5, not higher. And a parlay needs both: two good legs are still two chances to lose. The edge is in each leg individually; the parlay just prices it up.
