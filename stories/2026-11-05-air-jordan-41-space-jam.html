Air Jordan 41 'Space Jam' drops today as the 30th anniversary collection tips off

By Elijah

The Air Jordan 41 'Space Jam' leads the first wave of Jordan Brand's 30th anniversary Space Jam collection today — $205, grey mesh with a light blue lattice and a glow-in-the-dark outsole.

What happened

The Air Jordan 41 "Space Jam" drops today, November 5, leading the first wave of Jordan Brand's 30th anniversary Space Jam collection. It retails for $205 (style code HV6474-003) through Jordan Brand and select retailers.

The shoe is grey mesh with a light blue lattice, a glow-in-the-dark blue outsole, and a 30th anniversary insole. It is the flagship of a campaign Jordan Brand is calling "Greatness Is Never Out of Reach," fronted by Azzi Fudd, Aaron Judge, Rema, and Teyana Taylor — plus a six-part documentary, Space Jam: Beyond the Jam, on Jordan Brand's YouTube channel.

The rest of the first wave

The Jordan 41 is not dropping alone. The first wave also includes:

• Air Jordan 1 Mid SE "Space Jam" — $140

• Air Jordan 1 Mid "Space Jam" — $165

• Air Jordan 1 Low "Lola Bunny" — $130

• Air Jordan 11 Mid MCS Cleat "Space Jam" — $225 to $255 depending on retailer

One honest note: some release trackers flag that the Jordan 41's date has not been fully locked in, so check SNKRS the morning of the drop. The November 5 date is listed across multiple release calendars, but this one has moved before.

What else drops today

November 5 is a stacked day beyond the Space Jam collection:

• Nike Air Force 1 Low Premium "What The LA" — $125

• Nike Air Max Foamdome "Electro Purple" — $265

• Nike Air Foamposite Pro college pack — FSU, LSU, Ohio State, and TCU colorways at $250 each (some listings show $240)

• atmos x adidas ZX 8000 "G-SNK Chlorophyll" — through atmos, with the raffle open on the atmos Tokyo site. Green mesh and leather with glow-in-the-dark snakeskin, a callback to atmos' 2011 "Aqua" ZX 8000.

Why this one matters

Thirty years on, Space Jam is still the most reliable heat Jordan Brand has. The original 1996 film made the black-and-royal Jordan 11 a grail, and every anniversary revisit since has sold through. This time the brand is leading with a brand-new silhouette — the 41 — instead of a retro, which is a bet that the Space Jam name alone carries the shoe.

At $205 with a glow outsole and a full campaign behind it, this is the November drop to watch. If the 41 moves the way Jordan Brand hopes, expect the rest of the 30th anniversary collection to follow fast.
