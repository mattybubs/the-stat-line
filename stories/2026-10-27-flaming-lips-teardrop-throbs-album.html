The Flaming Lips Announce 'The Teardrop Throbs' — Their First Album in Nearly Seven Years

By Matt

The Flaming Lips will release their 17th album, 'The Teardrop Throbs,' on January 22 via Third Man Records — their first new music since 2020's 'American Head' and their first without longtime members Michael Ivins and Steven Drozd.

The announcement

The Flaming Lips are back. The band announced their 17th studio album, The Teardrop Throbs, arriving January 22 via Third Man Records — their debut release on Jack White's label and their first new album since 2020's American Head.

The first single

Lead single "God Will Control the Werewolves" is out now. Frontman Wayne Coyne says the song grew out of a game of hide-and-seek with his two sons, easing their fears about wolves in the woods under a full moon with the promise that God would control the werewolves for a little while.

Coyne says the album is inspired by fatherhood above all: "There will never be anything in my life as important as my two little boys."

The tour

To support the album, the band will play two-night stands in Nashville (January 22–23), New York (January 26–27), Los Angeles (February 23–24), Chicago (February 27–28) and London (April 2–3). Night one pairs the new album in full with songs from 1986 to 1999; night two reimagines the new material alongside songs from 2002 to 2020.

Why it matters

• Nearly seven years between albums is the longest wait of the band's career — and the first without Drozd, a member since 1991.

• A Third Man debut puts the Lips on a label built for exactly this kind of left-field institution.

• The two-night format — new album straight through, then reimagined — is a real event, not a victory lap.
