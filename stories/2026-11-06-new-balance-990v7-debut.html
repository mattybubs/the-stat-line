New Balance 990v7 debuts today at $200

By Elijah

The seventh generation of New Balance's flagship 990 line arrives today — the Made in USA 990v7 in 'Kool Gray' for $200, with a campaign fronted by ROSALÍA, Cooper Flagg, and Josh Allen.

What happened

The New Balance Made in USA 990v7 debuts today, November 6. The seventh generation of the brand's flagship 990 franchise launches in the "Kool Gray" colorway for $200, available at 10 a.m. ET through New Balance and select retailers.

This is the drop the sneaker calendar has been building toward all month — DraftKings Network calls it the top sneaker drop of November 2026, and it is easy to see why. The 990 is the shoe that built New Balance's reputation with runners, dads, and fashion crowds all at once, and a new generation does not come around often.

The shoe

The 990v7 keeps the series' familiar suede-and-mesh construction — pig suede, tight-knit mesh, breathable mesh toe boxes — and streamlines the upper with updated proportions. Underneath, the cushioning setup pairs FuelCell with ENCAP, the two technologies New Balance has been building its performance line around.

The launch colorway, Kool Gray (style code U990GR7), is draped in the grey the brand is known for: Kool Gray, Calcium, and Light Arctic Gray, with a dark grey tumbled leather heel tab and a red "V7" lace holder on the leather tongue. It is, deliberately, the most New Balance-looking shoe New Balance could launch with — grey on grey on grey.

The campaign behind it is star-heavy: ROSALÍA, Cooper Flagg, Josh Allen, and IU front the launch. That is a deliberately broad net — pop, basketball, football, and K-pop — for a shoe that has always sold across tribes.

Also dropping today

November 6 is stacked beyond the 990v7:

• Nike SB Dunk Low "Champagne" — $130 at select skate shops, with a wider SNKRS drop to follow. A green patent-leather Dunk dressed like a bottle of champagne — dark green, metallic gold, a little cork under the surface — marking the run-up to the SB Dunk's 25th anniversary in 2027. Style code IQ0431-300.

• Kith x adidas Stan Smith Ballet — three coffee-toned colorways (Brown, Aurora Coffee, Chalky Brown) with the "Kith and Kin" crest on the heel, dropping at 11 a.m. across New York, London, Paris, and Tokyo time zones via Kith stores, site, and app.

• Reebok DMX Trailzillo — two general-release colorways (blue/orange and black/white/neon green) at $120 via Reebok, an archive-inspired trail hybrid with the line's moving-air cushioning.

Why this one matters

The 990 line is New Balance's crown jewel, and the v6 had a long, beloved run. A new generation is a statement that the brand thinks it can improve on a classic without breaking what made it one — and the grey-on-grey launch colorway is a bet that the core audience wants evolution, not reinvention.

At $200, it is also priced like the flagship it is. If the Kool Gray sells through today, expect the 990v7 to anchor New Balance's holiday season the way the v6 did for years.
