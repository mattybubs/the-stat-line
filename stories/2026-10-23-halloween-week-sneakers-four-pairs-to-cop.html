Halloween-Week Sneakers: Four Pairs to Cop Through Saturday

By Matt

Halloween week is the season's best sneaker stretch — a zombie Wemby GT Cut 4 with a mask in the box, Jordan's cozy Fireside pack, and two new Pharrell Jellyfish colorways all drop before Saturday.

Halloween week is the season's best sneaker stretch, with four real drops landing between today and Saturday: Nike's spooky-season basketball pairs, Jordan's cozy Fireside pack, and two new Pharrell Adistar Jellyfish colorways (IAHHM).

The one to chase: Wemby GT Cut 4 "Halloween"

The Victor Wembanyama x Nike GT Cut 4 "Halloween" is the week's crown jewel — and the one that won't sit. The details (IAHHM, SI):

• Release: Friday, October 23 — today

• Price: $210, style code IX5395-300

• Upper: Layered glossy Vapour Green with a black Swoosh, the Wemby "Alien" logo abstracted across it

• Tongue: Zombie-style graphic of Wemby with green skin and yellow teeth

• Box: Comes with a wearable Halloween mask of the same face

• Sizes: Adult sizes only, and Sneaker Files reports Nike cut production

It's the centerpiece of Nike Basketball's Halloween 2026 collection — ten athletes, ten costumes, each pair shipped with its own wearable mask. The rest of the lineup rolls out on a rolling schedule: LeBron's Pennywise-inspired 24 "Storm Drain," KD's skeletal "Slim Reaper," Sabrina Ionescu's vampire "Count Ionescu," Ja Morant's zombie Ja 4, and more (The Spun).

Jordan's Fireside pack

Jordan Brand counters spooky with cozy. The Air Jordan 12 "Fireside" lands Saturday, October 24 at $215 (style CT8013-105) in Light Orewood Brown with a smooth Cacao Wow nubuck mudguard, metallic brown eyelets, and Team Red hits — full family sizing from adult down to toddler, so the whole crew can match (IAHHM).

The strategy

Halloween drops that come with a story — a mask, a costume, a personal fear — move faster than generic orange-and-black colorways. Nike leaned into storytelling this year and every pair in the ten-model collection has its own lore. With Wemby's cut production and adult-sizes-only run, that GT Cut 4 is the pair that goes first.
