Devils Land Connor Hellebuyck in Blockbuster Trade With Jets

By Matt

The saga is over — the Devils acquired three-time Vezina winner Connor Hellebuyck from Winnipeg for Dawson Mercer, Nico Daws, Alexander Command, Nick Bjugstad and two picks. The Olympic gold-medal goalie reunites with Jack Hughes in New Jersey.

The Connor Hellebuyck saga finally ended Friday — in New Jersey.

The Trade

• Devils get: G Connor Hellebuyck

• Jets get: F Dawson Mercer, F Nick Bjugstad, C Alexander Command, G Nico Daws, 2029 second-round pick, 2027 seventh-round pick

• Announced by Devils GM Sunny Mehta; trade call complete Friday afternoon (TSN's Darren Dreger first reported)

Why It Matters

• Hellebuyck, 33, is a three-time Vezina Trophy winner (2019-20, 2023-24, 2024-25) and the 2023-24 Hart Trophy winner as league MVP

• Career: 345-208-55, 2.58 GAA, .916 SV%, 45 shutouts in 625 games — all with Winnipeg

• Fresh off backstopping Team USA to Olympic gold in February (.956 SV%, 1.18 GAA, tournament Best Goaltender) — the Americans' first hockey gold in 46 years

• Signed through 2030-31 at $8.5M; the Devils had just $1.16M in projected cap space (PuckPedia), so more moves may be coming

The Backstory

Hellebuyck went public with his trade request in August, skipped Jets camp, and was hit with an unpaid suspension that cost him roughly $925,000 in forfeited salary. The suspension was set to lift Friday — instead, he got traded.

The Fit

New Jersey's goaltending has been the weak link: Jake Allen (.883) and Nico Daws (.874) combined for roughly an .880 team save percentage through 10 games. Hellebuyck immediately becomes the starter under Sheldon Keefe, with Allen shifting to backup.

It's also a gold-medal reunion — Hellebuyck teams up again with Jack Hughes, who scored the Olympic winner in February.

The Read

Mehta said in September he was "reluctant to invest significant assets" in goaltending because of "randomness at the position." Then he traded two roster forwards, a top prospect, a goalie and two picks for the best goalie of the decade. When a Hart-winning goalie hits the market at 33, philosophy goes out the window. The Devils (6-4-0) just went all-in.
