Modern Warfare 4's Multiplayer Is Live Today

By Matt

Competitive multiplayer opened today on PS5, Xbox, PC, and — for the first time in over a decade — Switch 2. October 19 figures had it at 92% Very Positive on Steam, the best-rated Call of Duty in over a decade.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is fully live. Competitive multiplayer opened today on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Switch 2 — the first Call of Duty on a Nintendo platform in over a decade (Pixel Herald).

The rollout

Activision staggered the launch: pre-orders got the campaign on October 16, then the DMZ extraction mode opened October 20 (Stevivor), and today the full competitive multiplayer suite unlocked for everyone. The Switch 2 launch is the milestone — Fairgate Report has it headlining October's busiest release week.

The reception

Early numbers are the story. October 19 Eurogamer figures put Modern Warfare 4 at a Very Positive 92 percent on Steam — the highest of any Call of Duty in over a decade. That's a genuine turnaround narrative for a franchise whose recent entries have been review-battered.

Why it matters

A Call of Duty that lands at 92% on Steam changes the conversation around annual shooters. DMZ's return as a full extraction mode alongside the classic multiplayer suite gives the game three distinct ways to play — campaign, competitive, and extraction — which is part of why the Steam numbers look the way they do.
