God of War Laufey delayed to May 20, 2027

By Matt

Santa Monica Studio pushed God of War Laufey from February 16 to May 20, 2027 — three more months of polish for Faye's first outing as the series lead, still a PS5 exclusive.

What happened

Santa Monica Studio is delaying God of War Laufey by three months. The game was set to launch February 16, 2027. It will now arrive May 20, 2027, still as a PlayStation 5 exclusive.

The studio announced the move Wednesday, November 4, in a short statement on X. No specific problem was named — the extra time, the studio said, goes to "putting in the finishing touches and pushing the game to its fullest potential." More updates are promised soon, with no date attached to them.

The timeline

This one moved fast from reveal to release date, which makes the delay sting a little less — and a little more.

• June 2026: Revealed at PlayStation's State of Play, closing the show. Faye — Laufey, Kratos' wife and Atreus' mother — wakes after her own funeral in the Everywhen, an afterlife where gods from different mythologies cross paths. Deborah Ann Woll plays Faye; Jack Quaid voices Phranque, a gelatinous cube traveling with her.

• July 2026: The February 16, 2027 date is confirmed at San Diego Comic-Con.

• September 29, 2026: Pre-orders open, with a Standard Edition and a Digital Deluxe Edition.

• November 4, 2026: The delay to May 20, 2027 — 93 days later.

That is a quick turnaround from announcement to launch by modern standards, and the February date lasted barely four months. If you pre-ordered, the studio's statement says nothing about pre-order terms, bonuses, or editions changing — treat those as unchanged until Sony says otherwise.

Why it matters

Laufey is the first God of War where you do not play Kratos. Faye leads, with a legendary sword, a Serpent's Bow, and combat built around speed and vertical movement — a deliberate break from the weightier Norse games. The reveal trailer ran more than 20 minutes of uncut gameplay, and it introduced two gods standing in Faye's way: Sekhmet and Begtse.

Santa Monica has done this before. God of War Ragnarök slipped from 2021 to November 2022 under similar "polish" framing, and it went on to be one of the best-reviewed games of its year. A 93-day delay this late in the cycle reads like a game that is content-complete and needs optimization and bug fixes — not a rescue job.

The new date also buys breathing room. February 2027 is crowded — Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis lands February 12, Final Fantasy XIV: Evercold on February 23 — and Sony's own Until Dawn 2 arrives January 28. May is, for now, a much quieter month.
