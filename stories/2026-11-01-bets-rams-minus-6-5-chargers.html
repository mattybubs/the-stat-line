Rams -6.5: A Winless Chargers Team Meets the Wrong Offense

By Matt

The Chargers are 0-6, last in the NFL in scoring, and down their best defensive player. The Rams are 3-4 with Matthew Stafford rolling. Lay the 6.5 at SoFi.

The game slip

Matchup: Los Angeles Chargers (0-6) at Los Angeles Rams (3-4)

Kickoff: Sunday, November 1, 4:05 PM ET — FOX, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood

Line: Rams -6.5 | Total 45.5 | Moneyline: Rams -278, Chargers +225 (via Statsalt)

Chargers injury report: OUT — S Derwin James Jr. (hamstring), WR Brenen Thompson (quad), CB Deane Leonard (concussion), G Kayode Awosika (leg). Questionable — CB Donte Jackson (hamstring). Returning — RT Joe Alt (neck) and WR Ladd McConkey (concussion) are expected back after the bye.

Rams injury report: OUT — WR Davante Adams (hamstring surgery), DE Myles Garrett (knee, IR), DT Kobie Turner (IR), DT Poona Ford (IR). Doubtful — LS Joe Cardona (ankle). Questionable — CB Trent McDuffie (groin), WR Jordan Whittington (back), TE Colby Parkinson (rib), C Coleman Shelton (hip).

Storylines driving the bet:

1. The Chargers are the NFL's only winless team and rank dead last in scoring at 15.0 points per game. Justin Herbert has thrown 5 touchdowns against 8 interceptions and taken 21 sacks behind a battered line.

2. Matthew Stafford leads the Rams' attack with 1,989 passing yards and 14 touchdowns, with Puka Nacua as the clear focal point while Adams is out. The Rams just beat the Raiders and are playing to get back to .500.

3. The Chargers' one real hope is the bye-week reset: Alt and McConkey return, and the Rams' defensive front is missing its biggest names. But a healthier Chargers offense still has to score against a team that can put up points in bunches.

The bet

Rams -6.5. The number is fair, not generous — and that's the point. This isn't a trick play. The Chargers have lost six straight, they score 15 points a game, and their best defensive player is out. The Rams don't need a perfect game to win by a touchdown; they need their normal one. Stafford at home, against a defense missing Derwin James, with Kyren Williams and Blake Corum to keep the chains moving — that's a recipe for a two-score margin.

The honest caveat: the Rams are banged up on defense too, Garrett and both starting defensive tackles are out, and a post-bye Chargers team with Alt and McConkey back is the most dangerous version of this team we've seen. If Herbert finally gets clean protection, a backdoor cover is live late. But the gap between these two teams right now is wider than 6.5 points, and I'm laying it.
