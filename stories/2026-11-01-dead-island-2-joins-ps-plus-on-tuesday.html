Dead Island 2 joins PlayStation Plus on Tuesday.

The November batch

Dead Island 2 arrives in a November batch that technically counts as five games — the extra count comes from a bundled collection in the batch, not from five separate standalone titles. If you have been waiting to try it, Tuesday is the day it lands in the lineup.

What to do before it arrives

The current batch is replaced when the new one arrives. That means the window on the outgoing games closes as the November batch comes in — if there is anything in the current batch you still want, claim it before the swap.
