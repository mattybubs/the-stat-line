Tristan Luneau's goal two minutes into the third period put Anaheim ahead for good in a 5-4 win at Philadelphia. Matvei Michkov scored twice for the Flyers, giving him eight goals this season.

The turning point

Luneau struck early in the third — just two minutes in — and that was enough. The goal put Anaheim ahead for good, and the Ducks held on for the 5-4 win on the road.

Final score: Ducks 5, Flyers 4

Decisive goal: Tristan Luneau, two minutes into the third period

Flyers standout: Matvei Michkov, 2 goals (8 this season)

Michkov's night, in defeat

Two goals from Michkov kept Philadelphia in it, and pushed his season total to eight. It was not enough on a night when Anaheim found the go-ahead goal early in the third and made it stand up.
