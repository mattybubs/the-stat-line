Nikola Jokic Records His 200th Triple-Double as the Nuggets Beat the Pelicans 104-91

By Matt

Nikola Jokic posted 21 points, 13 rebounds and 10 assists in Denver's 104-91 win at New Orleans — his 200th career regular-season triple-double, making him just the second player in NBA history to reach 200.

The milestone

Final: Denver Nuggets 104, New Orleans Pelicans 91 — Wednesday, October 28, 2026, in New Orleans.

Nikola Jokic recorded his 200th career regular-season triple-double, finishing with 21 points, 13 rebounds and 10 assists on 8-of-13 shooting from the field and a perfect 5-of-5 from the free-throw line. He is only the second player in NBA history to reach 200 triple-doubles, joining Russell Westbrook, who retired after last season with 209. Jokic is nine away from tying Westbrook's record and ten away from passing it outright.

The milestone came two nights after Jokic finished three rebounds short of it against Golden State on Monday. It also came against the same team he posted No. 199 against: on Saturday, Jokic put up 35 points, 15 rebounds and 10 assists as Denver overcame a 23-point deficit to beat New Orleans in overtime in Denver.

How the game went

Denver never trailed. The Nuggets shot 62% in the first quarter while building a 37-18 lead, stretched the margin to 44-22 on a Marvin Bagley runner with about nine minutes left in the second quarter, and kept New Orleans at arm's length most of the way.

New Orleans made one real push: Zion Williamson's fourth-quarter three pulled the Pelicans as close as 88-83. Jamal Murray answered with a three and a driving finger roll to push the lead back to 10, and Denver closed it out.

Derik Queen (NOP): 11 points, 12 rebounds

Jeremiah Fears (NOP): 11 points

Denver, which used just nine players while dealing with several injuries, improved to 2-2 and swept the two-game, home-and-home series with New Orleans. The Pelicans fell to 0-4 under first-year coach Jamahl Mosley. The Pelicans shot just 7-of-30 (23.3%) from three-point range.

Jokic had already reached double figures in rebounds and assists by the end of the third quarter, grabbing five of his 13 rebounds in the fourth. It was not his cleanest night — he committed seven turnovers — but the history is what stands: 200 triple-doubles, exactly 11 years after his NBA debut, with Westbrook's all-time mark now in sight.
