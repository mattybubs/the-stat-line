Warriors win sixth straight over Heat

By Matt

Steph Curry shook off a cold first half, passed Oscar Robertson on the all-time scoring list, and dropped 29 as Golden State ran away from Miami 120-100 for its sixth straight win.

Steph Curry scored 29 points as the Golden State Warriors beat the Miami Heat 120-100 on Thursday night at Chase Center, extending their winning streak to six games after an 0-3 start to the season. The Warriors are now 6-3 — and, per Warriors Wire, tied with the San Antonio Spurs for the longest active winning streak in the NBA.

Down early again, then the third quarter happened

The pattern is becoming familiar: the Warriors fell behind early once more. Miami led 29-26 after the first quarter, and the game was tied 53-53 at halftime. Then Golden State blew it open.

The Warriors outscored the Heat 38-23 in the third quarter and won the second half 67-47, stretching the lead to 30 early in the fourth before Curry rested with the game out of hand.

Curry's night: slow start, scorching finish, history made

Curry began 1 for 8 from the field and was 2 of 10 at halftime. Then he caught fire: 22 of his 29 points came in the second half on 7-of-9 shooting, including 5 of 7 from three-point range. He finished 9 of 19 from the floor, 6 of 12 from deep, a perfect 5 of 5 from the line, with four assists, one rebound, and zero turnovers in 28 minutes.

The 29 points left him one short of a fourth straight 30-point game — he sat once the game was decided.

The bigger moment came early. With a three-pointer at the 7:03 mark of the first quarter, Curry passed Oscar Robertson (26,710 career points) to move into 17th place on the NBA's all-time scoring list. He finished the night with 26,739 career points. It's the second time in less than a week Curry has climbed the all-time list, after passing Dominique Wilkins for 18th.

Lendeborg keeps filling the box score

Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg stuffed the stat sheet again: 17 points, eight rebounds, six assists, and three steals — falling just short of a triple-double. Draymond Green drove the defensive effort against Giannis Antetokounmpo and chipped in a game-high 11 assists, a season high.

The bench was a big part of the story, too: Golden State's reserves outscored Miami's 53-27.

The Heat side

Giannis Antetokounmpo finished with 20 points and 14 rebounds for Miami, and Davion Mitchell led the Heat with a team-high 22 points. Bam Adebayo added 18.

Context matters for Miami, though: the Heat were playing the second night of a back-to-back after needing overtime to beat the Los Angeles Clippers 117-116 on Wednesday. They fall to 3-6.

There was a familiar face on the other side, too. Klay Thompson, in his first appearance at Chase Center as a member of the Heat, scored 11 points on 3-of-10 shooting with two three-pointers in 23 minutes.

What's next

Golden State hosts the Orlando Magic on Saturday night at Chase Center. Miami visits the Sacramento Kings on Sunday.
