Devils extinguish Flames in comeback

By The Stat Line

Jack Hughes piled up four points and his brother Luke scored twice as New Jersey erased a third-period deficit to beat Calgary on Black Friday.

Jack Hughes willed the New Jersey Devils past the Calgary Flames on Black Friday afternoon, finishing with a goal and three assists as New Jersey scored three unanswered goals in the third period to win 5-3 at Prudential Center in Newark.

The Devils trailed 3-2 entering the final frame and erased a pair of deficits on the day. It was their third straight win — and their league-leading 10th comeback victory of the season.

The Hughes brothers ran the show

Jack Hughes' night was the seventh four-plus-point game of his career, and the fourth of those to come in calendar year 2026. His brother Luke scored twice, including the empty-netter that sealed it, and the pair combined for six points and three goals.

Luke opened the scoring burst for New Jersey less than a minute into the second period, carrying the puck from the blue line to the left dot and picking the top far corner over Devin Cooley's glove. That was his eighth goal of the season, tying his brother Quinn for fourth-most by a defenseman in the NHL.

Twelve seconds later, Timo Meier buried a backdoor feed from Jack Hughes into a vacant cage — the fastest two goals of the season for New Jersey, flipping a 1-0 deficit into a 2-1 lead in the opening 68 seconds of the period.

Meier makes Swiss history

Meier's tally was the 249th of his career, making him Switzerland's all-time leading goal scorer in the NHL. He passed Nino Niederreiter, who sits at 248 — and who faces the Devils on Saturday.

How the third period turned

Calgary had reclaimed the lead before the second intermission. Matt Coronato tied it at 2-2, then Zach Whitecloud's rising wrister beat Jake Allen late in the period to put the Flames up 3-2.

Jack Hughes answered midway through the third with the goal of the game. He collected a pass in the neutral zone, sped around Whitecloud one-on-one, fell to one knee in the slot, and still got a shot off from a bad angle as his momentum carried him across the crease.

> "Not how I wanted to play the 1-on-1. (Whitecloud) gave me some gap because was on the ice, so I got around him. The second part, I don't really know." — Jack Hughes

With under four minutes to play, Connor Brown gave New Jersey the lead for good, poking home a loose puck at the crease after a pretty passing sequence between Jack Hughes and Meier. Luke Hughes added the empty-netter with 11 seconds left.

The win was nearly undone late: Jonas Siegenthaler took a tripping minor with 2:22 left, and the Devils' penalty kill — already down two regular killers in Johnathan Kovacevic and Brenden Dillon — held to preserve the lead.

What it means

The Devils keep stacking comeback wins — ten of them now, the most in the league — and this one came with a patched-up blue line. Vladislav Kolyochonok and Anton Silayev drew in with Dillon suspended and Declan Chisholm ill, and David Rittich was recalled to back up Allen, who was making his first start in 17 days and earned his first win since Nov. 7.

For Calgary, Yegor Sharangovich had two assists against his former team in a losing effort. The Flames fall to 11-14-2.

New Jersey is right back at it Saturday night at Prudential Center, hosting Winnipeg at 7:08 p.m. ET, with Connor Hellebuyck facing his former team.
