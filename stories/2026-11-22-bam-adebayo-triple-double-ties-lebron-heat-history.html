Bam Adebayo just tied LeBron James — and the Heat keep winning

By Matt

Bam Adebayo posted 24 points, 15 rebounds and 12 assists with zero turnovers in Miami's 123-113 win over Oklahoma City — his ninth career triple-double, tying LeBron James for the second-most in Heat history.

A triple-double with nothing wasted

Bam Adebayo did everything on Sunday night, and he did it without giving the ball away once.

Adebayo finished with 24 points, 15 rebounds and 12 assists in the Miami Heat's 123-113 win over the Oklahoma City Thunder at Kaseya Center — a triple-double with zero turnovers, plus two steals and three blocks in 37 minutes. He shot 8-of-11 from the field and 8-of-9 from the free-throw line.

The Heat never trailed. They led by 12 after the first quarter, by 13 at halftime, and by 15 going into the fourth. Oklahoma City kept pushing — Jared McCain had 20 points and Bennett Stirtz added 21 — but Miami had an answer every time, and most of the answers ran through Bam.

Tied with LeBron

The triple-double was the ninth of Adebayo's career. That ties LeBron James for the second-most triple-doubles in Heat history. Jimmy Butler leads the franchise with 13. Dwyane Wade is next at five.

It is the kind of company that says more than the box score. Adebayo is the Heat's all-time rebounding leader — he passed Udonis Haslem for that mark on October 29 — and now he sits alongside LeBron on another franchise list, four triple-doubles behind Butler.

The zero turnovers are the detail that makes this one special. Twelve assists, no giveaways, while grabbing 15 rebounds and guarding everything. That is a complete game from a center who runs the offense as much as he finishes it.

Seven in a row

The win is Miami's seventh straight. The Heat came into Sunday having won six in a row, including a 137-130 win over Milwaukee on Wednesday in which Giannis Antetokounmpo had 27 points and 14 rebounds and Adebayo added 26.

Pelle Larsson helped carry the scoring load on Sunday with 21 points on 9-of-14 shooting. The Heat are 10-6, and the streak has turned a good start into the best run in the East right now.

What it means

Miami's next game is Tuesday night at home against the Philadelphia 76ers, 5:00 PM ET. The streak will be on the line, and so will Adebayo's run of form — but the bigger story is what this version of Bam looks like. He is not just the Heat's defensive anchor and rebounding king anymore. He is the player the whole offense runs through, and on nights like this one, he does not make a mistake.
