Minamo, School at Water's Edge jumped 50% this week

By Matt

The Tales of Middle-earth Commander printing of Minamo, School at Water's Edge was Magic's biggest riser in Cardometer's week-46 report, up 50% to €1,717.50 — while the card's cheapest copies were actually falling ahead of its Star Trek reprint.

The week's biggest riser

Minamo, School at Water's Edge was the biggest riser in Magic: The Gathering in Cardometer's week-46 market report, covering November 9–15, 2026. The Tales of Middle-earth Commander printing climbed 50% against its 7-day average and now sits at €1,717.50.

The move is measured against the 7-day average of the Cardmarket trend, and the report covers 307,050 priced cards across 19 catalogues. Cardometer excludes cards under €5.00 and jumps above 50% — the kind of spike that usually traces back to a single sale — so this one landed exactly on the line.

The other side of the week

The biggest drop went the other way just as hard. Dragonlord Silumgar from Double Masters 2022 fell 50% in the same report, down to €5.16. One card's week, two opposite directions: a scarce Commander printing spiking, a recent Masters reprint sliding.

Why this printing, why now

Minamo is a legendary land from Champions of Kamigawa, illustrated by Jeremy Jarvis. It taps for blue mana with no drawback and untaps a legendary permanent for a single blue — a combination that has kept it a Commander staple for more than two decades.

The price action is about scarcity, not the card in general. This printing was a box topper in Tales of Middle-earth Commander, and Minamo had only ever been reprinted in The List and as that box topper before this week. So when demand ticks up, there is almost no supply to absorb it.

Tales of Middle-earth Commander (box topper): €1,717.50, up 50% this week

Cheapest printing overall: About $21.76 and falling

That split is the real story. The same week the box-topper printing jumped, the card's cheapest copies were sliding — Multiverse Dispatch tracked the cheapest printing falling from $56.53 to $23.74 on TCGplayer in the week ending November 11, below its low of the preceding month.

The reason is sitting on shelves right now. Minamo was just reprinted as Academy on the Bay in the Star Trek Stardates bonus sheet — 30 mythic reprints that launched with the Star Trek set on November 13, 2026. A fresh printing takes pressure off the cheapest copies, and the market is repricing accordingly. The old scarce printing, meanwhile, is moving on its own track: collectors chasing the original, not players chasing a copy to sleeve up.

What to watch

If you own the Tales of Middle-earth Commander printing, this is the kind of week that rewards holding and watching rather than rushing to list — a 50% weekly jump on a thinly traded printing can retrace fast. If you just want the card to play, the Stardates version is the cheap route in, and its price is still settling after release week.
