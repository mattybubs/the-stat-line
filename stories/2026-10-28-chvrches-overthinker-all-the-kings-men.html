Chvrches Drop 'Overthinker,' Second Single From 'All The King's Men' Out January 22

By Matt

Chvrches have released 'Overthinker,' the second single from their fifth album 'All The King's Men' (January 22, 2027) — debuted live at a 100-fan charity gig in Glasgow with Biffy Clyro's Simon Neil as surprise guest.

The single

Chvrches have dropped 'Overthinker,' the second single from their first album in nearly six years. Where lead single 'Roses' took aim at the state of modern Britain, 'Overthinker' turns inward — a song about complicated family dynamics, carried by Lauren Mayberry's sweeping vocal over driving synths.

"Should I lie to you? / Keep my thoughts from you?" Mayberry sings, describing a relationship where the person you're with is both "your shelter and your storm" — "You ask after my friends, if I'm getting thinner / Not sure how you raised such a chronic overthinker." The track has been named BBC Radio 1's Hottest Record.

The album

'All The King's Men' arrives January 22, 2027 via Futures Music Group — the Glasgow trio's fifth album and their first since 2021's 'Screen Violence.' The 11-track record was written and recorded in intensive three-week stints in Glasgow, ditching the remote process behind its predecessor, and pulls from British and Scottish influences ranging from Massive Attack and PJ Harvey to Nick Drake.

The gig

The single arrives after an intimate charity show at Assumption Studios in Glasgow on October 27 — the converted church where the album was recorded, played to just 100 fans with phones banned. Across a 14-song set, Chvrches debuted eight new songs from the album for the first time, and Biffy Clyro's Simon Neil joined as surprise guest for two unreleased tracks plus a rendition of Biffy's 'Machines.' A second intimate gig follows in London on October 29.

The tour

A full tour across the UK, Ireland and North America accompanies the album, kicking off February 13 in Philadelphia, running through Washington, Chicago and Los Angeles, then heading across the Atlantic in April and May — including London's O2 Shepherd's Bush Empire on May 6. Tickets go on sale Friday, October 30 at 10:00 a.m. local time via Ticketmaster.

Why it matters

• Six years after 'Screen Violence,' the comeback is now a full campaign: two singles, a dated album, and a tour — not a tease.

• 'Overthinker' shows the other side of the record: less societal pressure, more personal tension — and Mayberry writing at her most direct.

• The Glasgow gig is the kind of room that makes a return feel real: 100 fans, no phones, eight new songs, one surprise guest.
