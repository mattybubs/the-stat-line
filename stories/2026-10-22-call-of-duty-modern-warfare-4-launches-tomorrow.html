Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 Drops Tomorrow

By Matt

Full launch Friday on PS5, Xbox, PC and Switch 2 — campaign, 12 core multiplayer maps, and the return of DMZ. $69.99 standard, and no, it's not on Game Pass.

The biggest shooter of the fall goes live tomorrow.

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launches Friday, October 23 on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2. West Coast players get in at 9pm PT tonight thanks to the staggered rollout.

What's in the box

• Campaign: a North Korea–South Korea war at the center, with missions in New York, Paris and Mumbai. You play Private Park, a young South Korean soldier seeing combat for the first time, plus a Captain Price storyline running outside the system he once served.

• Multiplayer: 12 core 6v6 maps at launch, Gunfight's return, large-scale Big War, and a new map called Kill Block with over 500 dynamic layout configurations. Notable gameplay change: hipfire bloom is gone.

• DMZ: the extraction mode is back. Early access opened Oct. 20; it unlocks for everyone at launch.

The business

Standard edition is $69.99, Vault Edition $99.99 (cosmetics, weapon content, a BlackCell season, DMZ bonuses). And a heads-up for Xbox players: MW4 is not on Game Pass at launch — Activision says don't expect it until holiday 2027 at the earliest.

Campaign early access has been live since Oct. 16 for digital pre-orders, and early campaign reviews call it a fitting conclusion to the rebooted series — with amazing visuals and a rushed-feeling villain.
