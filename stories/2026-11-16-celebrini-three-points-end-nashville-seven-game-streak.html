Celebrini's three points end Nashville's seven-game streak

By Matt

Macklin Celebrini had a goal and two assists as San Jose beat Nashville 4-2 on Sunday — his 26th career three-point game, tying Dale Hawerchuk for fifth most in NHL history before age 21.

The night

Macklin Celebrini had a goal and two assists as the San Jose Sharks beat the Nashville Predators 4-2 at SAP Center on Sunday, ending Nashville's seven-game winning streak. It was his 26th career three-point game, tying Dale Hawerchuk for the fifth most in NHL history before age 21.

Will Smith matched him point for point — a goal and two assists of his own — and it was the eighth time the two have each posted three points in the same game. Ivar Stenberg added a goal and an assist, and the Sharks never trailed.

How it happened

Smith opened the scoring 12:25 into the first period, finishing a breakaway set up by Stenberg and Celebrini. Collin Graf made it 2-0 late in the period, and Stenberg buried the winner at 4:45 of the second — a tap-in at the left post off a saucer pass from Smith after Juuse Saros lost his stick.

Nils Hoglander got Nashville on the board late in the second, but Celebrini restored the three-goal lead at 7:40 of the third. He fed Smith cross-ice on the right wing, skated into the slot, and buried the return pass with a wrist shot past Saros' glove for his ninth goal of the season. Steven Stamkos answered on the power play at 16:35 for his 12th of the season, and that was as close as Nashville got.

San Jose was outshot 34-24 but Alex Nedeljkovic stopped 32 of them. The win was the Sharks' second straight night — they were on the second half of a back-to-back after Saturday's 6-5 shootout win at Utah — and their fourth straight game with a point (3-0-1). Nashville, meanwhile, hadn't lost since October 29.

The kids line

The story of the night was the top line's birth certificates: Stenberg is 19, Celebrini is 20, Smith is 21 — and each of them scored. Smith's first-period goal was also his 50th career goal, making him the fifth-fastest Shark ever to reach 50, trailing only Celebrini, Logan Couture, Devin Setoguchi and Pat Falloon.

Celebrini's read on why it clicked was simple: "We were just playing free. We weren't second-guessing ourselves. We were playing fast. That's our game."

He's been playing that way for a while now — 11 points (four goals, seven assists) in his past seven games, and 9 goals with 18 assists through 21 games this season.

The history

The milestone puts Celebrini on a list with exactly the names you'd expect:

1: Wayne Gretzky; 73

2: Mario Lemieux; 31

3: Sidney Crosby; 30

4: Jimmy Carson; 29

5: Macklin Celebrini; 26

5: Dale Hawerchuk; 26

Gretzky's 73 is in another universe — nobody is catching that. The realistic chase is Carson at 29, three ahead, and Celebrini is still 20 with the rest of the season to add to his total before he ages out of the list. For scale on what he's already done: his 115 points last season rank as the sixth-most ever in a single season by a player under 21.
