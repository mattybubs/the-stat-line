Anthony Edwards to Have Knee Surgery, Out Up to Two Months

By Matt

Timberwolves star Anthony Edwards will undergo an arthroscopic procedure on his ailing right knee and could miss up to two months — one night after dropping 47 points in 41 minutes in an overtime loss at Cleveland.

The news

Minnesota Timberwolves star Anthony Edwards will undergo an arthroscopic procedure on his ailing right knee that will sideline him for up to two months, sources told ESPN's Shams Charania on Tuesday night. The Athletic's Jon Krawczynski reported the team's hope is that Edwards returns sometime in December, fully ready to go.

Edwards had been listed as questionable before Monday's game at Cleveland, then played a season-high 41 minutes and scored 47 points in a 127-125 overtime loss. It was his second game with at least 45 points this season, and the seventh 47-point game of his career.

The context

This is the same right knee Edwards dealt with all last season — soreness and inflammation through the regular season and postseason, plus a hyperextended left knee and bone bruise in last season's playoffs. He took about two months off over the summer to rest both knees without surgery. He began grabbing the right knee during Sunday's home-opening win over Toronto; coach Chris Finch called it a "daily situation" before Monday's game and said at the time he wasn't worried about it being long-term.

The timing stings because Edwards was off to the best start of his career: 33.3 points, 4.8 rebounds and 3.5 assists on 48% shooting (40% from three) through four games, with Minnesota at 3-1.

What it means

• Minnesota hosts Golden State on Wednesday night without its best player — and the Wolves' big offseason swing, pairing Edwards with LaMelo Ball, is on hold for an extended stretch.

• The load shifts to Ball, Jaden McDaniels and Ayo Dosunmu, who will have to step up in Edwards' absence.

• The early-season injury wave keeps growing: Jalen Williams, Cason Wallace, Paolo Banchero, Kawhi Leonard, Jaren Jackson Jr., Jalen Suggs and Jalen Duren were already out, and now the league loses one of its brightest young stars for up to two months.
