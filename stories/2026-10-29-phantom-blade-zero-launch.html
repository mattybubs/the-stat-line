Phantom Blade Zero Is Out Today — and It's Already Over 500,000 Concurrent Players on Steam

By Matt

S-GAME's kung-fu-punk action RPG launched today on PS5 and PC at $59.99 with an 88 on Metacritic — and crossed 500,000 concurrent Steam players on day one, with an estimated 850,000 digital copies already sold before launch.

The wait is over, and the launch is loud. Phantom Blade Zero, S-GAME's long-awaited action RPG, is out today on PlayStation 5 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store) for $59.99 — and it debuted to more than 500,000 concurrent players on Steam, with a "Mostly Positive" rating from nearly 5,000 early user reviews.

That is a staggering day-one number for a new studio's debut, far ahead of other recent action RPG launches. Market firm Alinea Analytics estimated roughly 850,000 digital copies sold across Steam and PS5 before launch — about $43 million in pre-launch revenue — with total sales likely past one million once China-only storefronts are counted.

What it is

You play Soul, an assassin framed for his master's murder, left with a fatal heart injury and 66 days to live. The "Kung Fu Punk" world blends wuxia, steampunk and occult elements, built in Unreal Engine 5, with more than 30 weapon types and a combat system built around attack, defense and evasion flowing in a single breath.

Critics have largely bought in: the game sits at 88 on Metacritic and around 90 on OpenCritic, with near-unanimous praise for the combat and boss design. The dissent is real too — Eurogamer's 6/10 stands out, and reviewers repeatedly note a convoluted story and some technical issues. Wccftech's review calls it "one of the best action RPGs of the generation and a real Game of the Year 2026 contender."

Before you download

A few practical notes from launch day: the PC install needs about 145 GB of space (S-GAME recommends freeing roughly 300 GB for the install process), a roughly 5 GB day-one patch is live, and the studio has warned PC players to avoid Nvidia driver 621.01, which can cause instability — roll back to 617.42 until Nvidia issues a fix. There is no Xbox version at launch and no day-one Game Pass; physical PS5 editions are planned for 2027.

After years of "is this game even real?" skepticism following its 2023 reveal, day one answered that question pretty emphatically.
