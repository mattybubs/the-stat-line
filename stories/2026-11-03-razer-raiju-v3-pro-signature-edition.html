Razer Unveils $350 Raiju V3 Pro Signature Edition — Only 1,337 Units

By Matt

Razer's new Raiju V3 Pro Signature Edition wraps a tournament-grade PS5 controller in a magnesium alloy exoskeleton — and only 1,337 serialized units will ever be made in this initial run.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Razer has unveiled the Raiju V3 Pro Signature Edition, a limited-edition PlayStation 5 controller that turns its competitive flagship into a collector's piece. The price is $349.99, and the run is capped at 1,337 individually serialized units worldwide.

A magnesium exoskeleton

The headline is the shell. The Signature Edition swaps the standard body for a magnesium alloy exoskeleton with an open polygonal design across the top and back plates — you can see the internals through it. Chamfered edges and a nano coating keep debris out while leaving that open-air look intact.

The rest of the materials match the premium billing: a laser-etched Razer logo on a semi-transparent, tinted glass touchpad, matte black micro-textured TPU grips built to resist oils and abrasion, and double-shot PBT buttons so the legends never fade. The whole controller weighs just 260 grams.

Razer calls it its most ambitious PS5 controller to date, and the design celebrates a decade of Raiju development — the line debuted in 2016.

Same tournament-grade core

Underneath the new shell, it's the same competitive feature set as the standard Raiju V3 Pro:

• Contactless TMR thumbsticks in a symmetrical layout — anti-drift sensors for precise aim

• Six remappable multi-function buttons: two claw-grip bumpers and four mouse-click back buttons

• Up to 2,000Hz HyperPolling Wireless on PC for sharper input reporting

• Officially licensed for PS5, optimized for PC

The honest read, per the coverage: the $349.99 premium over the standard model (which launched at $220) buys materials and exclusivity, not new sensors or extra buttons. This is a statement piece that happens to be tournament-grade, not a performance upgrade over the base V3 Pro.

What's in the box

Every unit ships with a collector-grade kit: a leatherette carrying case, alternate thumbstick caps, four interchangeable back button covers, an anodized aluminum back button tool, and a HyperSpeed Wireless dongle.

When and where to get one

The Raiju V3 Pro Signature Edition goes on sale November 10, exclusively at Razer and select RazerStores, in regionalized drops. Units are first-come, first-served, and Razer will share drop timing and access details on its social channels on November 6.

Two things worth knowing before you set an alarm: the 1,337 figure is described as an initial supply, so a future run isn't fully ruled out — and Razer hasn't said how units split by region, so buyers outside the US shouldn't assume local stock until allocation is confirmed. The timing is no accident either: the launch lands just before GTA 6, and Razer is clearly positioning this as the upgrade to have in hand for it.
