UFC 333: Volkanovski vs. the Unbeaten Evloev — Plus a Title Trilogy

By Matt

Saturday in Abu Dhabi: Alexander Volkanovski defends the featherweight crown against 20-0 Movsar Evloev, and Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili settle their rivalry at 1-1. Main card 2pm ET on Paramount+.

Two belts, one afternoon in Abu Dhabi.

UFC 333 goes down Saturday, Oct. 24 at Etihad Arena, with a pair of championship fights headlining — and for U.S. fans, it's an early start: main card at 2pm ET on Paramount+ (prelims noon ET, early prelims 10am ET).

The main event: Volkanovski vs. Evloev

Alexander Volkanovski (28-4), the two-time featherweight champion in his second reign, defends against undefeated Russian contender Movsar Evloev (20-0).

Evloev is 10-0 in the UFC — and here's the wild part: every one of those wins came by decision. His majority-decision win over Lerone Murphy in March finally earned him the shot. Volkanovski, 37, regained the belt in 2025 and defended it against Diego Lopes in April.

The market can't split them: oddsmakers had it at essentially 50/50. And Volkanovski is already talking about what's next — a non-title superfight with Justin Gaethje, with both champions vacating their belts and retiring, if he gets past Evloev.

The co-main: Yan vs. Dvalishvili III

Bantamweight champion Petr Yan and former champ Merab Dvalishvili meet for a third time, tied 1-1. Dvalishvili won the first meeting in 2023; Yan took the rematch at UFC 323 in December 2025 to reclaim the belt, ending Merab's 14-fight win streak.

The twist: Yan has never successfully defended a UFC title. Dvalishvili defended it three times before losing it. The market leans Yan at roughly 59%.

Also on the main card: Alexander Volkov vs. Rizvan Kuniev at heavyweight and Aaron Pico vs. Losene Keita (Keita stepped in after Arnold Allen withdrew with injury).
