Byredo x Iittala's Five-Piece Glass Scent Collection Drops Oct 16

By Matt

Byredo's five-piece LJUS glassware collab with Iittala — mouth-blown vessels for candles, diffusers, and incense — goes on sale worldwide October 16.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Two heavyweight Scandinavian names just made scent an object of design. On October 14, Byredo teased its five-piece LJUS collaboration with Finnish glass house Iittala with a two-word promise: "Fire meets ice."

The collection — launching worldwide on October 16 — is a set of mouth-blown glass vessels built around Byredo's candles, diffusers, and incense. Iittala, the storied Finnish glassmaker, has been crafting mouth-blown glass since the 19th century, and this collab leans squarely into that craft tradition: vessels designed to hold scent and flame, rendered in the clear, elemental glass the house is known for.

What the collection is

LJUS ("light" in Swedish) is a five-piece glassware line where each piece is a vessel for home fragrance — candles, reed diffusers, and incense. This isn't Byredo dipping into a new fragrance; it's Byredo and Iittala turning the ritual of scent into collectible design objects.

Pricing

UK pricing runs £155–£300 per piece, which puts the full five-piece set firmly in design-object territory rather than casual-candle territory. No US pricing has been confirmed yet.

Why it's worth noting

• Crossover prestige: Byredo (luxury fragrance) × Iittala (Finnish glassmaking heritage) is a genuine design-world collaboration, not a licensing badge on a candle jar.

• Five pieces, one ritual: The collection is built around the full home-fragrance ritual — candles, diffusers, and incense each get their own vessel.

• Craft story: Mouth-blown glass means every piece carries the subtle variations of handcraft — a real differentiator against mass-market scented-candle hardware.

• Timing: An October 16 launch lands squarely in holiday-gift season, and limited design collabs in this lane tend to sell out fast.

Where to buy

The collection goes on sale worldwide October 16. Expect it through Byredo's own channels and Iittala retailers. With UK pricing at £155–£300, this is a considered purchase — but for design-minded fragrance fans, it's the rare home-scent object worth keeping long after the candle burns down.
