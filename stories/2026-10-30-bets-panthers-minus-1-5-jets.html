Panthers -1.5: Florida Already Beat Winnipeg by Four — Now the Jets Come to Sunrise on a Back-to-Back

By Matt

Florida beat Winnipeg 5-1 on Tuesday, the Jets have lost seven straight in regulation-or-OT terms (0-5-2), and now they play the second night of a back-to-back in Sunrise. Lay the 1.5 at plus money.

Game slip: Winnipeg Jets at Florida Panthers

• Matchup: Winnipeg Jets (4-6-3) at Florida Panthers (7-4-2)

• Time/channel: Friday, 7:00 p.m. ET, ESPN+, from Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise

• Odds (FanDuel): Panthers -230; Jets +188; total 6.5; puck line Panthers -1.5 (+104)

• Injury report: Panthers — Aleksander Barkov out (lower body), Brad Marchand out (leg), Jacob Markstrom out (upper body), Jonah Gadjovich out (upper body). All known, long-term absences — Florida has been winning without them. Jets — Josh Morrissey out (upper body), Vladislav Namestnikov out (undisclosed), Nick Bjugstad out (pectoral), Garrett Brown out (lower body). Note: neither starting goaltender was confirmed at preview time, so treat the goalie matchup as provisional.

• Storylines: (1) The rematch: Florida beat this same Jets team 5-1 on Tuesday in Winnipeg, with Carter Verhaeghe scoring twice. (2) Winnipeg is 0-5-2 over its last seven and 2-6-2 over its last ten, and now plays the second night of a back-to-back after playing in Tampa on Thursday. (3) Florida is home, rested since Tuesday, opening a three-game homestand on two straight wins.

The bet: Panthers -1.5 (+104)

This is a rematch play with a scheduling edge and plus money attached. Florida already beat Winnipeg by four goals three days ago, with the same injury absences on both sides — nothing about the matchup has changed except the venue (now Sunrise) and the schedule (now a Jets back-to-back). The Panthers are 6-4-0 over their last ten, scoring 3.3 per game; the Jets are 2-6-2, scoring 2.3 and giving up 3.2.

The honest caveat: laying -1.5 means you need a two-goal win, and Florida's crease is thin with Markstrom out — Akira Schmid is the expected fill-in, and his season save percentage sits below .890. If the Jets' goaltending holds up early, this can stay a one-goal game late. But at plus money, with a four-goal rematch result and a tired opponent, the puck line is the value side.
