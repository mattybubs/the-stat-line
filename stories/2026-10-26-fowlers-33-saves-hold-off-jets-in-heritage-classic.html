Fowler's 33 Saves Hold Off Jets in Heritage Classic

By Matt

Jacob Fowler made 33 saves as Montreal held on for a 2-1 win over Winnipeg in the Heritage Classic at Princess Auto Stadium on Sunday.

The finish

Fowler had the shutout in sight until late in the third period, when Isak Rosen scored to spoil the shutout bid and cut the lead to one. Montreal held on from there to close out the outdoor win.

Final score: Canadiens 2, Jets 1

Fowler saves: 33

Venue: Princess Auto Stadium

Event: Heritage Classic, Sunday

Late twist: Rosen's third-period goal spoiled Fowler's shutout bid

Why it matters

Outdoor games have a way of turning into goalie games, and this one was no exception. Fowler's 33 saves were the difference: he carried Montreal through Winnipeg's push and still got the win after the shutout slipped away late. A 2-1 outdoor win on the road, with the shutout bid alive into the third period, is exactly the kind of performance that sticks in a season.
