McDavid's point streak hits 20 — only the fourth player ever to open a season like this

By Matt

Connor McDavid picked up an assist in the Oilers' 3-2 overtime win over the Flyers, running his season-opening point streak to 20 games. Only Gretzky, Sundin and Heatley have ever done that.

The night

The Edmonton Oilers needed overtime to beat the Philadelphia Flyers 3-2 at Rogers Place on Wednesday, and the game-winner came 47 seconds into the extra session — Evan Bouchard one-timing a feed from Leon Draisaitl short side, off the right post and in. It was Bouchard's 11th goal of the season, the most by any NHL defenseman, and his second overtime winner of the year.

The goal that mattered most for the history books came earlier. Midway through the second period, Connor McDavid found Zach Hyman posted in front of the net on the power play, and Hyman redirected it through Dan Vladar's legs for his second goal of the night. The assist ran the captain's season-opening point streak to 20 games.

Hyman's first multi-goal game of the season could not have come at a better time — he entered the night with one goal all year. Tristan Jarry made 30 saves, and the Oilers improved to 14-3-3, matching the second-best start in franchise history through 20 games. They have won three straight and are 12-0-2 when they score first.

Philadelphia, for its part, did not roll over. The Flyers erased a two-goal deficit on goals from Tyson Foerster and Travis Sanheim to force overtime, and they outshot Edmonton 32-24. They fell to 5-10-6, losers of four of their last five.

The history

Twenty games is where this streak stops being a hot start and becomes a line in the record book. McDavid is just the fourth player in NHL history to record a point in each of his team's first 20 games of a season. The other three: Wayne Gretzky (three times, including the record 51-game run to open 1983-84), Mats Sundin (30 games in 1992-93) and Dany Heatley (22 games in 2005-06).

It is also the second 20-game streak of McDavid's own career — he had one last season, too — making him the eighth player ever with multiple 20-game point streaks. The 20-game mark ties his career high.

The scoring lead is not close anymore. The captain has 49 points — 11 goals and 38 assists — through 20 games, 14 clear of Minnesota's Kirill Kaprizov. For context, he had more points than the Flyers had total goals (45) before the puck dropped on Wednesday.

What's next

The next stop is 21, then Heatley's 22. Sundin's 30 is the realistic horizon; Gretzky's 51 is a different sport. Edmonton starts a six-game road trip Saturday in Toronto, then faces these same Flyers again Sunday in Philadelphia.
