Justin Herbert in concussion protocol after Chargers' 27-24 loss to the Jets — Week 12 status in doubt

By Matt

The Chargers announced after Sunday's loss to the Jets that Justin Herbert has entered the NFL's concussion protocol, leaving his availability for next week's game against the Patriots up in the air.

The news

Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert has entered the NFL's concussion protocol, the team announced after Sunday's 27-24 loss to the New York Jets at SoFi Stadium.

Herbert played the entire game, but a member of the Chargers' public relations team announced postgame that he would not be available to the media because he had entered the protocol. The exact play on which the head injury occurred has not been specified.

What happened Sunday

Herbert was efficient when he had the ball — 15-of-21 passing for 131 yards and two touchdowns, a 119.3 passer rating that was his best of the season — and he added eight rushes for 32 yards, including a 10-yard touchdown run on the final play of the first half.

The protection was the problem again. Herbert was sacked six times by the Jets, bringing his season total to 39, a league high. The loss dropped the Chargers to 1-9, and it came against a Jets team that had dropped five straight.

Los Angeles had only six possessions all game and came away with points on four of them. The Jets scored on five of their six possessions, milking the clock on their final drive while the Chargers couldn't get the ball back.

What comes next

Herbert will have to progress through the NFL's mandatory five-step return-to-participation protocol, which requires clearance from both the team physician and an independent neurological consultant, before he can play again.

The Chargers host the New England Patriots next Sunday, November 29. If Herbert doesn't clear the protocol in time, backup DJ Uiagalelei would be in line to start — with Trey Lance on injured reserve and Jake Browning on the practice squad.

The Chargers will provide further updates when practice resumes later this week.
