TSL Bets: The Friday parlay (~+264) — Hawks -7.5 at the 1-9 Bulls, Grizzlies -3.5 at the Pelicans

By Matt

Two road favorites against two of the league's worst teams. Atlanta is laying 7.5 in Chicago against a 1-9 Bulls team missing its rookie highlight, and Memphis is laying 3.5 in New Orleans. Two -110 legs, about +264.

The games

Atlanta Hawks (7-5) at Chicago Bulls (1-9) — 8:00 PM ET, United Center, Chicago

Hawks -7.5 (-110), Bulls +7.5 (-110), total 245.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Atlanta: Jalen Johnson is back from the left ankle sprain that cost him time — he returned Wednesday against the Wizards. Rookie point guard Kingston Flemings is questionable with a back issue; if he sits, CJ McCollum returns to the starting unit. Asa Newell is out with a toe injury, and Mouhamed Gueye, Keshon Gilbert and Henri Veesaar remain out.

Injuries — Chicago: Rookie Caleb Wilson is out after fracturing his right hand — surgery, four to six weeks, at a moment when he was playing 30.8 minutes a night and serving as the nightly highlight for the franchise. Norman Powell is questionable with a shoulder injury. Tobe Awaka is out with a back injury.

Storylines: The Hawks have won four of five and are rested, kicking off a three-game road trip still annoyed about Monday's 124-114 loss to the Lakers. The Bulls are 1-9 and coming off the worst kind of loss: they shot 85 percent in the first quarter Wednesday, built a 22-point lead against San Antonio — their largest of the season — and lost anyway. This is an NBA Cup group game, Atlanta 1-0 in East Group C, Chicago 0-1. The Hawks are the better team, the healthier team, and the team with something to prove after Monday.

Memphis Grizzlies (7-4) at New Orleans Pelicans (4-8) — 8:00 PM ET, Smoothie King Center, New Orleans

Grizzlies -3.5 (-110), Pelicans +3.5 (-110), total 235.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Memphis: Zach Edey is out with an ankle injury. Scotty Pippen Jr. is out with a back injury. Quinten Post is out in concussion protocol.

Injuries — New Orleans: Trendon Watford is out with an ankle injury. Trey Murphy III, Yves Missi, Bennedict Mathurin and Bryce McGowens are all game-time decisions.

Storylines: Memphis is 7-4 and laying only 3.5 on the road against a 4-8 Pelicans team that has half its rotation on the injury report. The Grizzlies are shorthanded up front without Edey, but the Pelicans' list is longer and more important — four game-time decisions on top of an out. This is also an NBA Cup group game, and the number is small enough that Memphis does not need a statement win, just a win.

The bet

2-leg parlay (~+264): Hawks -7.5 (-110) + Grizzlies -3.5 (-110). Two road favorites against two of the league's worst teams, both laying numbers that reflect the gap rather than exaggerating it. Neither leg is a big spread — 7.5 and 3.5 — and both favorites are healthier, in better form, and playing teams whose injury reports read like a roster sheet. The Hawks are catching a Bulls team that just blew a 22-point lead at home and lost its best young player for a month. The Grizzlies are catching a Pelicans team that might not know its starting five until an hour before tip.

The honest caveat

Both legs are road favorites, and road favorites lose games they should win — Chicago is at home, desperate, and the Hawks are on the first night of a road trip with a rookie point guard who might not play. The Memphis leg is the shakier one: the Grizzlies are missing their center and two rotation players, and if Murphy and Missi both suit up for New Orleans, the Pelicans are a different team than the injury report suggests. A parlay needs both, and the second leg is the one that keeps this honest.

The pick

Hawks -7.5 + Grizzlies -3.5, about +264. A $10 ticket returns about $36.40. Two favorites, two bad teams, two small numbers.
