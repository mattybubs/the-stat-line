PS Plus November Is Live: Dead Island 2, the Tomb Raider Darkness Trilogy, and Monster Train 2

By Matt

November's PlayStation Plus Monthly Games are claimable today through November 30: Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered, and Monster Train 2. Because the Tomb Raider collection holds three games, the month technically delivers five games for the subscription you're already paying for.

November's PlayStation Plus Monthly Games are live today, and this is one of the stronger months of the year. Sony announced the lineup on October 28 on the PlayStation Blog, and as of today, November 3, all three titles are claimable through Monday, November 30, for members at every tier — Essential, Extra and Premium alike. Claim them in that window and they stay playable for as long as your subscription stays active.

The lineup

Dead Island 2 (PS5, PS4) is the headline. Dambuster Studios' first-person zombie action RPG drops you in HELL-A — a quarantined, blood-soaked Los Angeles — for melee combat built around the FLESH system, which renders zombie damage in spectacularly gross detail, solo or in three-player online co-op.

Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered (PS5, PS4) is the three-for-one. Aspyr's collection bundles the whole Darkness Trilogy: The Last Revelation (Lara in Egypt, racing her mentor Von Croy to an ancient tomb), Chronicles (a globe-trotting set of flashback adventures told after Lara is presumed dead), and The Angel of Darkness (the darker, divisive Paris-and-Prague chapter) — with a toggle between original and remastered visuals.

Monster Train 2 (PS5 only) is the one gap for PS4-only subscribers: there is no PS4 version in this offer. The deckbuilding roguelite sequel has you leading clans of former angels and demons aboard trains through Hell, Heaven and the Abyss, with five new clans in the mix.

The fine print, and the regional wrinkle

October's games — F1 25, Hunt: Showdown 1896 and Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2 — left the claim list after Monday, November 2, so if you missed that deadline, they're gone. November's window runs the other way: claim by November 30, play whenever.

One regional note: subscribers in Japan get Secret Neighbor in place of Dead Island 2, a Japan-only swap. Everyone else gets the lineup as listed. Sony has not yet detailed November's Extra and Premium Game Catalog additions — those usually land mid-month — but the Monthly Games trio alone makes this a five-game month in practice, and Dead Island 2 is the kind of big-budget open-world game that usually anchors a month on its own.
