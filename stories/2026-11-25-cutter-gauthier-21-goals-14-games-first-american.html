Cutter Gauthier is the first American ever with 21 goals in 14 games — and one goal from the NHL's monthly record

By Matt

Cutter Gauthier's power-play goal in the Ducks' 7-0 rout of the Sharks made him the first American in NHL history with 21 goals in a 14-game span — the first player from any country since Mario Lemieux in 1992-93 — and left him one goal shy of the calendar-month record with two games left in November.

The night

Cutter Gauthier needed less than sixteen minutes of the first period to make history. With 4:43 left in the opening frame against the San Jose Sharks on Wednesday night, the Anaheim Ducks winger took a feed from Jackson LaCombe and Mikael Granlund and snapped a power-play shot home from just above the left face-off circle for a 3-0 lead — his 23rd goal of the season, pulling him level with Edmonton's Leon Draisaitl for the NHL lead.

The Ducks never looked back. Anaheim scored three goals in 100 seconds in the first period and rolled to a 7-0 win over the Sharks at a sold-out Honda Center, snapping a four-game losing streak in the most emphatic way possible. Laurent Brossoit, recalled from AHL San Diego that morning, made 22 saves for the first shutout by a Ducks goalie in 188 regular-season games — and 200 total games including the playoffs. The last one, coincidentally, also came against the Sharks, when Lukas Dostal blanked them 2-0 in the 2024-25 season opener. The 7-0 margin ties the franchise record for the largest shutout victory, set by the Mighty Ducks against Calgary on December 20, 1996.

Six different Ducks found the net. Judd Caulfield opened the scoring, Alex Killorn scored twice — including a mid-air rebound batted in for the 2-0 lead — and Ian Moore, Roger McQueen and Frank Vatrano added goals alongside Gauthier's. Granlund finished with three assists. Alex Nedeljkovic stopped 23 shots for San Jose, which has now lost two straight.

The record

The goal was Gauthier's 21st in his last 14 games — a pace no American has ever reached, and no player from any country has reached since Mario Lemieux in 1992-93. Since the start of the 1925-26 season, only five other players have scored 21 or more in a 14-game span: Lemieux (twice), Teemu Selanne, Alexander Mogilny, Bernie Nicholls and Wayne Gretzky, who did it three times.

The run started on October 29, when Gauthier scored twice against Buffalo, and it has included back-to-back hat tricks against Ottawa and Vancouver. He is the first player to hit the mark since Lemieux, more than three decades ago.

November is a second record chase running in parallel. The goal was his 19th of the month, making him just the eighth player in NHL history with 19 or more goals in a calendar month and the second American to do it, after Pat LaFontaine — and the first player since Pavel Bure in March 1994. One more ties the all-time calendar-month record of 20, shared by Selanne (March 1993) and Joe Malone (February 1921). It was also his eighth power-play goal of November, tying Paul Kariya (March 2001) and Selanne (February 1999) for the most in a single month in Ducks history.

What it means

This is the fourth Stat Line post on Gauthier's run in under a month, and each one has moved the goalposts — from a goal streak, to hat tricks, to a Ducks record. This one is different: it is not a franchise mark or a hot stretch. It is a league-wide first for an American player, in company that reads like a Hall of Fame ballot.

The Ducks needed the night, too. At 12-13-1 with 25 points, the win moved Anaheim back into the third and final playoff spot in the Pacific Division, over the Sharks, Flames and Kraken. A 7-0 rout with a shutout ends a four-game skid about as loudly as a skid can end.

What's next

Gauthier gets two more cracks at the monthly record this week, and both come against the same opponent. The Ducks open a Thanksgiving weekend home-and-home with the Los Angeles Kings on Friday, November 27, at home, then travel to Crypto.com Arena on Saturday, November 28. One goal ties Selanne and Malone at 20. Two makes him the first player in NHL history with 21 goals in a calendar month.
