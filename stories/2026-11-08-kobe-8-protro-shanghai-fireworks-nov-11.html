Nike Kobe 8 Protro 'Shanghai Fireworks' drops November 11 for $200

By Matt

The Nike Kobe 8 Protro 'Shanghai Fireworks' drops Wednesday, November 11 for $200 — the latest Kobe Protro to hit shelves, and one of the busiest release days of the month.

What happened

The Nike Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" releases on Wednesday, November 11, 2026, for $200. Both Claqa and Sneaker Bar Detroit list the date and the price, and the shoe is one of the headliners of a stacked November 11 release day.

The "Shanghai Fireworks" is a bright, loud take on the Kobe 8 Protro — orange and gold tones with a firework-inspired finish, built on the low-cut Protro silhouette that has made the Kobe 8 one of the most popular retro basketball shoes on the market. The Protro line has been a steady hit for Nike, and the Kobe 8 in particular has become a fixture in both the basketball and the sneaker worlds.

A busy release day

November 11 is a crowded day. Also dropping that day: the Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" in women's sizing for $255, and the Story mfg. x ASICS GEL-TRANSCEND FJ. The Reebok x Pierre Gasly Club C Revenge drops two days earlier, on November 9, for €110.

For Kobe collectors, the "Shanghai Fireworks" is the one to watch. The Protro releases have been selling through quickly, and a $200 price point on a limited colorway means the usual drill: be ready at drop time, or be ready to pay resale.

The details

Shoe: Nike Kobe 8 Protro "Shanghai Fireworks" (IO6259-800)

Release date: Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Price: $200

Also dropping Nov 11: Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" (women's, $255), Story mfg. x ASICS GEL-TRANSCEND FJ
