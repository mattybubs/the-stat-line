TSL Bets: The Thanksgiving night parlay (~+248) — Cook anytime TD, Rice OVER 46.5 receiving yards

By Matt

James Cook has scored in five straight games and Rashee Rice has gone over 46.5 receiving yards in 8 of his last 10 — including all three meetings with Buffalo. Two legs, about +248, under the lights in Orchard Park.

The game

Kansas City Chiefs (8-2) at Buffalo Bills (9-1) — 8:20 PM ET, Highmark Stadium, Orchard Park, NY (NBC/Peacock)

Bills -3 (-102), total 46.5 (Over -108 / Under -110), moneyline Bills -150 / Chiefs +125 (BetMGM via USA Today Sportsbook Wire, November 25). Forecast: rain, mid-30s, wind gusts up to 24 mph.

Injuries — Kansas City: RB Kenneth Walker III OUT (groin — third straight game missed). OT Jaylon Moore OUT (ankle). DT Omarr Norman-Lott OUT (knee). FB Ben VanSumeren OUT (knee). TE Noah Gray QUESTIONABLE (rib). DT Peter Woods QUESTIONABLE (neck). QB Patrick Mahomes CLEARED (knee/right elbow — full practice). WR Rashee Rice CLEARED (knee — full practice). G Trey Smith CLEARED (concussion — full practice).

Injuries — Buffalo: CB Christian Benford QUESTIONABLE (groin — missed Week 11). TE Dalton Kincaid QUESTIONABLE (thumb/ankle). DL T.J. Sanders QUESTIONABLE (shoulder). G O'Cyrus Torrence QUESTIONABLE (ankle). LB Dorian Williams QUESTIONABLE (calf).

Storylines: This is the first home Thanksgiving game in Bills franchise history, and the AFC's top seed is on the line — Buffalo is 9-1, Kansas City 8-2, the third-highest combined winning percentage (.850) in a Thanksgiving game since 1950. Buffalo has won the last five regular-season meetings; Kansas City owns the postseason, with four straight playoff wins over Josh Allen's Bills. Mahomes has been surgical since Week 8: 1,235 passing yards, 13 touchdowns, zero interceptions, with 300-plus yards, three touchdowns and no picks in four separate games this season. Allen threw for 304 yards with a touchdown and two interceptions against Miami last week, and the Bills have won six straight. With Walker out again, rookie Emmett Johnson starts at running back for Kansas City for the third straight game.

Leg 1 — James Cook anytime touchdown

Cook has a rushing touchdown in each of his last five games — one apiece against Miami, the Jets, Minnesota, Baltimore and Las Vegas — and 10 rushing touchdowns this season. He has scored in 9 of his last 10, and in 3 of 6 against this opponent. He ran for 89 yards and a touchdown against Miami last week. Odds Index's model puts his touchdown chance at 56.0% against 52.6% implied by the price, and rates this one HIGH. Line: anytime touchdown (-111, Odds Index; -110, DraftKings via SI Betting).

Leg 2 — Rashee Rice OVER 46.5 receiving yards

Rice has 40-plus receiving yards in five straight games (53, 82, 49, 53, 88) and has gone over this line in 8 of his last 10 — and in all three meetings with Buffalo, including a career-best 80 yards against them. His target share is 24%, and Buffalo's top corner, Christian Benford, is questionable with a groin injury after missing Week 11. Mahomes has thrown 13 touchdowns without an interception since Week 8, and Rice is his first read. Line: OVER 46.5 receiving yards (-120, Odds Index).

The bet

James Cook anytime touchdown (-111, Odds Index) + Rashee Rice OVER 46.5 receiving yards (-120, Odds Index) — about +248. A $10 ticket returns about $34.85.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Cook is a near-even-money touchdown scorer on a five-game scoring streak, and Rice is a yardage prop with a five-game floor, a 3-for-3 record against this opponent, and a banged-up corner across from him. Neither leg depends on the other — different players, different sides of the ball, different markets. That is the juice rule working as intended: two props with real trend edges, about +248 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

The weather is the shared risk — rain, mid-30s, wind gusts to 24 mph in Orchard Park can suppress the passing game, and Rice's leg is the exposed one. Both legs live in the same game, so one bad night in Buffalo sinks both. Cook's touchdown depends on red-zone touches in a game Buffalo could win through the air, and Rice's knee was on the injury report this week even though he practiced in full and is cleared. And a parlay needs both legs — either one can sink it alone. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 22-44 in November, 26-65 all-time.
