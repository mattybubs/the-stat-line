TSL Bets: Luka Doncic OVER 37.5 points vs the Jazz (+104)

By Matt

The Lakers are 13-2 on a nine-game win streak, Austin Reaves is out, Utah's frontcourt is stripped by injuries, and Luka Doncic leads the league at 38.8 points per game. The play: Doncic OVER 37.5 points (+104) against the 2-13 Jazz.

The game slip

Utah Jazz (2-13) at Los Angeles Lakers (13-2)

Wednesday, 10:00 PM ET — Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

• Spread: Lakers -10.5

• Total: 229

• Moneyline: Lakers -455 / Jazz +360

• Odds source: Median across 12 US books via MoneyMaverick; moneyline via SportyTrader odds comparison, November 18

Injury report — Lakers: G Austin Reaves OUT (left heel bruise, per ESPN's morning injury report — the Lakers' second-leading scorer at 24.7 PPG). C Walker Kessler game-time decision (right lower-leg soreness; coach JJ Redick said he expects Kessler to be available).

Injury report — Jazz: F Lauri Markkanen OUT (two non-displaced right-hand fractures). F Jaren Jackson Jr. OUT (right MCL tear). C Jusuf Nurkic OUT (partial plantar plate tear, right foot). C Jaxson Hayes OUT (left knee strain). Utah's frontcourt is stripped to Tony Bradley and Kyle Filipowski behind Mo Bamba.

Storylines: The Lakers have won nine straight and own the best record in the NBA at 13-2, averaging 127.4 points a game at home, where they are 6-1. Doncic leads the league at 38.8 points per game and has scored at least 30 in 11 straight games — and he hung 52 on Utah in a 132-123 win at Salt Lake City on October 25. The Jazz are 2-13 and 1-6 on the road, conceding 117.3 points a game away from home, and they are on the second night of a back-to-back after snapping their 12-game losing streak behind Brice Sensabaugh's 40 on Tuesday. Both teams played last night; neither has a rest edge.

The bet

Luka Doncic OVER 37.5 points (+104, best price Novig via OPTIMAL)

Why this prop

Doncic is the NBA's scoring leader at 38.8 points per game, and the line sits below his average. He has gone over 37.5 points in 7 of his last 10 games, and OPTIMAL projects him for 37.4 against Utah — right at the number, with the best available price paying plus money.

The injury report is the engine here again. With Reaves out, the shot-creation load shifts almost entirely onto Doncic, and Kessler's status is uncertain. The matchup is the softest possible landing spot: Utah is 2-13 with its frontcourt gutted by injuries, and Doncic already scored 52 against this same team three weeks ago. He is taking 13.3 three-point attempts a game and making 5.4 of them.

This is the props-first single by the book: a player prop with a real matchup edge, a line sitting below the scoring leader's average, and plus-money juice on the over.

The honest caveats

A blowout could trim Doncic's fourth-quarter minutes — the Lakers are laying 10.5, and if this one gets away early, the fourth quarter belongs to the bench. He also played 41 and a half minutes in an overtime game last night, so the legs are a real question on the second night of a back-to-back. And the projection is not a slam dunk: it sits at 37.4 against a 37.5 line, and one model range has him lower. If Reaves ends up playing after all, some usage comes back. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 21-34 in November, 25-55 all-time. Props are 7-2 in November — the only winning singles category — that's why the daily single is a prop.
