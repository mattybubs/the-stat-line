Kai Cenat launches gaming channel after GTA VI marathon

By Matt

Fresh off a two-week GTA marathon, Kai Cenat opened @KaiCenatGaming — a separate channel for recorded play, horror games, and whatever his viewers ask for next.

Kai Cenat has a new home for his gaming. On November 25, he launched @KaiCenatGaming, a separate YouTube channel built for recorded play — not livestreams — and opened it with a video titled "Welcome To My Gaming Channel..."

The pitch is simple: offline gaming, horror games, and every other genre, with viewers asked to comment what they want to see him play next. Kai said he has plenty lined up but is keeping the specifics secret for now, and that the channel exists for the times he just wants to game and dive deeper into it.

Why now

The launch lands right after one of the biggest gaming events of his career. Kai spent November 5 to 11 playing all five earlier mainline GTA games back to back — GTA III, Vice City, San Andreas, GTA IV, and GTA V — finishing the run in just over 130 hours, ahead of GTA VI's release.

He then moved straight into GTA VI, streaming for roughly 67 hours before finishing the story. The back-to-back marathons turned his GTA run into one of his biggest gaming events yet, and the new channel is the natural next step: a place for the edited, recorded side of that same habit.

Gaming has always been a real share of his output, not a side quest. About 31% of his time streaming games on Twitch across his career has been spent playing video games, with the recent GTA marathons and Minecraft runs making up a large portion of that.

What to expect

Kai framed the channel as a consistency play. In the welcome video, he said he hopes to stay consistent, that he has a lot of fun stuff lined up, and that viewers should tell him what games to play — the channel's direction will follow what the audience asks for.

The first video sets the tone: horror games, fun games, and everything in between, recorded and edited rather than streamed live. The live marathons stay where they are; this channel is where the gaming lives after the stream ends.
