TSL Bets: Vikings +3 (-102) — the home dog with the No. 2 defense on Monday night

By Matt

Two 6-1 teams meet on Monday Night Football, and the home dog is getting a field goal. The Vikings have the No. 2 defense in the league, Carson Wentz has already won games for this team, and Buffalo's offensive line is banged up. Vikings +3 (-102) is the play.

The game

Buffalo Bills (6-1) at Minnesota Vikings (6-1) — 8:15 PM ET, U.S. Bank Stadium, ESPN/ABC

Bills -3 (-118), total 44, moneyline Bills -175 / Vikings +145 (BetMGM, via USA TODAY Sportsbook Wire). Buffalo is 5-2 against the spread and 5-2 to the over this season. Minnesota is 4-3 ATS and just 1-6 to the over — this defense keeps games low-scoring. The total opened at 46.5 and has been bet down to 44, which tells you what the market expects: a tight, low-scoring game. Tight, low-scoring games are where home dogs getting a field goal make their money.

Injuries — Buffalo: WR Josh Palmer (hamstring) is out. LT Dion Dawkins (rib) and OL Alec Anderson (ankle) are questionable after missing Thursday and Friday practices, and DT Greg Gaines (calf) and WR Tyrell Shavers (knee) are questionable too. The Bills' injury report has swollen to 21 players, and the worst of it is on the offensive line and in the receiver room.

Injuries — Minnesota: QB Kyler Murray (hamstring) is out, so Carson Wentz starts. WR Jauan Jennings (calf) is out. S Theo Jackson (quad) and S Jay Ward (knee) are questionable. RB Demond Claiborne (knee) is out.

Storylines: Two first-place teams, both 6-1, and the whole game turns on the quarterback change. Murray is week-to-week with the hamstring, and Wentz — who helped the Vikings win in relief in Week 1 and started the rain-affected game at Chicago the week after — gets the start. The Vikings' defense is No. 2 in the league in EPA per play, and it has carried them all season: they are 6-1 despite shaky quarterback play. Buffalo's defense, by contrast, ranks 26th in EPA per play and sits in the bottom 10 in passing yards allowed. The Bills got to 6-1 on a soft schedule and some big injuries to opponents. Minnesota is coming off its first loss of the season, 19-15 at Detroit. Buffalo blasted Baltimore 36-10 on November 1.

The bet

Vikings +3 (-102, BetMGM). The home dog, getting a field goal, with the No. 2 defense in football, against a road favorite whose offensive line is held together with tape and whose defense is one of the league's worst by EPA. Wentz is not the reason to bet Minnesota — the defense is. If this game lands where the total says it will, in the low 40s, three points is a lot of points. The Vikings have already won games with Wentz under center this season. They do not need him to be great. They need him to not lose it, and let the defense and the home crowd do the rest.

The honest caveat

Wentz is a backup for a reason, and Buffalo has Josh Allen. If the Bills' banged-up line holds up for one night, Allen is capable of winning this game by more than a field goal on his own — and the Vikings' offense, without Murray's legs, has struggled to sustain drives all season. The Bills are 5-2 ATS for a reason, too. Nothing here is a lock. But at home, with that defense, getting three? That is the side.

The pick

Vikings +3 (-102). A $10 ticket returns about $19.80. Two 6-1 teams, one field goal, and the better defense is the one getting the points.
