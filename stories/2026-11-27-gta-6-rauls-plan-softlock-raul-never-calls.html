GTA 6 has a second story softlock — Raul never calls Lucia, and the fix starts with a jewelry job

By Matt

A second game-breaking softlock is stopping GTA 6 players from finishing the story — this time in Chapter 4, where Raul's call to Lucia never comes. Rockstar's first patch didn't fix it, but players have found a workaround, and in a lot of cases the call is just waiting on unfinished jobs.

GTA 6 has a second story softlock, and this one hits even later than the first. Players reaching Chapter 4 are getting stuck on a mission called Raul's Plan, where Lucia is supposed to wait for a phone call from Raul — and for some players, the call simply never comes, no matter how much in-game time passes. The story stalls at roughly three-quarters complete, with dozens of hours already sunk into the save.

Rockstar's first post-launch patch, update 01.002.002, went out November 23 and specifically fixed story-related phone calls that weren't triggering. This one survived it. Rockstar has not acknowledged this particular bug, and there is no dedicated patch for it yet — so for now, players who hit the wall are relying on a community workaround.

First, make sure it's actually a bug

Before you assume your save is broken, check the jobs the game is quietly waiting on. Raul's Plan is a waiting mission, and the call won't come until earlier Chapter 4 tasks are finished — even ones that look optional.

The checklist, as reported by GameRant, TalkEsport and PlayerOne: finish all three of Yasmani's jewelry jobs, including the optional ones where you pay him for robbery tips; pay Abe Gonzalez's $75,000 lawyer fee; and answer every text, missed call and reminder on Lucia's phone. In a lot of cases, the call is being held back by one of those — not by a bug at all.

If you've done all of that, waited an in-game day or two, and the phone still hasn't rung, your save has most likely hit the softlock.

The workaround players are using

The fix that has worked for players comes from the community, and GameRant's own writer used it to save a stuck playthrough. The steps:

1. Finish any outstanding Yasmani jobs, including the optional paid-tip ones.

2. Clear every notification and reminder from Lucia's phone.

3. Make sure Jason and Lucia are split up, not hanging out together.

4. Make a manual save as Lucia inside her Vice City hotel room.

5. Fully shut down your console — hold the power button for 10 to 30 seconds, not rest mode — then turn it back on.

6. Load the save and leave the hotel room as Lucia, alone.

If it works, the first call Lucia gets isn't from Raul at all — it's Valentina, with a new main story mission. Finish that, pay the lawyer fee if you haven't already, and Raul's call finally comes through. It isn't guaranteed, and players who are still stuck should report the bug to Rockstar Support and keep an older save in a separate slot in case a future patch needs a clean starting point.

Not the same softlock as the last one

This is a different bug from the Chapter 3 Missing Persons softlock we covered on November 26, where Jason stays greyed out on the character wheel after the side case ends. That one is still live too, and its only reliable way out is an older manual save. Two separate story blockers, both unpatched, both in the biggest game of the year — and both with the same advice attached: make a manual save in a new slot before every major mission, because story missions can't be replayed.
