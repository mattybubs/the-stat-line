Ravens down to third quarterback for Thursday night

By Matt

Undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano makes his first career start tonight against Jacksonville after injuries to Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley left Baltimore down to its third quarterback.

What happened

Joe Fagnano makes his first career start tonight against the Jacksonville Jaguars at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video.

Baltimore is down to its third quarterback after losing Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley to injury against Buffalo last week.

How the Ravens got here

Fagnano was Baltimore's emergency third quarterback. He was forced into action in the second quarter of Sunday's 36-10 loss to the Bills after Jackson and Huntley both went down, and now the job is his for Thursday night.

His NFL debut came in rough circumstances: 10-of-21 passing for 91 yards and an interception, with no first-team reps during the week. He also took a hit to his ankle in Buffalo, but he practiced fully in Baltimore's final two practices and carries no injury designation.

Who is Joe Fagnano?

Fagnano's path to a Thursday night start is about as unlikely as it gets. He began his college career as a walk-on at Maine, transferred to UConn after four years, and put together a career year as a seventh-year senior in 2025: 3,448 yards, 28 touchdowns, and just one interception.

He went undrafted in the 2026 NFL draft, then led all quarterbacks in passing yards in the preseason on his way to a spot on the 53-man roster. He becomes only the second undrafted rookie quarterback in Ravens franchise history to start a game, joining Anthony Brown, who started against Cincinnati in 2022.

What to watch tonight

The matchup is unforgiving. Jacksonville comes in at 5-2 and just put a 42-7 beating on the Colts, while Baltimore sits at 4-4 after three straight losses. The Jaguars bring the NFL's top-ranked rushing defense and second-ranked scoring defense into M&T Bank Stadium.

Baltimore does get one piece of help: rookie right guard Vega Ioane cleared the final injury report and is expected back, which matters with a rookie quarterback who needs protection.

Fagnano's turn under center is likely a one-week job. The Ravens are finalizing a trade with the Arizona Cardinals for veteran Jacoby Brissett, who has made 73 starts over 11 seasons, but Brissett won't officially join the roster until after tonight's game. Thursday night belongs to Fagnano.
