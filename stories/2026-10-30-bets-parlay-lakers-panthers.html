2-Leg Parlay (~+154): Lakers ML + Panthers ML

By Matt

Two favorites in the best spots on the night — the Lakers with Doncic back, the Panthers in a home rematch against a tired Jets team. Together they pay about +154.

The parlay (2 legs, ~+154)

• Lakers moneyline (-130) — Doncic is off the injury report, the Lakers already beat Golden State by 22, and the Warriors are short-handed. Full case for the Lakers here.

• Panthers moneyline (-230) — Florida beat Winnipeg 5-1 on Tuesday, the Jets are 0-5-2 in their last seven, and now Winnipeg plays the second night of a back-to-back in Sunrise. Full case for the Panthers here.

Combined at those prices: about +154. Two favorites, no lottery tickets, no ten-leg nonsense. The idea is simple — take the two cleanest favorite spots on the night and let them pay a little better together than they do alone.

The honest caveat: parlays only pay when every leg wins, and road favorites in the NBA Cup opener plus a home favorite with a backup goalie are not locks. Odds move through the day — check the book before you bet.

Skipped on purpose

• Rockets at Mavericks: Houston is 5-0 and tempting, but Alperen Sengun is questionable with a back strain after missing Wednesday's win — a fresh flag on a key player, so this one stays untouched under the house rule.

• Heat vs. Pacers: The line moved from -3.5 to -7.5 during the day — too much movement to trust the number.

• Devils at Golden Knights and Kraken at Flames: Both are near coin flips in the market. No clean edge, no bet.
