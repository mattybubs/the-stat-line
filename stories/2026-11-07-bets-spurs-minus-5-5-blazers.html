Spurs -5.5 (-110): A shorthanded Blazers backcourt meets the hottest big man alive

By Matt

Portland is down Morant, Sharpe, and Krejci on the second night of a back-to-back, and Victor Wembanyama just put up 52 and 30 in his last two games. Laying 5.5 with the Spurs is the side.

Game slip

San Antonio Spurs (7-2) at Portland Trail Blazers (6-2)

Saturday, November 7, 2026 — 10:00 PM ET — Moda Center, Portland, OR — DAZN-SAS, KUNP, BlazerVision

Spread: Spurs -5.5

Total: 227.5

Moneyline: Spurs -202 / Trail Blazers +170

Odds source: Consensus across major US books, Nov. 7

### Injury report

San Antonio: F Tobias Harris (calf) — out. F/C Jayden Quaintance (knee) — out. Neither has played this season, so the rotation is effectively intact: De'Aaron Fox, Stephon Castle, Dylan Harper, Devin Vassell, and Victor Wembanyama are all averaging 30-plus minutes. Wembanyama rolled his ankle near the end of the first half Friday against Sacramento but finished the game — worth watching, not a reason to fade.

Portland: G Ja Morant (Grade 2 left hamstring strain) — out. G/F Shaedon Sharpe (torn meniscus) — out. F Vit Krejci (left leg fracture) — out. G/F John Tonje — out. That leaves Damian Lillard, Scoot Henderson, and Jrue Holiday to cover the guard minutes, and Henderson is shooting 31.7% from the field this season.

### Storylines

• Wembanyama is on a tear that barely needs adjectives. He dropped 52 points, 24 rebounds, and 8 blocks on Dallas on November 5, then followed it with 30 points and 14 rebounds in Friday's 124-109 win over Sacramento. He is averaging 29.1 points per game this season and 34.6 over his last five.

• Both teams are on the second night of a back-to-back. The difference is who is missing: Portland just lost 124-118 to the Lakers on Friday night in Los Angeles, with Lillard and Deni Avdija each playing heavy minutes, and now comes home to face the West's hottest team without three rotation players.

• The Spurs are 7-2 and playing like the team that won the Western Conference last spring. Fox, Castle, Harper, and Vassell give San Antonio four more creators around Wembanyama; Portland's shorthanded backcourt has one.

• Portland is 6-2 for a reason — this team competes — and the Moda Center is a real home-court edge. The Blazers beat the Lakers in Los Angeles on October 27 and took them to the wire again Friday. They will not roll over.

The bet

San Antonio Spurs -5.5 (-110)

The number is fair, and the matchup is not. Portland's three best answers for Wembanyama's gravity — Morant's pace control, Sharpe's athleticism, Krejci's size on the wing — are all in street clothes. The Blazers' remaining guards shot a combined 31.7% (Henderson) on a night when they needed everything to go right just to hang with the Lakers. Now they get Wembanyama, who has scored 82 points in his last two games, on no rest, with a frontcourt rotation that has no answer for him. The Spurs' depth wins the minutes when Wembanyama sits, and Wembanyama wins the minutes when he plays.

Why

The math is simple and it is on our side. San Antonio is the better team at full strength, and Portland is nowhere near full strength. The Spurs' floor is high — four guards who can create, a center who bends the entire defense, and a defense that travels. Portland's 6-2 record is real, but it was built with Morant and Sharpe in the lineup. Without them, the Blazers are a different team, and the market knows it: this line opened at -4.5 and has been bet up to -5.5.

The honest caveat

Laying 5.5 on the road, on the second night of a back-to-back, is never free money. Wembanyama rolled his ankle Friday — he finished the game and scored 30, but ankles are ankles, and if he is limited or sits, this ticket is in trouble. Portland is 6-2, tough at home, and just played the Lakers to a six-point game with this same shorthanded group. And the total (227.5) tells you the market expects points, which means a backdoor cover is always live if the Spurs ease off late. This is a bet on the better, healthier team — not a lock.

The pick

Spurs -5.5 (-110). The hottest player in the league against a backcourt held together with tape. Lay the points.
