TSL Bets parlay: Brissett OVER 215.5 passing yards + Nuggets -10.5 (~+270)

By Matt

A Monday night debut against a winless team, and a seven-game winning streak against a winless-on-the-road team. Jacoby Brissett OVER 215.5 passing yards (-111) plus the Nuggets -10.5 (-105) pays about +270.

Denver won the first meeting 127-120 in Phoenix on October 30.

The bet

Jacoby Brissett OVER 215.5 passing yards (-111, DraftKings via HelloRookie) + Denver Nuggets -10.5 (-105, consensus) — pays about +270

Why these legs

Brissett is a veteran quarterback making his debut for a new team with 11 days to prepare, against the worst team in football. He's thrown for 1,719 yards this season at 214.9 per game — right at the line, which is the honest truth about this leg: the edge isn't the trend, it's the spot. A rested Baltimore offense against an 0-8 Chargers team that is missing starters on both sides of the ball, with a quarterback who has started 46 games in this league and knows how to run an offense cold. The Chargers' defense has given up points to everyone; Brissett doesn't need a career night, he needs his average.

The Nuggets leg is the mismatch. Denver has won seven straight and is at home against a Suns team that is winless on the road, has lost those road games by more than 18 points a night on average, is playing its fourth game in seven days on zero rest, and is missing its starting center. The Nuggets just won in Minnesota without two starters. The 10.5 is a big number, but the road form supports it — and it's the only big-spread leg in this parlay, as it should be.

The honest caveats

Brissett's season average sits a hair under the line, so this leg is a coin flip on the numbers alone — if the Ravens lean on Derrick Henry and the run game, Brissett might not get the volume. And the Nuggets are also shorthanded without Braun and Cam Johnson, so a flat shooting night could keep this inside the number. Two legs, two teams, two sports — that's the point of the parlay, and both legs have to clear on their own.

The record

TSL Bets is 19-32 in November, 23-53 all-time. Two-leg parlays are 4-4 in November at about +250 — the only parlay format that pays.
