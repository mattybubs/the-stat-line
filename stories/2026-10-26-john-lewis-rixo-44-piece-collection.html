John Lewis x RIXO: the 44-Piece Party-Season Collection, in Stores Now

By Matt

John Lewis' biggest fashion collaboration to date — 44 vintage-inspired pieces with British label RIXO, built around an 1849 archive floral print — is in stores and online now, from £65 to £525.

John Lewis' biggest fashion collaboration to date is in stores now. The 44-piece John Lewis x RIXO collection, launched October 22, pairs the department store's heritage with the vintage-inspired occasionwear of British label RIXO — and it is timed squarely for party season.

The collection

The range covers dresses, knitwear, blouses, skirts, jackets, scarves, shoes and bags, priced from £65 for accessories to £525 for the most elaborate dresses. The hero piece is The Vesper, a full-length dress in an archive print with cap sleeves and a ruffle neckline.

The story inside the story is the print itself. Designers working in the John Lewis Partnership Archive found a floral motif from an 1849 block-printed textile — the first time an archive print has been used in a John Lewis fashion collaboration — and it now runs across two dresses, a blouse, a skirt and a scarf. Around it: vintage florals in autumn colours, plus velvet, sequins, cashmere and lamé, finished with fur and lace trims, ruffles and metallic embroidery.

The brands

RIXO was founded by friends Henrietta Rix and Orlagh McCloskey and built its following on vintage-inspired dresses and distinctive prints. McCloskey called the collaboration "a true extension of RIXO — taking the heritage of John Lewis, leaning into its incredible archive, and bringing it to life with that RIXO magic." John Lewis fashion director Rachel Morgans called RIXO "an iconic British brand adored by so many, much like John Lewis."

The collection is available in John Lewis stores and online. As a limited edition, the pieces worth having are the ones to grab early — the line's own coverage puts it bluntly: identify your favourites sooner rather than later.
