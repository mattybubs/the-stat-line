New Era's Harry Potter '25 Years of Magic' headwear drops — house caps and beanies for every house

By Matt

New Era's Harry Potter '25 Years of Magic' headwear is here — distressed black 9FIFTY caps and knit beanies in all four house colors, plus the Hogwarts Crest merino wool beanie at $41 and the Quidditch at Night 9FIFTY at $46.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

What happened

New Era put its Harry Potter "25 Years of Magic" headwear front and center on Monday, November 9 — a full headwear drop marking 25 years of the first Harry Potter film. The centerpiece is a flat lay of eight pieces: four distressed black snapbacks and four knit beanies, one for each Hogwarts house.

This is a hat drop, not a full apparel line. Every piece is headwear, every piece carries a house crest, and the whole thing is built around the anniversary branding — "25 Years of Magic" is on the product itself, not just the marketing.

The pieces

The drop breaks down into a few clear groups:

The house caps. Four acid-wash black 9FIFTY strapback/snapback caps, each with a circular house crest patch — Gryffindor, Slytherin, Hufflepuff, and Ravenclaw. New Era also showed two darker-art caps in the same drop: one embroidered with "HORCRUX" in gold and red stitching, and another with a white triangular Horcrux symbol and gold splatter.

The house beanies. Four speckled knit beanies in the house colors — maroon for Gryffindor, mustard for Hufflepuff, green for Slytherin, and navy for Ravenclaw — each with a circular embroidered house crest patch. The standout on specs is the Hogwarts Crest merino wool winter beanie: cuffed, dual-layer knit for warmth, textured Hogwarts crest front patch, and an embroidered Hogwarts logo at the rear.

The Quidditch at Night 9FIFTY. A black 9FIFTY snapback with a Hogwarts "H" front logo and a nighttime Quidditch scene — chasing the Golden Snitch — across the front panels, with metallic embroidery, a placid gray undervisor, a Hogwarts rubberized wax seal detail underneath, and a Golden Snitch pin included.

The 9TWENTY dad hats. Vintage-wash 9TWENTY adjustable dad hats for each of the four houses, with the house name embroidered in script above the crest patch.

Prices and where to buy

Two prices are verified from myfitteds, an authorized New Era dealer:

Hogwarts Crest merino wool winter beanie: $41

Hogwarts Quidditch at Night 9FIFTY snapback: $46

The wider Harry Potter line is live on New Era's own US store and in the New Era app — other Harry Potter 9FIFTY snapbacks there are listed at $42.99–$45.99, so the drop pieces land right in the usual New Era collab range. The collection is also stocked at authorized dealers like myfitteds.

Why this one works

New Era has been doing Harry Potter headwear for a while — the June house-cap drop and the September House Pride collection both sold through — but this one is the most complete version of the idea. The distressed black base keeps the caps wearable outside of cosplay, the house colors live on the beanies where the color actually belongs, and the 25th anniversary gives it a reason to exist beyond "another collab." If you had to pick one piece, the Quidditch at Night 9FIFTY is the one with the most going on — the Snitch scene, the pin, the wax seal under the brim.
