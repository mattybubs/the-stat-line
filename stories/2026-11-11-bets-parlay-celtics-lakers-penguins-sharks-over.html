TSL Bets: The Wednesday parlay (~+600) — Celtics -7.5, Lakers -11.5, Penguins-Sharks Over 6.5

By Matt

Three legs, three different bets: the Celtics covering in Toronto, the Lakers rolling a one-win Bucks team, and a goals total in San Jose. One big spread, one smaller one, one total — about +600.

Leg 1: Celtics -7.5 at Raptors

Boston Celtics (9-2) at Toronto Raptors (5-5) — 7:00 PM ET, Scotiabank Arena

Celtics -7.5 (-110), total 215.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Boston: Nobody on the injury report. The rotation is fully healthy.

Injuries — Toronto: Scottie Barnes is out with a groin injury (third straight game), and Kawhi Leonard is out with a knee injury (out at least another week). Immanuel Quickley is questionable with a hamstring injury, and Collin Murray-Boyles and Trayce Jackson-Davis are questionable with illnesses.

Storylines: The Celtics have won four straight and are going for a fifth, after beating the 76ers 123-101 on Tuesday — Jaylen Brown scored 36 and Jayson Tatum added 21 points, 6 rebounds and 4 assists. Toronto is missing its two best players. The caveat is real: Boston is on the second night of a back-to-back, and there was a note earlier in the week that Brown might get a rest day on Wednesday — he played Tuesday after resting Monday, so the rest risk is worth naming. But the Raptors without Barnes and Kawhi are a different team, and the Celtics are healthy.

Leg 2: Lakers -11.5 at Bucks

Los Angeles Lakers (10-2) at Milwaukee Bucks (1-10) — 8:00 PM ET, Fiserv Forum

Lakers -11.5 (-110), total 232.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Los Angeles: Austin Reaves is back — off the injury report after the illness that kept him out Monday. Luka Doncic is clear. Quentin Grimes is questionable with a right finger sprain.

Injuries — Milwaukee: Tyler Herro is out with a right knee contusion — and he is the Bucks' leading scorer at 21.4 points a game.

Storylines: The Bucks are 1-10. They have lost six straight, and they lost to the Knicks 117-85 on Tuesday. The Lakers are 10-2, on a six-game win streak, and they just won in Atlanta without Reaves. This is the parlay's one big spread — the Lakers are the only favorite laying more than 8.5 — and it is the leg with the most room to be wrong, because 11.5 points on the road is a big number. But the Bucks are missing their leading scorer, they are losing by double digits most nights, and the Lakers are the best team in the league right now.

Leg 3: Penguins at Sharks Over 6.5

Pittsburgh Penguins (6-10-2) at San Jose Sharks (8-11-1) — 10:00 PM ET, SAP Center

Penguins -125, total 6.5, Over -110 (DraftKings via ESPN). Projected goalies: Arturs Silovs (PIT) vs Yaroslav Askarov (SJ).

Injuries — Pittsburgh: Bryan Rust is on IR with a lower-body injury, Ryan Graves is on IR with a knee injury, and Elmer Soderblom is on IR. Ville Koivunen is day-to-day with a lower-body injury, and James van Riemsdyk is on IR.

Injuries — San Jose: Igor Chernyshov is on IR, Quentin Musty is on IR-NR, and Michael Keyser is on IR. All depth.

Storylines: Macklin Celebrini is at 199 career points in 170 games (77 goals, 122 assists) and has 21 points in 18 games this season — he took six shots on Monday against the Islanders and came away with no points, so he is due. The goalie matchup is two young netminders, Silovs and Askarov, and both teams leak goals — Pittsburgh is 29th in the NHL, San Jose 25th. Rudebets projects a 6.86 total for this game. The Over needs seven goals, and these two teams give up enough of them.

The bet

Parlay: Celtics -7.5 (-110) + Lakers -11.5 (-110) + Penguins-Sharks Over 6.5 (-110) — about +600.

One big spread, one smaller spread, one total. That is the whole point of the construction: the Lakers are the only leg laying more than 8.5, and the other two legs are built on different kinds of bets — a healthy favorite covering a manageable number, and a goals total between two leaky teams. None of these legs are copies of the single-game bets today, and each one clears on its own merits.

The honest caveat

The Lakers are the risk. Eleven and a half points is a big number on the road, and if the Bucks keep it close — or if the Lakers take their foot off the gas in the fourth — the whole ticket dies on the second leg. The Celtics are on a back-to-back with a rest risk on their best player, and the Over needs seven goals from two teams that can both go quiet. A parlay is a parlay: three things have to go right, and one of them is a double-digit road spread. Bet accordingly.

The pick

Celtics -7.5 + Lakers -11.5 + Penguins-Sharks Over 6.5 — about +600. A $10 ticket returns about $70. One big spread, one smaller one, one total — three different bets, one ticket.
