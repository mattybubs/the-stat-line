Banana Republic x Zankov: 'Future Heirlooms' Holiday Capsule in Stores Now

By Matt

Banana Republic's 27-piece holiday capsule with New York designer Henry Zankov is out now — brushed-cashmere knits, a burgundy leather bomber and a 'Future Heirlooms' campaign, priced $90 to $600.

Colour is the whole point. Banana Republic's limited-edition holiday capsule with ZANKOV — the New York label founded by Henry Zankov — is available now at select stores and online, after launching October 20, 2026.

The collection

The 27-piece collection spans menswear, womenswear and accessories, built around brushed cashmere, proprietary wool yarns, premium leathers and engineered plaids, with relaxed proportions and universal sizing designed to mix and match. Prices run $90 to $600.

The heroes:

• The Auden sweater — brushed cashmere in vivid stripes and solid colours, inspired by vintage sportswear

• The Sasha cardigan — navy and pistachio stripes in brushed cashmere

• The Frankie polo — a classic long-sleeve polo, subverted with a pop of malachite green on the placket

• The burgundy bomber — leather, with Zankov's signature stripe concealed inside the cuff

The campaign

The launch campaign is titled "Future Heirlooms," shot by photographer Andrew Jacobs and paired with commissioned work by Belgian artist Katrien de Blauwer — a nod to Zankov's idea that these are pieces to live with, love, and eventually pass down.

Why it matters

Zankov won the CFDA American Emerging Designer of the Year award in 2024, and this is his knitwear language — vivid stripes, saturated colour, tactile fabric — at Banana Republic scale and prices. For a holiday capsule, that's the rare collaboration where the designer's actual signatures survive the translation.
