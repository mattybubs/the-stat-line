Panthers make NHL history with natural hat tricks in 3 straight games

By Matt

Carter Verhaeghe's natural hat trick in Saturday's 8-3 rout of Seattle made Florida the first NHL team ever with natural hat tricks in three straight games — and Sam Bennett added one too, a Panthers franchise first.

First team ever

The Florida Panthers did something no NHL team has ever done. Carter Verhaeghe's natural hat trick in Saturday's 8-3 rout of Seattle completed a run of natural hat tricks in three consecutive games — the first time in league history.

The streak: Brady Tkachuk went natural against Columbus on Tuesday, went natural again against Vancouver on Thursday, and Verhaeghe finished it Saturday night in Sunrise.

Verhaeghe did it fast — two goals in the final minutes of the first period (17:56 and 19:07), then the third at 6:02 of the second.

Two in one night, too

Sam Bennett joined in. His hat trick gave the Panthers two hat tricks in the same game — a franchise first. Note for the record books: Bennett's was not natural. Shane Wright scored for Seattle between Bennett's second and third goals.

Bennett's third goal was also his 400th career point. Verhaeghe and Bennett are the first Panthers teammates ever with hat tricks in the same game.

The company this keeps

Tkachuk's back-to-back natural hat tricks are the first in the NHL since Petri Skriko did it in November 1986. He was held scoreless Saturday — two assists, no goals — so the streak ends at three, but the record stands.

Florida is also the first team in more than 100 years to record four hat tricks before its 10th game. The last team to do it: the 1922-23 Toronto St. Patricks.

One to watch

Matthew Tkachuk piled up three assists but left late after colliding with Bennett. One report had him returning to the bench, so his status bears watching before Wednesday — when the Panthers visit Ottawa, the team that traded Brady Tkachuk to Florida in June. Revenge game, meet record chase.
