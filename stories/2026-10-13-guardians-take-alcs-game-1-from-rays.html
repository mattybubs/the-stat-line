Guardians take ALCS Game 1 from Rays

By Matt

Cleveland beat Tampa Bay 7-3 in the ALCS opener, piling up seven runs over the final four innings on 14 hits — Game 2 is Tuesday night in St. Petersburg.

The Guardians opened the ALCS by punching the Rays square in the mouth late. Cleveland took Game 1 7-3 on Monday night in St. Petersburg, doing all of its damage when it mattered most.

How it happened

The scoring, inning by inning:

8th: Nathaniel Lowe's two-run single breaks a 2-2 tie; 4-2 CLE

9th: Jo Adell's two-run double puts it out of reach; 7-3 CLE (final)

Seven runs over the final four innings. Fourteen hits in the series opener. That's a lineup that woke up at exactly the right time.

What it means

Cleveland stole home field advantage from Tampa Bay in a single night. Game 2 is set for Tuesday night in St. Petersburg — the Rays now have to win on their own field to avoid heading to Cleveland down 0-2.
