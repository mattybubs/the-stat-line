Delta Reign singles are spiking while Star Trek crashes on release day — this week's TCG market movers

By Matt

Pokémon's Delta Reign is a week old and its singles are climbing — Aarune up 120% in five days — while Magic's Star Trek set hits paper today with its priciest foils already down 80% from their pre-release highs.

The movers

A week into Pokémon's Mega Evolution — Delta Reign, the singles market is doing what it always does with a new set: sorting the real chases from the launch-week noise. Aarune, a holofoil at #139/103, is the week's biggest climber across every set tracked — $25.36, up from $11.52 on November 7, a 120 percent jump in five days. Kecleon, holofoil #117/103, is up 32 percent to $31.02. The sealed side is moving the other way: the Pokémon Center-exclusive Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box is down 27 percent to $152.16 from $208.63, as more boxes hit the market and the early premium bleeds off.

Magic's Star Trek set is the mirror image — and it lands on paper today. The priciest foils have already crashed from their pre-release highs: Mr. Spock, Science Officer in borderless foil is down 82 percent to $66.12 from $358.79, and General Picard, Earth Warlord in borderless foil is down 80 percent to $49 from $250. That is the normal release-week pattern — prices fall as more boxes get opened — but the scale of the drop says the pre-release market got way ahead of itself. The climbers in the set are the cheaper foils: Deep Space 9 foil is up 113 percent to $22.08, and Starbase Yorktown borderless is up 107 percent to $17.90.

The old guard is still moving, too. Sun & Moon Prism Star cards top TCGplayer's latest 30-day risers, led by Giratina ◇ from Ultra Prism — up $52.79 to $92.43 — and Base Set Unlimited Charizard is on the same list at $987.13. In the 30th Celebration set, the Mew G/RGB holofoil is up 9 percent to $3,456.93.

Release day, with a catch

Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek hits tabletop today, November 13, three days after its Arena debut. But not everything is arriving on time: Wizards says several North American products are delayed, including Commander decks, and some EMEA, Asia-Pacific and New Zealand products may also miss the launch date. Play Boosters and Prerelease Packs are on schedule — prereleases ran November 6 through 12 — so the set is playable today even where the Commander decks are late.

One more date for the calendar: Riftbound bans are announced tomorrow, November 14.

What's next

Delta Reign's singles will keep sorting themselves out over the next two weeks, and Star Trek's prices will settle once the delayed products actually land. The pattern to watch is the same one every release shows: the cards people are actually playing climb, the ones people were speculating on fall. Aarune is the first test of which kind it is.
