LaMelo Ball Becomes the Fastest Player in NBA History to 1,000 Threes

By Matt

LaMelo needed three triples on Monday night. He hit all three in the opening minutes, reaching 1,000 career threes in 311 games — 32 games faster than anyone before him — then finished with a season-high 34 in a 112-100 win over Milwaukee.

LaMelo Ball didn't wait long. Needing three triples to reach 1,000 for his career, the Timberwolves guard hit all three in the opening minutes against Milwaukee on Monday night — a logo three about two minutes into the first quarter sealed it — and became the fastest player in NBA history to reach 1,000 career three-pointers.

He did it in 311 games.

The record

The number to beat was 343, set by Duncan Robinson with Miami on March 17, 2024. Ball took 32 games off it. Before Robinson, Buddy Hield held the mark at 350. Ball is just the 188th player in league history to reach 1,000 threes at all — and he got there faster than every one of them.

This wasn't even his first speed record from deep. Ball was also the fastest to 900 threes, reaching that mark in 287 games and beating Robinson's 305.

LaMelo Ball: 311

Duncan Robinson: 343

Buddy Hield: 350

For perspective on how far the road goes from here: Stephen Curry's all-time record sits at 4,270 triples and counting.

The game

The milestone was the start of the night, not the whole of it. Ball finished with a season-high 34 points on 12-of-21 shooting, including 6-of-13 from three, with 6 rebounds and 3 assists in 33 minutes as Minnesota beat Milwaukee 112-100 at Target Center.

He did it banged up, too — listed as questionable with a right wrist contusion before tip. He scored 13 of Minnesota's first 17 points and had 24 by halftime, equaling his previous season high for a game. His sixth three of the night, midway through the fourth quarter, pushed the lead to 96-81 and put the game away.

The Wolves needed him. Anthony Edwards is out after right knee arthroscopy and Donte DiVincenzo is out after Achilles surgery, so the offense ran through Ball and the secondary ball-handlers. Ayo Dosunmu added 19 points and a season-high 12 rebounds, and Jaden McDaniels chipped in 13.

The road here

The milestone came in Ball's eighth game as a Timberwolf, after Minnesota acquired him from Charlotte in a four-team trade on July 10. Of the 1,000 threes, 977 came in a Hornets uniform across 303 games. He came into Monday averaging 3.2 made threes per game for his career — the volume, night after night, is what made 311 possible.
