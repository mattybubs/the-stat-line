Jason Robertson's natural hat trick — his sixth career — powers the Stars past the Flyers for a fifth straight win

By Matt

Jason Robertson scored a natural hat trick — two in the second period, one on the third-period power play — as Dallas beat Philadelphia 5-1 for its fifth straight win, his third straight three-point game.

The night

Jason Robertson didn't just score three goals on Saturday night. He scored the only three that mattered, in order, without interruption — a natural hat trick that turned a tight game into a rout and kept the Dallas Stars rolling.

Robertson struck twice in the second period and once more on a third-period power play as the Stars beat the Philadelphia Flyers 5-1 at American Airlines Center for their fifth consecutive win. It was his sixth career hat trick, his second career natural hat trick, and his third straight three-point game.

Lian Bichsel opened the scoring with his first goal of the season, and then Robertson took over. He doubled the lead at 11:39 of the middle period, tapping in a loose puck after Tyler Seguin lost control of it on a partial break, then scored again before the period ended. He completed the natural hat trick at 9:29 of the third, snapping a shot from the slot under the glove of Flyers goaltender Dan Vladar on the power play. Roope Hintz assisted on all three goals.

Seguin added a goal and an assist of his own, Christian Dvorak scored Philadelphia's only goal, and the Stars closed out a 5-1 win that was never really in doubt after Robertson's second.

The hottest week in hockey

Robertson didn't just have a big night — he had the biggest week in the NHL. He was named the league's First Star of the Week for his six goals and nine points across three games, a stretch that started with a comeback win over Ottawa on November 11 and peaked with Saturday's hat trick.

The numbers stack up fast: a four-game point streak (six goals, four assists), 14 points over his last eight games, and three straight three-point games. When the Stars host the Islanders on Wednesday, Robertson has a chance to become the first player in Dallas franchise history to record four consecutive three-point games.

Dallas improved to 12-4-3 with the win and has points in 12 of its last 13 games (9-1-3), tied for the second-best record in the NHL. The Stars have won 10 of their last 11 meetings with Philadelphia and nine straight at home against the Flyers — and they've outscored their opponents 12-1 over the last two games.

The Flyers fell to 9-6-3, their five-game point streak over. Before the puck dropped, Dallas announced defenseman Thomas Harley is out week-to-week with a lower-body injury — a notable absence for a team that keeps winning anyway.
