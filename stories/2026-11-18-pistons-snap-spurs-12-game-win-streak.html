The Pistons just ended the Spurs' 12-game win streak — Cade's 27, Jenkins' 25, and a fourth quarter San Antonio never solved

By Matt

Cade Cunningham scored 27 points, Daniss Jenkins added 25 off the bench, and Ausar Thompson finished a rebound shy of a triple-double as Detroit rallied from 14 down to beat San Antonio 105-93, ending the Spurs' 12-game winning streak.

The night

The San Antonio Spurs had not lost since October 23. They had won 12 straight, owned the best record in the West, and had Victor Wembanyama rolling toward another MVP case. The Detroit Pistons walked into Frost Bank Center on Tuesday night, fell behind by 14, and beat them anyway, 105-93.

Cade Cunningham led Detroit with 27 points, five rebounds and six assists. Daniss Jenkins added 25 points, five rebounds and five assists off the bench. Ausar Thompson finished a rebound shy of a triple-double with 14 points, 12 assists and five steals.

The Pistons trailed 55-54 at halftime and were down six entering the fourth, 78-72. Then they held San Antonio to 21 fourth-quarter points, led by as many as 11 multiple times in the period, and never let the Spurs' final push get closer than five. Dylan Harper cut it to five with a layup at the 2:35 mark, then slipped and fell heading downcourt with a little more than a minute left, suffering an apparent groin injury. Detroit pulled away from there.

"Have fun. Play basketball. It's another game," Cunningham said after the game. "Obviously, they're a good team, but nobody in that locker room has fear in his heart for anybody. So it's just about coming out here, playing basketball, doing what we do best, executing our game plan."

The streak

Twelve straight wins is the kind of run that starts showing up in franchise history books. Per the Spurs, it was their 25th double-digit win streak since the 1976-77 season, their first in the NBA after moving from the ABA — the most such streaks in the league, ahead of the Lakers (21), Celtics (16), Cavaliers (15) and Thunder (14). The NBA record is 33, set by the 1971-72 Lakers.

The loss drops San Antonio to 12-3, still among the strongest early-season teams in the West. It also held the Spurs below 100 points for the first time this season — 93 was their lowest scoring total of the year.

Wembanyama had 25 points and 18 rebounds in the loss, his 11th double-double of the season. He was blunt afterward: "We didn't do the right things. I think we should have been smarter. As soon as we stepped off the court after the end of the fourth quarter, things looked pretty straightforward and our mistakes looked kinda obvious."

The context

This was not a fluke night for Detroit. The Pistons shot 49 percent from the field, handed out 31 assists, and improved to 9-4 — third in the East — with a 6-2 road record. They have won three of four, and they did this one on the second night of a back-to-back, a night after Cade's 52-point game against Washington last week made him the first player in NBA history with 50 points and 10 assists and zero turnovers in a game.

Jenkins' 25 was the second-half engine: Detroit's bench outplayed San Antonio's depth all night, and the Pistons' defense held a team averaging well over 110 to its worst offensive night of the season.

What it means

Streaks end. The question was always who would be the team to do it, and Detroit answered it with defense, depth and a star who wanted the moment. The Spurs are still 12-3 and still terrifying. But the "when will they lose?" watch is over — and the team that ended it is 9-4 and playing like it belongs in the East's top tier.

Up next: the Pistons visit the New Orleans Pelicans on Wednesday night. The Spurs host the Clippers on Thursday.
