Rituals Steps Into Haute Parfumerie

By Matt

Rituals steps into haute parfumerie with eight unisex fragrances created with perfumers at dsm-firmenich, each designed to last up to 24 hours.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Rituals is stepping into haute parfumerie — and it is doing so with a serious first move rather than a tentative one.

The collection at a glance

Size: Eight fragrances

Audience: Unisex

Perfumers: Created with perfumers at dsm-firmenich

Longevity: Designed to last up to 24 hours

Positioning: Haute parfumerie — Rituals' step up into high perfumery

Why the details matter

Two numbers in that table do most of the talking. Eight unisex fragrances is a full collection, not a single test scent — and it is unisex across the board, so the line is built to be worn by anyone rather than split down a men's/women's divide.

The other number is the 24-hour wear claim. That is the haute parfumerie standard Rituals is aiming at: a fragrance designed to last from morning to the next morning, not one that fades by lunch. And the perfumers behind it come from dsm-firmenich, one of the fragrance industry's major houses — the kind of partner that signals this is a perfumery play, not a bath-and-body line extension.

What it means for Rituals

Rituals built its name on bath, body and home fragrance — scents woven into daily routines. Haute parfumerie is a different lane: higher craft, higher ambition, and a customer who buys perfume as a signature rather than a routine. This collection is Rituals saying it belongs in that conversation.
