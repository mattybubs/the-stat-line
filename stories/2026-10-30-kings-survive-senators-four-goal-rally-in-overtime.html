Kings Survive Senators' Four-Goal Rally in Overtime

By Matt

Quinton Byfield scored in overtime to give the Kings a 6-5 win over the Senators on Thursday, after Ottawa's four-goal third period — led by two from Dylan Cozens — erased a Kings lead.

The Los Angeles Kings needed overtime to finish what looked like a done deal. Quinton Byfield scored in the extra period to give the Kings a 6-5 win over the Ottawa Senators on Thursday night — a win that only exists because Los Angeles survived one of the wildest third periods of the season.

The winner

Byfield ended it in overtime. After sixty minutes couldn't separate two teams that had already combined for eleven goals, it was Byfield who delivered the one that counted, sending the Kings to a 6-5 victory.

The rally

This game turned on Ottawa's third period. The Senators scored four goals in the final frame to claw their way back into a game that had been slipping away from them. Dylan Cozens led the charge, scoring twice during the four-goal third period.

A four-goal period is a statement on its own. Doing it in the third, with the game on the line, is how a team erases a deficit and forces overtime — and Ottawa did exactly that, turning what looked like a Kings win in regulation into a coin-flip extra period.

Surviving the surge

The story of this game is really two stories. There's the Kings team that built a lead big enough to absorb a four-goal period, and there's the Senators team that nearly made that lead irrelevant. In the end, Los Angeles held on just long enough — then Byfield finished it in overtime, before Ottawa's comeback could become a full reversal.

Games like this are why the third period matters more than the first two combined. A lead is only as safe as the team protecting it, and on Thursday night the Kings found that out the hard way — and still walked away with two points.
