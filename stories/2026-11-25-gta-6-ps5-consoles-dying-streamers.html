Two streamers' PS5s died playing GTA 6 — and now players are checking their vents

By Matt

A Twitch streamer's PS5 froze mid-game with green artifacts across the screen, then refused to boot at all. Days earlier, another streamer's console started smoking while GTA 6 was paused. Nobody has proven the game is to blame — but a repair tech measured it pulling more power than a typical game ever does.

GTA 6 is the biggest game ever made, and it might also be the hardest a PlayStation 5 has ever had to work. Two Twitch streamers have now lost their consoles mid-session while playing it — one to a dead GPU, one to smoke — and players across Reddit are suddenly very interested in the state of their vents.

Nobody has proven the game is killing consoles. That matters, and it is the first thing to say. But the two cases are real, they happened on camera, and the power numbers behind them explain why people are worried.

The two consoles

The most dramatic case is Twitch streamer shivjam, whose PS5 died live on stream on November 24. The game froze as Jason got on a bike with Lucia, the screen went pixelated and green — the classic look of failing graphics hardware — and then the console failed outright. "GTA6 tonight fried my PS5," she posted on X. "The GPU is done and the console is gone."

It was not the first warning sign in that same broadcast: the PS5 had already crashed once earlier in the stream, and she thought the issue had resolved itself until the console fully died. When people blamed dust, she showed the inside of her console in a follow-up post — very minimal coating on the fans, kept in open space and good condition.

Days earlier, shortly after launch, streamer JoJoJosiah_ had the scarier version. He paused the game, left the room, and came back to find his PS5 smoking. "Was playing GTA VI on stream but had to go poop," he posted. "Paused it and put on a sensory vid for my chat. PS5 started just smoking out its a** and now I need to get a replacement." The smoke set off alarms, and he had to disconnect the machine to stop further damage.

The power numbers explain the worry

Console repair technician modyfikatorcasper put a meter on a PS5 running GTA 6 to find out what the game is actually demanding. Standing next to a car with little happening on screen, the console pulled 220 watts. Under maximum load, the meter recorded 274.2 watts at the wall.

A typical game peaks at 180 to 220 watts. Sony designed the PS5 to draw up to 340 to 350 watts, so GTA 6 is still inside the console's power budget — but it sits at the high end of it, well above what most games ask for. The technician called the game "the toughest real-world PS5 stress test I've seen," and suspects the heavy load is exposing weak cooling in older base PS5 models, where the liquid metal between the processor and heatsink can shift over time. Sony modified newer base models and the PS5 Pro to mitigate exactly that.

That is the most plausible explanation anyone has offered: not that GTA 6 breaks healthy consoles, but that it is the first game demanding enough to expose cooling problems in aging ones. With only a handful of public cases so far, it is too early to say whether the game is to blame or simply pushing consoles that were already on their way out.

The crashes are the bigger story

Dead hardware is rare. Crashes are not. Players are reporting frequent crashes and sudden shutdowns while playing GTA 6, with error code CE-108255-1 the most common — and reports say Rockstar's first patch did not fix it. That is the part affecting far more people than two streamers, and the part with no answer yet.

Neither Sony nor Rockstar has commented on the hardware failures. Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S consoles have not had similar issues reported, which lines up with the console comparison from earlier this week: the Series X is handling the game more smoothly than the base PS5, with steadier frame pacing and 120Hz VRR support the base PS5 lacks.

If you are playing on an older base PS5, the practical advice from all of this is unglamorous: make sure the console can breathe, clean the vents, and do not bury it in a cabinet. A game this demanding will find whatever weakness your cooling already had.
