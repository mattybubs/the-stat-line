TSL Bets: 4pm window slip — JSN TD, Bowers TD, Texans -5.5 (about +725)

By Matt

The 4pm window slip is the stars-and-chalk slip: Jaxon Smith-Njigba to score (-125) against the league's leakiest pass defense, Brock Bowers to score (+140) against a banged-up 49ers secondary, and the Texans -5.5 (-110) against a winless Chargers team. Three legs, about +725.

The games

Arizona Cardinals (3-5) at Seattle Seahawks (4-4) — 4:25 PM ET, Lumen Field, FOX

Seahawks -8.5, total 39.5, moneyline Seahawks -420 / Cardinals +340 (Win Daily, FanDuel/DraftKings). Seattle opened as a 13.5-point favorite; the line dropped to 8.5 when Sam Darnold was ruled out, and the total fell from 44.5 to 39.5. Darnold is out with a quad injury and a cracked rib suffered Monday night against Chicago, so Drew Lock starts — and Lock has already beaten Arizona this season, 31-7 in Week 2, with three touchdowns and a 139.1 passer rating. Gardner Minshew starts for the Cardinals. Injuries: Arizona is without Bam Knight (concussion), James Conner (foot, IR), Trey Benson (knee, IR), Paris Johnson Jr., Will Johnson, and Kendrick Bourne. Seattle is without Darnold, Charles Cross (ankle), Quenton Nelson (knee), Leonard Williams (stinger), Uchenna Nwosu (ankle), and Rashid Shaheed (knee). Storylines: Jaxon Smith-Njigba leads the NFL in receiving yards and receiving touchdowns; the Cardinals have allowed 18 passing touchdowns (tied for the most in the league) and 29 total touchdowns through eight games (the most); and Lock is 72.9% completions with four touchdowns and zero interceptions this season.

Las Vegas Raiders (4-4) at San Francisco 49ers (6-1) — 4:25 PM ET, Levi's Stadium, FOX

49ers -9.5, total 52.5, moneyline 49ers -475 / Raiders +364 (optimal-bet). The Raiders score 27.6 points per game, and Kirk Cousins ranks top-10 in EPA per play and success rate. Injuries: Las Vegas has a clean report — Thomas Booker (ankle) is out, but all offensive starters are good to go. San Francisco is banged up: Mike Evans (Achilles), Christian Kirk (knee), and Deebo Samuel (hamstring) are all out, along with Ricky Pearsall, and Upton Stout (shoulder) and Nate Hobbs (adductor) are questionable. Storylines: the 49ers are 9.5-point favorites with their top three receivers out; the Raiders' offense is the healthiest unit on the field; and the total of 52.5 says the market expects points even with San Francisco's injuries.

Houston Texans (2-5) at Los Angeles Chargers (0-7) — 4:05 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, CBS

Texans -5.5 (-110), total 40.5, moneyline Texans -235 / Chargers +200 (Win Daily, FanDuel/BetMGM). The Chargers opened as 2.5-point favorites; the line has reversed all the way to Houston -6 at some books. Injuries: Houston is without David Montgomery (groin) and Reed Blankenship (concussion/hand). Los Angeles is without Derwin James Jr. (hamstring), Rashawn Slater (ankle), Deane Leonard (concussion), and Kendall Williamson (calf), with Brenen Thompson (quad/hamstring) and David Njoku (fibula) doubtful. Storylines: the Chargers are 0-7, score a league-worst 14.1 points per game, and Justin Herbert has five touchdowns against 10 interceptions while being sacked 23 times. C.J. Stroud has 11 touchdowns against one interception for 1,867 yards. Covers' top pick is Texans -5.5.

The legs

Leg 1 — Jaxon Smith-Njigba anytime touchdown (-125, DraftKings)

JSN leads the NFL with 985 receiving yards, 64 catches, and nine receiving touchdowns — the most in the league. He has scored in six of eight games, averages 123.1 yards per game, and has topped 102 yards in seven of eight. Against Arizona specifically, with Lock at quarterback, he went 8-for-122-and-1 and 9-for-155-and-3 in Week 2. The Cardinals have allowed the most touchdowns in the NFL. This is the best receiver in football against the worst pass defense in football, at -125.

Leg 2 — Brock Bowers anytime touchdown (+140, Sports Interaction)

Bowers at plus money is the value leg of the slip. The Raiders score 27.6 points per game, Cousins is playing efficient football, and the 49ers' secondary is held together with tape — their top three receivers are out, but so is the depth behind them, and the defense has a bevy of injury question marks. Bowers is the Raiders' best red-zone weapon, and +140 for a touchdown from a tight end in a game with a 52.5 total is a fair price.

Leg 3 — Houston Texans -5.5 (-110)

The one spread anchor in the slip, and it is the most lopsided matchup on the board. The Chargers are 0-7, cannot score, cannot protect their quarterback, and are missing Derwin James and Rashawn Slater. The Texans are 2-5 but a much better team than their record — Stroud is playing clean football, and the line has moved from Chargers -2.5 to Texans -6, which is the market telling you everything. Laying 5.5 on the road is never fun, but this is the right spot for it.

Why this slip

Two star-player touchdown legs at fair prices, plus a spread anchor on the most one-sided game of the window. JSN and Bowers are both the best red-zone options on their teams, and both are playing defenses that give up touchdowns. The Texans leg is the chalk that holds the slip together — it does not need to be pretty, it needs to be a win by a touchdown against a team that has not won a game all season.

The honest caveat

Drew Lock is the risk in leg one. He has been good this season, and he torched Arizona in Week 2, but he is a backup quarterback with an elbow injury who was limited in practice all week. If Lock struggles, JSN's volume shrinks. Bowers at +140 is plus money for a reason — tight end touchdowns are volatile, and the 49ers' defense is still good even when it is hurt. And the Texans are laying 5.5 on the road, which is always a live number against a desperate team. The slip pays about +725 because all three legs have to hit, and the Bowers leg is a coin flip at best.

The pick

3-leg 4pm window slip, about +725: Jaxon Smith-Njigba anytime TD (-125) + Brock Bowers anytime TD (+140) + Houston Texans -5.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $82.50. The best receiver in football, the best tight end in football, and the worst team in football. That is the bet.
