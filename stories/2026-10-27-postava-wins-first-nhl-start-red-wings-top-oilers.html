Postava Wins First NHL Start as Red Wings Top Oilers in Shootout

By Matt

Michal Postava stopped every shootout attempt against Edmonton to win his first career NHL start, after Dylan Larkin forced overtime with 1:17 left in the third.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The story

Michal Postava won his first career NHL start — and he did it the hard way, stopping every shootout attempt against Edmonton.

How it got there

Dylan Larkin forced overtime with 1:17 left in the third period. Without that late equalizer, there is no shootout for Postava to win.

The shootout

With the game on the line, Postava stopped every Edmonton attempt in the shootout. A first career start, a late comeback to even reach overtime, and then a perfect shootout — that is a debut start players remember for a career.

Why it matters

• A first NHL start ending in a win is rare enough; winning it in a shootout, against Edmonton, makes it a story.

• Larkin's 1:17-left equalizer is the swing moment — the Red Wings were a minute and change from a regulation loss.

• Postava's perfect shootout is the exclamation point: no goals allowed when it mattered most.
