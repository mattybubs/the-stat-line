TSL Bets: The Black Friday parlay (~+236) — Coleman anytime TD, Freiermuth OVER 21.5 receiving yards

By Matt

Jonah Coleman owns Denver's backfield with J.K. Dobbins suspended, and Pat Freiermuth has cleared 21.5 receiving yards in three straight games. Two legs, about +236, in the rain in Pittsburgh.

The game

Denver Broncos (8-2) at Pittsburgh Steelers (6-4) — 3:00 PM ET, Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh, PA (Prime Video)

Broncos -4.5 (-110), total 36.5 (Over -114 / Under -106), moneyline Broncos -235 / Steelers +194 (BetMGM via USA Today Sportsbook Wire, November 26). Forecast: 90% chance of rain, low 40s, windy.

Injuries — Denver: S Taylor Rapp OUT (quadriceps). DE Sai'vion Jones OUT (hamstring). RB Tyler Badie QUESTIONABLE (thumb). RB J.K. Dobbins SUSPENDED for this game. S Brandon Jones returns after missing two games (hamstring — no game status). CB Pat Surtain II CLEARED.

Injuries — Pittsburgh: LB Alex Highsmith OUT (ankle). G Gennings Dunker OUT (knee). LB Cole Holcomb QUESTIONABLE (heel). WR Ben Skowronek on injured reserve (calf). QB Mason Rudolph starts — his first start of the season, after Aaron Rodgers was benched Tuesday night and released Wednesday. WR Michael Pittman Jr. activated from injured reserve (foot), no designation. WR DK Metcalf CLEARED.

Storylines: The Black Friday game is pure chaos on one sideline and a machine on the other. Denver arrives riding six straight wins and sitting in the thick of the AFC's top seeds. Pittsburgh is clinging to the final wild-card spot after two straight losses, and the week has been a storm: Rodgers benched, Rodgers released, Rudolph — making his 21st career start — thrown in against one of the league's best defenses in the rain. The Steelers will lean on the run and the short passing game, which is exactly where both of these legs live. Coleman has seen his share of Denver's backfield grow in each game since coming off injured reserve four games ago, and he was a one-man wrecking crew in Week 11: three touchdowns and 129 total yards. With Dobbins suspended, the backfield is his outright.

Leg 1 — Jonah Coleman anytime touchdown

Coleman has six touchdowns this season and just put up three in one game last week, and now the only other back who was taking his goal-line work is suspended. Denver is a 4.5-point favorite with a run-first game plan in bad weather — the exact script for a lead back to find the end zone. Line: anytime touchdown (-130, BetMGM via USA Today Sportsbook Wire; -135, DraftKings).

Leg 2 — Pat Freiermuth OVER 21.5 receiving yards

Freiermuth has cleared this number in three straight games — 39 yards at Philadelphia, 35 at Cincinnati, 35 against Cleveland — and he is the safety valve a first-time starter leans on: short routes, quick throws, move the chains. Rudolph will be under pressure from Denver's pass rush all afternoon, and the tight end is where the ball goes when the pocket collapses. He has 32 catches for 309 yards and 5 touchdowns this season. Line: OVER 21.5 receiving yards (-111, BetMGM via USA Today Sportsbook Wire).

The bet

Jonah Coleman anytime touchdown (-130, BetMGM) + Pat Freiermuth OVER 21.5 receiving yards (-111, BetMGM) — about +236. A $10 ticket returns about $33.63.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Coleman is the lead back on a 4.5-point favorite in a run-weather game with the goal-line work to himself, and Freiermuth is a yardage prop with a three-game floor and a quarterback who needs him. Neither leg depends on the other — different players, different sides of the ball, different markets. That is the juice rule working as intended: two props with real trend edges, about +236 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

The weather is the shared risk — rain, low 40s and wind in Pittsburgh can turn this into a slog where neither offense sustains drives, and Freiermuth's leg is the exposed one. Both legs live in the same game, so one bad afternoon in the rain sinks both. The total is just 36.5, so points are expected to be scarce, and Freiermuth's target volume is thin — two to five targets a game — so one quiet half can end it. And a parlay needs both legs — either one can sink it alone. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 23-45 in November, 27-66 all-time.
