Pokémon's Dual Fusion Is Live — and the Espeon and Umbreon Decks Land Tomorrow

By Matt

Pokémon TCG Pocket's Dual Fusion expansion is out today with the game's first dual-type cards — Mega Mewtwo X ex and Mega Greninja ex lead the set — and tomorrow the physical game gets its 30th Celebration battle decks, Espeon ex and Umbreon ex, ready to play out of the box for $39.99.

Pokémon's 30th anniversary year keeps stacking releases, and this week is one of the biggest yet — on both sides of the game. The app gets its first dual-type cards today. The physical game gets two ready-to-play decks tomorrow. Here's the full picture.

Dual Fusion is live today

Pokémon TCG Pocket's Dual Fusion expansion launched today, and its headline mechanic is something the app has never had before: dual-type cards. One card, two types at once. The two stars are Mega Mewtwo X ex (Psychic and Fighting) and Mega Greninja ex (Water and Darkness), split across two booster packs — one for Mega Mewtwo X, one for Pikachu — with new versions of Pikachu ex, Mew ex, Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise, and Mega Dragonite ex in the mix.

For players, dual-type cards change deckbuilding in a real way. A Psychic/Fighting attacker doesn't just hit harder — it opens lines your deck couldn't run before, because a single card can answer matchups that used to need two different attackers. That's the kind of mechanic that reshuffles a meta overnight, and it's why this set is getting more attention than a normal Pocket drop.

The launch comes with a lot around it. Six Dual Fusion rental decks are free to play through December 15, so anyone can try the new cards without building from scratch. A Special Pack called First Partners runs October 31 to December 26, featuring the first partner Pokémon from all nine regions. And second anniversary events run from today into mid-December, with an anniversary ticket exchangeable for a booster pack with a guaranteed high-rarity card.

The bigger structural news is the schedule change that arrives with the new C Series: sets move from roughly a four-week cycle to roughly six weeks. The developers said it plainly — players told them big sets were making collecting hard. Collectors asked for breathing room, and they got it. That's a rare thing in a live game: a publisher slowing down because the players asked.

The Espeon and Umbreon decks land tomorrow

If physical cards are more your thing, tomorrow (October 30) brings the Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration Battle Decks — Espeon ex and Umbreon ex. Each is a ready-to-play, all-foil 60-card deck for $39.99, with a playmat, coin, and deck box in the box. The Espeon ex deck runs with Victini; the Umbreon ex deck runs with Zeraora. No chasing packs to play — open it and you're in a game.

That's the part worth noticing. In a year full of chase products and 30 different Pikachu variants to collect, a pair of decks built for actually playing — every card foil, everything you need in the box — is a different kind of release. It's aimed at the person who wants to sit down and play tonight, not the person hunting a pull. For a 30th anniversary celebration, that's a good look: the game remembering it's a game.
