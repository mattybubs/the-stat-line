Jordan Love Deals: 288 Yards, 3 TDs as Packers Hold Off Panthers 27-19

By Matt

Love spread three touchdown passes to three different targets, the defense sacked Bryce Young six times, and Green Bay survived a late Carolina push on Thursday night.

Jordan Love didn't need a perfect night. He needed a Thursday night where the ball went where he wanted it, and that's exactly what Green Bay got: 30 completions on 41 throws for 288 yards and three touchdowns, no sacks taken, and a 27-19 win over Carolina that was more comfortable than the final score makes it look.

The Packers led 27-10 in the third quarter, watched the Panthers claw back to within eight, and then did the most boring, most winning thing a team can do: they held on.

Love spread it around

All three of Love's touchdown passes went to a different target, which is a polite way of saying Carolina couldn't key on anybody. Tucker Kraft opened Green Bay's scoring with a 12-yard grab in the first quarter. Matthew Golden hauled in a 24-yarder late in the second. Josh Jacobs capped the third-quarter scoring with a 4-yard catch.

Love finished with a 106.6 passer rating and an 80.0 QBR, and the cleanest number on his line might be the zero under sacks. Carolina never got him on the ground once. His one blemish was an interception to Zakee Wheatley, returned 17 yards, and it never turned into anything that mattered.

Watson did the heavy lifting between the 20s

Christian Watson didn't score, and he still might have been Green Bay's most important pass catcher. Eight catches for 113 yards, including a 53-yard strike, kept the chains moving while the touchdown catches went to other people. Every good passing night has a guy like that, the one doing the unglamorous work up and down the field so the red-zone stuff looks easy.

Golden finished with seven catches for 79 yards and the score. Kraft added six for 53 and his touchdown.

Jacobs scored twice the hard way

Josh Jacobs' rushing line looks like a typo until you see the touchdowns: 21 carries for 39 yards, a 1.9 average, and still two trips to the end zone. He punched in a 1-yard run in the second quarter and caught the 4-yarder in the third.

Carolina's front did its job against the run. Green Bay just didn't need the run to work. When the ground game gives you less than two yards a carry and you still put up 27, that's a quarterback win, plain and simple.

The defense feasted on Young

While Love stood clean, Bryce Young spent the night running for his life. Green Bay sacked him six times for 33 yards in losses, and the pressure never let Carolina's passing game breathe. Young finished 23 of 41 for 209 yards, one touchdown, one interception, and a 68.0 rating.

Evan Williams led Green Bay with 10 tackles, and the pass rush did the rest. It's hard to mount a comeback when your quarterback is picking himself up every third drive.

Carolina wouldn't go away

Credit the Panthers: they didn't fold at 27-10. Chuba Hubbard ground out 22 carries for 81 yards and a touchdown, plus seven catches for 38 more, in a true workhorse performance. Tetairoa McMillan caught six for 58. And with 5:37 left in the fourth, Young found Jalen Coker for a 26-yard touchdown that cut it to 27-19.

The two-point try failed, and that was the last scoring play of the night. Carolina got close enough to make it interesting, but never close enough to make it scary.

What it means

Green Bay wins a Thursday-nighter behind its quarterback's arm and a pass rush that lived in Carolina's backfield. The run game will need to be better than 1.9 yards a carry against better fronts, but on a night when the passing game hummed and the defense hunted, that was a problem for another week.

For Carolina, it's another night where the effort was real and the result wasn't. Hubbard deserved better. Young needs more time. And the Panthers are still looking for the win that turns a hard-luck season around.
