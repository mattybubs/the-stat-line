Pokémon TCG's biggest week of the year lands Friday: Delta Reign and the 30th Celebration collections

By Matt

Mega Evolution—Delta Reign launches Friday, November 6 alongside the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection, Ditto Premium Collection, and Figure Collection — the biggest single release day of Pokémon's 30th anniversary year.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

What happened

Friday, November 6 is the biggest release day of Pokémon's 30th anniversary year. The new Mega Evolution expansion, Delta Reign, launches alongside a trio of 30th Celebration products — and after weeks of shortages and raffles, this is the day collectors have been circling.

Here is everything hitting shelves.

Mega Evolution—Delta Reign

Delta Reign is the new Mega Evolution expansion, headlined by the return of Mega Rayquaza ex. The set brings new Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, more than 35 Pokémon and Trainer cards with special illustrations, and a brand-new mechanic: Legendary Stadiums.

The products out Friday are the Booster Display Box and the Elite Trainer Box. The ETB carries a $49.99 MSRP, though expect to pay more wherever stock is tight — Walmart's raffle this week listed it at $60.00.

The 30th Celebration wave

The anniversary line gets its final batch of products on the same day:

• 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection (Day and Night) — the Eevee-themed centerpiece of the anniversary line. MSRP is $179.99, but the market has already spoken: Target and Barnes & Noble list it at $249.99, GameStop at $399.99, and Walmart's Wednesday raffle offered it at $224.97.

• 30th Celebration Ditto Premium Collection — MSRP $39.99, listed at $49.97 in Walmart's raffle.

• 30th Celebration Figure Collection — the Mew and Mewtwo figure collections round out the wave.

UK retailers have flagged that the Ultra-Premium and Ditto collections may arrive in waves, with allocation shortages pushing some stock past the November 6 date — so if your shop does not have them Friday, it may not mean you missed out.

How the Walmart raffle went

Walmart ran its weekly Wednesday collectibles raffle on November 4, and this one was loaded: six Pokémon products, five of them pre-orders for Friday's releases, including the Ultra-Premium Collection, the Ditto Premium Collection, the Delta Reign ETB, a Delta Reign 3-pack blister at $15.97, and a Prismatic Evolutions Elite Trainer Box at $69.00 against a $49.99 MSRP.

Entry opened at 2:00 p.m. PST and lasted exactly one hour, with winners drawn at random. It cost nothing to enter — and with the UPC going for more than double its MSRP at some retailers, it was the cheapest shot anyone got at one this week.

What to watch

Delta Reign is the set, but the Ultra-Premium Collection is the story. It is the most hyped product of the entire 30th Anniversary lineup, it has not had a clean retail release yet, and Friday is the first real test of whether supply can meet demand. If the UPC sells through at the big retailers on day one, the secondary market does what it always does — and the $179.99 MSRP becomes a memory.

Get to your shop early on Friday. This is the one to be in line for.
