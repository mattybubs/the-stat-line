Bucs stun Cowboys 24-16 for first win

By Matt

Undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels won his second career start, Bucky Irving ran for 165 yards and two scores, and Tampa Bay stunned Dallas 24-16 in Arlington.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The upset

Undrafted rookie Jalon Daniels turned his second career start into Tampa Bay's first win of the season, a 24-16 upset of the Cowboys in Arlington on Thursday night.

The rookie delivered

Daniels was starting in place of the injured Baker Mayfield — and on the road, on a short week, against a Dallas team that had cashed 10 straight Overs, he managed the game and made the throws that mattered. First career win, in prime time.

The ground game closed it out

Behind him, Bucky Irving scored twice as the ground game never let Dallas back in. Tampa ripped off a 17-0 third quarter to break the game open, and the Cowboys never answered.

### By the numbers

• Final: Buccaneers 24, Cowboys 16

• Irving rushing yards: 165

• Irving touchdowns: 2

• Bucs 3rd quarter: 17-0

• Daniels: second career start, first career win (undrafted rookie)
