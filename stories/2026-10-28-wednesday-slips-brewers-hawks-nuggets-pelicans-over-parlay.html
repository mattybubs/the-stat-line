Wednesday's Slips: Brewers to Clinch, Hawks -3.5, Nuggets-Pelicans Over 240.5 — Plus a 2-Leg Parlay (~+226)

By Matt

Three clean plays for Wednesday night: Milwaukee to finish the job in Game 5 behind its ace, Atlanta laying a short number against the league's only winless team, and the points in a rematch that just went for 276. A brutal injury slate — six games skipped on purpose.

Game slip: Milwaukee Brewers at Tampa Bay Rays — World Series Game 5

• Matchup: Milwaukee Brewers (103-59, 10-2 this postseason) at Tampa Bay Rays (98-64, 8-4)

• Time/channel: Wednesday, 8:00 p.m. ET, FOX, from Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg

• Odds (DraftKings, via SI): Brewers -141, Rays +117; run line Brewers -1.5 (+148); total 6.5 (Over -110 / Under -110)

• Pitching: Brewers: Jacob Misiorowski (16-5, 1.80 ERA in the regular season; 4 ER in 13.1 IP this postseason; one run in five innings in Game 1). Rays: TBD as of early Wednesday morning — a bullpen game is the likely route, possibly Drew Rasmussen on short rest.

• Injury report: Brewers — Grant Anderson (biceps), Brian Fitzpatrick (elbow), Quinn Priester (wrist), Angel Zerpa (forearm), all on IL. Rays — Gavin Lux (shoulder), Jon Heasley (elbow), Shane McClanahan (forearm), Edwin Uceta (shoulder), Jacob Melton (hamstring), Ryan Pepiot (hip), all on IL. No fresh flags on either side — the uncertainty here is the Rays' unnamed starter, not an injury.

• Storylines: (1) Milwaukee is one win from its first World Series title. (2) Tampa Bay's bullpen covered eight innings in the 12-inning Game 4 win and now faces the best pitcher in baseball. (3) Milwaukee has not lost back-to-back games since September 5-6.

The bet: Brewers moneyline (-141). An ace against a bullpen game, with the other bullpen taxed from a 12-inning night. The honest caveat: the Rays just proved they will not go quietly, and a TBD starter means Tampa Bay could still open with a rested arm — this is a moneyline play on the pitching mismatch, not a run-line one.

Game slip: Miami Heat at Atlanta Hawks

• Matchup: Miami Heat (0-4) at Atlanta Hawks (2-2)

• Time/channel: Wednesday, 7:00 p.m. ET, local TV, from State Farm Arena in Atlanta

• Odds (FanDuel): Hawks -3.5 (-110); total 240.5; moneyline Hawks -148, Heat +126

• Injury report: Heat — Simone Fontecchio doubtful (illness); Pelle Larsson probable (ankle). Hawks — Mouhamed Gueye out (foot surgery); Aaron Wiggins questionable (left knee contusion); Jock Landale cleared to play with a nasal fracture. No star-level flags on either side.

• Storylines: (1) Miami is the league's only winless team — 0-4 despite Giannis Antetokounmpo's triple-double (29 points, 14 rebounds, 11 assists) in Monday's 113-109 loss to Dallas. (2) Atlanta is 2-2 after two losses to Houston, with Jalen Johnson scoring 21 on Monday. (3) The Hawks rank seventh in defensive efficiency, allowing 111.8 points per game.

The bet: Hawks -3.5 (-110). A short number at home against a winless team that has yet to score more than 111 points in a game. The honest caveat: Miami won three of four against Atlanta last season, including both games in Atlanta by a combined 46 points — this is a form play, not a matchup lock.

Game slip: Denver Nuggets at New Orleans Pelicans

• Matchup: Denver Nuggets (1-2) at New Orleans Pelicans (0-3)

• Time/channel: Wednesday, 8:00 p.m. ET, NBA League Pass, from Smoothie King Center in New Orleans

• Odds (FanDuel/PickDawgz): Nuggets -4.5; total 240.5; Over 240.5 (-108)

• Injury report: Nuggets — Cam Johnson doubtful (ankle sprain). Pelicans — Yves Missi out (knee bone bruise); Jordan Poole day-to-day (illness). No fresh flags on either team's core scorers.

• Storylines: (1) These teams just met Saturday: Denver won 141-135 in overtime — 276 total points. (2) New Orleans has lost its first three games by a combined 10 points; the offense is not the problem. (3) Denver leaked 74 second-half points in Monday's 134-114 home loss to Golden State — the defense is very much a problem.

The bet: Over 240.5 (-108). Two leaky defenses, a rematch that just cleared this number by 35, and no unders here — this is a scoring game by every recent number. Caveat: Denver's pace can grind when Jokic runs the offense through the post, so this needs both teams to keep scoring, not just one.

The parlay (2 legs, ~+226)

• Brewers moneyline (-141) + Hawks -3.5 (-110) — about +226. Two plays, both on form, no lottery tickets.

Skipped on purpose

• Warriors at Timberwolves: Anthony Edwards is out for knee surgery (up to two months). Fresh flag on the game's best player — easy pass.

• Rockets at Bucks: Houston's Alperen Sengun is out with low back soreness, Marcus Smart questionable, Steven Adams out. The 4-0 team's best player is missing — pass.

• Thunder at Mavericks: Chet Holmgren and Alex Caruso out, Jalen Williams and Cason Wallace out long-term, Ajay Mitchell questionable — and Kyrie Irving questionable for Dallas. Too many moving parts.

• Spurs at Jazz: Jaren Jackson Jr. tore his MCL (six to eight weeks) and Lauri Markkanen is questionable with a sprained wrist. Pass.

• Magic at Raptors: Paolo Banchero and Jalen Suggs out for Orlando, Kawhi Leonard out weeks for Toronto. Pass.

• Lakers at Clippers: Brandon Ingram, Bradley Beal and Rui Hachimura all out for the Clippers, and the Lakers are on a back-to-back. Pass.
