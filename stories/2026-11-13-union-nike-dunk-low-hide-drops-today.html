Union's leopard Dunk Low 'Hide' goes wide today — $130, and the heel folds down into a mule

By Elijah

The Union x Nike Dunk Low 'Hide' gets its wider release today, November 13, for $130 through Nike. Tan suede, leopard spots, no lining — and a soft heel designed to fold down so the shoe wears as a slip-on mule.

The drop

The Union x Nike Dunk Low "Hide" goes wide today, November 13, for $130 through Nike. The shoe first launched at Union Los Angeles on October 30, giving the boutique the first window, as Union releases usually do. Today is the moment it reaches everyone else — Nike SNKRS and neighborhood retailers.

The colorway is Palomino, University Gold and Light Cream, style code IX2929-200. The palette is warm and earthy, closer to a natural hide than a loud animal-print statement: a tan suede Dunk Low covered in leopard spots, built without a lining. And then there is the detail nobody can stop talking about — the heel. It is soft, with no structure, and designed to fold down, so the shoe can be laced up and worn as a normal Dunk Low, or the heel can be collapsed and it slips on and wears like a mule.

The pack

The "Hide" is part of Union's "Natural Selection" pack, and the wider release is split across channels — Union took the first window, and Nike SNKRS and retailers handle today's rollout. Official photos landed November 10, three days out, which is usually the signal that the general release is locked and the SNKRS calendar entry is the one to watch.

The Dunk Low is the most remixed silhouette in Nike's modern catalog, and most collaborations change its colors or materials. This one changes how the shoe works. An unlined, fold-down heel on a leopard suede Dunk is a strange idea on paper. On feet, it is either a gimmick or the most comfortable Dunk Union has ever made — and today is the first day most people get to find out which.

Where to get them

The Union x Nike Dunk Low "Hide" releases today, November 13, 2026, for $130 through Nike SNKRS and select retailers. Style code IX2929-200.
