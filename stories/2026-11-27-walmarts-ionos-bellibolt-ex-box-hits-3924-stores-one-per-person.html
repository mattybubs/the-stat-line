Walmart's Iono's Bellibolt ex box hits 3,924 stores, one per person

By Matt

Walmart put the Pokémon TCG: Iono's Bellibolt ex Premium Collection on shelves at 3,924 stores at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday — one box per person, no Walmart+ membership needed.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Walmart opened its doors at 6 a.m. local time on Black Friday with one Pokémon box on the shelf that collectors have been waiting weeks for: the Pokémon Trading Card Game: Iono's Bellibolt ex Premium Collection, available at 3,924 participating stores while supplies last. The limit is one box per person, and you don't need a Walmart+ membership to buy one in store.

The price at the shelf is $49.97, according to Polygon — a markup over the $39.99 MSRP listed by Pokémon. That gap is worth knowing before you go, because the sealed box has been trading well above both numbers: TCGplayer market price sat at $62.42 as of November 26. Even recent eBay sales of sealed boxes ranged from $31.40 to $56.99 between November 16 and 20, so Walmart's in-store price lands in the middle of the real market — not a steal, but a fair shot at a box that's been hard to find at retail.

What's in the box

This isn't a new product. The collection first launched on April 11, 2025, built around Iono — the streamer gym leader from Pokémon Scarlet and Violet — and her electric partner Bellibolt. Per Pokémon, each box contains:

• 1 full-art foil promo card featuring Iono's Bellibolt ex

• 1 foil card featuring Iono's Tadbulb

• 1 sheet of acrylic standees featuring Iono, Bellibolt, and more

• 1 double-sided backdrop display

• 1 photo sticker featuring Iono and Bellibolt

• 6 Pokémon TCG booster packs

• A code card for Pokémon TCG Live

Polygon reports this restock's packs come from sets such as Chaos Rising and Pitch Black. Pokémon doesn't name the packs, and the original 2025 release carried different sets, so check the back of the box before you assume what's inside.

Why this one matters

The Bellibolt box is the centerpiece of Walmart's second Pokémon wave of the season. The first wave landed November 12 with 30th Celebration ex boxes and tins, and the retailer has more coming online for Cyber Monday, November 30 — including the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection. For collectors who struck out on the November 12 drop, this is the second real chance at a marquee box in two weeks, and the only one that requires showing up in person.

The promo card itself is the draw for players, too. Iono's Bellibolt ex carries the Electric Streamer Ability, which lets you attach extra Lightning Energy to Iono's Pokémon — a card that has stayed relevant in the Delta Reign format, where it pairs with the new Mega Rayquaza ex.

Before you drive over

Two warnings from the early reports: some Walmart stores put the boxes out ahead of schedule, so your local store may already be picked over — and some stores had Bellibolt signage up while actually selling the Garchomp box. Look at the box in your hand, not the sign above it. With the limit in place, the odds are decent if you get there early, but this is a while-supplies-last drop, and there's no online backup for this one.
