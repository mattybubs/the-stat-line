Jeff Petry announces his retirement from the NHL

By Matt

Defenseman Jeff Petry announced his retirement on Wednesday's episode of the 'Never Offside' podcast, closing a career that ran from Edmonton to Minnesota by way of a Stanley Cup Final with Montreal.

How he said it

Jeff Petry announced his retirement from the NHL on Wednesday's episode of the "Never Offside With Julie & Cat" podcast, the show co-hosted by his wife, Julie Petry, and Cat Toffoli. The National Hockey League Players' Association marked the moment with a congratulatory post of its own, confirming what Petry had just said himself: after a career that stretched across a decade and a half, he is hanging up his skates.

Petry had not played at all this season. He last suited up in 2025-26, splitting his final campaign between the Florida Panthers and the Minnesota Wild, and he went unsigned into the fall as a free agent. The announcement makes official what the empty season already suggested.

The career in numbers

Petry was a second-round pick, 45th overall, by the Edmonton Oilers in the 2006 draft, and he went on to skate in 1,048 NHL games over 16 seasons with six teams: Edmonton, Montreal, Pittsburgh, Detroit, Florida and Minnesota. He finished with 96 goals, 298 assists and 394 points, along with 428 penalty minutes.

For a defenseman who was never the flashiest name on a roster, that is a serious body of work. Year after year, coaches trusted him to eat hard minutes and not hurt them, and he kept answering the call.

The Montreal years

The longest chapter came in Montreal. The Oilers dealt Petry to the Canadiens at the trade deadline on March 2, 2015, and he spent just over eight seasons there — the heart of his career. His most productive stretch came in those years: four consecutive 40-point seasons from 2017-18 through 2020-21, when he was one of the steadiest two-way defensemen in the league.

The peak was the spring of 2021, when Petry helped the Canadiens reach the Stanley Cup Final — the run Canadiens fans will remember him for.

The last stop, and the international stage

After Montreal, Petry's career became a tour of the league's map: a trade to Pittsburgh in the summer of 2022, a move to his home-state Detroit Red Wings, then the one-year deal with Florida in July 2025 and the mid-season trade to Minnesota that closed out his final year.

He wore the USA jersey, too. Petry represented the United States four times at the IIHF World Championship, in 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2024, winning a bronze medal at the 2013 tournament in Sweden and Finland.

A defenseman who lasted a decade and a half in the league and kept getting the call for his country — that is a career worth the quiet announcement, and the respect that followed it.
