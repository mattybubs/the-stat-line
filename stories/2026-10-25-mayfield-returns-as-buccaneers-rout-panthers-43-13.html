Mayfield Returns as Buccaneers Rout Panthers 43-13

By Matt

Baker Mayfield threw his 200th career touchdown pass in his return as Tampa Bay rushed for 258 yards in a 43-13 rout of Carolina.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Baker Mayfield's return could hardly have gone better. Back under center for Tampa Bay, Mayfield threw his 200th career touchdown pass as the Buccaneers routed the Carolina Panthers 43-13 on Sunday (Bucs Wire live coverage).

The ground game did the damage

Tampa Bay rushed for 258 yards on the day — the kind of number that lets a returning quarterback settle in rather than carry the offense. The Bucs controlled the game on the ground and never let Carolina back into it.

Final score: Buccaneers 43, Panthers 13

Tampa Bay rushing yards: 258

Mayfield career TD passes: 200

The milestone

The 200th career touchdown pass is a real marker: only a select group of quarterbacks ever reaches it. That Mayfield did it in his first game back — and in a 30-point divisional rout — made the return that much sweeter for Tampa Bay.
