Nike, Georgia and Outkast Release the Outkast U Collection

By Matt

The first Outkast U drop lands today through SNKRS, Class Trip and select Nike retail partners in Atlanta, led by the red wool varsity jacket.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The drop

The first drop of the Outkast U collection lands today, October 27, through SNKRS, Class Trip and select Nike retail partners in Atlanta.

It is a three-way link-up: Nike, the University of Georgia, and Outkast.

The lead piece

The red wool varsity jacket leads the range. It is the piece that sets the tone for the whole collection — Georgia football heritage crossed with Outkast's Atlanta sound.

Why it matters

• Three names, one collection: a sportswear giant, a college football program, and one of hip-hop's defining duos.

• The first drop is Atlanta-only at retail, through SNKRS, Class Trip and select Nike partners in the city.

• The varsity jacket as the lead piece says everything about the collection's lane: collegiate, classic, and built to be worn, not just collected.
