Snake is back on the calendar — Delta Force's Metal Gear Solid collab runs November 27 through December 31

By Matt

Team Jade revived the postponed Delta Force × Metal Gear Solid Δ: Snake Eater crossover: it goes live November 27 and runs through December 31, with a Naked Snake appearance for Nox, a free Legendary SR-3M, and the Cardboard Box Stealth action for logging in.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The wait is over — literally. Team Jade announced today that the Delta Force × Metal Gear Solid Δ: Snake Eater collaboration goes live on Friday, November 27, and runs through December 31. Naked Snake is coming to Delta Force after all, nearly a year after the crossover was supposed to arrive and then vanished.

A year in the wilderness

This one has been a long time coming. Konami and Team Jade first confirmed the crossover in September 2025, with the events originally set for December 19, 2025 and the Naked Snake appearance for Nox slated for January 1, 2026. Then, on December 11, 2025, both dates were pulled — postponed, with no replacement date and no reason given. The crossover became one of those announced-but-never-seen collabs, and the silence stretched on for eleven months.

Today's announcement ends that. The update lands across November 26 and 27 with no downtime and no interruption to matchmaking, according to the official announcement — if your client is out of date, you'll need to update before you can log in.

Naked Snake × Nox

The centerpiece is the same one that was promised last year: a legendary collab appearance that turns Delta Force's stealth operator Nox into Naked Snake, the protagonist of Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater and Konami's 2025 remake. It comes with the voice to match — David Hayter, the original voice of Snake, recorded more than 500 new lines for the appearance, a detail Team Jade's game director confirmed when the collab was first revealed.

The appearance sits in a timed prize pool that runs for the full event. Pulls use Delta Tickets or Delta Coins, and every attempt guarantees either an appearance reward from the pool or Naked Snake Coins. The pool also holds a Metal Gear Solid Δ S12K shotgun, a name card, avatar, calling card, spray paint, and voice lines — and unspent Naked Snake Coins disappear when the event ends, so spend them before the window closes.

The free Legendary is the real prize

The best reward in the event doesn't cost a thing. The Collab Legendary Gift runs November 27 through December 27: play Operations, Warfare, or Detonation and complete activity missions — mostly staying alive in matches — to climb milestones and earn Tactical Armament Vouchers, with the ultimate reward a free Legendary collab firearm appearance, the Metal Gear Solid Δ SR-3M. Survival time carries over between matches, so a bad run doesn't reset your progress.

Then there's the box. Log in during the event and you get the Action — Cardboard Box Stealth, the single most Metal Gear thing ever put in a shooter. In Operations, logging in between November 27 and December 17 claims it outright; in Warfare and Detonation, in-match missions unlock it. MGS-themed boxes also spawn in Operations matches, and searching them can turn up rare loot, including limited-edition figurines.

What else is in the update

The update also folds in a DFI Celebration event from November 29 through December 11, marking the end of the Delta Force Invitational global tournament in Sweden with login rewards, including a supply pack and an Invitational AKM. There are also Warfare balance tweaks — the Thermobaric Bomb's burn radius grows from 30m to 40m and its tick damage from 5 to 6, while the fixed Vulcan Cannon drops from 1,000 to 700 HP — plus the usual round of bug fixes.

The timing is no accident. The collab lands the day after Thanksgiving, in the same week GTA 6 is still eating every other game's player count, and it runs through New Year's Eve — a five-week window for one of the most requested crossovers in the free-to-play shooter space. Snake waited a year. Now he's on the clock.
