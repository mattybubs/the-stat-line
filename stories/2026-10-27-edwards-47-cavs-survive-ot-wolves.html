Edwards Drops 47, but the Cavs Survive in OT and Hand the Wolves Their First Loss

By Matt

Anthony Edwards scored a season-high 47 points, but Cleveland rallied from 15 down to beat previously unbeaten Minnesota 127-125 in overtime.

The result

Final (OT): Cleveland Cavaliers 127, Minnesota Timberwolves 125.

Anthony Edwards poured in a season-high 47 points, but the previously unbeaten Timberwolves fell for the first time as Cleveland rallied from a 15-point deficit.

How it ended

Donovan Mitchell hit the tiebreaking midrange jumper with 48 seconds left in overtime, finishing with 26 points and seven assists. Edwards missed a wide-open three with 3.9 seconds left that would have tied it; Peyton Watson grabbed the rebound and hit two free throws to push Cleveland's lead to 127-122 before Edwards' buzzer three closed the scoring.

The supporting casts

James Harden added 19 points and nine assists for Cleveland, Evan Mobley posted 18 points and 10 rebounds, and rookie Meleek Thomas scored 16 off the bench in his NBA debut. For Minnesota, Rudy Gobert grabbed 20 rebounds and LaMelo Ball had 11 points — but seven turnovers.

Why it matters

• Edwards' 47 was 21 points clear of the next-highest scorer in the game — and still not enough.

• Cleveland improves to 2-1 behind a balanced attack; Minnesota drops to 3-1.

• The Wolves' first loss comes in the game where their star was at his best — a reminder that one man, even at 47, doesn't beat five.
