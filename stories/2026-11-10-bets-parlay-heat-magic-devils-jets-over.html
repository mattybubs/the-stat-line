TSL Bets: Heat -9.5, Magic -5.5, Devils-Jets Over 6 — the Tuesday parlay, about +600

By Matt

Heat -9.5 at the 1-9 Jazz, Magic -5.5 at the 1-9 Kings, and Devils-Jets Over 6. Three legs at -110, about +600. One big spread, two more that stand on their own.

The legs

Leg 1 — Miami Heat -9.5 (-110) at Utah Jazz. Heat (4-6) at Jazz (1-9), 9:00 PM ET, Delta Center. Miami -9.5, total 228.5. The Jazz have lost eight straight. Keyonte George is out with an ankle injury and Lauri Markkanen is questionable with a wrist injury. Miami has no players out, and Giannis Antetokounmpo is probable with a wrist injury. The Heat are the better team by a distance, and Utah is running out of healthy bodies.

Leg 2 — Orlando Magic -5.5 (-110) at Sacramento Kings. Magic (2-7) at Kings (1-9), 10:00 PM ET, Golden 1 Center. Orlando -5.5, total 222.5. The Kings are 1-9 and banged up: Domantas Sabonis is questionable with a knee injury after missing Sunday, Keegan Murray is questionable with a wrist injury, and Maxime Raynaud is out with a knee injury. Orlando is 2-7 and not exactly rolling, but the Kings are the worse team in a worse spot.

Leg 3 — New Jersey Devils at Winnipeg Jets Over 6 (-110). Devils (13-5) at Jets (7-8), 7:00 PM ET. Total 6. The Jets are without Josh Morrissey (wrist, injured reserve), Vladislav Namestnikov, Nick Bjugstad and Elias Salomonsson. The Devils are without Anthony Mantha and Seamus Casey. Two offenses that can score, two defenses missing pieces — the over is the play.

The bet

Heat -9.5 (-110) + Magic -5.5 (-110) + Devils-Jets Over 6 (-110), about +600. Three legs at -110, each one a bet worth making on its own. The Heat are laying a big number against a 1-9 team that has lost eight straight and is missing its starting point guard. The Magic are laying a small number against a 1-9 team missing its best player. And the Devils-Jets total is 6 with both defenses short-handed.

The parlay spread rule holds: only one big spread in the ticket. The Heat -9.5 is the one. The other two legs are a small spread and a total — different kinds of bets, different kinds of risk.

The honest caveat

Parlays are parlays. The Heat leg is the obvious danger — nine and a half points is a lot to cover on the road, and if Miami coasts in the fourth quarter, the back door is wide open. The Magic are 2-7 themselves, so laying points with them is a leap of faith. And the Devils-Jets over needs seven goals, which is never a guarantee. Any one leg can kill the ticket — that's the deal. But each leg clears on its own merits, and that's the only way to build one of these.

The pick

Heat -9.5 + Magic -5.5 + Devils-Jets Over 6, about +600. A $10 ticket returns about $70. Three legs, each one defensible, one big spread and two more that stand on their own.
