Resident Evil Requiem takes Game of the Year at the Golden Joysticks — and a two-person indie matched it

By Matt

Capcom's Resident Evil Requiem won Ultimate Game of the Year at the 44th Golden Joystick Awards in London. The night's other story: Meccha Chameleon, made by two people in five months, won four awards — the most ever by an indie at the ceremony.

The night

The 44th Golden Joystick Awards took over the Troxy in London on Wednesday night, and the headline result went the way most people expected: Resident Evil Requiem is the Ultimate Game of the Year.

Capcom's survival-horror sequel did not just win the big one. It took Console Game of the Year, Best Game Trailer and Best Lead Performer for Angela Sant'Albano as Grace Ashcroft — four awards for one game, and seven for Capcom overall, including Studio of the Year. PRAGMATA and Onimusha: Way of the Sword added to the haul. Requiem has now sold 8.5 million copies.

The rest of the major prizes spread out: Crimson Desert won PC Game of the Year, The Witcher 4 took Most Wanted Game, CONTROL Resonant won Best Visual Design and Onimusha took Best Audio Design. The show also premiered the announcement trailer for Conan: The Black Pyramid, a roguelite action RPG with two-player online co-op, planned for PC in 2027.

The other winner

The best story of the night was not the favorite winning. It was the tie.

Meccha Chameleon — a hide-and-seek party game made by two people, Lemorion and Haganeiro, in roughly five months — also won four awards, matching Requiem for the most on the night. It beat Marathon, Valheim and ARC Raiders for Best Multiplayer, topped Mewgenics and Cairn for Best Self-Published Indie, and won the Developers' Choice award over Mortal Shell 2 and Orbitals. It won every category it was nominated in except Best PC Game, which went to Crimson Desert.

Those four wins are the most any indie game has ever taken home from a single Golden Joystick ceremony in the awards' 44-year history. The game has sold more than 20 million copies — more than Pokémon Legends Z-A, God of War Ragnarök and Resident Evil Village managed in their lifetimes.

One more record fell on the night: with its Best Remake/Remaster win, The Witcher 3 now has 10 Golden Joystick wins in total, the most of any game in the ceremony's history.

The fine print

The Golden Joysticks run early in the fall, and the cutoff shows: November releases like the Ocarina of Time remake and Grand Theft Auto VI were not eligible for Ultimate Game of the Year. The Game Awards, the other big show of the season, is still to come — and those two games will be very much in that conversation.
