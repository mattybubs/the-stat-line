Kith's New Balance 2011 'Monuments' pack drops today — three NYC landmark colorways for $200

By Elijah

The Kith x New Balance 2011 'Monuments' collection releases today, November 9 — three colorways inspired by New York City landmarks at $200 a pair, led by the Knicks-themed 'Banner' in the first Kith Night since the Knicks won the championship.

What happened

The Kith x New Balance 2011 "Monuments" collection releases today, November 9. Three colorways, each inspired by a famous New York City landmark, each $200 — and the timing is the whole story, because this is the first Kith Night since the Knicks won the NBA championship.

The three colorways: "Park" takes the Central Park theme Kith first explored back in 2014, "Banner" draws from the New York Knicks and their championship win last season, and "Garden" takes after floral gardens — a nod, almost certainly, to Madison Square Garden. The "Park" and "Banner" colorways hit all Kith retail locations. The "Garden" is exclusive to the New Balance at Kith store in West Hollywood. All three pairs are also available through Kith.com and the Kith App.

Why this one hits different

For the fifth consecutive year, Ronnie Fieg and Kith will celebrate "Kith Night" at Madison Square Garden — this time on Tuesday, November 10, when the Knicks take on the Chicago Bulls. It is the first Kith Night since the Knicks won the title, and the "Banner" colorway is the shoe version of that: a championship celebration you can wear.

Fieg's relationship with the Knicks has only grown stronger since he was named the franchise's first-ever creative director in 2022. He designs the team's City Edition uniforms and courts, and Kith's New Balance collabs stand as the only officially licensed Knicks sneakers on the market. Jalen Brunson and Josh Hart are genuine fans, often spotted in Kith apparel in the tunnel. The 2011 itself is an original tech runner silhouette Fieg debuted earlier this year — and Fieg recently popped out in a previously unseen Knicks colorway of it, white with blue overlays and orange accents, that had the timeline talking before this pack was even announced.

The "Monuments" concept is a revival, too. Kith explored a similar idea back in 2014 with the New Balance 530 and 850, dressing the former after Central Park and the latter in a Brooklyn Bridge theme. The 2026 collection brings the Central Park theme back and adds two new ones — a full-circle moment for a collaboration that has been running for over a decade.

The details

Release: Today, November 9

Price: $200 per pair

"Park": Central Park theme — all Kith retail

"Banner": Knicks championship theme — all Kith retail

"Garden": Floral gardens theme — West Hollywood exclusive

Online: Kith.com and the Kith App

Kith Night: Tuesday, Nov 10 — Knicks vs. Bulls at MSG
