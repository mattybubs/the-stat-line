Hurricanes -1.5 (+124): A 10-Game Win Streak Meets a .500 Predators Team

By Matt

Carolina is 12-2-2, riding a 10-game win streak behind a goaltender who has won all 10 of his starts. Nashville is 5-4-5 and starting a goalie at 3-3-4. Laying 1.5 at plus money is the value side of the NHL's best team.

The game slip

Matchup: Carolina Hurricanes (12-2-2) at Nashville Predators (5-4-5)

Puck drop: Tuesday, November 3, 8:00 PM ET — ESPN+, Bridgestone Arena, Nashville

Line: Hurricanes -192 | Predators +158 | Total 6.5 (via FanDuel); Hurricanes -1.5 at +124 (via ESPN lines)

Hurricanes injury report: OUT — Jordan Staal (calf), Taylor Hall (upper body). Alexander Nikishin (not injury related) is working back as an extra. Staal's absence is real — he's the captain — but it's a longer-term absence, not a fresh flag, and Carolina has won 10 straight with this group.

Predators injury report: OUT — William Trudeau (upper body, since early October). Nashville is otherwise healthy, which makes the gap in the standings harder to explain away — this isn't an injury story, it's a talent and form story.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. Carolina is the NHL's best team by record (12-2-2), the defending champion, and the No. 1 team in ESPN's power rankings every week since the opener. The Hurricanes just shut out Winnipeg 7-0 on Sunday.

2. Pyotr Kochetkov has won 10 straight starts to open the season (10-0-0, 1.50 GAA, .929 save percentage, two shutouts). Nashville counters with Juuse Saros at 3-3-4 with a 3.00 GAA and .902 save percentage. That goaltending gap is the bet.

3. The last time these teams met twice in 11 days last December, Carolina swept both games by three goals. Nashville's top four scorers — Forsberg, Stamkos, O'Reilly, Josi — are all 32 or older and carrying the offense; Forsberg is hot (four goals, nine points in his last four), but one hot line doesn't beat this Carolina team.

The bet

Carolina Hurricanes -1.5 (+124). The moneyline at -192 is the safe play and a bad price for this column. The puck line pays plus money because Carolina has to win by two — and this Carolina team wins by two a lot: a 7-0 shutout Sunday, a sweep by three goals in the last head-to-head set, and a goalie who hasn't lost yet. Nashville is 5-4-5 for a reason.

The honest caveat: Forsberg is genuinely on fire, Saros can steal a game, and a one-goal Carolina win — the most common way good teams win on the road — burns this ticket while cashing the moneyline. If you can't stomach that, take the -192 and move on. But at +124, the puck line is where the value lives tonight.
