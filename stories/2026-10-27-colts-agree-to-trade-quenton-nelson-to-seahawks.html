Colts Agree to Trade Quenton Nelson to Seahawks

By Matt

The Colts have agreed to trade eight-time Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson to the Seahawks for a 2027 third-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick.

The deal

The Colts have agreed to trade eight-time Pro Bowl guard Quenton Nelson to the Seahawks, per multiple reports. Seattle will send draft capital back to Indianapolis in return.

The return

Seattle Seahawks: Quenton Nelson, eight-time Pro Bowl guard

Indianapolis Colts: 2027 third-round pick

Indianapolis Colts: 2028 fifth-round pick

The pick details come from USA TODAY's account of the deal.

Why it matters

• Nelson is an eight-time Pro Bowl guard — a proven, top-end interior lineman changing teams mid-season.

• For Seattle, this is a win-now move: adding a player of Nelson's standing to the offensive line.

• For Indianapolis, the return is future draft capital — a 2027 third-rounder and a 2028 fifth-rounder — rather than a player coming back in the deal.
