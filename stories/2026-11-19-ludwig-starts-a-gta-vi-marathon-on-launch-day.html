Ludwig starts a GTA VI marathon on launch day

By Matt

Ludwig kicks off a GTA VI marathon at 3 p.m. ET on launch day, streaming non-stop until the game is finished — at his own pace, with a code for viewers every hour on the hour.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Ludwig is spending launch day the way only he would: live, non-stop, and in no hurry. The streamer kicks off a Grand Theft Auto VI marathon at 3 p.m. ET on Thursday, November 19 — the day the game launches — and the stream does not end until the game is done.

Taking his time

The hook here is the pace. Ludwig has been running marathon streams for five years, and he says he has noticed a pattern lately: streamers speed through a game to beat it as fast as possible, and he has been guilty of it himself. His verdict is that rushing cheapens the experience — so for GTA VI, he is taking his time.

The plan was announced by his company, Mogul Moves, on Monday, November 16 at 8:00 p.m. ET: "Ludwig's GTA VI Marathon starts Thursday, November 19." The same post added the one-line rule for the run: "Stream doesn't end until the game is done." About an hour later, Ludwig quoted the announcement with his thinking in full:

> "i have been marathoning games on stream for 5 years and ive noticed a trend recently where it feels like people speed through the game to try to beat it as quickly as possible"

>

> "ive been guilty of this as well and i realized it cheapens the experience"

>

> "so for GTA VI im TAKING MY TIME"

A viewer then asked for an exact start time, and Ludwig replied it "should start at noon PT" — 3 p.m. ET.

The hourly code

There is a reason to keep the stream open even when he is wandering Vice City instead of pushing the story. Ludwig is giving out a code for viewers every hour on the hour for the entire marathon. His stream recap describes the giveaway as game codes, handed out hourly across a run expected to last around 60 hours — though the announcement itself does not say what the code is for, and no platform was named in the post.

Launch day is a marathon for everyone

Ludwig is not the only big name clearing the calendar. The launch has turned into a streaming event of its own:

Kai Cenat: Full playthrough from 8 p.m. ET Wednesday, November 18, on YouTube and Twitch

IShowSpeed: Starts 9 p.m. ET Wednesday, November 18, in 10- to 12-hour days — no marathon

Ludwig: Non-stop from 3 p.m. ET Thursday, November 19, at a relaxed pace

YouTube: 24-hour launch livestream from midnight ET November 19, with an RDCWorld pre-show at 11 p.m. ET November 18

The contrast is the story: Cenat goes first and goes all-in, Speed deliberately paces himself across days, and Ludwig splits the difference — non-stop, but refusing to race. In a launch week where being first to the credits is the default flex, the streamer who built his name on endurance broadcasts is betting that slowing down is the better show.
