iShowSpeed just won Streamer of the Year at the Esports Awards — his second Streamer of the Year trophy in five days

By Matt

iShowSpeed won Streamer of the Year at the Esports Awards 2026 in Miami on Monday night, beating Kai Cenat, CaseOh, Jynxzi and Caedrel — five days after taking the same title at the Streamer Awards in Los Angeles.

The night

Darren "iShowSpeed" Watkins Jr. won Streamer of the Year at the Esports Awards 2026 on Monday night, November 17, at the Adrienne Arsht Center in Miami.

He beat 11 other nominees for the title: Kai Cenat, CaseOh, Jynxzi, Caedrel, OhnePixel, JasonTheWeen, Cinna, StableRonaldo, Snax Gaming, Coringa and TheBurntPeanut.

It is his second Streamer of the Year trophy in five days. Last Thursday, November 13, Speed won the same title at the sixth annual Streamer Awards in Los Angeles — his third straight win there — taking three trophies on the night. The Esports Awards is a separate ceremony, esports' own biggest night, and Speed just swept the top streamer prize at both of them in the same week.

The other winners

Marc "Caedrel" Lamont was honored as Esports Personality of the Year, and Jynxzi took Esports Content Creator of the Year. The ceremony returned to Miami for what the organizers call the biggest and most prestigious night in esports, with the night's winners announced across the full list of categories.

The year

The award caps a year in which Speed kept expanding past streaming. He did his World Cup Tour this year, partnering with FIFA to livestream soccer fans around the globe, and last month he became an executive producer on "The Eyes of Ghana," a documentary alongside former President Barack Obama and Michelle Obama.

He also has GTA 6 on the calendar: Speed and Kai Cenat have both announced plans to stream the game when it releases — Cenat starting at 8 p.m. ET on November 18, Speed an hour later at 9 p.m. ET.

What it means

Two ceremonies, two Streamer of the Year trophies, five days apart. Whatever you think of the awards circuit, the industry just told the same story twice in one week: right now, the biggest streamer in the world is Speed — and the gap between him and everyone else is not particularly close.
