Darnold out for Week 9, Lock in line to start

By Matt

Sam Darnold will miss Seattle's Week 9 game against Arizona with a quad injury, and Drew Lock is in line to start in his place.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

What happened

Sam Darnold will miss the Seattle Seahawks' Week 9 game against the Arizona Cardinals. Head coach Mike Macdonald announced the news on Wednesday.

Darnold suffered a quadriceps injury late in Monday's loss to the Chicago Bears, and that injury is what keeps him out this week.

Next man up

With Darnold sidelined, Drew Lock is in line to start in his place against Arizona.

Why it matters

Seattle heads into a divisional matchup without its starting quarterback, and the timing stings — the injury came at the end of a Monday night loss, leaving the Seahawks to turn to their backup on a short week of preparation.

The details

Player out: Sam Darnold

Injury: Quadriceps

When it happened: Late in Monday's loss to the Chicago Bears

Announced by: Head coach Mike Macdonald, on Wednesday

Game missed: Week 9 vs. the Arizona Cardinals

In line to start: Drew Lock

Sources: USA Today and NBC Sports / Pro Football Talk.
