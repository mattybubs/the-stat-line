Scott Pilgrim is officially coming to Fortnite — the creator confirmed it himself

By Matt

Scott Pilgrim is coming to Fortnite, and the confirmation came from the top: series creator Bryan Lee O'Malley revealed the crossover himself in an 18-image Instagram post that ends on the Fortnite battle bus. No date or pricing yet — but the files are already in the game.

Scott Pilgrim is coming to Fortnite. The confirmation did not come from a leaker or a datamine — it came from series creator Bryan Lee O'Malley himself, who revealed the crossover in an 18-image post on his Instagram account on November 23.

The post is a slow zoom-out. The first image shows Ramona Flowers holding Scott Pilgrim's hand as the two fall from the sky. Each slide pulls back a little further on the strange object in the distance until the outline resolves into something unmistakable: the Fortnite battle bus, the vehicle that drops players onto the island at the start of every match. O'Malley did not add a date or any details. He did not need to. The bus is the announcement.

What the files say — and what they don't

Dataminers dug into the latest Fortnite update and found the cosmetics the collaboration is expected to bring: two skins, one emote, two pickaxes, and two instruments — a bass and a drum kit. The skins are expected to be Scott and Ramona, which fits the teaser. The instruments are where it gets interesting, and where the leaks stop being a straight line. Scott plays bass in his band Sex Bob-Omb, so the bass is his. Ramona does not play an instrument. Kim Pine is the drummer. So either the drums belong to Kim — meaning the crossover includes more characters than the teaser shows — or the instrument set is just a bundle and the logic does not matter. Either way, the cosmetics themselves are still unconfirmed by Epic, and so is everything about how they will be sold.

An earlier leak pointed the same direction. On November 12, veteran leaker HYPEX flagged an encrypted file found during downtime for update 43.10, with cosmetics matching Scott and Ramona — two skins with LEGO variants, back blings, and an emote built around the Power of Love sword. At the time Epic had said nothing. Now the creator has said it himself, which moves the whole thing from "leaked" to "confirmed, details pending."

Why this one lands differently

Fortnite's recent crossovers have leaned hard into anime, and Treasure Hunters is the peak of that: the One Piece crew has been rolling out in waves since the season launched November 1. Scott Pilgrim is a lane change. The franchise started as O'Malley's graphic novel series, became the 2010 cult film Scott Pilgrim vs. the World, and expanded again into the Netflix animated series Scott Pilgrim Takes Off, produced by Science Saru. It is a story about a bass player in a Toronto band fighting his crush's seven evil exes — funny, scrappy, and twenty years into its cult status. It fits Fortnite's anything-goes island, but it is not another anime tie-in. It is a comic-book world dropping into a battle royale.

Epic has not announced an official release date, V-Bucks pricing, or bundle contents for the Scott Pilgrim crossover. The teaser is official, the files are in the game, and the creator has confirmed it — so the launch should not be far off. When it lands, the question will not be whether Scott and Ramona show up. It will be whether Kim Pine and the rest of the band come with them.
