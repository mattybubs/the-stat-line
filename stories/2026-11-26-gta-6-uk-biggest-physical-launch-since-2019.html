No disc, no problem: GTA 6 is the UK's biggest physical launch since 2019 — £32 million in three days

By Matt

The box holds a download code, not a disc — and it still outsold every physical launch in Britain since 2019. The no-disc gamble didn't slow GTA 6 down at the till. It barely registered.

Rockstar made the most controversial packaging decision in modern gaming history: Grand Theft Auto VI has no disc. The physical edition is a box with a download code inside. Fans argued about it for months — and then went out and bought it anyway. In its first three days on sale in the UK, GTA 6 took £32 million at retail, making it the biggest physical game launch in Britain since FIFA 20 in September 2019, according to NielsenIQ data reported by The Game Business.

That is a code in a box outselling every disc launch of the last seven years.

The all-time list tells the story

GTA 6 now ranks No. 17 for first-week boxed revenue in the UK since records began in 1995 — only 16 titles in three decades have taken more in their opening week, and the most recent of those was FIFA 19 in 2018. Its predecessors still sit ahead of it: GTA V is No. 1 all-time and GTA IV is No. 11. Launch day alone, Thursday, November 19, ranked as the 20th biggest day in UK games retail revenue since NielsenIQ's records began in 2000.

The concentration was extreme. GTA 6 accounted for around 74 percent of all physical software revenue in the UK that week, and 88 percent of its own first-week sales came on launch day. People did not trickle in over the weekend. They showed up on Thursday.

The whole shop came with it

The launch lifted everything around it. Total UK games retail revenue hit £142 million across the three-day launch period, up 99 percent week on week. Boxed software revenue reached £43 million — a 409 percent jump and the biggest week for physical games since Christmas 2018.

Hardware rode the same wave. Console sales rose 38 percent to their highest level since late 2025, with PlayStation 5 up 46 percent and Xbox Series up 75 percent week on week. Accessories generated £21 million, up 123 percent. Joypad volumes jumped 194 percent — PS5 pads alone were up 355 percent — headset volumes rose 94 percent, and external console SSDs had their biggest week of 2026 so far.

The context that makes it land

Here is the number that frames all of it: boxed software accounts for just 15 percent of UK games retail revenue so far in 2026, down from 50 percent in 2008. Physical is a shrinking slice of the market — and GTA 6 just posted one of the biggest physical weeks ever inside that shrinking slice. As NielsenIQ's Dorian Bloch put it, the performance shows the enduring retail power of one of gaming's biggest franchises, even as the market shifts toward digital downloads.

It was not just Britain, either. The Game Business reports more than a million boxed copies sold in France in three days, and the biggest physical launch in Spanish chart history. And the migration was visible on the other side of the industry too: VGC notes live-service games took a hit after launch, with players leaving Call of Duty, Fortnite and other online games to play through GTA 6. One game that benefited was GTA 5, which saw its own sales rise.

The digital numbers already told us GTA 6 was the biggest launch in entertainment history — the Alinea Analytics estimate we covered Monday put it at 44.9 million copies in four days. The UK retail data adds the part nobody was sure about: the box still matters. Rockstar bet that players would pay full price for a box with no disc in it, and the tills answered in three days.
