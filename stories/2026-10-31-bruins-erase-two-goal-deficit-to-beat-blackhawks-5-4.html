Bruins Erase Two-Goal Deficit to Beat Blackhawks 5-4

By The Stat Line

Morgan Geekie tied it in the final seconds of regulation and David Pastrnak passed Patrice Bergeron for third on the Bruins' all-time goals list, reaching 428, in Boston's 5-4 win over Chicago.

The comeback

Boston fell behind by two goals in the third period against Chicago — and refused to lose. Morgan Geekie tied the game in the final seconds of regulation, capping a rally that turned a late deficit into a 5-4 Bruins win over the Blackhawks.

It was the kind of finish that erases everything that came before it: down two in the third, the Bruins found a way to force the game back level with the clock nearly gone, then finished the job for the win.

Pastrnak passes Bergeron

The night also belonged to David Pastrnak. His goal moved him past Patrice Bergeron for third on the Bruins' all-time goals list, reaching 428 career goals in a Boston uniform.

Passing Bergeron — a franchise icon and the standard for two-way greatness in Boston — puts Pastrnak in rare company. Third on the all-time list for an Original Six franchise is the kind of milestone that defines a career, and Pastrnak reached it on a night when his team needed every bit of offense it could find.

Why it matters

• The rally: Erasing a two-goal third-period deficit takes more than talent — it takes a team that doesn't blink when the game looks lost.

• The milestone: 428 goals and counting, with Pastrnak now alone in third in Bruins history behind only the franchise's greatest scorers.

• The timing: Geekie's tying goal in the final seconds kept the night alive and set up the 5-4 finish.
