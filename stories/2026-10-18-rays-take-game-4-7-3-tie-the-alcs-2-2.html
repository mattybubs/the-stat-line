Rays take Game 4 7-3, tie the ALCS 2-2

By Matt

Jonathan Aranda's three-hit night woke up Tampa's middle of the order as the Rays beat the Guardians 7-3 in Cleveland, squaring the ALCS at 2-2 with Game 5 tonight.

The middle wakes up

The Tampa Bay Rays finally got their offense out of neutral. Jonathan Aranda's three-hit night woke up the middle of Tampa's order, and the Rays took Saturday's Game 4 in Cleveland 7-3, evening the American League Championship Series at two games apiece.

The result guarantees the series keeps going — and it sets up a decisive turn at Progressive Field with the pennant race dead even.

What happened

Aranda was the difference. His three-hit night powered the Tampa offense and finally got the middle of the order producing at the moment the Rays needed it most. Cleveland, playing at home at Progressive Field, couldn't keep pace as the game got away late.

With the 7-3 final, the ALCS is now tied 2-2 — a best-of-three sprint for the American League pennant.

Game 5 tonight

The series doesn't wait long. Game 5 goes tonight at 5:08 p.m. ET at Progressive Field, with Guardians rookie Parker Messick starting opposite Drew Rasmussen for the Rays.
