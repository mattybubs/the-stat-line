Magic's Star Trek set beams onto Arena today — tabletop lands Friday

By Matt

Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek hits MTG Arena today, with the tabletop release following Friday. Four Commander decks, signed Headliner cards from seven Star Trek actors, and a full event schedule are live.

What happened

Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek arrives on MTG Arena today, Tuesday, November 10. The tabletop release follows on Friday, November 13, with prerelease events running November 6–12. It's the 60th anniversary of Star Trek, and Wizards of the Coast is marking it with a full Universes Beyond set — Standard-legal, with new mechanics and a Commander lineup built around the franchise's biggest names.

The Arena schedule is live now: Premier Draft runs November 10 through February 2, Sealed runs November 10 through December 8, and Arena Direct Sealed runs November 13–22, where seven wins earns a Collector Booster box. There's also a Draft Night four-player Pick-Two kit for the tabletop side.

What's in it

Four Commander decks anchor the set: Federation Fleet, led by Jean-Luc Picard; Landing Party, led by Spock; Klingon Fury, led by Worf; and We Are the Borg, led by the Borg Queen. New mechanics include Assimilate, Face a Dilemma and Federation, and Scene cards recreate iconic moments across the franchise.

The chase cards are the Headliners: signed cards from seven Star Trek actors, roughly 250 copies of each, available only in Collector Boosters. One note for the tabletop release — Commander decks are delayed in North America and may arrive after November 13, so check with your local shop before Friday.
