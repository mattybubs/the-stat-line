TSL Bets parlay: Cook OVER 91.5 rushing + Kittle OVER 61.5 receiving (~+255)

By Matt

Two props, two stars, two bad defenses. James Cook runs at a Jets front giving up 128 yards a game on the ground, and George Kittle goes at a banged-up Cowboys secondary while chasing Terrell Owens on the 49ers' all-time list. Cook OVER 91.5 + Kittle OVER 61.5 pays about +255.

The bet

James Cook OVER 91.5 rushing yards (-113) + George Kittle OVER 61.5 receiving yards (-113) — two-leg parlay, about +255

Leg 1 — James Cook OVER 91.5 rushing yards

### The game slip

Buffalo Bills (7-1) at New York Jets (2-7)

Sunday, 1:00 PM ET — MetLife Stadium — CBS

• Spread: Bills -7.5

• Odds source: OddsLive

Injury report — Bills: OT Spencer Brown DOUBTFUL (ankle), CB Christian Benford questionable (wrist).

Injury report — Jets: LB Trevin Wallace OUT (groin), WR Garrett Wilson questionable (concussion) but expected back, WR Adonai Mitchell questionable (knee).

Storylines: The Bills have the No. 1 scoring offense and the No. 2 rushing offense (146.6 yards per game). The Jets are 2-7 and give up 128.0 rushing yards per game, 26th in the league — three rushers have already cleared 100 against them.

### Why Cook

Cook is having a career year: 814 yards on 169 carries, 101.8 per game, 8 rushing touchdowns, and a seven-game touchdown streak. Last week he carried 31 times for 107 yards against Minnesota. And the last time he faced the Jets, he ran for 132 yards and two touchdowns.

The matchup is the bet: a top-two rushing offense against a bottom-six run defense, with a back who's averaging over 100 a game.

### The honest caveat

Blowout risk. The Bills are 7.5-point favorites, and in last season's 35-6 blowout of the Jets, Cook got just two carries. If Buffalo is up three scores at halftime, the starters sit. The Jets also lead the league in forced fumbles (13), so ball security matters.

Leg 2 — George Kittle OVER 61.5 receiving yards

### The game slip

San Francisco 49ers (7-1) at Dallas Cowboys (4-5)

Sunday, 4:25 PM ET — AT&T Stadium — FOX

• Spread: 49ers -4.5

• Total: 55.5

• Moneyline: 49ers -218 / Cowboys +180

• Odds source: OddsLive

Injury report — 49ers: WR Brandon Aiyuk OUT, WR Chris Evans OUT, WR Ricky Pearsall on IR, OT Trent Williams questionable (shoulder), all three starting cornerbacks questionable. Deebo Samuel RETURNS.

Injury report — Cowboys: TE Jake Ferguson OUT, WR Jonathan Mingo OUT, DE Donovan Ezeiruaku OUT (ankle), CBs DaRon Bland and Shavon Revel Jr. questionable.

Storylines: The 49ers are 7-1 and the highest-scoring team in the league, and they're short at receiver — which means the middle of the field is Kittle's. And there's a milestone in play: Kittle (8,491 career receiving yards) needs 82 to pass Terrell Owens on the 49ers' all-time list.

### Why Kittle

Kittle has 38 catches for 483 yards and 5 touchdowns (60.4 per game), and he's gone over 61.5 in five of eight games. His last three weeks: 88 at Atlanta, 58 against Las Vegas, 69 against Washington — a 71.7-yard average, targeted 8.3 times a game over that stretch. The projection models like him too: OPTIMAL projects 69.0 yards.

With Aiyuk out, Evans out, and Pearsall on IR, the 49ers' passing game runs through Kittle and the returning Deebo. The Cowboys are down a tight end and two corners. The line sits right at his season average — and he's been above it in three straight weeks.

### The honest caveat

Deebo's return could eat targets, and 61.5 is Kittle's season average, not a discount. This leg is the steadier of the two, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 18-31 in November, 22-52 all-time. Two-leggers have paid about +250 when they hit — this one pays about +255, right in the zone.
