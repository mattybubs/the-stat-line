Kith and Salomon Debut the XT-4K Mid in Their Winter 2026 Capsule

By Matt

The XT-4K Mid turns Salomon's low-top XT-4K into a mid-cut silhouette, headlining the Kith for Salomon Winter 2026 collection launching globally today.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The Kith for Salomon Winter 2026 collection launches globally today, Monday, October 26, at 11 AM regional time, available via Kith shops, Kith.com, and the Kith App. Headlining the drop is a brand-new silhouette: the XT-4K Mid.

The XT-4K Mid

The XT-4K Mid takes the low-top XT-4K and rebuilds it as a mid-cut silhouette. The upper pairs mesh with synthetic overlays, giving the shoe a technical, trail-ready look with a little more coverage than the original.

Silhouette: XT-4K Mid

Base model: Low-top XT-4K, rebuilt as a mid-cut

Upper: Mesh and synthetic overlays

Colorways: Black, Green Gaze, and Olive Night

The colorways

The debut colorways keep things grounded: Black, Green Gaze, and Olive Night. It's a restrained, outdoorsy palette that fits the Winter 2026 season — no loud color blocking, just a clean mix that works with the mid-cut shape.

Where to get it

The collection launches globally today at 11 AM regional time through Kith shops, Kith.com, and the Kith App. The XT-4K Mid is the headline of the capsule, so if you're after the new silhouette specifically, that's the one to watch at launch.
