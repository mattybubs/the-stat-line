TSL Bets: The Thanksgiving Eve parlay (~+382) — Williams anytime TD, Adebayo OVER 1.5 steals

By Matt

Kyren Williams is the Rams' goal-line back with both top receivers out, and Bam Adebayo is averaging 2.1 steals a game and just posted a triple-double. Two legs, about +382.

The games

Miami Heat (10-6) at Philadelphia 76ers (14-4) — 5:00 PM ET, Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia, PA (ESPN, NBA Cup)

76ers -1.5, total 238.5, moneyline 76ers -115 / Heat -105 (USA Today Sportsbook Wire).

Injuries — Miami: F Giannis Antetokounmpo QUESTIONABLE (right groin tendinitis — sat out Sunday, told reporters "I'm good").

Green Bay Packers (6-4) at Los Angeles Rams (6-4) — 8:00 PM ET, SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, CA (Netflix)

Rams -2.5, total 44.5, moneyline Rams -137 / Packers +120 (DraftKings via NBC Sports).

Injuries — Green Bay: RB Josh Jacobs QUESTIONABLE (finger — unlikely to play). EDGE Micah Parsons QUESTIONABLE (knee — activated off PUP, expected to make his season debut). LB Zaire Franklin QUESTIONABLE (shin). G Kevin Zeitler QUESTIONABLE (triceps). C Sean Rhyan QUESTIONABLE (ankle).

Injuries — Los Angeles: WR Puka Nacua OUT (IR, knee — season over). WR Davante Adams OUT (IR, hamstring). G Steve Avila OUT (hamstring). C Coleman Shelton OUT (hamstring). TE Terrance Ferguson OUT (hamstring/ankle). RB Kyren Williams PROBABLE (toe).

Storylines: With Nacua and Adams both on injured reserve, the Rams' passing game has struggled and the ground game has become the focal point — and the goal line runs through Williams. He leads the Rams with seven touchdowns this season and has scored in 6 of his last 10, with 14 red-zone targets and 84 red-zone carries. On the other side, Bam Adebayo just posted a triple-double against Oklahoma City on Sunday (24 points, 15 rebounds, 12 assists) and averages 2.1 steals a game. With Giannis questionable and Embiid doubtful, Bam shoulders more of everything for Miami — scoring, playmaking, and defense — against a Sixers team that would be playing smaller and faster without its center.

Leg 1 — Kyren Williams anytime touchdown

Williams is the Rams' goal-line back in a game where Los Angeles has no choice but to run it near the end zone: both starting receivers are out, the offensive line is banged up, and the passing game has thrown an interception in four straight games. He has 7 touchdowns this season, has scored in 6 of his last 10, and carries the ball 14.2 times a game with top-10% red-zone usage. His toe injury is listed probable after a full Monday practice. Line: anytime touchdown (+115, FanDuel). Odds Index's model gives him a 49.1% chance to score; +115 implies 46.5%.

Leg 2 — Bam Adebayo OVER 1.5 steals

Adebayo averages 2.1 steals a game and has cleared 1.5 in 12 of 16 games this season and 7 of his last 10. He is coming off a triple-double and plays 32-plus minutes a night as Miami's defensive anchor, jumping passing lanes and cleaning up in help. With Giannis questionable, his defensive load only grows, and if Embiid sits, Philadelphia plays smaller and faster — more perimeter touches for Bam to disrupt. Line: OVER 1.5 steals (+124, Odds Index). The +124 price implies 44.6%, well below his hit rate.

The bet

Kyren Williams anytime touchdown (+115, FanDuel) + Bam Adebayo OVER 1.5 steals (+124, Odds Index) — about +382. A $10 ticket returns about $48.16.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Williams is a plus-money goal-line back in a game script built for him, and Bam is a 2.1-steals-a-game defender at a line he clears in 7 of his last 10. Neither leg depends on the other — different players, different sports, different games. That is the juice rule working as intended: two plus-money legs, about +382 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

Williams shares goal-line work with Blake Corum, who has scored in six straight games — the Rams have two backs who can vulture the touchdown. Micah Parsons is expected to make his season debut for Green Bay, and his biggest impact in this game may be as a run defender. Bam's steals are a high-variance market, and OPTIMAL projects him for 1.4 steals tonight — under the line — even though he has hit in 7 of his last 10. If Giannis sits, Miami's game script changes, and if Embiid plays, Philadelphia slows down and posts him up instead of living on the perimeter. And a parlay needs both legs — either one can sink it alone. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.
