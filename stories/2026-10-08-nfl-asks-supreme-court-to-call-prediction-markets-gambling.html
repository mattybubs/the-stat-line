NFL asks Supreme Court to call prediction markets gambling

By Matt

The NFL filed an amicus brief backing New Jersey and 39 state AGs, asking the Supreme Court to let states regulate prediction markets like Kalshi as gambling.

The filing

The NFL filed an amicus brief Thursday backing New Jersey and the attorneys general of 39 states, asking the Supreme Court to let states regulate prediction markets like Kalshi as gambling.

The league's case is scale

The number the NFL is pointing to: $1.8 billion in prediction-market trading on football on opening Sunday alone. That's the scale argument — this isn't a niche product anymore, and the league wants it under state gambling regulation.

Why it matters

If the Court agrees, prediction markets face state-by-state gambling rules instead of operating in their current gray area. If not, the $1.8-billion Sundays keep growing untouched. Either way, it's the biggest legal fight in sports betting since PASPA fell.
