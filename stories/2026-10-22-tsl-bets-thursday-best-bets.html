TSL Bets: Thursday's Best Bets — Bruins, Red Wings and a 3-Leg Parlay

By Matt

Thursday's slate is loaded: two NBA games on ESPN and eight on the ice. Three best bets we're actually confident in, plus a 3-leg parlay that doesn't need a miracle.

Thursday gives us a full evening: two NBA games on ESPN (Cavs at Sixers, Nuggets at Thunder) and eight NHL games. No ten-leg lottery tickets here — three best bets with real reasoning behind them, plus one parlay.

Best Bet 1: Mammoth/Kraken Over 6.5 (-110)

Seattle games are flying over. Eight of the Kraken's ten games have gone over 6.5 this season, and five of Utah's ten have too. Combined, these teams are averaging 7.5 goals per game — a full goal clear of the total. The Mammoth score (3.8 per game, 5th in the NHL) and the Kraken bleed (50% as underdogs, wide-open games). Over it is.

Best Bet 2: Red Wings moneyline (-155) at Canucks

Vancouver is 2-8-1. That's not a slump, that's one of the worst starts in the league, and they're at home getting faded for a reason. Detroit (-155) is simply the better team against a Canucks squad that can't keep the puck out. Road favorite, fair price.

Best Bet 3: 76ers -2.5 vs Cavaliers (-115)

Philly just walked into Madison Square Garden and spoiled the Knicks' ring night 106-100, covering as 4.5-point dogs. LeBron James (14-9-7 in his Sixers debut) and Jaylen Brown (19-8-6) are already bending games. Cleveland opens its season on the road, and while the Cavs swept the season series last year, this isn't last year's Sixers. Lay the 2.5 at home.

The Parlay (+320ish)

Three legs, nothing crazy:

• Bruins ML (-150) vs Predators — Boston is 7-2-1 at the Garden against a 3-2-3 Nashville team sitting 24th in goals.

• Mammoth/Kraken Over 6.5 (-110) — see above.

• Thunder ML (-300) vs Nuggets — SGA's 141-game 20-point streak rolls on at home against a Denver team opening its season on the road.

Bottom line: no injury flags on any of these games as of Thursday morning. Lines move — grab the numbers you like early.
