TSL Bets: The Thursday parlay (~+550) — Kaprizov to score, Wild moneyline, Avalanche-Senators Over 6.5

By Matt

Three legs, three different bets: Kirill Kaprizov to score in Toronto, the Wild to beat a reeling Leafs team, and goals in Ottawa where the over is 9-1 in the last ten meetings. About +550.

Leg 1: Kirill Kaprizov anytime goal (+105)

Minnesota Wild (13-3-4) at Toronto Maple Leafs (8-10-2) — 7:00 PM ET, Scotiabank Arena, Toronto

Wild -170, total 6.5.

Injuries — Minnesota: Filip Gustavsson is on injured reserve with a hip injury. Brock Faber is out with a skull fracture — he has resumed skating but is not expected back until Saturday. Jared Spurgeon is out with a lower-body injury. Michael McCarron is questionable with an upper-body injury.

Injuries — Toronto: Morgan Rielly could return after missing two games with a lower-body injury. Kirill Marchenko and Colton Sissons remain sidelined with lower-body injuries. Max Domi, Miles Wood, Nick Blankenburg, Elvis Merzlikins, Cade Webber, Marshall Rifai and William Villeneuve are all out.

Storylines: Kaprizov has 17 goals — second in the NHL — and 32 points in 20 games. He has scored in five of the Wild's last six road games, and he scored the overtime winner 21 seconds in at Montreal on Tuesday, extending Minnesota's point streak to nine games. The Leafs have lost four straight, allowed 17 goals four games into a six-game homestand, and are still looking for their first win of November. Backup Anthony Stolarz could get the nod in net, with the starter having lost five straight. Kaprizov against a battered Toronto defense is the best goalscorer bet on the board tonight.

Leg 2: Wild moneyline (-170)

Same game, same reasoning, different bet. Minnesota is 13-3-4, riding a nine-game point streak, with the league's second-leading goalscorer and a defenseman in Quinn Hughes who just became the second-fastest blueliner in league history to 80 points with a franchise — 68 games. Toronto is 8-10-2, has lost four straight, and is missing half its defense corps. The Wild are road favorites for a reason, and the -170 is the price of betting the best form in the league against the worst.

Leg 3: Avalanche at Senators Over 6.5 (+100)

Colorado Avalanche (11-6-2) at Ottawa Senators (11-6-2) — 7:00 PM ET, Canadian Tire Centre, Ottawa

Avalanche -120, total 6.5, Over +100 (Fanatics).

Injuries — Colorado: Josh Manson is on injured reserve (long-term) and Zachary L'Heureux is on injured reserve. Nathan MacKinnon is healthy and leads the team with 30 points.

Injuries — Ottawa: Andre Burakovsky, Warren Foegele and William Eklund are on injured reserve. Nick Cousins is out. Linus Ullmark is day-to-day with a lower-body strain — he is 7-1-0 with a 1.33 goals-against average and a .945 save percentage when he plays, which is exactly why this leg carries a warning label.

Storylines: The over is 13-6 in Colorado games this season, and 9-1 in the last ten meetings between these two teams. The Avalanche average 4.11 goals a game, just won 6-3 in Toronto for their second straight win, and have scored 10 goals in two games. Ottawa lost 3-2 in overtime to Washington on Tuesday and is banged up up front. If Ullmark plays, the over gets harder — he is the best goalie in this matchup by a wide margin. If he sits, two offenses that score for fun get a backup. Either way, seven goals is the number, and these two teams have cleared it in nine of their last ten meetings.

The bet

Parlay: Kaprizov anytime goal (+105) + Wild moneyline (-170) + Avalanche-Senators Over 6.5 (+100) — about +550.

A prop, a favorite, and a total — three different kinds of bets, and none of them copies a single from today. The Kaprizov goal and the Wild moneyline are correlated, which is the point: if Minnesota wins in Toronto, the odds are good that Kaprizov is the reason. The over is the independent leg, and it is built on the strongest totals trend on the board tonight.

The honest caveat

Two of the three legs live in the same game, so a bad night in Toronto kills the ticket twice over. Kaprizov can be held off the scoresheet — it happens to everyone — and the Wild are missing their starting goalie and their best defenseman. The over is the riskiest leg on its own terms: if Ullmark plays, a 1.33 goals-against average is a real obstacle to seven goals. A parlay is a parlay: three things have to go right, and one of them is a goalie decision nobody has confirmed yet. Bet accordingly.

The pick

Kaprizov anytime goal + Wild moneyline + Avalanche-Senators Over 6.5 — about +550. A $10 ticket returns about $65. A scorer, a favorite, and the best over trend on the board.
