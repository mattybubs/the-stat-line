Call of Duty just extended its launch-month sales streak to 19 straight years — and it did it in a down market

By Matt

Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game in the US in October, extending the franchise's run of topping the chart in its launch month to a 19th consecutive year — a streak that began with World at War in 2008.

Some streaks survive because the competition is weak. This one survived the opposite. Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game in the United States in October 2026, according to Circana's monthly sales report — extending the franchise's run of topping the US chart in its launch month to a 19th consecutive year.

The number comes from Circana senior director Mat Piscatella, who publishes the monthly US market report. GameSpot reported the result on Tuesday, and Nintendo Life, VGChartz and GamesIndustry.biz all carried the same Circana figures. It is not a projection from a single outlet. It is the industry's standard sales tracker, and it says Call of Duty did it again.

The streak, and where it started

The run began in November 2008, when Call of Duty: World at War outsold Guitar Hero: World Tour and Gears of War 2 to become the best-selling game of its month. Every Call of Duty release since — across four console generations, three publishers' eras of the franchise, and a calendar that moved launch month from November to October this year — has topped the US chart in the month it launched. Modern Warfare 4, released October 23 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2, is the latest.

October's top three tells the rest of the month's story. Gears of War: E-Day finished second and Star Wars: Galactic Racer third, with EA Sports FC 27 fourth — and six of the month's ten best sellers were new releases. Call of Duty beat a crowded field, not an empty one.

A win in a shrinking month

What makes this one different from the other 18 is the market it landed in. Total US spending on video games fell 12% year over year in October, to $4.2 billion. Content sales were down 14%, mobile down 16%, and console content down 18%, per Circana — the comparison dragged down by last October, when Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A launched in the same window. Hardware was the one bright spot, up 2% to $357 million, with the Switch 2 still leading the market in both units and dollars.

So the streak did not survive on a rising tide. It survived a month where almost everything else shrank. That is the kind of detail that separates a habit from a record.

The catch, and what's next

There is one wrinkle worth stating plainly. Despite winning October, Modern Warfare 4 did not crack Circana's year-to-date top 10 for 2026 — the first time that has happened to a Modern Warfare title since the original's era. October was a strong month, but a single month does not make a year.

And the November chart will not belong to Call of Duty. GameSpot's own read on the month ahead is blunt: GTA 6, which launched November 19, is all but certain to take November and finish 2026 as the year's best-selling game overall. The streak is about launch months, not calendar years — and November was never Call of Duty's month to win this time.

But the record stands on its own terms. Nineteen years, nineteen launch months, nineteen times at No. 1. No other franchise in the Circana era has done anything like it.
