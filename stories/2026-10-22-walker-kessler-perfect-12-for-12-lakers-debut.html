Walker Kessler Goes a Perfect 12-for-12 in Historic Lakers Debut

By Matt

26 points, 10 dunks, zero misses. Walker Kessler joined Wilt Chamberlain in Lakers history in the 127-105 opening-night rout of the Warriors — and Shaq christened him 'Milk Chamberlain.'

The Lakers' new era started with a perfect night.

Walker Kessler scored 26 points on a perfect 12-for-12 from the field — 10 of them dunks, nearly all alley-oops — with nine rebounds and two blocks in his Lakers debut Wednesday, a 127-105 blowout of the Golden State Warriors at Crypto.com Arena. The crowd gave him a standing ovation when he subbed out with six minutes left.

Lakers history

Kessler became just the second player in Lakers franchise history to make at least 12 field goals without missing, joining Wilt Chamberlain (14-for-14 in 1969). His 10 dunks were the most by a Laker since Shaquille O'Neal in 2003.

Shaq, on Inside the NBA's postgame coverage, gave Kessler a new nickname — "Milk Chamberlain" — and a bigger compliment: "Walker Kessler is the best big man in the NBA."

The new Big Three

This was the first game of the post-LeBron era in Los Angeles — James left as a free agent for Philadelphia — and the Lakers' new core looked seamless. Luka Dončić (28 points, 8 rebounds, 7 assists) closed the first half with a bank shot from near halfcourt and fed Kessler a behind-the-back dime in the third. Austin Reaves added 23.

The Lakers outscored Golden State 36-16 in the third quarter to blow it open, leading by as many as 35. Stephen Curry scored 23 but managed just three in the third and sat the entire fourth.

The price tag on Kessler — two unprotected first-round picks (2031, 2033) plus pick swaps in 2028 and 2030 — looked worth it by the second quarter.
