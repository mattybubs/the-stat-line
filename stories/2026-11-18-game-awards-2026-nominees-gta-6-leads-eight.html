GTA 6 leads The Game Awards 2026 with eight nominations — including Game of the Year — two days before it even launches

By Matt

Grand Theft Auto VI picked up eight nominations at The Game Awards 2026, the most of any game, with Game of the Year among them — revealed on Tuesday, two days before the game launches on November 19. Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem follow with six each.

The nominees are out

The Game Awards revealed its full 2026 nominee list on Tuesday, November 17, in a livestream hosted by creator Geoff Keighley — and the game at the top of the list is not out yet.

Grand Theft Auto VI leads the field with eight nominations, including Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, Best Performance for Manni L. Perez as Lucia, and Best Action/Adventure Game. Control Resonant and Resident Evil Requiem follow with six nominations each, and Phantom Blade Zero has five.

The Game of the Year field is six games deep this year instead of the usual five, after a tie in the voting: 007 First Light, Control Resonant, Grand Theft Auto VI, Phantom Blade Zero, Resident Evil Requiem, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

The timing

GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19 — two days after the nominations were announced, and one day before The Game Awards' eligibility cutoff of 9 p.m. PT on Friday, November 20. That is the whole reason it qualifies at all: the game had to be commercially available by the cutoff, and it clears the line with a single day to spare.

It is a strange position for a frontrunner. Nobody outside Rockstar's review program has played the game yet, and the winners will not be announced until the ceremony on December 10 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles — three weeks after launch, when millions of players will have had their say in the other direction. Fan voting is open now and closes December 9 at 6 p.m. PT, with the winners decided by a global jury of more than 160 media publications and creator outlets combined with the fan vote.

The context

This is not GTA 6's first time at the show. It won Most Anticipated Game in both 2024 and 2025, back when the wait was the story. Now the wait is over and the game is the story — and it is competing in the categories that actually judge a game, not just the hype around one.

The other contenders are not pushovers. Control Resonant is up for Game of the Year, Best Game Direction and Best Narrative. Resident Evil Requiem matches it at six nominations. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Nintendo's remake, which sold 5.2 million copies in four days — rounds out a Game of the Year field with no obvious weak link.

What it means

Eight nominations for a game nobody has played is either a coronation or a dare. By December 10, the jury will have had three weeks with the actual game — and so will everyone else. If GTA 6 is as good as the nominations assume, the ceremony is a formality. If it is not, this list will look very different in hindsight.
