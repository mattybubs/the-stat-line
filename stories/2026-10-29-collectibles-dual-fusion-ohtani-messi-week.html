Welcome to Collectibles: Pokémon's Dual Fusion Is Live and Ohtani's $3.24M Card Caps a Monster Auction Week

By Matt

The Stat Line's new Collectibles tab is live — trading cards, action figures, and everything popular in the hobby, mostly Pokémon and sports. This week: Pokémon TCG Pocket's Dual Fusion drops with the game's first dual-type cards, the 30th Celebration battle decks arrive tomorrow, and Ohtani's $3.24M Logoman leads a record auction week.

Welcome to Collectibles — the newest tab on The Stat Line. Trading cards, action figures, and everything popular in the hobby, with Pokémon and sports leading the way. Same verification standard as everything else here: real prices, real sales, no rumor-mill numbers. And what a week to launch on.

Pokémon: Dual Fusion is live today

Pokémon TCG Pocket's Dual Fusion expansion is out today, and it brings something the app has never had before: dual-type cards. One card, two types at once. The headliners are Mega Mewtwo X ex (Psychic and Fighting) and Mega Greninja ex (Water and Darkness), split across two booster packs — one for Mega Mewtwo X, one for Pikachu — with new versions of Pikachu ex, Mew ex, Venusaur, Charizard, Blastoise, and Mega Dragonite ex in the mix.

The launch comes with a lot around it: six Dual Fusion rental decks you can play free through December 15, a Special Pack called First Partners running October 31 to December 26 featuring the first partner Pokémon from all nine regions, and second anniversary events running into mid-December. The bigger structural news is the schedule change that comes with the new C Series: sets move from roughly a four-week cycle to roughly six weeks, because players said big sets were making collecting hard. Collectors, in other words, asked for breathing room — and got it.

Pokémon: the 30th Celebration decks land tomorrow

If physical cards are more your thing, tomorrow (October 30) brings the Pokémon TCG: 30th Celebration Battle Decks — Espeon ex and Umbreon ex. Each is a ready-to-play, all-foil 60-card deck for $39.99, with a playmat, coin, and deck box in the box. The Espeon ex deck runs with Victini; the Umbreon ex deck runs with Zeraora. No chasing packs to play — open it and you're in a game. For a 30th anniversary year that has already been stacked with releases, a pair of decks built for actually playing (instead of just hunting) is a nice change of pace.

Sports cards: Ohtani's $3.24M Logoman leads the week

Over in sports cards, it's been a monster auction week. The headline: Shohei Ohtani's 2025 Topps MLB MVP The Game Dynasty Black Auto Patch 1/1, graded PSA 9, sold for $3.24 million through Fanatics Premier on October 23 — the most expensive Los Angeles Dodgers card ever sold at auction, and the third-biggest Ohtani sale of all time. Right behind it on the same week's board: a 2025 Topps Motif Logoman Auto 1/1 Cooper Flagg (unscratched redemption) at $2.16 million, and a 2026 Topps Chrome Dual MVP Gold Logoman Auto 1/1 of Aaron Judge and Ohtani at $1.23 million.

And the record that isn't an auction at all: Lionel Messi's Topps NOW "Final Farewell" card is now the best-selling Topps NOW card of all time at 664,325 copies sold, passing Shohei Ohtani's 50/50 card (653,737). Retirements and historic moments have become instant seven-figure revenue events in this hobby — and the Messi farewell is the latest proof.

Vintage had its moment too: a nearly complete 1962 Topps football set — 175 of the 176 cards, the top set on PSA's All-Time Finest registry at a 9.337 grade point average — sold for $1,473,638 in Mile High Card Company's Fall Auction, the highest price ever paid for a football card set.
