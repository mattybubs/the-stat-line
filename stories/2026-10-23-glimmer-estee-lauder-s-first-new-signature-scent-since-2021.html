Glimmer: Estée Lauder's First New Signature Scent Since 2021 Is a Gourmand

By Matt

Estée Lauder goes gourmand for the first time ever with Glimmer — an amber floral built around a Macaron Rose Accord of rose, vanilla, and raspberry mousse, fronted by Hailee Steinfeld.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Darius Halavi's review of Glimmer landed today: the house's first-ever gourmand, composed by Dominique Ropion, Anne Flipo, and Jean-Marc Chaillan. The read: an amber floral built around a Macaron Rose Accord of rose, vanilla, and raspberry mousse.

This is Estée Lauder's first new signature scent pillar since 2021 — and its biggest fragrance launch in more than a decade, backed by a global multimedia campaign.

The composition

Official note breakdown from Estée Lauder:

Top: Raspberry Mousse Accord, Orange Flower, Mandarin Oil

Heart: Macaron Rose Accord, Rose, Vanilla Bean, Cashmeran

Base: Shimmering Patchouli, Amber Embrace Accord, Sandalwood, Cedarwood, Tonka Bean

It's a polished, contemporary take on gourmand — creamy indulgence balanced with luminous florals rather than straight sugar.

The campaign

Academy Award-nominated actress and singer Hailee Steinfeld fronts the global campaign as the face of Glimmer. The one-minute campaign film, directed by Nathan Copan, features Steinfeld in dreamy vignettes speaking of "moments so small, you almost miss them" — and she recorded a cover of "Feeling Good" for the soundtrack. (Campaign US)

Glimmer Eau de Parfum retails for $130/50ml and $160/100ml, with a full Glimmer collection (perfumed body lotion, shower gel) available for layering.
