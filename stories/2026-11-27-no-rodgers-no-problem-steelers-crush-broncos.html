No Rodgers, no problem: Steelers crush Broncos

By The Stat Line

T.J. Watt and Jalen Ramsey each scored a defensive touchdown as Pittsburgh routed Denver 27-5 on Black Friday — the Steelers' first game since releasing Aaron Rodgers.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The Pittsburgh Steelers capped the wildest week of their season with a 27-5 rout of the Denver Broncos on Black Friday at Acrisure Stadium, winning their first game since releasing Aaron Rodgers — and they barely needed their offense to do it.

Mason Rudolph started in Rodgers' place and played safe, mistake-free football. The defense did the rest, scoring twice off Bo Nix turnovers while holding Denver out of the end zone all afternoon in the rain.

The defense scored twice

The tone was set on Denver's second possession. Nix dropped back under heavy pressure from T.J. Watt and Nick Herbig, the ball slipped out of his hand as he was sacked in the end zone, and Watt fell on it for the first touchdown of the game at 6:03 of the first quarter.

Rudolph then directed a quick 85-yard drive — rookie Germie Bernard turning a crossing route into a 58-yard catch-and-run — and lofted a back-shoulder fade to DK Metcalf for a 22-yard touchdown with 1:40 left in the first. It was Pittsburgh's first 14-0 first-quarter lead since 2019.

Denver's only points came on a safety and a field goal. Alex Singleton blew up a run and dropped Jaylen Warren in the end zone at 11:14 of the second quarter, and Wil Lutz's 44-yarder cut the lead to 14-5. Chris Boswell answered with a 47-yard field goal, and 18 seconds later Ramsey stepped in front of a Nix pass and weaved 36 yards down the left sideline for a 24-5 halftime lead. Boswell's 33-yarder in the fourth closed the scoring.

Rudolph did what was asked

Rudolph completed 9 of 15 passes for 142 yards and a touchdown with no interceptions, good for a 113.8 passer rating. Pittsburgh took a conservative approach early, running on eight of its first 11 plays, and never asked its backup quarterback to win the game on his own.

Metcalf finished with three catches for 36 yards and the score, his leaping grab at the goal line standing as the game's only offensive touchdown. Rico Dowdle added 71 yards on 11 carries.

Nix had his worst day as a pro

Nix completed 22 of 40 passes for 169 yards with no touchdowns and two interceptions — Ramsey's pick-six and a second one by Daylen Everette — and was sacked three times. Denver managed just 205 total yards and never reached the end zone.

The loss ends the Broncos' six-game winning streak and drops them to 8-3, raising questions about how far they can go with Nix at quarterback after a game in which the offense never found any rhythm.

What it means

Pittsburgh improves to 7-4 and snaps a two-game skid, with the losses to Cincinnati and Philadelphia that set the stage for Rodgers' exit now firmly behind them. The Steelers played with an energy that had been missing in those two defeats, and they did it less than 48 hours after the franchise removed the signs and pictures of No. 8 around Acrisure Stadium.

It was the first game of a difficult stretch that includes meetings with Houston and Jacksonville. If the defense plays like this, the post-Rodgers Steelers look like a playoff team.
