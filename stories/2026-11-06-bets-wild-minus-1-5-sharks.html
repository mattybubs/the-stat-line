Wild -1.5 (+102): Kaprizov past 500 and rolling against the Sharks

By Matt

Minnesota is 11-3-3 and first in the Central, Kaprizov is rolling after reaching 500 career points on November 1, and the Sharks come to St. Paul at 7-9-1. Laying 1.5 at plus money is the value side.

Game slip

San Jose Sharks (7-9-1) at Minnesota Wild (11-3-3)

Friday, November 6, 2026 — 8:00 PM ET — Grand Casino Arena, Saint Paul, MN — NHL Network

Spread: Wild -1.5 (+102)

Total: 6.5

Moneyline: Wild -258 / Sharks +210

Goalies: Jesper Wallstedt (MIN) vs. Yaroslav Askarov (SJ)

Odds source: DraftKings numbers via BetLegend, updated Nov. 6, 12:32 AM PT

### Injury report

Minnesota: G Filip Gustavsson (hip) — injured reserve, out until November 19. D Brock Faber (skull fracture) — injured reserve, expected back November 10. D Jared Spurgeon (lower body) — injured reserve, expected back November 10. F Marcus Foligno (lower body) — injured reserve, expected back November 12. C Joel Eriksson Ek missed Thursday's practice but it was a maintenance day — he will play Friday.

San Jose: C Alexander Wennberg (upper body) — injured reserve, expected back November 7. LW Igor Chernyshov (upper body) — injured reserve, expected back November 14. LW Quentin Musty (upper body) — injured reserve, expected back December 1.

### Storylines

• Kirill Kaprizov reached 500 career points on November 1 — an empty-netter against Washington, his 15th goal of the season. He is the second player in Wild history to reach the mark, after Mikko Koivu, and did it in 413 games, tied for fourth-fastest among active players. His 14 goals in October led the NHL and earned him Second Star of the Month.

• Minnesota is 11-3-3, first in the Central Division, and just beat Philadelphia 4-2 on Tuesday behind Jesper Wallstedt's 25 saves. The top line — Kaprizov, Eriksson Ek, and Matt Boldy — is intact.

• San Jose is 7-9-1 and starting Yaroslav Askarov in net. The Sharks have been game, but they are a bottom-half team walking into one of the league's best home environments against a team chasing a milestone for its star.

• The Wild's blue line is banged up — Faber and Spurgeon both out — which is the one crack in the armor. Minnesota has managed it so far: 11 wins in 17 games with a plus goal differential that sits among the league's best.

The bet

Minnesota Wild -1.5 (+102, DraftKings)

The moneyline at -258 is unplayable. The puck line at plus money is the way in. Minnesota wins games by two or more at home as a matter of routine — this is a team that beat Washington 5-1 on the road last weekend and has won 11 of 17 — and San Jose's defense is not built to keep this close for sixty minutes. Kaprizov coming off the 500-point milestone in front of the home crowd is the kind of thing that turns a two-goal lead into a three-goal lead.

Why

The gap between these teams is real. Minnesota is first in the Central; San Jose is 7-9-1 and playing its second game in three nights on the road. The Wild's top line is healthy and producing, Wallstedt has been steady filling in for Gustavsson, and the Sharks' injury list hits their middle six. Laying 1.5 at plus money on the better team, at home, with their star rolling off a milestone — that is the value side of this board.

The honest caveat

The Wild's defense is genuinely thin right now. Faber (skull fracture) and Spurgeon are both out, and San Jose has enough skill to punish mistakes if Minnesota gets loose. A one-goal game is the nightmare scenario for a -1.5 ticket, and the Sharks have kept games close this season. If the Wild's depleted blue line has a bad night, this can end 3-2 and the ticket dies on the margin. But the price is plus money for a reason — and the reason is that Minnesota is supposed to win this game by two.

The pick

Minnesota Wild -1.5 (+102). The best team in the Central, at home, at plus money, with their star one point from history. That is the bet.
