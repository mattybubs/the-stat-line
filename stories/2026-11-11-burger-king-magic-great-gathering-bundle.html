Burger King is selling a Magic: The Gathering deck box with your Whopper — Italy only

By Matt

Burger King Italy has launched 'The Great Gathering' — a €19.90 bundle that comes with a rigid burger-styled Magic deck box and 100 sleeves. No promo cards, but nine restaurants are running Pauper tournaments with a final at Milan Games Week.

The bundle

Magic: The Gathering has done a lot of crossovers. A fast-food one was not on the list until this week.

Burger King Italy has launched "Burger King Quest: The Great Gathering," a bundle that runs from November 10 through November 30 at participating Italian locations. The price is €19.90 — about $22 — and it gets you a choice of sandwich, fries and a drink, plus the thing everyone is actually here for: a rigid Burger King x Magic deck box, styled to look like a burger, and a pack of 100 card sleeves.

The sandwich is your choice of the Chicken Krispper, the Double Cheese King, or a plant-based Whopper. The box itself is the prize — an orange, sesame-seed-dotted deck box with the Burger King logo on one side and the Magic logo on the other — and Polygon reports it is already being resold online, with scalpers flipping the box before the promotion is even halfway through.

There are no promo cards in the bundle. The accessories are the product.

The tournaments

This is not just a meal with a toy in it. Nine Burger King restaurants across Italy are hosting Pauper tournaments as part of the promotion — competitive Magic in the cheap, commons-only format — with qualifiers running through the month and a final at Milan Games Week & Cartoomics, November 27–29.

It is a real competitive structure wrapped around a fast-food promotion, which is exactly why it is working. The Pauper community gets a tournament circuit with a national final; Burger King gets a month of people coming through the door for something they cannot get at a card shop.

Why it matters

Wizards of the Coast has never done a fast-food partnership like this. The closest comparison is Pokémon's Happy Meal runs, and those move staggering numbers — the recent U.S. Pokémon Happy Meal launch had McDonald's locations selling out within days. Burger King is betting the same energy works for an older, tournament-playing audience, and the resale market says they were right to try.

The math is worth noting, too. A regular Burger King meal in Italy runs around €11.50. The bundle at €19.90 means the deck box and sleeves are effectively costing about €8.40 — less than most standalone deck boxes, and this one looks like a burger.

The catch, of course, is that it is Italy only. No U.S. dates, no wider European rollout announced. If you want one, you either know someone in Milan or you pay the reseller price — which, given how this promotion is going, is not going down.
