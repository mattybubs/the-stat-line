Senators at Sabres Over 6.5 (-118): Two Leaky Nets, Two Hot Attacks, One Obvious Total

By Matt

Ottawa's No. 1 goalie is on injured reserve, Buffalo's goalie sits at .874, both penalty kills rank in the bottom ten, and the total is 6.5. The over at -118 is the cleanest total on tonight's NHL board.

The game slip

Matchup: Ottawa Senators (9-6-1) at Buffalo Sabres (7-4-3)

Puck drop: Wednesday, November 4, 7:30 PM ET — ESPN+, KeyBank Center, Buffalo

Line: Sabres -125 (LetsBetMD) | Senators +110 (DraftKings/Fanatics) | Total 6.5, Over -118 (via FanDuel, per rg.org's morning prices)

Senators injury report: OUT — Linus Ullmark (lower body, injured reserve, targeting around November 7), Andre Burakovsky (lower body, injured reserve), Warren Foegele (knee, injured reserve), William Eklund (ankle, injured reserve). Ullmark is the one that changes this game: he has a .945 save percentage in eight starts, and he is not playing.

Sabres injury report: OUT — Matt Grzelcyk (lower body), Conor Timmins (lower body), Justin Danforth (upper body), Jack Rathbone (undisclosed), Konsta Helenius (upper body), Alex Lyon (upper body, days away from a return). DAY-TO-DAY — Jiri Kulich (concussion, expected back November 4). Zach Benson missed a practice last week on a maintenance day but returned to the ice Monday and is available for Wednesday's game. Buffalo's absences are mostly on defense, which is not what you want to hear when the total is 6.5.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. The goaltending gap is the whole bet. With Ullmark out, Ottawa turns to Samuel Ersson (.866) and Jonas Johansson (.848) — a long way from .945. Buffalo's Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen sits at .874 in nine starts. Neither net is stealing a low-scoring game tonight.

2. Neither penalty kill helps keep this under. Buffalo's kill ranks 28th at 68.6 percent; Ottawa's ranks 23rd at 72.9 percent. Power plays should produce, and both teams have the finishers to convert them — Tim Stutzle leads Ottawa with 21 points, and Tage Thompson leads Buffalo with 10 goals.

3. Buffalo has won three straight and is playing with confidence at home, while Ottawa is 9-6-1 and scores enough to keep up in a track meet. This is not a bet on either team winning — it is a bet that seven goals is a low bar for two offenses facing backup-level goaltending and bottom-ten penalty kills.

The bet

Over 6.5 goals (-118 at FanDuel). Seven goals is the ask, and the case is structural, not vibes: a .945 goalie is out, his replacements are at .866 and .848, the other goalie is at .874, and both penalty kills are in the bottom ten. The over is -118 at FanDuel, -120 at Fanatics and BetRivers, and -122 at DraftKings — shop it, but the number is the number.

The honest caveat: totals are the cruelest bets in hockey. One goalie gets hot for a night, one period goes scoreless on a handful of posts, and a 6-0 game can still stay under if the third period goes quiet. Seven goals is not a guarantee just because the goalies look shaky on paper — it is a probability play, and the probability is on the over's side tonight.
