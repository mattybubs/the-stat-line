John Legend Releases 'Muse' — 11th Studio Album, Fully Produced by Pharrell Williams

By Matt

Out today: Legend's 16-track 'Muse' via Republic Records — his first full project with Pharrell Williams, who co-wrote it and appears on six songs, with Clipse featured on 'Bodies On The Floor.'

John Legend is out with "Muse" today — his 11th studio album, and a genuine departure.

The Album

• 16 tracks, via Republic Records

• Fully produced by Pharrell Williams — his first full project with Williams, who also co-wrote it and appears on six songs including the title track

• Clipse (Pusha T and Malice) feature on "Bodies On The Floor"

The Story

Legend calls it a deliberate break from his piano-ballad formula — recorded starting January 2025 at Williams' studio inside Louis Vuitton's Paris HQ. He celebrates with a Mall of America promotional appearance the day after release.

The rare case of a superstar 11 albums in actually changing the sound. Worth a spin.
