GTA 6 pre-orders are 'far above' expectations — and the download starts today

By Matt

Take-Two says GTA 6 pre-orders are tracking 'far above' initial expectations, with third-party estimates at 7.71 million digital pre-orders already — and pre-loading opens today ahead of the November 19 launch.

What happened

Take-Two Interactive reported its second-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Monday, and the headline was the one everyone was waiting for: Grand Theft Auto 6 pre-orders are tracking "far above our initial expectations," according to CEO Strauss Zelnick, who added they are "far in excess" of what the company expected and "continuing to reach unprecedented levels."

The rest of the quarter was more mixed. Net bookings came in at $1.6 billion, below the $1.62–1.67 billion guidance range, and NBA 2K27 came in below forecast despite the second-best launch in franchise history. Take-Two maintained its full-year fiscal 2027 bookings outlook of $8.0–8.2 billion and raised its revenue guidance to $7.98–8.18 billion.

Third-party estimates put a number on the demand. Sensor Tower estimates 7.71 million digital pre-orders — roughly $751 million — as of November 3, split about 5.74 million on PlayStation and 1.97 million on Xbox. Sensor Tower's Karl Kontus expects around 25 million pre-orders by launch.

What it means

The game launches Thursday, November 19, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and pre-loading begins today, Tuesday, November 10 — the download runs up to 212 GB, so the nine-day head start is not a courtesy, it's a necessity. The launch trailer dropped October 15 with a Justice score featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard, and Miami is getting a launch event, "GTA VI: The Festival," on Saturday, November 21 at Mana Wynwood with Cardi B on the bill, tickets distributed by lottery.

The pre-order number is the story here, not the quarter. Take-Two missed its own bookings guidance and still had its CEO talking about unprecedented demand, because the entire fiscal year now runs through one date. Nine days out, the only question left is whether the launch can hold up under the weight of the numbers already on the board.
