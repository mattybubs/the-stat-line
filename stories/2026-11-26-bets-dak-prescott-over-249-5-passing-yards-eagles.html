TSL Bets: Dak Prescott OVER 249.5 passing yards vs. Eagles (-196)

By Matt

Dak Prescott has thrown for 300+ yards in three straight games and cleared 250 in 8 of 11 this season, and he faces an Eagles defense starting a quarterback who was on Dallas' practice squad six weeks ago. The play: Prescott OVER 249.5 passing yards (-196, Odds Index) on Thanksgiving afternoon.

The game slip

Philadelphia Eagles (3-7) at Dallas Cowboys (5-6)

Thursday, 4:30 PM ET — AT&T Stadium, Arlington, TX (FOX)

• Spread: Cowboys -8.5 (-110)

• Total: 47.5 (Over -120 / Under +100)

• Moneyline: Cowboys -480 / Eagles +370

• Odds source: FanDuel via USA Today Sportsbook Wire, November 25

Injury report — Eagles: QB Jalen Hurts OUT indefinitely (blood clots in his lungs, sidelined since Week 6). LB Arnold Ebiketie OUT (oblique). CB Jonathan Jones OUT (ankle).

Injury report — Cowboys: TE Jake Ferguson OUT (knee/ankle). RG Tyler Booker OUT (ankle). WR Jonathan Mingo OUT (ankle). S P.J. Locke OUT (concussion). CB Devin Moore QUESTIONABLE (hip). S Jalen Thompson QUESTIONABLE (hamstring).

Storylines: This is the best version of Dak Prescott we have seen all season, and it is not close. He has thrown for 300-plus yards in three straight games, with 11 touchdowns against one interception in that stretch, and he averages 277.2 passing yards a game. He has cleared 250 yards in 8 of his last 11 games, and the line sits at 249.5 — below his recent output and below his season average. Dallas is third in the league at 30.6 points a game and just hung 51 on Tennessee, with Prescott throwing four touchdown passes. The other side of the ball is a revenge game with a twist: Joe Milton III starts for Philadelphia against the team that had him on its practice squad six weeks ago. Milton averages 155.0 passing yards a game at 5.6 yards per attempt, and his last two starts went for 141 and 169 yards. The Eagles are 31st in the league at 16.7 points a game and 2-8 against the spread. Dallas won the first meeting 38-21 in Philadelphia on October 26, and the Cowboys have won on Thanksgiving in each of the past four seasons.

The bet

Dak Prescott OVER 249.5 passing yards (-196, Odds Index)

Why this prop

The trend and the matchup point the same way. Prescott has gone over this number in 8 of his last 11 games and in three straight by 50-plus yards, and the line is set below both his season average (277.2) and his recent form (300-plus, three straight). Philadelphia's offense cannot keep up — Milton averages 155 yards a game — so Dallas should have the ball plenty, and the Eagles' defense is 18th in scoring defense (23.7 points a game) and 21st in total defense. Odds Index rates this one HIGH. At -196 the price is real, but the edge is a quarterback playing the best football of his season against a 3-7 team starting its third quarterback.

The honest caveats

The price is doing real work — -196 means the market agrees with us, and there is no plus-money here. The first meeting is the counter-evidence: Prescott threw for only 212 yards against Philadelphia in Week 7, albeit with three touchdowns. And Dallas is an 8.5-point favorite: if the Cowboys pull away early the way they did against Tennessee, the fourth quarter belongs to the backups and the clock. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.
