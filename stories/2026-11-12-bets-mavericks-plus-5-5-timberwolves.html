TSL Bets: Mavericks +5.5 (-110) at Timberwolves — Flagg is rolling and Edwards is out

By Matt

Cooper Flagg just dropped 43 in Indiana and the Mavericks have won three straight. Minnesota is 8-3 but without Anthony Edwards, and Dallas is getting 5.5 points. Take the points.

The game

Dallas Mavericks (7-4) at Minnesota Timberwolves (8-3) — 8:00 PM ET, Target Center, Minneapolis, NBA TV

Timberwolves -5.5 (-110), moneyline Timberwolves -222, total 227.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Dallas: Dereck Lively II is out following right foot surgery, Tarik Biberovic is out with right heel bursitis, and Sergio de Larrea is out with a right knee sprain. Morez Johnson Jr. is probable with a left shin contusion, and Caleb Martin is available with a right thumb splint. Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving are not on the report.

Injuries — Minnesota: Anthony Edwards is out after right knee arthroscopy — he has missed seven games, and the Timberwolves are 5-2 without him. Donte DiVincenzo is out after right Achilles tendon repair surgery. Joan Beringer is available while wearing a face mask.

Storylines: Cooper Flagg is coming off the best game of his young career — 43 points, 10 rebounds and 6 assists at Indiana on Tuesday, making him just the second teenager in NBA history with a 40-10-5 game, after Kevin Durant. Kyrie Irving is averaging 22.9 points in nine games, and the Mavericks have won three straight. Minnesota has won four straight and sits at 8-3, with LaMelo Ball leading the team in scoring at 201 points in 11 games. The Wolves are good without Edwards — 5-2 says so — but they are not the same team without him, and they are laying only 5.5 at home against a team with the hottest teenager in the league.

The bet

Mavericks +5.5 (-110). Five and a half points for a team that has won three straight, with a rookie who just put up a line only Durant has matched as a teenager, against a favorite missing its best player. Dallas is thin up front without Lively, but Flagg and Irving are healthy and the Wolves have to prove they can cover a number like this without Edwards' scoring. Take the points.

The honest caveat

Minnesota is 5-2 without Edwards for a reason — this is a deep, well-coached team, and LaMelo Ball is playing the best basketball of his season. The Wolves are 8-3, on a four-game win streak, at home, and the Mavericks are starting a three-game road trip. Flagg is 19 and coming off a career night; the follow-up is never guaranteed. And Dallas without Lively has no answer if Minnesota decides to pound the paint. The +5.5 is a bet on the form and the matchup, not a lock — but the number is generous for a team playing this well.

The pick

Mavericks +5.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $19.09. Flagg is rolling, Edwards is out, and you're giving me five and a half points.
