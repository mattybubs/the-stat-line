Derrick Henry Under 70.5 Rushing Yards (-109): The league's best run defense meets a back on a cold streak

By Matt

Derrick Henry has topped 70 rushing yards just four times all season and has gone 53, 45, 58 in his last three games. Tonight he faces the NFL's No. 1 run defense by EPA — with a rookie third-string quarterback under center.

Game slip

Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) at Baltimore Ravens (4-4)

Thursday, November 5, 2026 — 8:15 PM ET — M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD — Prime Video

Spread: Jaguars -10 (-110)

Total: 39.5

Moneyline: Jaguars -535 / Ravens +415

Odds source: DraftKings, via Fantasy Alarm, updated Nov. 5, 5:40 AM ET; consensus via The Sport Stack, Nov. 5, 6:40 AM ET

### Injury report

Jacksonville: RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot) — questionable, game-time decision. DE Travon Walker (rib) — questionable. S Eric Murray and DT DaVon Hamilton — season-ending IR. S Antonio Johnson — cleared, available.

Baltimore: QB Lamar Jackson (thumb) — OUT, surgery, injured reserve, possibly season-ending. QB Tyler Huntley (toe) — OUT for the season, turf toe surgery. S Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) — OUT. CB Nate Wiggins (stinger/illness) — questionable. QB Joe Fagnano (ankle) — full participant, no designation, starting his first NFL game.

### Storylines

• Baltimore is down to its third quarterback. Fagnano, an undrafted rookie, completed 10 of 21 passes for 91 yards with two turnovers in relief against Buffalo on Sunday.

• Jacksonville just hung 42-7 on Indianapolis. Trevor Lawrence went 10 of 14 for 210 yards and 3 touchdowns — and only had to throw 14 times.

• The Ravens have not topped 13 points in three straight games. The Jaguars sit second in the NFL in points allowed per game.

• The line has moved a staggering 13 points from its open — Baltimore -3 to Jacksonville -10 — and the total has crashed from 48.5 to 39.5.

The bet

Derrick Henry Under 70.5 rushing yards (-109, DraftKings)

Why

Henry is the only proven weapon left in this Ravens offense, and that is exactly the problem. Jacksonville knows it too.

The numbers are stark. Henry has topped 70 rushing yards just four times all season. His last three games: 53, 45, and 58 yards, at 3.5 yards per carry. And now he runs into the worst possible matchup: Jacksonville ranks No. 1 in the NFL in EPA per rush, allowing the fewest yards per carry (3.4) and the fewest total rushing yards (542) in the league this season.

The game script works against him as well. Baltimore is a 10-point underdog starting a rookie quarterback making his first start. If the Jaguars jump ahead — and everything about this matchup says they will — the Ravens have to throw to catch up, and Henry's carries dry up in the second half. Even in a close game, Baltimore's path to yards is through a stacked box, because no defense is respecting Fagnano's arm on a short week.

The honest caveat

History is the one thing arguing against this bet. Henry has recorded 84 or more rushing yards in seven of his last eight appearances against the Jaguars, and he owns this matchup in a way few backs own any opponent. If Baltimore leans on him early and the game stays close, he can clear this number on volume alone. But the 2026 version of Henry — 3.5 yards a carry over his last three, against the league's best run defense, with a third-string quarterback — is not the back who built that history.

The pick

Derrick Henry Under 70.5 rushing yards (-109, DraftKings). A cold back, the league's best run defense, and a game script that points away from the run. That is the bet.
