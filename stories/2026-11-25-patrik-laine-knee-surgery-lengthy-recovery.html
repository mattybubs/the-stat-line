Patrik Laine just had knee surgery — and his road back to the NHL got a whole lot longer

By Matt

Patrik Laine announced Wednesday that he recently underwent knee surgery after a setback in training, and faces a lengthy recovery. The 28-year-old winger is unsigned, hasn't played an NHL game since October 2025, and gave no timeline for a return.

The announcement

Patrik Laine announced on Wednesday, November 25, that he recently underwent knee surgery and is facing a lengthy recovery.

The 28-year-old winger shared the news himself on Instagram, saying the operation followed a setback in his training. "I recently underwent knee surgery following a setback in my training," Laine wrote. "I have a lengthy recovery ahead, and my focus right now is on rehabilitation, putting in the work and taking things one day at a time."

He thanked his family, friends, and medical team for their support, and everyone who has reached out with messages of encouragement.

Laine gave no timeline for a return. He did not say which knee was operated on.

A season that never got going

The surgery is the latest blow in a stretch of hockey that has mostly been defined by absence.

Laine appeared in only five games for the Montreal Canadiens last season, recording a single assist, before core muscle surgery in October ended his campaign. He eventually returned to practice and was cleared by the team's medical staff, but he never drew back into the lineup for the remainder of the season.

It was not his first extended spell on the shelf in Montreal. He missed 24 games with a left knee sprain during the 2024-25 season, his first with the Canadiens.

At the team's locker-room cleanout on June 1, Laine sounded certain his NHL career was far from over. "I know I'll be playing in this league for a long time," he said. "That's not a question. Never something I've doubted. Just going to need an opportunity."

That opportunity never came. His four-year, $34.8 million contract — a deal carrying an $8.7 million cap hit — expired, and the Canadiens elected not to bring him back. Laine has been an unrestricted free agent ever since, and he has not played an NHL game since October 2025.

What Laine has been

This is not a fringe player trying to hang on. Laine was drafted second overall by the Winnipeg Jets in 2016, and his shot made him one of the most feared young scorers in the league — a 44-goal season at 19 years old, and a hat-trick record in a calendar month that Cutter Gauthier only just matched this November.

Across his career with the Jets, Columbus Blue Jackets, and Canadiens, Laine has 224 goals and 422 points in 537 games. Montreal acquired him before the 2024-25 season from Columbus in exchange for defenceman Jordan Harris and a draft pick.

He also represented Finland at the 4 Nations Face-Off last season, recording three assists in a fourth-place finish.

What comes next

There is no return date, and no team waiting. With the season well underway and a lengthy recovery ahead, a return to NHL ice in 2026-27 looks unlikely.

For now, the comeback Laine was sure of in June is on hold — again — and the only work in front of him is the rehab he described in his announcement: one day at a time.
