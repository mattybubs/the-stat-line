Jason Robertson's Natural Hat Trick in 5:43 Sparks Stars Past Capitals 6-5

By The Stat Line

Jason Robertson scored three goals in the opening 5:43 of the second period — his seventh career hat trick — as Dallas built a 4-0 lead and held on to beat Washington 6-5.

Jason Robertson needed less than six minutes to turn a close game into a rout — and then the Dallas Stars needed the rest of the night to survive what Washington threw back at them. Robertson's natural hat trick in the opening 5:43 of the second period powered Dallas to a 6-5 win over the Capitals on Tuesday night at Capital One Arena.

The blitz

Dallas carried a 1-0 lead into the second period, thanks to Joel Kiviranta's first-period goal — the only tally of a low-shooting opening frame. Then Robertson took over, scoring three consecutive goals against Capitals goaltender Logan Thompson and chasing him from the net.

Robertson (power play): 0:23; 2-0 Dallas; Wyatt Johnston, Roope Hintz

Robertson: 3:18; 3-0 Dallas; Arttu Hyry, Tyler Myers

Robertson: 5:43; 4-0 Dallas; Thomas Harley, Roope Hintz

Three goals in 5:43, on just four shots. It was the seventh hat trick of Robertson's career — and because no Capital scored in between, it was a natural hat trick.

The comeback

A 4-0 lead was not enough to put Washington away. The Capitals clawed back to 5-3 by the second intermission, then kept pushing in the third. Roope Hintz briefly restored breathing room, scoring 1:23 into the third — assisted by Esa Lindell and Robertson, giving Robertson his fourth point of the night — to make it 6-3.

Washington answered again. Justin Sourdif scored at 12:55 of the third to cut it to 6-4, and Ryan Leonard made it a one-goal game with 1:02 left. But Dallas held on through the final minute to escape with the 6-5 win.

• Jason Robertson: 3 goals, 1 assist, 4 points, 5 shots on goal in 17:05 of ice time — the game's top scorer.

• Roope Hintz: 1 goal, 2 assists, including assists on two of Robertson's three goals.

• Dallas scorers: Joel Kiviranta, Jason Robertson (3), Mikko Rantanen, and Roope Hintz.

• Washington's late push: goals from Justin Sourdif and Ryan Leonard in the final eight minutes of the third period made it 6-5.

Why it matters

Robertson has made a habit of these bursts — this was his seventh career hat trick — but the speed of this one stands out: three goals before the second period was six minutes old, against a goaltender in Logan Thompson who had no answer for any of them. Dallas built a four-goal cushion off one player's stick, then showed just enough resolve to protect it when Washington refused to go quietly.
