Purdy Becomes NFL's All-Time Passer Rating Leader as 49ers Set MNF Wins Record in 23-17 Win Over Commanders

By Matt

Brock Purdy crossed 1,500 career attempts on Monday Night Football and took over the NFL's all-time career passer rating lead at 105.6 — while the 49ers' 23-17 win over Washington gave them a record 56th MNF victory and a 6-0 start.

Monday night at Levi's Stadium was a two-record night. Brock Purdy officially became the NFL's all-time career passer rating leader, and the 49ers beat the Commanders 23-17 for their 56th Monday Night Football win — sole possession of the NFL record, breaking the tie with the Steelers.

Purdy owns the record books

Purdy entered Monday needing just five attempts to hit the 1,500-attempt threshold required to qualify for the official career leaderboard. He got there on San Francisco's opening drive — a six-yard completion to Christian McCaffrey — and with it took the record from Lamar Jackson.

The all-time leaderboard now reads:

• Brock Purdy — 105.6

• Lamar Jackson — 102.7

• Aaron Rodgers — 101.8

• Joe Burrow — 101.3

• Patrick Mahomes — 100.8

Purdy entered the night at 105.4, and the record was mathematically certain before kickoff: even five straight interceptions would have left him around 103.7, still clear of Jackson. Instead he went 21 of 29 for 264 yards, one touchdown, no picks — a 111.9 game rating — plus 28 rushing yards, nudging the career mark up to 105.6.

Through 55 career games: 1,038 of 1,524 (68.1%), 13,145 yards, 98 touchdowns, 39 interceptions. From the final pick of the 2022 draft to the most efficient passer in NFL history.

One more for the resume: Purdy joined Tom Brady as the only quarterbacks ever to start a season 6-0 with 15+ total touchdowns and two or fewer turnovers. Brady did it in 2015. Purdy's doing it now.

The 56th MNF win

San Francisco jumped out 20-3 at halftime and stretched it to 23-3 late in the third before Jayden Daniels rallied Washington. Daniels finished 29 of 40 for 300 yards and two touchdowns — a one-yarder to Stefon Diggs and a nine-yarder to Rachaad White — cutting it to 23-17 with nine minutes left.

The 49ers defense slammed the door twice:

• Malik Mustapha broke up a fourth-and-3 pass to Treylon Burks at the San Francisco 17 with two minutes left.

• Romello Height and Keion White sacked Daniels for an 18-yard loss on the game's final play with six seconds on the clock.

The win is San Francisco's 56th on Monday Night Football, passing Pittsburgh for the most in NFL history. It's also the franchise's first 6-0 start since 2019 and only the fifth in team history (1948, 1984, 1990, 2019).

Stat block — 49ers 23, Commanders 17:

• McCaffrey: 16 carries, 64 yards, 2 TDs; 9 catches, 53 yards

• Kittle: 5 catches, 69 yards, 11-yard TD

• Evans: 3 catches, 94 yards (left with Achilles injury)

• 49ers defense: 4 sacks of Daniels; held Washington to 53 rushing yards

San Francisco visits Atlanta on October 25. Washington heads into its bye at 1-4-1.
