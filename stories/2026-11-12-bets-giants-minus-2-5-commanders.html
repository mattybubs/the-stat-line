TSL Bets: Giants -2.5 (-110) vs Commanders — the healthier team, at home, in a rematch

By Matt

The Giants are 5-2-1, 6-2 against the spread, and getting Cam Skattebo back with J.J. McCarthy expected to play. Washington is 2-5-1, without McLaurin for the season and Tunsil on IR. Lay the 2.5 at home.

The game

Washington Commanders (2-5-1) at New York Giants (5-2-1) — 8:15 PM ET, MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ, Prime Video

Giants -2.5 (-110), moneyline Giants -134, total 41.5 (DraftKings via ESPN).

Injuries — Washington: Terry McLaurin is out for the season. Tackle Laremy Tunsil is on injured reserve until around December. Guard Lucas Patrick is doubtful with a concussion. Wide receiver Treylon Burks is questionable with a foot injury and tight end Colson Yankoff is questionable with a knee injury. Jacory Croskey-Merritt is on injured reserve and Jeremy McNichols is out for the year, leaving the backfield thin. Jayden Daniels has a left elbow issue but practiced fully on Monday and Tuesday.

Injuries — New York: Quarterback J.J. McCarthy is expected to play despite the hip pointer he suffered against Philadelphia — he missed 16 snaps in the third quarter but was a full participant in practice Tuesday. Running back Cam Skattebo, who missed the Philadelphia game with a hamstring issue, was also full Tuesday and is expected to be full go. Both starting cornerbacks are out: Paulson Adebo and Jarrick Bernard-Converse are on injured reserve, and Trey Amos is on IR for Washington as well.

Storylines: These two played to a 27-27 overtime tie on October 11, after Washington let a 27-13 fourth-quarter lead slip away — and a Skattebo fumble in overtime cost the Giants a win. The winner on Thursday takes the head-to-head tiebreaker and the NFC East lead. The Giants are 6-2 against the spread this season, including 3-1 at home, and rookie linebacker Arvell Reese was just named NFC Defensive Player of the Week after 12 tackles, three sacks and a forced fumble against the Eagles. Daniels has not thrown an interception this year and has nine total touchdowns, but the Commanders have won only one game he has started, and Myles Garrett sacked him three times last week. The total has fallen from 47.5 at open to 41.5 — the market expects a grind, in the rain (67% chance of precipitation, 53°F at kickoff).

The bet

Giants -2.5 (-110). The healthier offensive line, the more reliable run game, the better defense, and home field in a short-week divisional rematch. Skattebo ran for a season-high 188 yards against Houston in Week 7, and Washington's run defense has to deal with him behind a line that is intact — while the Commanders' line is missing its left tackle and possibly its left guard, with their best receiver out for the year. Lay the 2.5.

The honest caveat

McCarthy is playing hurt, and the 16 snaps he missed against Philadelphia showed what the offense looks like without him — 3 of 7 for 26 yards. Daniels has not thrown a pick all season, and divisional Thursday games are weird by nature; the first meeting ended in a tie, which tells you how close these teams are. The rain and the short week cut both ways, and the total dropping six points says the market thinks points will be scarce — a low-scoring game makes 2.5 a bigger number than it looks. But the Giants are the better team right now, and the injuries say so even more loudly than the record does.

The pick

Giants -2.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $19.09. Healthier, at home, with the better run game — lay the small number.
