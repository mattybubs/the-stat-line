Ravens Rule Out Lamar Jackson vs. Bengals

By Matt

Baltimore downgraded Lamar Jackson to out on Saturday for Sunday's game at Cincinnati — Tyler Huntley makes his third consecutive start.

Baltimore downgraded Lamar Jackson to out on Saturday for Sunday's game at Cincinnati. In his place, Tyler "Snoop" Huntley will make his third consecutive start.

What changed

The downgrade came on Saturday — less than 24 hours before the AFC North rivals meet in Cincinnati on Sunday. Jackson will now miss a third straight game, with Huntley stepping in again.

Out: Lamar Jackson

In: Tyler "Snoop" Huntley, third consecutive start

Game: Ravens at Bengals, Sunday

Why it matters

A divisional game on the road is a hard spot to be without your starting quarterback, and this one now rests on Huntley for a third straight week. The call coming on Saturday, rather than at kickoff, at least gives Baltimore a full day to prepare around him.
