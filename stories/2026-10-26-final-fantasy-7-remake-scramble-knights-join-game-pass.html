Final Fantasy 7 Remake and Scramble Knights Online Join Xbox Game Pass

By Matt

Six games join Xbox Game Pass between October 26 and 29, headlined by Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade — plus Scramble Knights Online.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The drop

Six games are scheduled to join Xbox Game Pass between October 26 and October 29, 2026, across Xbox Series X|S, PC and Cloud.

The headliner

Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade headlines the drop, reimagining the classic with updated graphics. It is the name that carries this wave — the remake of one of gaming's most beloved RPGs, now included with a Game Pass subscription.

Also joining

Scramble Knights Online joins the service in the same wave, giving the October 26–29 window a second notable addition alongside the Final Fantasy headliner.

The details

Window: October 26–29, 2026

Platforms: Xbox Series X\; S, PC, Cloud

Headliner: Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade

Also joining: Scramble Knights Online

Total games in wave: 6
