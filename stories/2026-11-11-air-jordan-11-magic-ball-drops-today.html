Air Jordan 11 'Magic Ball' drops today — the Space Jam 30th anniversary women's exclusive, $235

By Elijah

The Air Jordan 11 'Magic Ball' drops today, November 11 — women's sizes only, $235, via Nike SNKRS, Nike.com and Foot Locker. It's the first drop in the Space Jam 30th Anniversary Collection, inspired by the ball the Monstars used to steal the NBA's talent.

The drop

The Air Jordan 11 "Magic Ball" drops today, Wednesday, November 11 — women's sizes only, $235, available through Nike SNKRS, Nike.com and Foot Locker, plus select retailers.

It is the first release in the Space Jam 30th Anniversary Collection, and the concept is as fun as anything Jordan Brand has done in a while: the shoe is inspired by the magic ball the Monstars used to steal the NBA players' talents in Space Jam. The iridescent patent leather — gold shifting to green depending on the light — is the visual version of that idea, with black laces and an icy outsole to finish it.

Why this one

The 11 is the Space Jam shoe. The original "Concord" is the sneaker Michael Jordan wore in the film, and every anniversary cycle since has used the 11 to anchor the collection. This year's slate has the "Magic Ball" today, the "Green Screen" on November 14, and the main "Space Jam Galaxy" course after that.

The "Magic Ball" is also the most interesting of the three on its own terms. It is not a retro reissue of a colorway everyone already owns — it is a new idea, a women's exclusive, and it lands on Singles' Day, November 11, which has become one of the biggest sneaker dates of the year. The combination of a fresh concept and a limited sizing run is exactly what makes a release like this sell through.

Where to get it

Nike SNKRS is the first stop, with Nike.com and Foot Locker carrying it as well. Women's sizing only — if you wear men's sizes, this one is not for you, but the rest of the Space Jam slate will have wider runs.
