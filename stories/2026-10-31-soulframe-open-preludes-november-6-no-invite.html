Soulframe Opens to Everyone on PC November 6 — No Invite Needed, and Wolf Mounts Are Coming

By Matt

Digital Extremes announced during Soulframe Devstream 18 that Open Preludes drops the invitation requirement on Friday, November 6 — anyone on PC can play the fantasy RPG's alpha, with rideable wolf mounts arriving in Preludes 17 later in November.

The invite wall is coming down.

Digital Extremes announced October 30, during Soulframe Devstream 18, that Soulframe's Open Preludes will open to all Windows PC players on Friday, November 6 — no invitation, no key hunt, no Founders purchase required. Sign-ups are open now at soulframe.com, and players will access the game through Digital Extremes' own PC launcher.

It is the single biggest inflection point in the game's development since its first pre-alpha builds began in 2023. Until now, access has been invite-only — keys distributed in waves by email and via Twitch drops during devstreams — with a paid Founders program (launched November 25, 2025) as the only guaranteed way in. That program stays active for supporters, but buying in is no longer necessary to play.

What is Soulframe?

Soulframe is Digital Extremes' free-to-play fantasy action RPG — the Warframe studio's open-world cooperative follow-up, set on the island of Midrath, where players take on the role of an Envoy working to heal a world corrupted by the invading Ode. It is still a pre-alpha, not a finished release: there is no Steam launch and no console version as part of this opening, though the game can be wishlisted on Steam for its eventual full release.

Preludes 17: Verse of the Veilands

Alongside the open-access date, the studio detailed Preludes 17: Verse of the Veilands, the first major content update after the gates open, arriving later in November. The headliner is the community's most-requested feature since Soulframe was first shown publicly: rideable Omen Wolf mounts.

Players who have been raising wolf pups will finally be able to mature them into mounts — completing the pup's training, purifying two Veilands regions, then crafting a Bairnday Biscuit and feeding it to the pup so it grows into a fully rideable Omen Wolf. The update also introduces the Veilands themselves, a new environmental progression system built around reclaiming seven regions corrupted by the Ode, plus a new Ode Shrike Pact focused on traps and deception, new weapons and armor, enemy variants, fish and cave generation updates.

After years of watching from behind an invite wall, anyone with a PC can finally walk Midrath for themselves on November 6. The wolves, mercifully, are coming with them.
