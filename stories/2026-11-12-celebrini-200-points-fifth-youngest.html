Macklin Celebrini is the fifth-youngest player ever to 200 points — and he did it in a loss

By Matt

Macklin Celebrini scored his 200th career point at 20 years and 151 days old — fifth-youngest in NHL history, behind only Crosby, Gretzky, Carson and Lemieux — then added his 201st in the same game.

The night

The San Jose Sharks lost 3-2 in a shootout to the Pittsburgh Penguins on Wednesday night at SAP Center, blowing a game they had clawed back from 2-0 down in the third period. Ben Kindel scored in regulation and had the lone shootout goal for Pittsburgh. Sergei Murashov made 34 saves for the Penguins.

But the night belonged to the losing captain anyway.

With just over eight minutes left in the third, Macklin Celebrini broke up Murashov's shutout, pulling San Jose within one. That goal was the 200th point of his NHL career, in his 171st game. Four minutes later he added his 201st, the primary assist on Mason Marchment's tying goal — Marchment's 100th career goal.

The history

At 20 years and 151 days old, Celebrini is the fifth-youngest player in NHL history to reach 200 career points. The four names ahead of him are the entire argument for why this matters: Sidney Crosby (19 years, 207 days), Wayne Gretzky (19 years, 347 days), Jimmy Carson (20 years, 107 days) and Mario Lemieux (20 years, 133 days).

He is also just the 14th player ever to pass the 200-point mark before turning 21, and the fifth-fastest active player to get there. Within his own franchise, the pace is unmatched — no player who began his career with the Sharks has ever reached 200 points this fast. The previous franchise mark belonged to Logan Couture, who needed 273 games. Celebrini did it in 171.

The context around him makes it louder. Celebrini is the No. 1 pick of the 2024 draft, the youngest captain in Sharks history and the sixth-youngest captain in NHL history. In July he signed a five-year, $94 million extension — $18.8 million a year — starting in 2027-28. San Jose has already decided what he is. Nights like this one are the receipt.

What's next

The Sharks hit the road Saturday at Utah. The milestone watch is over; the next one is 300, and at this pace it is not that far away.
