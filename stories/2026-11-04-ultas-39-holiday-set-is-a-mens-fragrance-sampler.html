Ulta's $39 holiday set is a men's fragrance sampler

By Matt

Eighteen mini men's fragrances for $39, a $77 stated value, plus a $15 gift card toward a full-size bottle of one scent in the set.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Ulta's holiday lineup includes a men's fragrance sampler built for one specific job: trying a lot of scents before committing to one bottle. The Beauty Finds set gathers mini versions from a wide field of men's houses, so instead of guessing on a single full-size fragrance, you get a tour of the field first.

What's in the set

The set is a collection of mini versions — not full bottles. That is the whole point: each mini is a low-stakes way to test a scent on skin, over a full day, before spending full-bottle money on it.

• 18 scents from a wide field of men's fragrance houses

• Mini versions throughout — a sampler format, not a single-fragrance gift set

• Holiday positioning — built as a seasonal gift set

The numbers

Price: $39

Stated value: $77

Scents included: 18

Gift card included: $15

At $39 against a $77 stated value, the set prices out at roughly half its stated value — and that is before the gift card is counted.

How the gift card works

The set also includes a $15 gift card to put toward a full-size version of one of the fragrances in the set. That is the part that makes the sampler more than a one-off trial: test all 18 minis, find the one you would actually wear every day, and the gift card takes $15 off the full bottle of that exact scent.

Put together, the logic is simple. The $39 buys the discovery phase — 18 minis, wide range — and the $15 gift card subsidizes the decision that comes out of it.

Who this is for

This is a gift-set shape that works best for two people: someone shopping for a man who does not already have a signature scent locked in, and someone who likes fragrance but has not found the one worth a full bottle yet. For either, 18 minis beat one blind guess.
