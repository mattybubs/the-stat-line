Patriots Rout Raiders 45-17 Behind Maye's Five Touchdowns

By Matt

Drake Maye accounted for five touchdowns in a 45-17 statement win over the Raiders — his best game in a Patriots uniform.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The Complete Version of This Offense

Foxborough finally saw it: a 21-7 halftime lead at Gillette Stadium on Sunday turning into a full 45-17 rout of the Raiders. New England went over 500 yards, and Drake Maye accounted for five touchdowns — the second time in his career he's done it.

By the Numbers

Final: Patriots 45, Raiders 17

Halftime: Patriots 21, Raiders 7

Total offense: 500+ yards

Drake Maye TDs: 5 (career-high-tying 2nd time)

Maye milestone: First back-to-back 3-passing-TD stretch; first Patriot to do it since Tom Brady in 2018

The Scoring Parade

• Pop Douglas — 35-yard TD and 8-yard TD

• Rhamondre Stevenson — 5-yard run

• Hunter Henry — 11-yard TD before halftime

• Kyle Williams — 34-yard TD

• Drake Maye — 3-yard run

• Andy Borregales — 28-yard field goal

Brock Bowers countered for the Raiders with four catches for 69 yards and a first-half touchdown, but Las Vegas never kept pace.

The Visual of the Day

New England did it in the red throwbacks, worn for the first time this season. Adam Vinatieri was honored at halftime with his Hall of Fame ring.

The Bottom Line

Maye's second career five-TD game, a 500-yard offense, and the first back-to-back three-passing-TD stretch for a Patriot since 2018 — this was the offense Foxborough has been waiting for.
