Quinn Hughes Hits 500 Points in His Return to Vancouver — Second-Fastest American Defenseman Ever

By Matt

Quinn Hughes scored his 500th NHL point in his first game back in Vancouver since the blockbuster trade, reaching the mark in 520 games — second-fastest ever by an American defenseman, behind only Brian Leetch.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The moment

In his first game back at Rogers Arena since being traded to Minnesota on December 12, 2025, Quinn Hughes scored on the power play for his 500th NHL point — then added an assist to finish at 501 — as the Wild beat the Canucks 4-2.

Hughes reached 500 points in 520 games, making him the second-fastest American defenseman in NHL history to the mark. Only Brian Leetch was faster, doing it in 496 games.

The reception

Hughes was booed every time he touched the puck — and got a mix of cheers during a first-period video tribute. Maxim Shabanov opened the scoring on the power play, Bobby Brink added his first goal of the season, and Kirill Kaprizov scored his league-leading 13th goal to make it 4-0 early in the third.

Why it matters

• 500 points in 520 games puts Hughes in Leetch territory — that's the company he keeps.

• Doing it in the building he used to call home, against the team that traded him, is the kind of script sports rarely writes this cleanly.

• Minnesota's win moved the Wild two points ahead of Utah into first place in the Central Division.
