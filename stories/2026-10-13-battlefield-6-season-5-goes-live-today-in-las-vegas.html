Battlefield 6's Season 5 goes live today in Las Vegas

Battlefield 6's Season 5, Siege of Vegas, launches today with the Vegas Strip and Area 51 maps — plus a Dead Space crossover running Oct 20 – Nov 7.

Battlefield 6's newest season is here. Season 5: Siege of Vegas goes live today, taking the fight to two of the most famous locations in America.

What drops today

The headline additions:

• Vegas Strip — a playable map built around the neon casino corridor

• Area 51 — a second playable map at the legendary desert facility

The real headliner: Dead Space crossover

The official Season 5 trailer's big reveal isn't the maps — it's what's coming next. A Dead Space crossover event runs October 20 through November 7, bringing the sci-fi horror series into Battlefield's world.
