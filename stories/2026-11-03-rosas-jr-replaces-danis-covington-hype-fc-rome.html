Rosas Jr. Steps In as Danis Pulls Out, Faces Covington Tonight in Rome

By Matt

Dillon Danis is out of tonight's Hype Fighting main event in Rome, and Raul Rosas Jr. volunteered from the press-conference audience to face Colby Covington in a 10-minute, submission-only grappling match.

Dillon Danis is out, and Raul Rosas Jr. is in. Tonight's Hype Fighting main event in Rome has a new headliner after Danis pulled out of his scheduled grappling match with Colby Covington — and his replacement volunteered on the spot, from the audience.

The switch

Hype Fighting CEO Armand Martirosian announced Danis' withdrawal at Sunday's press conference in Rome. No official reason has been given for the pullout. It is Danis' third withdrawal from a signed bout in under a year.

Rosas Jr. was in the room. When Martirosian called for a replacement, the UFC bantamweight walked down from the audience, accepted the match, and faced off with Covington right there at the press conference.

"Unfortunately, Dillon Danis could not come," a Hype Fighting organizer told the room. "If there's someone who wants to wrestle with Colby, we can organize it."

The matchup

Covington vs. Rosas Jr. headlines Hype Fighting's Italy debut at the PalaPellicone in Ostia, Rome, tonight, Tuesday, November 3. It is a 10-minute, submission-only grappling match — no striking, no judges' decision, no points. The only way to win is to force the other man to submit, by choke or joint lock. Takedowns and dominant positions alone will not earn a victory.

The size gap is real. Rosas Jr. normally competes at bantamweight, around 135 pounds, while Covington is a former interim UFC welterweight champion who usually fights around 170. Covington praised Rosas Jr. for taking the match on short notice.

The backstory

Rome was originally booked as a rematch of sorts. Covington defeated Danis by 14-4 technical fall at RAF 7 in Tampa in March, and the Hype booking was pitched as Danis' chance to answer back under a different ruleset. Danis has since lost on the RAF mat to Khamzat Chimaev and Arman Tsarukyan as well.

Rosas Jr. is no stranger to this stage. He previously went the full 10 minutes with former UFC flyweight champion Deiveson Figueiredo at an earlier Hype FC event, a match ruled a draw. He is a top-10 UFC bantamweight contender riding a six-fight winning streak in MMA, fresh off his first UFC main-event win over Raoni Barcelos in September.

How to watch

Hype Fighting Rome streams live and free on DAZN and YouTube, with coverage starting at 1:00 p.m. ET (7:00 p.m. local time in Rome). The main event closes the show, so Covington vs. Rosas Jr. lands later in the evening, depending on how long the preceding bouts run. The rest of the 11-bout card mixes in bare-knuckle boxing and dirty boxing.
