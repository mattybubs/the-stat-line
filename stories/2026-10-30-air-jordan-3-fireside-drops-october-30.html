Air Jordan 3 'Fireside' Drops Today: $205, Velvet Brown, and a Spare Set of Pink Laces

By Matt

The women's-exclusive Air Jordan 3 'Fireside' releases Friday, Oct. 30 for $205: Velvet Brown tumbled leather, suede trim, tonal elephant print, a gum outsole — and a spare set of pink laces in the box.

Fall got its Jordan 3 — and it reads exactly like a night by the fire.

The Air Jordan 3 "Fireside" releases Friday, October 30, 2026 for $205, a women's-exclusive general release through Nike SNKRS (10:00 a.m. ET) and select retailers. No raffle gauntlet, no verified sellout story — just a Friday-morning drop, with individual stores running their own systems.

The shoe, up close

This one is restraint, not noise. The main panels are tumbled leather in Velvet Brown, with suede taking over the tongue, collar and eyestays — so the shoe reads in three textures of the same brown instead of one flat color. The elephant print, the Jordan 3's signature, goes tonal at the heel over the visible Air unit, and a dark brown midsole sits over a gum outsole, carrying the campfire palette all the way to the ground.

The branding stays quiet to match: the Jumpman on the tongue and heel is Pearl White — it and the fitted laces are the only light notes on the shoe.

Then the box opens up the one loud option. Every pair comes with a second set of laces in pale pink — Rust Pink, in the official colorway string (Velvet Brown / Light British Tan – Rust Pink – Pearl White). Laced Pearl White, the shoe is all ember and bark. Swap in the pink and it's a different sneaker entirely.

The one warning

Sizing is women's. Read the size chart before you check out, not after the box lands. Extended women's runs exist at some retailers — Sneaker Politics lists the shoe up to a women's 14.5 — but stock in the biggest sizes is never the deep end of the pool. Check your retailer's listing before Friday morning rather than during the drop.

The details: Air Jordan 3 "Fireside" (CK9246-200), $205, women's sizing, Nike SNKRS and select retailers, Friday, Oct. 30.
