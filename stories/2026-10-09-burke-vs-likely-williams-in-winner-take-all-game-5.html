Burke vs. likely Williams in winner-take-all Game 5

By Matt

Saturday's ALDS Game 5 in Cleveland comes down to Sean Burke for the White Sox against in-all-likelihood Gavin Williams for the Guardians — winner opens the ALCS in Tampa on Monday.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The matchup

Saturday night's Game 5 in Cleveland comes down to Sean Burke for the White Sox — making his second start of the postseason — and in all likelihood Gavin Williams for the Guardians.

How we got here

The Guardians answered a 2-0 series deficit with back-to-back elimination-game wins in Chicago, including the 9-5 Game 4 comeback powered by a six-run sixth inning. The series that looked like a White Sox coronation is now a single coin-flip game in Cleveland.

The stakes

The winner opens the ALCS in Tampa on Monday against the rested Rays, who swept their way into the championship series while these two have been trading haymakers. Whoever survives Saturday walks straight into the buzzsaw.

### By the numbers

• Game 5: Saturday night, Cleveland

• White Sox starter: Sean Burke (second postseason start)

• Guardians starter: likely Gavin Williams

• Series: tied 2-2

• Next up: ALCS opens Monday in Tampa vs. the Rays
