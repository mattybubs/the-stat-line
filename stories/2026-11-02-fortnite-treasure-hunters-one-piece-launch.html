One Piece Sets Sail in Fortnite: Treasure Hunters Is Live

By Matt

The Straw Hats are in Fortnite. Treasure Hunters launched November 1 with a new 80-player island, Devil Fruit Mythics, and the One Piece crew rolling out in waves through mid-November.

The wait is over, and the Going Merry has replaced the Battle Bus. Fortnite's pirate mini-season, Treasure Hunters, launched Sunday, November 1 with update v43.00, and its centerpiece is the One Piece crossover fans have wanted for years — the first time the anime has gotten a dedicated Fortnite season.

This is not a skins-only collab. One Piece is built into how the season plays.

A new island built for a treasure hunt

Chapter 7 Season 4, Override, is out. In its place is Crossbones Isle, a skull-shaped island that holds 80 players instead of the usual 100, and the whole season is structured around the chase: track targets, crack chests, beat rival crews, and claim treasure before anyone else does. Sprites are staying too, with new variants and Mastery Mondays.

Two of the island's locations come straight out of the anime: Arlong Park and Windmill Village. And the season's opening shot says everything about the tone — the Going Merry sailing through fog under a full moon, carrying players into the drop.

The powers are the point

The gameplay trailer, which dropped October 30, shows the One Piece abilities working as Mythic items rather than cosmetic nods. Luffy's Gum-Gum Fruit gives you the stretchy rubber attacks, including the Gum-Gum Pistol. Ace's Flame-Flame Fruit brings the fire. Zoro's Three Sword Style and Usopp's slingshot are in as Mythics too, alongside a tangerine healing item for the Nami fans.

That is what separates this from most crossovers. You are not just dressed as the Straw Hats. You are fighting like them.

Who you can play, and when

Here is the part that tripped a lot of people up: only two One Piece characters are in the Battle Pass. Portgas D. Ace and Nico Robin headline the pass (reported at 800 V-Bucks), alongside a new original character called Captain X — the voice you hear at the One Piece chest on the island — and anime-style remixes of Jonesy, Cuddle Team Leader, and Charlotte.

The rest of the crew comes through the Item Shop in waves:

• November 1 (launch day): Monkey D. Luffy, Nami, and Usopp

• November 7: Roronoa Zoro, Sanji, and the Chopper Sidekick

• November 13: Buggy

So if you want the full crew, pace your V-Bucks. Luffy is not in the pass, and Zoro and Sanji are a week out.

The season keeps rolling

Treasure Hunters is a mini-season — about five weeks, closing with a Chapter 7 live event on December 5 — and it does not stand still. Each week brings a new captain with their own crew and point of interest, on what Epic calls Captain's Days: November 1, 7, 12, 14, and 21, each expanding the loot pool with new weapons and Sprites. The Straw Hats and A$AP Rocky — who has his own battleship on the map and a signature Shock Rocket — are first. Lil Uzi Vert follows, and Cap'n Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto close out the lineup.

One Piece is not stopping at Fortnite, either. Rocket League's limited-time event runs November 1 through November 23, with challenges unlocking a Luffy Straw Hat Topper, a Straw Hat Crew Boost, and a Going Merry Sea Trail.
