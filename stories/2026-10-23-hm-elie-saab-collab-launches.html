H&M x Elie Saab Collaboration Launches — Couture Glamour at Mass-Market Prices

By Matt

Lebanese couturier Elie Saab's first-ever menswear plus a ~55-piece womenswear and accessories collection landed at H&M yesterday — lace, dégradé sequins and embroidered gowns starting at €29.99, with a bridal gown as the nod to his origins.

Elie Saab x H&M launched yesterday — and it brings the Lebanese couturier's red-carpet vocabulary to mass-market prices.

The Drop

• First-ever Elie Saab menswear, plus ~55 pieces of womenswear and accessories

• Lace, dégradé sequins, embroidery, embroidered gowns — starting at €29.99

• The centerpiece: a bridal gown with a hand-embroidered veil, a nod to Saab's bridal origins

• Standouts: green-to-black bustier gown, gold-to-black sequined blazer, chocolate-brown leather funnel-neck jacket (€399)

• Campaign fronted by Imaan Hammam, shot in Paris

The Scene

London shoppers queued outside H&M's Regent Street store before the 9 a.m. launch. Designer collabs are supposed to be a fading formula — this one behaved like a drop.
