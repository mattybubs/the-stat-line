Baker Mayfield Returns to a Major Challenge for Buccaneers

By Matt

Baker Mayfield is expected to start for Tampa Bay on Sunday against Carolina after missing three games with a dislocated right thumb — but he returns without Emeka Egbuka and Luke Goedeke.

Baker Mayfield is expected to start for Tampa Bay on Sunday against the Carolina Panthers after missing three games with a dislocated right thumb, according to Bucs Wire.

The return

Three games out with a dislocated right thumb — and now the starter is back for Sunday's game against Carolina. That is the headline, and it is a welcome one for Tampa Bay.

The catch

Mayfield returns without wide receiver Emeka Egbuka and right tackle Luke Goedeke, both ruled out for Sunday, according to Reuters.

Returning: Baker Mayfield (dislocated right thumb, missed three games)

Ruled out: Emeka Egbuka, Luke Goedeke

Game: Buccaneers vs. Panthers, Sunday

Why it is a major challenge

Getting your quarterback back is only half the equation. Doing it without a starting receiver and a starting right tackle — against a divisional opponent — is why this return comes with a real test attached. Mayfield is back, but the supporting cast around him is thinner than Tampa Bay would want.
