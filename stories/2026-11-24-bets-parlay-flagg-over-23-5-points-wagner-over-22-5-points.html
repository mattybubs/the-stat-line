TSL Bets: The Tuesday parlay (~+283) — Flagg OVER 23.5 points, Wagner OVER 22.5 points

By Matt

Cooper Flagg is the Mavericks' lead scorer against a soft Denver defense at plus money, and Franz Wagner is averaging 27.4 a game with Paolo Banchero out. Two legs, about +283.

The games

Detroit Pistons (10-6) at Orlando Magic (7-9) — 7:00 PM ET, Kia Center, Orlando, FL

Pistons -2.5, total 213.5, moneyline -135 / +114 (ESPN).

Injuries — Detroit: C Jalen Duren OUT (hamstring). G/F Duncan Robinson OUT (quadriceps). F Taurean Prince OUT (knee). G Cade Cunningham day-to-day (knee). F Ebuka Okorie OUT (oblique).

Injuries — Orlando: F Paolo Banchero OUT (ankle). F Jonathan Isaac OUT (knee). F Noah Penda OUT (hamstring). F Izaiyah Nelson OUT (ankle).

Dallas Mavericks (10-7) at Denver Nuggets (10-5) — 10:00 PM ET, Ball Arena, Denver, CO

Nuggets -7.5, total 233.5, moneyline -310 / +251 (ESPN).

Injuries — Denver: No players listed on the injury report.

Injuries — Dallas: C Dereck Lively II OUT (foot). F P.J. Washington day-to-day (illness). F Tobi Lawal OUT (groin). G Sergio de Larrea OUT (knee). F Tarik Biberovic OUT (heel).

Storylines: With Banchero out, Wagner has become Orlando's offense — 27.4 points a game over his last 10, including 35 against Brooklyn on Nov 20 and a 35-point night against Milwaukee. Detroit is without its starting center in Duren, and Cunningham is day-to-day with a knee issue, so the Pistons' defense is thinner than the -2.5 spread suggests. In Denver, Flagg is the Mavericks' lead scorer at 24.3 a game with a 28.7% usage rate, coming off 28 points at Utah, and he faces a Nuggets defense ranked 21st in the league (115.6 defensive rating) with no Lively to protect the rim.

Leg 1 — Cooper Flagg OVER 23.5 points

Flagg averages 24.3 points a game and drives 16.4 times a night, scoring 9.4 points a game on those drives alone. He just put up 28 at Utah and 33 against Golden State, and Dallas staggers him with Irving so he gets plenty of possessions as the lead scorer. Denver's defense is 21st in the league and short on rim protection. Line: OVER 23.5 points (+100, Splash Sports).

Leg 2 — Franz Wagner OVER 22.5 points

Wagner is scoring 27.4 points over his last 10, up from 24.5 for the season, and has cleared 22.5 in 7 of those 10 games. His usage is 29.7%, he attacks the rim 21.9 times a game, and with Banchero out he carries even more of the shot creation. He is averaging 29.3 points, 7.6 rebounds and 7.6 assists over his last seven games. Line: OVER 22.5 points (-109, Bally Bet).

The bet

Cooper Flagg OVER 23.5 points (+100, Splash Sports) + Franz Wagner OVER 22.5 points (-109, Bally Bet) — about +283. A $10 ticket returns about $48.30.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Wagner is a 27.4-points-a-game scorer over his last 10 at a line nearly five points below that, with the volume and usage to back it, and Flagg is a plus-money lead scorer against a soft defense who just dropped 28. Neither leg depends on the other — different players, different games, different conferences. That is the juice rule working as intended: a plus-money leg plus a near-even leg, about +283 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

Flagg is the volatile leg: he has cleared 23.5 in only 4 of his last 10, with 12- and 17-point nights in that stretch — the plus money is the market telling you this is close to a coin flip. Wagner just scored 16 at Washington on Sunday, so the hot streak is not a straight line. And a parlay needs both legs — either one can sink it alone. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.
