TSL Bets: Luka Doncic OVER 35.5 points vs the Hornets (-112)

By Matt

The Lakers are 12-2 on an eight-game win streak, Austin Reaves is out again, and Luka Doncic leads the league at 39.0 points per game. The play: Doncic OVER 35.5 points (-112) against a Hornets defense that allows 40.6% from three.

The game slip

Charlotte Hornets (4-9) at Los Angeles Lakers (12-2)

Tuesday, 11:00 PM ET — crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles

• Spread: Lakers -8.5

• Total: 218.5

• Moneyline: Hornets +302 / Lakers -381

• Odds source: DraftKings via NBC Sports, November 17

Injury report — Lakers: G Austin Reaves OUT (left heel bruise — second straight game; 24.7 PPG, the Lakers' second-leading scorer). C Walker Kessler OUT (right lower-leg soreness). G Collin Sexton game-time decision (eye). G Bronny James questionable (illness).

Injury report — Hornets: F Kon Knueppel OUT (hamstring). G Dennis Schroder OUT (hamstring). F Moussa Diabate OUT (quad). F Grant Williams OUT (hamstring).

Storylines: The Lakers extended their winning streak to eight with a 131-123 victory at Phoenix on Sunday, and Doncic erupted for 43 points in that win. Los Angeles is 12-2 with the No. 1 offensive rating in basketball at 122.1 and a league-best 122.8 points per game. Charlotte is 4-9 with a -10.4 point differential and the 30th-ranked offensive rating at 102.5 — and the Hornets allow 40.6% from three, also 30th in the league.

The bet

Luka Doncic OVER 35.5 points (-112, FanDuel)

Why this prop

Doncic is the NBA's scoring leader at 39.0 points per game, and he just dropped 43 on 13-of-28 shooting with nine assists against Phoenix on Sunday. He has gone over 35.5 points in 8 of his last 10 games, and OPTIMAL projects him for 36.4 against Charlotte.

The injury report is the engine here. With Reaves out — he averages 24.7 points — the shot-creation load shifts almost entirely onto Doncic, and Kessler's absence takes away another 14.5 points a night. The matchup is the softest possible landing spot: Charlotte allows 40.6% from three (30th) and 41.2% on non-corner threes (30th), the exact shots Doncic generates off the dribble, and he is shooting 48.6% from the field.

This is the props-first single by the book: a player prop with a real matchup edge, a line sitting below the scoring leader's average, and a projection that clears it.

The honest caveats

A blowout could trim Doncic's fourth-quarter minutes — the Lakers are laying 8.5, and if this one gets away early, the fourth quarter belongs to the bench. The projections aren't unanimous either: one model range has him at 30.8 to 33.9 points, below the line, even as OPTIMAL sits at 36.4. And 8 of 10 is a strong hit rate, not a guarantee — Doncic has also had quiet nights in this stretch. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 20-33 in November, 24-54 all-time. Props are 6-2 in November — the only winning singles category — that's why the daily single is a prop.
