Bills at Rams on Monday night tops the Week 5 slate

By Matt

USA TODAY is calling Bills-Rams a potential Super Bowl LXI preview — but Sunday gets there first with Eagles-Jaguars in London and 49ers-Seahawks for first place in the NFC West.

The headliner: Bills at Rams, Monday night

The weekend's main event is Monday night, when the Bills visit the Rams — a matchup USA TODAY is already calling a potential Super Bowl LXI preview. A heavyweight cross-conference clash under the lights is exactly what October football should feel like.

Sunday's undercard

Sunday gets there first with two games that matter:

• Eagles vs. Jaguars in London — the league's London slate rolls on, and both teams have reasons to treat this like a statement game.

• 49ers vs. Seahawks — first place in the NFC West is on the line in a divisional showdown with early tiebreaker implications.

Why this weekend matters

Early-October weekends like this are when the contenders start separating from the pretenders. A Bills win on the road in Los Angeles would be the statement of the season so far; a Rams win would plant their flag as the NFC's team to beat. Either way, the Week 5 storylines run straight through Monday night.
