Pokémon's Day & Night boxes are trading at double retail a week after launch — and the market is already cooling

By Matt

The Pokémon TCG 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections — Day with Espeon ex, Night with Umbreon ex — launched November 6 at $179.99. Pre-release market prices ran past three times retail, and even after a pullback they are still trading well above it.

The boxes

The peak product of Pokémon's 30th anniversary year landed on November 6: the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections, in two versions — Day, built around Espeon ex, and Night, built around Umbreon ex. Each one retails for $179.99 at the Pokémon Center and participating retailers.

What is inside justifies the price tag, at least on paper. Every box carries 30 packs — 29 from the 30th Celebration set plus one Classic Collection pack with three etched cards from the anniversary subset, including the Base Set Charizard reprint. Then the promos: a foil Pikachu ex illustrated by YOSHIROTTEN in a day or night version, and a foil Espeon ex or Umbreon ex depending on the box. A playmat, deck box, 65 sleeves, a metal coin and six damage-counter dice round it out.

The two versions are identical except for the theme — the featured Eeveelution, the Pikachu art, and the accessories. Espeon and Umbreon are the Gen II Eeveelutions, the pair that has anchored Pokémon collecting since Gold and Silver, and the 30th anniversary is the first time they have headlined a premium box together.

The market

The resale market did what it always does with a premium Eeveelution product: it went vertical, then started coming back down.

TCGplayer's pre-release market figures put both versions at more than three times retail — roughly 3.1 times for Day and 3.3 times for Night. At launch, market tracking had both boxes above $420. But the peak is already behind us: in the week before launch, Night fell 25% and Day fell 22.6% from their highs, as supply actually reached shelves.

Good luck choosing, though. Best Buy listed the collections with a one-per-order restriction and assigns buyers a random Day or Night version rather than letting them pick. In France, only Night released on November 6 — Day is expected November 27.

Why it matters

The 30th Celebration set itself launched September 16 — all-foil cards, 30 unique Pikachu cards with one guaranteed in every booster, a new Futuristic Rare rarity. The Day & Night boxes are the anniversary's premium capstone, and the Classic Collection pack inside is the only place to pull those gold-bordered reprints of three decades of icons.

The market cooling off a pre-release spike is normal. The question is where the floor is: at $179.99 retail with 30 packs and two exclusive promos, the box is fairly priced even before resale. If you can find one at retail, that is the buy. If you are paying $420, you are paying for the Eeveelutions, not the cardboard.
