Marchand, Barkov return as Panthers beat Senators 5-3

By Matt

Brad Marchand had three assists and Aleksander Barkov scored into an empty net as both returned from knee injuries to lift the Florida Panthers past the Ottawa Senators 5-3.

Brad Marchand had three assists and Aleksander Barkov scored into an empty net as both returned from knee injuries to lift the Florida Panthers past the Ottawa Senators 5-3 at Amerant Bank Arena on Wednesday night.

The returns

Marchand had missed 20 games since sustaining a knee injury in a 2-1 win at the Los Angeles Kings on Oct. 6. Barkov, the Panthers captain, had been out 22 games after a knee injury of his own, sustained in a 4-3 overtime loss at the San Jose Sharks on Oct. 1.

Barkov's road back was the longer one. He missed the entire 2025-26 season with a right knee injury, then hurt his left knee in the second game of this season. Wednesday was just the sixth regular-season game with both him and Marchand in the lineup — and the first since the 2024-25 season. It was the first time the two have dressed together since Florida's Stanley Cup-clinching win in 2025, and the first regular-season game with Barkov, Marchand, Seth Jones, Matthew Tkachuk and Brady Tkachuk all in the lineup at once.

Marchand wasted no time making his presence felt. When his name was announced for the assist on the opening goal, the crowd got to its feet.

How it went down

Marchand set up Eetu Luostarinen at 16:16 of the first, getting to a puck behind the net and sending a backhanded feed through the goal mouth. Ottawa answered with two goals 41 seconds apart early in the second — Dylan Cozens at 1:12, then Philip Tomasino splitting two Florida defenders on a rush at 1:53 — to take a 2-1 lead.

Niko Mikkola tied it midway through the second, fighting to the front of the net to deflect a Matthew Tkachuk point shot past Linus Ullmark. Then Marchand drove up the ice and fed Anton Lundell charging the goal at 18:55, and Lundell buried his own rebound for the lead going into the third.

Sam Bennett made it 4-2 on the power play at 9:17 of the third, and Barkov sealed it with the empty-netter at 16:08 — his first goal of the season. Michael Amadio pulled one back for Ottawa at 19:09 with Ullmark pulled for the extra attacker, and Sam Reinhart hit the crossbar with fourteen seconds left, but Florida closed it out.

What it means

Ottawa came into the night with wins in five of its past six and a five-point lead on the Panthers in the Atlantic — and left with its nine-game point streak (7-0-2) over. The win pulled Florida within one point of second in the division and wrapped up the homestand with two straight wins.

More than the standings, this was the first look at the team Florida envisioned in the offseason: the captain and Marchand back together, the Tkachuk brothers on the same side, and the full group healthy at the same time. If both stay in the lineup, the Panthers look a lot more like the team that lifted the Cup.
