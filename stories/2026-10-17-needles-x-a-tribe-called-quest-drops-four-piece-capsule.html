NEEDLES x A Tribe Called Quest drops four-piece capsule today

By Matt

A four-piece FW26 capsule — track suit, roll-neck cardigan, and a B.B. Jacket with a chenille Low End Theory back patch — drops today at NEPENTHES stores in Japan.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

NEEDLES and A Tribe Called Quest are dropping a four-piece Fall/Winter 2026 capsule today, October 17, at NEPENTHES stores in Japan. The whole thing runs on a tight red, green and black palette, and the embroidery is the story.

The four pieces

• Track Jacket — the label's signature tracksuit build, restriped in red and green

• Track Pant — matching bottoms with the ATCQ embroidery running from chest to thigh

• Roll Neck Cardigan — a knit option with the same embroidery mix

• B.B. Jacket — the centerpiece

The centerpiece: the B.B. Jacket

The standout is the chenille back patch on the B.B. Jacket — a recreation of the figure from The Low End Theory album cover. The timing isn't accidental: the album marked its 35th anniversary on September 24, and the drop lands in that same season. Chenille is a texture-first choice here — it's loud without being shiny, and on a varsity-style silhouette it reads like a trophy patch from a season that actually happened.

The repeating butterfly and ATCQ embroidery from chest to thigh on the tracksuit ties the four pieces together — classic NEEDLES motif language meeting Tribe iconography.

Why it works

This is a collaboration that actually understands both sides: NEEDLES gets ATCQ's graphics, ATCQ gets NEEDLES' track-suit vocabulary, and neither waters the other down. A four-piece capsule is tight enough to sell through — and given NEEDLES' track record with capsules like this, don't count on restocks.
