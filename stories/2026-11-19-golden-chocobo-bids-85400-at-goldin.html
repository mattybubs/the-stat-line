Golden Chocobo bids $85,400 at Goldin

By Matt

A PSA 8 serialized Golden Traveling Chocobo — #71 of just 77 ever printed — is bidding at $85,400 in Goldin's fall TCG auction, days after a sibling copy reportedly changed hands for $1.4 million.

The golden bird is back on the block. A PSA 8 serialized Golden Traveling Chocobo — card #71 of the entire print run — is bidding at $85,400 with buyer's premium in Goldin's 2026 Fall TCG Elite Auction, and the auction is still live.

The card

This is the 2025 Magic: The Gathering — Final Fantasy serialized Golden Chocobo (#0551f Traveling Chocobo), the gold-foiled chase card from the set. Wizards printed the serialized version in English Collector Boosters only, each copy stamped with its own number, and the odds of pulling one were under 0.1% per pack, according to CGC. ICv2 calls it one of the scarcest Magic cards in existence, and it rarely shows up at auction at all.

It's also not clear how many of the print run ever made it into collectors' hands — the rest could still be sitting in sealed product. Unopened Collector Boosters for the set are going for roughly $1,500 a box.

The timing is the story

This listing lands days after a sibling copy made headlines of its own. Copy #47 — a PSA Gem Mint 10 that also carries an MBA Gold Diamond evaluation — was reported sold in a private deal for $1.4 million, per MTG Rocks and Draftsim on November 17. The seller was named as Adam Cai, also known as Pristine_Collectibles, with the report traced to Collectr.

A note of caution on that figure: it is a reported private sale, not an auction result. No buyer identity, settlement document, or payment record has been published, and one extraordinary sale does not set a market price for the rest of the print run. The previous confirmed record for the card was $250,000 — less than a fifth of the reported number.

Recent auction comps put the Goldin bid in context. A CGC 8 copy realized $75,000 at Fanatics Collect in March, and another CGC 8 went for $67,100 at Goldin in September 2025. Draftsim notes most copies known to have changed hands have sold in the $40,000–$80,000 range, which is exactly where this listing sits right now.

The rest of the auction

Goldin's fall sale is stacked beyond the Chocobo. The current headline lots include:

Illustrator Pikachu: $488,000

1st Edition Base Set box: $231,800

Final Fantasy Collector Boosters (unopened): ~$1,500

The Chocobo is the Magic headliner, but the Pikachu is the auction's top lot by a wide margin.
