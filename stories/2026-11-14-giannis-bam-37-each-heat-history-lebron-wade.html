Giannis and Bam drop 37 apiece — only the second Heat duo ever to do it, and the first since LeBron and Wade

By Matt

Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo each scored 37 in Miami's 133-121 win over Cleveland — the second pair of Heat teammates ever to both score 35 or more in the same game, and the first since LeBron James and Dwyane Wade in 2013.

The night

The Miami Heat have a new big two, and on Friday night it looked like the old one. Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo each scored 37 points as the Heat beat the Cleveland Cavaliers 133-121 at Kaseya Center in NBA Cup play — and in doing so, joined the only duo in franchise history to ever do it.

Antetokounmpo shot 15-for-20 and added nine rebounds in 31 minutes. Adebayo went 13-for-19 from the field and 9-for-10 from the line, with 14 rebounds for the double-double. Both totals were season highs. Davion Mitchell ran the show around them with 22 points and a career-high-tying 17 assists against one turnover.

Miami took control of East Group B at 2-0, won its third straight, and improved to 6-6 after an 0-4 start. The Heat have won six of their last eight.

The history

Antetokounmpo and Adebayo are the second pair of Heat teammates ever to both score 35 or more in the same game. The only others: LeBron James and Dwyane Wade, who last did it in 2013. The exact match — 37 apiece — had also happened only once before in franchise history, by those same two.

It is a fitting comparison for what Miami is building. James and Wade were the centerpiece of the most successful era in franchise history. Antetokounmpo, acquired in the offseason, is averaging 27.7 points, 12.6 rebounds and 5.2 assists; Adebayo, in his 10th season and all of them in Miami, is at 17.8 points, 8.4 boards and a career-high 2.2 steals a night. Friday was the first time the pairing looked like it could carry the franchise the way the last one did.

Cleveland, meanwhile, lost its fifth game in seven. Donovan Mitchell led the Cavs with 25 points and seven assists.

What's next

The Heat host the Detroit Pistons on Saturday night — the second night of a back-to-back, and the next test of whether this duo is a one-night story or the new normal in Miami.
