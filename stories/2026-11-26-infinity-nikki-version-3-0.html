Infinity Nikki Version 3.0 is live — two new cities, guns and magic, and 160 free draws

By Matt

Infinity Nikki's biggest update yet is live today: Version 3.0 'Of Yesterday and Tomorrow' takes Nikki and Momo to the Twin Cities of Art, Cicia and Ceremius, with nearly 10 hours of new story, four new exploration abilities, firearms and magic combat, and 160 free draws plus 19 outfit sets to celebrate the update and Nikki's birthday.

Infinity Nikki just got its biggest update yet. Version 3.0, "Of Yesterday and Tomorrow," is live today on PlayStation 5, and it is less a patch than a second launch: two new cities, nearly 10 hours of cinematic story, four new abilities, a combat system that now includes firearms and magic, and a pile of free rewards to mark the occasion.

The update takes Nikki and Momo to the Twin Cities of Art — Cicia and Ceremius, two cities that share a common origin and could hardly be more different. Cicia is the capital of art, where Nikki attends an academy, runs into strange anomalies on the streets, and gets pulled into an investigation of a group known as the Rogues. Ceremius sits behind a wall of mist, and the story there ties the city's past to a present-day crisis and the mystery of the Goddess des Arts.

Four new abilities, and combat grows teeth

Version 3.0 hands stylists four new tools for getting around and solving puzzles. Veiling lets Nikki throw a Shadow Beetle and teleport to it — or take over the view of certain objects it lands on. Alchemy turns gathered materials into brewed potions, with new recipes discovered through trial-crafting. Enhanced Floating adds a midair double jump and forward air dash. And Gemwork lets her appraise uncut gemstones out in the world, then work them into gems for outfit crafting and Alchemy.

Combat gets its biggest expansion since launch. Two new systems arrive: firearms, with a short musket for rapid fire and a long musket for aimed shots, and magic, with a staff that casts different Oracle Arts depending on the selected Nature's Breaths and chant tiers. A new build system sits on top of both, so players can tune their fighting style instead of relying on one fixed set of moves.

A living city, and a birthday haul

Cicia is built to feel like a working city. Nikki can drive a car from the in-game Brise Automobile brand to complete passenger commissions, run her own clothing store — the Warmhearted Boutique — and style outfits for customers, or try side activities like the Scoop & Smile Creamery ice cream shop and Fuse Bead Art. Ceremius leans into its mystery instead, with relic restoration, old photo restoration, and divination tied to local lore. Puzzle dungeons across both cities draw on the new exploration abilities, and the open world now has larger ambient NPC crowds, flowing traffic, and richer interactions — taxi passengers with stories, street musicians, NPCs dancing together.

The headline outfit is the first Star Crown Resonance set, "Where Stars Shine Again," which comes with both new weapons — musket and staff — and lets unused dashes be stored and released all at once. A new 5-Star set, "When Light Falls Upon," unlocks Air Jumps and Air Dashes, and two 4-Star sets, "Game for the Immortal" and "Beyond the Silence," summon chess pieces and a fairy-tale phantom for playful interactions.

New and returning players can also jump straight in: Version 3.0 lets stylists skip the standard beginner tutorial, try a new transitional tutorial, and use chapter select to start from any story chapter released so far.

And the free haul is generous. PlayStation Blog puts the total at 160 draws and 19 sets of clothes, all free, across Version 3.0 and Nikki's birthday celebrations — including a first top-up double bonus reset, a Black Friday sale, and two 5-Star outfits among the login rewards.
