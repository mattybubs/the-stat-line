Brady calls Packers-Patriots from Gillette booth

By Matt

Tom Brady returns to Gillette Stadium today to call Packers-Patriots from the FOX booth at 4:25 p.m. ET — his first Patriots game as a broadcaster, with both teams at 5-3 and Drake Maye facing Jordan Love in the late-afternoon national window.

Tom Brady returns to Gillette Stadium today to call the Packers-Patriots game from the FOX booth at 4:25 p.m. ET. Both teams arrive 5-3, with Drake Maye and Jordan Love facing off in the late-afternoon national window.

Game info

Matchup: Green Bay Packers at New England Patriots

Venue: Gillette Stadium, Foxborough

Kickoff: Today, 4:25 p.m. ET

TV: FOX — "America's Game of the Week" (also streaming on FOX One)

Booth: Kevin Burkhardt (play-by-play), Tom Brady (analyst), Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi (sidelines)

Brady's first Patriots game in the booth

This is Brady's first time calling a Patriots game as a television analyst since he joined FOX's top NFL booth — and his first game at Gillette Stadium in the booth. FOX has spent the week promoting the return with a 76-second teaser built around Brady's best moments from his 20 seasons in New England.

Brady admitted the day will hit different. "It'll be certainly emotional," he told Colin Cowherd this week.

The game itself

The football is worth the window on its own. Both teams sit at 5-3, and the matchup is a showcase for two of the game's top young quarterbacks: Jordan Love's Packers against Drake Maye's Patriots. New England is coming off a 24-19 win over Miami, and the Patriots enter as three-point favorites.

It is a full Foxborough weekend, too. Rob Gronkowski is inducted into the Patriots Hall of Fame, with the ceremony taking place at halftime.

Why this one matters

Brady won six Super Bowl rings in New England. Today he watches the franchise he defined from the booth above the field where it happened — calling a game between two 5-3 teams, with the next generation of quarterbacks on the field below him.
