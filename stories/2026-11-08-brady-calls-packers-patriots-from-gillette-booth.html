Brady calls Packers-Patriots from Gillette booth

By Matt

Tom Brady returns to Gillette Stadium today to call Packers-Patriots from the FOX booth — his first Patriots game as a broadcaster — with both teams 5-3 and Drake Maye facing Jordan Love at 4:25 p.m. ET.

The setup

Tom Brady returns to Gillette Stadium today to call the Packers-Patriots game from the FOX booth at 4:25 p.m. ET. Both teams arrive 5-3, with Drake Maye and Jordan Love facing off in the late-afternoon national window.

It is FOX's America's Game of the Week, and the network is sending its top crew: Kevin Burkhardt on play-by-play, Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi on the sidelines, and Brady as the lead analyst. For Brady, it is the first time he has called a Patriots game at Gillette Stadium as a broadcaster.

Why this one matters

Brady spent 20 seasons with the Patriots and turned the franchise into the defining dynasty of the modern NFL. He won six Super Bowls in New England — XXXVI, XXXVIII, XXXIX, XLIX, LI, and LIII — and appeared in nine overall alongside Bill Belichick. He finished his Patriots tenure with 74,571 passing yards and 541 touchdown passes in the regular season, plus 30 playoff victories, before adding a seventh ring with Tampa Bay.

He has admitted this one will be emotional. Brady told reporters the assignment is a homecoming he has been thinking about since FOX announced it, and that covering his former team objectively — without sounding like he is talking about himself — is the hard part of the job.

This is also a full homecoming weekend in Foxborough. Rob Gronkowski, Brady's teammate for nine seasons and three of those Super Bowl wins, was inducted into the Patriots Hall of Fame on Saturday and will be honored at halftime. The Patriots are also hosting members of the Super Bowl LI championship team for a 10-year reunion.

The game itself

The football is worth the stage. Both teams are 5-3 and chasing stronger playoff positioning — New England second in the AFC East and unbeaten at home this season, Green Bay fourth in the NFC North and trying to improve its conference footing.

Maye and Love headline the matchup, two young quarterbacks who can stretch a defense downfield. The Patriots are trying to stay perfect at Gillette; the Packers are trying to win in Foxborough for the first time since 2002.
