Gibbs passes Barry Sanders for most rushing TDs before 25

By Matt

Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three touchdowns on Thanksgiving, and his first score passed Barry Sanders for the most rushing touchdowns by a player before turning 25 — in a 39-35 Lions loss to the Bears.

Jahmyr Gibbs ran for 205 yards and three touchdowns on 21 carries as the Detroit Lions fell 39-35 to the Chicago Bears on Thanksgiving at Ford Field — and his first score of the day put him past Barry Sanders in the record book. The 1-yard run early in the second quarter was the 53rd rushing touchdown of Gibbs' career, passing Sanders for the most by a player before turning 25.

Gibbs also caught five passes for 47 yards, giving him 252 yards from scrimmage on the day. It was his first career 200-yard rushing game — and his second 250-yard game from scrimmage, after the 252 he put up earlier this month against New England.

The record

Gibbs came into Thanksgiving tied with Sanders at 52 career rushing touchdowns, after scoring twice the previous Sunday in Detroit's win over Tampa Bay. One yard was all he needed to move clear.

The path to the mark:

2023 (rookie): 10

2024: 16

2025: 13

2026 (through 10 games): 13

Thanksgiving vs. Chicago: 3

Career total: 55

This is the second Sanders mark Gibbs has taken this season. In the win at Arizona, he passed Sanders for the most total touchdowns — rushing and receiving combined — before age 25, when his 58th career score broke the old mark of 57. Sanders finished his 10-year Lions career with 99 rushing touchdowns.

Gibbs does not turn 25 until March 20, 2027, so he has the rest of the regular season — and any playoff games — to keep adding to the record. The milestone score was also his 16th touchdown of the season, and he finished the day with 18 total touchdowns through 11 games. Since 2000, he is just the third player to record 16 or more touchdowns in three consecutive seasons, joining LaDainian Tomlinson (2003–07) and Shaun Alexander (2001–05).

A Thanksgiving record, in a loss

Gibbs' 205 rushing yards also broke Bob Hoernschemeyer's Lions record of 198 rushing yards in a Thanksgiving game. He did most of the damage after halftime, with 148 of the yards and two of the scores coming in the final two quarters, including a 32-yard run where he reversed direction to escape tacklers.

His third touchdown — a 70-yard sprint midway through the fourth quarter — gave Detroit a 35-24 lead with 8:20 to play. It looked like the game was over. It wasn't.

Chicago answered in three plays, with Caleb Williams finding Rome Odunze for a 41-yard touchdown and then running in the two-point conversion. Williams then engineered the final drive, hitting Odunze for a 6-yard touchdown in the back corner of the end zone with three seconds left — the Bears' first lead of the entire game.

The loss snapped Detroit's six-game winning streak and dropped the Lions to 8-3. Chicago won its sixth straight, improved to 9-2, and took sole possession of first place in the NFC North.

What it means

Gibbs is on a historic pace. Through 11 games he has 1,754 yards from scrimmage, projecting out to 2,711 over a 17-game season — which would be a single-season record. He leads the NFL in rushing yards and touchdowns, and he is doing it in a season where Detroit's offense is built around him.

The record is his even in a loss, and it is the kind of number that sticks: passing Barry Sanders in anything, in a Lions uniform, on Thanksgiving, in front of the home crowd. The win went to Chicago, but the day belonged to Gibbs — and with seven weeks left before his 25th birthday, the number is only going up.
