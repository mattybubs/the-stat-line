Fortnite's Treasure Hunters ends December 5 with the 'Out of Time' live event — and Treasure Week is your last shot at the gold

By Matt

Fortnite's Treasure Hunters mini season ends Saturday, December 5 with the newly announced 'Out of Time' live event at 2 p.m. ET — and Epic is giving players one final push first, with Treasure Week starting November 28 and tripled treasure odds in every chest.

Fortnite's Treasure Hunters season has an end date now, and a finale to match. Epic Games has confirmed the mini season wraps up on Saturday, December 5 with a live event called "Out of Time," kicking off at 2 p.m. ET / 7 p.m. UTC. Downtime follows the event, and Chapter 8 begins sometime after that. Before the curtain falls, Epic is running one last promotion to help players finish their collections: Treasure Week — also called Golden Week — starts Saturday, November 28 at 9 a.m. ET and runs right up to the event.

Treasure Hunters launched November 1 as a short Chapter 7 bridge season built around an 80-player island, Devil Fruit Mythics, and the One Piece crossover. It was always going to be a quick one, and the last real content drop already landed: Oscar's Captain's Day went live November 22 with a new boss, a Map Fragment, 17 sprites, and the Frenzy Auto Shotgun. Everything since has been staging for the finale, and today's update — v43.11, which went live this morning after downtime starting at 4 a.m. ET — is exactly that: a small patch that adds the files for the December 5 event and Golden Week, with no new weapons, no new sprites, and no map changes. The viral patch notes that circulated earlier in November, claiming Pirate Tokens were being removed from storm chests and the whole economy rebalanced, were fake. The real update is plumbing, and that is the point.

Treasure Week: triple the treasure, every gold reward back

Treasure Week works like the season's Treasure Hours, only stretched across the whole final week. The headline change is the treasure spawn rate in loot chests, which jumps from 1 in 62 to 1 in 21 — roughly three times as likely to unearth a treasure. Every gold reward from the season returns to the loot pool at the same time, so any gold skin variant, gold back bling, pickaxe, vehicle, jam track, or loading screen from the season can drop again. A bonus gold skin joins the pool too: gold Captain X becomes findable from November 28, alongside the returning gold A$AP Rocky, Lil Uzi Vert, Miku, and Kasane Teto skins.

The item-specific boosts are smaller than the headline number. Gold vehicles improve from 1 in 7,813 to 1 in 1,563, a fivefold jump, while gold skins move from 1 in 1,562,500 to 1 in 781,250. Pickaxe, back bling, jam track, loading screen, and gizmo odds stay where they were. Treasure Week also doubles Sprite Dust and Sprite XP all week, so the grind itself pays better even when the chest does not.

The update also added three free Solid Gold skins for players who do not want to chase chests at all. Battle Pass owners get the Gilded Charlotte-Sensei skin the next time they log in, anyone who buys the 2,400 V-Bucks bundle this season gets the Gilded Hunter Hope skin, and the Gilded Buccaneer Jones skin was available through Twitch Drops.

The Pirate Shop token still needs Treasure Hours

There is one catch, and it is the item most players actually want. The Pirate Shop Access token — the one that grants up to four weeks of free Item Shop goodies — does not get a week-long odds boost. Its drop rate only improves during Treasure Week's two Treasure Hours windows: Saturday, November 28 from 2 to 4 p.m. EST, and Saturday night from 9 to 11 p.m. EST.

Those two windows are the best shot anyone will get. During Treasure Hours the token's odds rise 1.5 times, from 1 in 137,500 to 1 in 91,667, while gold skin odds jump fifteenfold, from 1 in 1,562,500 to 1 in 104,167, and gold vehicles tenfold, from 1 in 7,813 to 1 in 781. After that, the season's treasure — token included — gets vaulted on December 5. The final Treasure Hunt Chest rewards land November 26, including the Solid Gold Captain X outfit, a Gold G-Wagon vehicle, and another round of Pirate Shop Access tokens, including a free 14-day token for 2027.

What happens December 5

The "Out of Time" event is the season's send-off and the bridge into Chapter 8. Epic has not said what the event itself contains — only the date and start time, and that downtime and Chapter 8 follow. Treasure Hunters kept Fortnite's player base active through the biggest launch week in gaming history, with GTA 6 dominating everything else, and the season's pirate-treasure loop clearly worked well enough to get a golden send-off. Whether Chapter 8 can match it is the open question; the treasure era ends in eleven days either way.
