Cavs Rally From 15 Down to Hand Wolves First Loss in OT

By Matt

Donovan Mitchell's tiebreaking jumper in overtime lifted Cleveland past Minnesota 127-125 after the Cavs erased a 15-point deficit — despite Anthony Edwards' season-high 47.

The result

Final (OT): Cleveland Cavaliers 127, Minnesota Timberwolves 125.

Cleveland rallied from 15 points down to hand Minnesota its first loss of the season, winning it in overtime.

The deciding shot

Donovan Mitchell's tiebreaking jumper in overtime lifted Cleveland past Minnesota. In a game that had already swung 15 points the other way, Mitchell delivered the shot that decided it.

Edwards' night — and why it wasn't enough

For the Timberwolves, Anthony Edwards poured in a season-high 47 points. It was the kind of night that usually wins a game on its own. Against this Cleveland comeback, it wasn't enough — the Cavs erased the deficit, forced overtime, and took the win.

Why it matters

• A 15-point comeback win is a statement early in the season, and it came against an unbeaten team.

• Edwards' 47 sets the bar for his season — and still ended in a loss, which says plenty about how Cleveland closed this one out.

• Minnesota's first loss comes with a rally attached: the Wolves had this game by 15 and watched it get away.
