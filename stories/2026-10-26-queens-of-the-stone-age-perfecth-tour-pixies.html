Queens of the Stone Age's 'Perfecth' Arrives Friday — Then a 2027 Tour With Pixies, Ending in Boston

By Matt

The band's ninth album 'Perfecth' lands October 30 via Matador, and the 2027 Perfecth World Tour runs May 1 in San Diego to May 24 at Boston's TD Garden with Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp in support.

Queens of the Stone Age are closing October with a new album and opening 2027 with a victory lap. "Perfecth," the band's ninth studio album, arrives Friday, October 30 via Matador Records — and the 2027 North American tour behind it ends at Boston's TD Garden on May 24.

The album

"Perfecth" runs nine tracks, produced by frontman Josh Homme and bassist Michael Shuman from sessions in Los Angeles and New Orleans. The deliberately warped title is the point, Homme has said: nothing in this world is perfect, yet everything seems to want to be.

Two singles are already out: "Easy Street" (featuring Nikki Lane) and "Insignificant Other." A third, "Where the Goth Girls At?", got its live debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on October 21. The album follows 2023's "In Times New Roman…" and, per the band, draws on kintsugi — the Japanese art of repairing broken pottery with gold — as it moves through different forms of love, loss and personal growth.

The tour

The 16-date U.S. leg of the Perfecth World Tour opens May 1 at Viejas Arena in San Diego and closes May 24 at TD Garden in Boston, with Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp supporting every date. Stops along the way include Los Angeles' Kia Forum, Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, and Milwaukee's American Family Insurance Amphitheater.

• Artist presale: October 28 at 10 a.m. local (registration was required by October 25)

• General sale: October 29 at 10 a.m. local

• HalloQueen events: October 31 at record stores worldwide, with exclusive merch and a limited-edition "Perfecth" picture disc

Why it matters

This is a band's band making a band's-band move: a deliberately imperfect album title, a tour with Pixies — themselves celebrating 40 years since forming in 1986 — and a Boston finale that puts the record in front of one of the country's loudest rooms. The presale window is already closing; the general sale opens Thursday.

Sources: Pollstar, Lambgoat, Far Out Magazine
