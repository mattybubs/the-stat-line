Rays Walk Off Brewers in 12 Innings to Win Game 4 and Force Game 5

By Matt

Ben Williamson's walk-off single in the bottom of the 12th gave Tampa Bay a 4-3 Game 4 win over Milwaukee, denying the Brewers a sweep and forcing a Game 5.

The result

Final (12 innings): Tampa Bay Rays 4, Milwaukee Brewers 3.

The Rays staved off elimination, avoided the sweep, and forced a Game 5. Milwaukee still leads the World Series 3-1, but the champagne stays on ice.

How Game 4 was set up

Brandon Woodruff was supposed to open Game 4 for Milwaukee until manager Pat Murphy used him in the ninth inning on Monday, leaving Dustin May as the call. Tampa Bay countered with right-hander Griffin Jax.

How it unfolded

2nd: Chandler Simpson doubled home Jonny DeLuca — Rays 1-0

4th: Cooper Pratt doubled home Sal Frelick — tied 1-1

4th: Junior Caminero scored on a David Hamilton throwing error — Rays 2-1

5th: David Hamilton solo home run — tied 2-2

12th: Cooper Pratt solo home run — Brewers 3-2

12th: Jonny DeLuca single scored Cedric Mullins — tied 3-3

12th: Ben Williamson walk-off single — Rays 4-3

The game went to extra innings tied 2-2, and it stayed that way until the 12th. Pratt's go-ahead homer in the top of the 12th looked like the clincher — Milwaukee was three outs from its first World Series title. Instead, Tampa Bay answered in the bottom half: Junior Caminero reached on a throwing error by Brewers third baseman Joey Ortiz, Liam Hicks singled, DeLuca tied it, and Williamson won it with a chopper over the first base bag with the infield drawn in.

Why it matters

• Tampa Bay's season survives: down 3-0, the Rays had to win, and they did it in the most dramatic way possible.

• Milwaukee's sweep bid is denied. The Brewers still lead 3-1 and still need just one win, but they will have to wait at least one more game.

• Game 5 is Wednesday at Tropicana Field. If Tampa Bay wins again, the series goes back to Milwaukee for Games 6 and 7.
