ARC Raiders' PvE Test Logged 30 Million Rounds — and Embark Called It 'Polarizing'

By Matt

Embark's week-long PvE test closed October 20 with more than 30 million rounds played — and a player survey going out now that sets up the November decision on whether PvE stays.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The numbers

Embark Studios' week-long PvE test in ARC Raiders closed on October 20, and the participation was massive: more than 30 million rounds were played during the test window.

But Embark called the experiment "polarizing" — a telling word choice. Thirty million rounds of engagement didn't settle the question; it sharpened it.

What's next

A survey for a representative sample of players starts going out now, and its results will set up Embark's decision in November on whether PvE becomes a permanent fixture in the extraction shooter.

The takeaway

Thirty million rounds in a week is a signal — but Embark's own framing says the signal cuts both ways. PvE purists showed up in force; the question is whether that volume represents the game's future or a novelty spike. November's decision is now the biggest content call in ARC Raiders' life.
