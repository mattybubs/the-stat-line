Dove Launches Its First-Ever Fine Fragrance in a One-Time Social Drop

By Matt

Dove Original Fragrance bottles the scent of the Original Beauty Bar, on sale today at noon ET as a one-time, limited-quantity drop for $19.57.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The drop

Dove Original Fragrance goes on sale today, October 27, at noon ET. It is a one-time, limited-quantity drop — when this run is gone, that is the point of the release.

Announced October 22, this is Dove's first-ever fine fragrance, and it bottles the scent of the Original Beauty Bar.

The price is the history

The drop costs $19.57 — a nod to 1957, the year the Dove Beauty Bar launched.

Why it matters

• A first for Dove: the brand has never done a fine fragrance before.

• A one-time social drop, not a permanent shelf launch — limited quantity by design.

• The price itself tells the brand story, tying the new bottle back to the 1957 original.
