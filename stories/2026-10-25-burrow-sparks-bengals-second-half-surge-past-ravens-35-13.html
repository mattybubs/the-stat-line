Burrow Sparks Bengals' Second-Half Surge Past Ravens 35-13

By Matt

Down 13-0 at halftime, Joe Burrow threw four second-half touchdown passes as Cincinnati ripped off 35 unanswered points to stun Baltimore 35-13.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

For a half, it looked like a lost afternoon. Then Joe Burrow and the Cincinnati Bengals flipped the game completely, scoring 35 unanswered points to beat the Baltimore Ravens 35-13 on Sunday in Baltimore (Reuters / NBC Sports).

The first half: all Ravens

Baltimore dominated the opening 30 minutes and still led only 13-0 at halftime. Burrow threw touchdown passes to Mike Gesicki and Tee Higgins to spark the second-half surge, and from there Cincinnati never stopped scoring.

The surge, drive by drive

3rd: 13-7; Burrow 6-yard TD pass to Mike Gesicki

3rd: 14-13; Burrow 13-yard TD pass to Tee Higgins

4th: 21-13; Samaje Perine 1-yard TD run

4th: 28-13; Burrow 2-yard TD pass to Ja'Marr Chase

4th: 35-13; Burrow 16-yard TD pass to Drew Sample

Burrow completed 16 of 18 passes for 249 yards in the second half alone, finishing 22-of-30 for 292 yards and four touchdowns. Tee Higgins caught six passes for 148 yards and a score.

The other side

Baltimore, playing without two-time NFL MVP Lamar Jackson (ankle, his third straight missed game), got a career-high 298 yards from backup Tyler Huntley — but Huntley also threw the interception that helped Cincinnati pull away, and the Ravens never scored again after halftime. Baltimore has now blown a double-digit lead for the second straight week.

Final score: Bengals 35, Ravens 13

Halftime score: Ravens 13, Bengals 0

Burrow: 22/30, 292 yards, 4 TD

Second-half Burrow: 16/18, 249 yards, 4 TD

Records: Bengals 4-2, Ravens 4-3
