Brewers Rout Rays 10-1 to Take 3-0 World Series Lead

By Matt

Milwaukee turned a 2-0 game into a 10-1 Game 3 rout with an eight-run fifth inning, and now stands one win from its first World Series title.

The result

The Milwaukee Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 10-1 in Game 3 of the World Series on Monday, October 26, at Tropicana Field, taking a 3-0 series lead. Milwaukee is now one win from the first World Series championship in franchise history.

Sources for the final outcome: USA Today Game 3 coverage and the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel live updates.

How it started

Christian Yelich and William Contreras delivered RBI singles in the third inning to put Milwaukee up 2-0 over Tampa Bay in Game 3. Robert Gasser had thrown just 44 pitches through four innings for the Brewers, keeping the game firmly in Milwaukee's control early.

The fifth inning that broke it open

Milwaukee scored eight runs in the top of the fifth inning — all with two outs — turning a 2-0 game into a 10-0 rout. Per USA Today's Game 3 coverage, it was a World Series first: eight runs with two outs in a single inning, built on six consecutive run-scoring hits.

Jackson Chourio staked his claim as a series star with four hits in the game, including a pair of doubles, giving him eight hits in the three games. William Contreras added three more hits, and his grounder in the fifth — with Tampa Bay throwing late to second for a force instead of to first — extended the inning and kick-started the rally.

The pitching line

Gasser became the first Brewer to complete six innings this entire postseason, per USA Today, and tied a postseason record by winning all four of his games on the road. Brandon Woodruff closed it out in the ninth in his World Series debut, allowing Tampa Bay's only run on two hits with one strikeout — the Rays' first baserunner since the opening hitter of the game, per the Journal Sentinel's live updates.

What 3-0 means

On 25 occasions, teams have taken a 3-0 lead in the World Series. All 25 won the championship, per the Journal Sentinel. Game 4 is Tuesday night in Tampa Bay, with Milwaukee one win from a title.
