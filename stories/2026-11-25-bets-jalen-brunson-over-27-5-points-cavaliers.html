TSL Bets: Jalen Brunson OVER 27.5 points vs. Cavaliers (-130)

By Matt

Jalen Brunson is averaging 27.9 points over his last 10 and 29.0 a game against Cleveland this season, and the Cavaliers are 22nd in defensive rating and missing Evan Mobley. The play: Brunson OVER 27.5 points (-130, FanDuel) on Wednesday night.

The game slip

Cleveland Cavaliers (9-9) at New York Knicks (11-6)

Wednesday, 7:30 PM ET — Madison Square Garden, New York, NY

• Spread: Knicks -3.5

• Total: 220.5

• Moneyline: Knicks -163 / Cavaliers +137

• Odds source: DraftKings via Stat Sniper / USA Today Sportsbook Wire, November 25

Injury report — Knicks: F OG Anunoby OUT (calf). G Jordan Clarkson OUT (foot). C Karl-Anthony Towns QUESTIONABLE (knee). G Josh Hart QUESTIONABLE (shoulder). G Miles McBride QUESTIONABLE (adductor).

Injury report — Cavaliers: F/C Evan Mobley OUT (toe sprain).

Storylines: Brunson is the Knicks' engine against a team he has already solved twice this season: 29.0 points a game on 50.1% shooting in two games against Cleveland, 27.9 over his last 10, and he has cleared 27.5 in 6 of those 10. He is playing 35.9 minutes a game over his last five at a 31.6% usage rate, and OPTIMAL projects him for 28.3 tonight. Cleveland ranks 22nd in the league in defensive rating (115.8), and with Mobley — its Defensive Player of the Year — out with a toe sprain, there is nobody to cut off Brunson's driving lanes. The Knicks are on a five-game win streak, and the spread has slid from -5.5 to -3.5 since Anunoby was ruled out, but the injuries only push more of the offense through Brunson: if Towns sits, his usage climbs further. The total sits at 220.5, and the Knicks are expected to do most of the scoring.

The bet

Jalen Brunson OVER 27.5 points (-130, FanDuel)

Why this prop

The trend and the matchup point the same way. Brunson has cleared 27.5 in 6 of his last 10, is playing nearly 36 minutes a night, and shoots 50.1% against this exact opponent. Cleveland is soft defensively (22nd in the league), down its best defender, and the Knicks are thin enough that the ball stays in Brunson's hands. At a near-even price, this is the props-first single by the book: a player prop with a real trend edge, a real matchup edge, and no chalk to pay.

The honest caveats

The line is doing real work — Brunson's season average is 26.5, below the number, and he has cleared 27.5 in only 6 of his last 10, so this is closer to a coin flip than a lock. He scored just 22 against Indiana on Sunday. The Knicks are banged up, and if Towns, Hart and McBride all sit, Cleveland can load up on Brunson with no second scorer to worry about. And the Knicks are 3.5-point home favorites on a five-game win streak: if they pull away early, the fourth quarter belongs to the bench. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.
