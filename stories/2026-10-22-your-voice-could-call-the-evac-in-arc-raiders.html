Your Voice Could Call the Evac in ARC Raiders

By Matt

Embark Studios opened the Speranza Security Recruitment drive — ARC Raiders players can record the voice lines of the ballistic pod operators running the Pendola Pass evacuation, and winners get their voices in the game across the Rust Belt.

Your voice, in the game

Embark Studios opened the Speranza Security Recruitment drive, inviting ARC Raiders players to record the voice lines of ballistic pod operators running the Pendola Pass evacuation.

Winners get their voices in the game across the Rust Belt, plus Raider Tokens for the trouble.

Why this is a smart move

Community voice casting is one of the cheapest ways to make players feel like owners of a game. The winning voices won't just sit in a credits scroll — they'll be the actual evac operators players hear during extractions across the Rust Belt, which is the highest-traffic audio real estate in the whole game.

And the Raider Tokens sweeten it: it's a real prize for a real job of work, not just exposure.
