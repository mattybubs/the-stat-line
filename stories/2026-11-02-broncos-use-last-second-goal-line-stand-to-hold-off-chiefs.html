Broncos Use Last-Second Goal-Line Stand to Hold Off Chiefs

By Matt

Denver scored 15 straight points in the fourth quarter to turn a 24-13 deficit into a 28-24 lead, then stopped Kansas City at the goal line in the final seconds — Malcolm Roach sacking Patrick Mahomes on the last play.

Denver did not just come back — it closed the door. The Broncos scored 15 straight points in the fourth quarter to turn a 24-13 deficit into a 28-24 lead, then held Kansas City out of the end zone in the final seconds to seal a win that flips the AFC West picture.

The fourth-quarter surge

Down 24-13, Denver ripped off 15 unanswered points in the fourth quarter. That run turned an 11-point deficit into a 28-24 lead — and it set up a finish where every yard mattered.

Fourth-quarter run: 15 straight points

Deficit erased: 24-13 down → 28-24 up

Final score: Broncos 28, Chiefs 24

The stand

Kansas City still had one last shot. The Chiefs reached Denver's 4-yard line with 35 seconds left, one score away from taking the game back. The Broncos held — and on the final play, Malcolm Roach sacked Patrick Mahomes to end it.

That is the kind of defensive stand that defines a division race: a goal-line stop, in the final seconds, against the team that has owned the AFC West.

Why it matters

A win like this does two things at once. It caps a fourth-quarter comeback with a defensive exclamation point, and it flips the AFC West — the Broncos took the game, and with it the momentum in the division, straight from the Chiefs.
