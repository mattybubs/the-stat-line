No disc, no problem: GTA 6 is the UK's biggest physical launch since 2019 — £32 million in three days

By Matt

Grand Theft Auto 6 took an estimated £32 million through UK shops in its first three days — the country's biggest physical game launch since FIFA 20 in 2019, and the 17th biggest since records began. The boxes contain a download code, not a disc. Nobody cared.

The box doesn't have a disc in it. It never did — Rockstar confirmed months ago that GTA 6's physical edition is a download code in a box, and collectors spent the whole autumn arguing about whether that even counts as a physical copy.

The UK just answered. Grand Theft Auto 6 took an estimated £32 million through British shops in its first three days on sale, according to market tracker NielsenIQ, as first reported by The Game Business. That makes it the UK's biggest physical game launch since FIFA 20 in September 2019 — and the 17th biggest physical launch in the country since records began in 1995.

The week the high street came back

GTA 6 alone accounted for about 74% of all physical software revenue in the UK during launch week. Total boxed software revenue hit an estimated £43 million for the week — up 409% on the week before, and the strongest week for physical games since Christmas 2018.

Launch day did most of the damage. NielsenIQ says 88% of the game's first-week boxed sales came on Thursday, November 19 itself, and that single day ranks No. 20 among all UK games retail days since records began in 2000.

The launch pulled hardware with it, too: UK console sales rose 38% week on week to their highest level since late 2025.

It wasn't just Britain

The same pattern played out across Europe. In France, more than one million boxed copies sold in the first three days. In Spain, GTA 6 is the biggest physical retail launch in the history of the country's chart. The Game Business first reported all three figures from NielsenIQ data.

That matters because the no-disc decision was supposed to be the risk. Physical collectors called the code-in-a-box edition a glorified gift card. PlayStation, for its part, is reportedly planning to go completely digital from 2028 — and GTA 6's launch is now the strongest evidence yet that the audience will follow the box even when the disc is gone. The premium edition, at around £89.99 against £69.99 for the standard edition, didn't slow anything down either.

The record that keeps growing

This is the third distinct sales record GTA 6 has set in its first week, and each one measures something different. Take-Two confirmed $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold before release. Sony said more than 39 million unique players jumped in on PS5 in the first four days, the biggest launch in PlayStation history. And an independent estimate published Friday puts the first week at roughly 53 million copies worldwide.

The UK number is the one that tells you something about how people actually buy games. Digital dominates the conversation, and the global estimates are dominated by digital pre-orders. But in Britain, in three days, a box with no disc inside outsold every physical launch of the last seven years — in a market where physical has been written off for a decade.

The collectors were wrong about the demand. The only question left is whether other publishers read this as permission to drop the disc too.
