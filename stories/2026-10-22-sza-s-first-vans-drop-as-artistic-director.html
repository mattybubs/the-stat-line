SZA's First Vans Drop Arrives as the Brand's First-Ever Artistic Director

By Matt

VANSZA Drop 1 is on sale now at Vans retailers and Vans.com — SZA's first product since being named the brand's first artistic director, reworking the Knu Skool Mule and Authentic Kickdown into outdoor-inspired gear.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

VANSZA lands

Vansza Drop 1 went on sale today at Vans retailers and Vans.com. It's SZA's first product since she was named Vans' first-ever artistic director last year — the first real test of what that title actually means.

What's in the drop

The two-part collection reworks skate classics into outdoor-inspired gear:

• Knu Skool Mule — the chunky skate silhouette, slip-on reworked

• Authentic Kickdown — the classic Authentics, outdoors-ified

• Outdoor-inspired apparel — gear to match the rugged reworks

Drop 2 follows on April 8, 2027.

The takeaway

Celebrity sneaker collabs are usually a colorway swap and a press photo. This one's built around actual silhouettes being re-engineered — a mule and a kickdown instead of just another limited colorway. Whether Drop 1 moves like a product or a press release, SZA's first swing as artistic director is bigger in ambition than most.
