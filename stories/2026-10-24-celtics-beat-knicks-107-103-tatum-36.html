Celtics Bounce Back, Beat the Champs: Tatum's 36 Lifts Boston Past the Knicks 107-103

By Matt

Jayson Tatum poured in 36 points with 9 rebounds and 7 assists as Boston beat the reigning champion Knicks 107-103 in its home opener at TD Garden.

Boston's home opener delivered. The Celtics beat the reigning NBA champion New York Knicks 107-103 on Friday night at TD Garden, bouncing back from their opening-night loss to Detroit and evening their record at 1-1.

Tatum's bounce-back night

Jayson Tatum led the way with 36 points, 9 rebounds, 7 assists and 3 steals. After a flat opener, the star forward was the best player on the floor — and he did it against the champs, a team built on late-game comebacks that Boston refused to let happen.

The supporting cast showed up

Paul George added 15 points and 7 rebounds, Derrick White chipped in 12 points and 6 assists, and Neemias Queta gave Boston 15 points, 6 rebounds and 3 blocks off the bench. Boston also won the possession battle — 87 shot attempts to New York's 80 — and forced 18 Knicks turnovers while committing just 11.

Why it matters

Beating the champs in your own building, two nights after blowing your opener, is a statement about who this team thinks it is. Boston held off a team famous for coming back late, and did it despite losing the rebounding battle 44-37. Next up for the Celtics: the Chicago Bulls visit on Monday.
