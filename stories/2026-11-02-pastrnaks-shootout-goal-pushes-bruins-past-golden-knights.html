Pastrnak's Shootout Goal Pushes Bruins Past Golden Knights

By Matt

David Pastrnak scored the shootout's only goal to give Boston a 3-2 win over Vegas at TD Garden, after the Golden Knights rallied from two goals down to force the shootout.

One shot decided it. David Pastrnak scored the shootout's only goal to give Boston a 3-2 win over Vegas at TD Garden on Monday night.

The comeback that forced extra hockey

Vegas would not go quietly. The Golden Knights rallied from two goals down to force the shootout, erasing Boston's lead and pushing the game past regulation. That comeback set the stage for a shootout where a single goal would be the difference.

Pastrnak delivers the difference

In the shootout, Pastrnak provided that difference. His goal was the shootout's only one — the lone finish in the tiebreaker — and it handed Boston the extra point at home over Vegas.

Final score: Bruins 3, Golden Knights 2 (shootout)

Decisive play: David Pastrnak — the shootout's only goal

Location: TD Garden, Boston

When: Monday night

How it got there: Vegas rallied from two goals down to force the shootout

Why it matters

Games like this one are won by the team that holds its nerve last. Boston built a two-goal lead, watched Vegas claw all the way back, and still found the winner when it mattered — with its star delivering the shootout's only goal.
