Bath & Body Works Launches Season of Wonder with Zooey Deschanel

By Matt

Bath & Body Works kicks off the holidays with Season of Wonder, starring Zooey Deschanel — led by the new Vanilla Cocoa Flurries scent.

The launch

Bath & Body Works launches Season of Wonder with Zooey Deschanel, kicking off its holiday season with a new campaign and a new scent leading the lineup.

The new scent

The new Vanilla Cocoa Flurries scent blends white cocoa, chilled coconut milk and vanilla woods — a cozy, winter-ready combination built for the holiday season.

A returning favorite, in a new form

Snowflakes & Cashmere returns as an eau de parfum for the first time — a notable upgrade for a returning favorite, giving fans a more concentrated way to wear it this holiday season.

The lineup at a glance

Vanilla Cocoa Flurries: New scent: white cocoa, chilled coconut milk, vanilla woods

Snowflakes & Cashmere: Returns as an eau de parfum for the first time
