Where to look

The clues aren't in a trailer or a patch-notes blog — they're on the world map itself. The corner near Volcano Rise has been dressed with notes, salvaged items and photographs, all pointing toward the next map. If you've been skipping past that corner of the map screen, that's the spot to check first.

The telling clue

The telling clue is a sketch of a destroyed sea wall. One image does a lot of work: a sea wall means a coast, and a destroyed one means the shoreline in Riven Tides isn't going to be a pretty beach — it's a broken edge, the kind of place ARC Raiders does best.

What it points to

Taken together, the notes, salvaged items, photographs and the sea-wall sketch point to a coastal map for the Riven Tides update. The Flashpoint update is doing the teasing in-game, not just in marketing — the next map is already hiding in the map you play on today.
