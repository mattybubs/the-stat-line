Atelier Cologne Launches Oolang Clair-Obscur

By Matt

Atelier Cologne's new Oolang Clair-Obscur joins the brand's Collection Rare, with perfumer Daniela Andrier pairing luminous yuzu with smoky oolong tea and leather.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The launch

Atelier Cologne has launched Oolang Clair-Obscur, a new unisex fragrance in the brand's Collection Rare. It follows Oolang Infini and Oolang Monochrome in the Oolang line, and it is available in a 100 ml Eau de Parfum.

The scent

Perfumer Daniela Andrier pairs luminous yuzu with smoky oolong tea and leather in what the brand calls a harmony of contrasts — bright citrus against smoke and sensual leather. The name says it plainly: clair-obscur, light and dark in the same bottle.

Yuzu: Luminous, bright citrus opening

Oolong tea: Smoky heart

Leather: Sensual, grounding base

Where it sits in the line

Oolang Clair-Obscur is the third chapter of the Oolang story in Collection Rare, after Oolang Infini and Oolang Monochrome. Where its predecessors explored the tea note on its own terms, Clair-Obscur frames it as a contrast — the brightness of yuzu played directly against smoke and leather.
