Mikheyev scores twice as Lightning beat Blackhawks 4-1 to stay perfect at home

By Matt

Ilya Mikheyev scored twice against his former club and Andrei Vasilevskiy made 25 saves in his 600th start as Tampa Bay extended its franchise-record home winning streak to 8-0-0.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The Tampa Bay Lightning beat the Chicago Blackhawks 4-1 on Thursday night at Benchmark International Arena, staying perfect at home to start the season and extending a franchise-record home winning streak to 8-0-0.

Ilya Mikheyev scored twice against his former club, and Andrei Vasilevskiy made 25 saves in the 600th start of his career.

How the game played out

Victor Hedman opened the scoring on the power play at 4:12 of the first period, working a give-and-go with Anthony Cirelli and driving the slot to bury his fourth goal of the season. Mikheyev doubled the lead with 1:38 left in the period, finishing a long shot from above the left circle for his third goal of the season.

The second period was scoreless — and physical. Chicago's Dillon Boucher and Tampa Bay's Jeff Viel dropped the gloves in a heavyweight bout early in the frame, and the Blackhawks kept out-hitting the Lightning, finishing the night with a 31-13 edge in hits.

Tampa Bay put it away early in the third. Ryan McDonagh came off the bench on a line change and belted a 93 mph slap shot at 2:38 for a 3-0 lead, and Mikheyev tipped in his second of the night at 5:08 to make it 4-0. Roman Kantserov spoiled Vasilevskiy's shutout bid with 49 seconds left, extending his point streak to seven games with his rookie-leading eighth goal of the season.

Vasilevskiy's 600th start

The milestone was the quiet headline of the night. Vasilevskiy's 600th career start puts him alongside Sergei Bobrovsky (802) and Connor Hellebuyck (618) as the only active goaltenders to reach the mark. He improved to 15-1-0 in his career against Chicago, and he was 49 seconds from a shutout before Kantserov's late tip-in.

Why it matters

Tampa Bay is now 10-3-2 overall and 8-0-0 at home — a franchise-record home winning streak to open a season. The Lightning controlled the game where it counted: 35 shots to Chicago's 25, two goals from Mikheyev against the team that let him go, and a milestone night from their franchise goalie.

Chicago, meanwhile, keeps sinking on the road. The Blackhawks fell to 0-7-1 away from home and 4-12-1 overall, swept in back-to-back games in Florida after Wednesday's 5-3 loss to the Panthers. Their four-game stretch features matchups against six of the past seven Stanley Cup winners — and it continues Saturday at Carolina, while the Lightning open a four-game trip at Minnesota the same night.
