Lenny Kravitz is HITMAN's new client — Bawma brings a voodoo-doll hit to Ambrose Island

By Matt

Lenny Kravitz lends his voice and likeness to Bawma, the Pirate King of Aleph from 007 First Light, in HITMAN's new Elusive Target The Mutiny — free from today through December 24, with a twist: this time, the celebrity is the client, not the target.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Agent 47 has a new client, and for once, the client is doing the briefing himself. Lenny Kravitz makes his World of Assassination debut as Bawma — the Pirate King of Aleph from IO Interactive's 007 First Light — in The Mutiny, a new Elusive Target mission live now in HITMAN World of Assassination through December 24. Kravitz lends both his likeness and his voice to the character, and the twist is the whole point: this time, the celebrity isn't the target. He's the one giving the orders.

Bawma is hiding out on Ambrose Island, and 47's job starts with finding him in the island's ruins and jungles and making contact. From there, Bawma directs the operation himself, staying in 47's ear throughout the mission — a first for the series, where Diana Burnwood normally runs the briefing. The targets are three of Bawma's own rogue lieutenants: Horace Stockton, Oswald Spoon, and Zui. They've turned on the Pirate King, and he wants them dealt with.

The voodoo doll

The signature way to take them down is pure HITMAN. Track down Madame Serafine on the island, gather the ritual ingredients, add them to her Ritual Bowl, and she'll hand you a Voodoo Doll. Then "kill" the doll — stab it, drown it, shoot it, blow it up, throw something sharp at it, set it on fire, or electrocute it — and the target goes down with it. It's one of the most creative elimination methods the series has ever offered, and it fits the mission's bright, upbeat, easygoing holiday tone — one of the most laid-back contracts in the World of Assassination, by IO's own description.

Bawma himself is a long way from a standard HITMAN target. The backstory IO lays out: a former child soldier who built Aleph, a sprawling black-market hub in the Western Hemisphere, into his personal kingdom. He commands fear and respect in equal measure — and he's paid the Agency generously, on one condition: he directs the operation himself.

Free, and what stays after December 24

The Mutiny is free for every HITMAN World of Assassination player, and newcomers can play it through the Free Demo — no full purchase needed. It's live globally on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, ROG Xbox Ally, PC, and Switch, starting today through Thursday, December 24, 2026.

As with all Elusive Targets, the mission itself leaves the game when it expires. But players who own HITMAN World of Assassination or the Ambrose Island location keep permanent access to The Retaliator, the Arcade version of the mission, plus its Special Assignment, for free. And anyone who completes The Mutiny with an IOI Account while it's live unlocks the Rock Royalty Duck — a regal rubber duck in flashy sunglasses that detonates when it detects motion.

The mission arrives as part of the Season of Bawma, a new roadmap that also brings a fresh Escalation, new Arcade and Special Assignment contracts, Featured Contracts, a new Twitch Drop, returning Elusive Targets, and holiday events. Launching alongside it is the Rockstar Pack, a $5.99 collection of outfits, weapons, gear, and Freelancer Safehouse cosmetics inspired by rock 'n' roll icons — the World Tour Tee, the Rockabilly Suit, the Wildcard Suit, the Wild Rose Baller, the R0-AR Wildcat, a Drum Stick, a Guitar Pick, the Powerchord-A Guitar, and a Pyrotechnic Device.

It's a neat piece of cross-pollination from a studio that now owns both franchises: the breakout character from its James Bond game walks straight into its assassination sandbox, and for once, he's on 47's side. You've got until Christmas Eve to take the contract.
