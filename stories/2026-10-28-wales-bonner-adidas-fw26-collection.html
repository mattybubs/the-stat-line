adidas Originals by Wales Bonner FW26 Drops Tomorrow: Croc Karintha, Green Gazelle, Pony BW

By Matt

The adidas Originals by Wales Bonner Fall/Winter 2026 collection drops globally October 29 — croc-stamped Karintha and Gazelle, an all-suede Adizero Adios, pony-hair BW Army pairs, and a bark leather blouson at the center of the apparel.

The drop

adidas Originals and Wales Bonner are back for Fall/Winter 2026, and this season Grace Wales Bonner revisits her own collaborative archive. The collection drops globally on Thursday, October 29, on the adidas website, through Wales Bonner, and at select retailers.

The shoes

• WB Karintha, dark brown croc-stamped leather — the signature silhouette Wales Bonner designed herself (not an archive pull) gets a sartorial upgrade: high-gloss crocodile-stamped leather, black leather Three Stripes, brushed gold hardware.

• WB Gazelle Snake, vivid green crocodile treatment — the indoor-football silhouette in glossy green croc leather with off-white stripes and a bright blue lining.

• WB Adizero Adios, all-suede "Wonder White" — the marathon racing flat rebuilt as a lifestyle shoe, cream suede with craft-focused custom Three Stripes stitching.

• WB BW Pony (Army Karintha pairs) — the minimal silhouette wrapped in pony hair with contrasting lizard-print overlays, in black and khaki colorways.

Pricing: $220 for the Gazelle, $200 each for the BW Army pairs and the Adizero Adios, all in unisex sizing.

The apparel

A supple leather blouson in Wales Bonner's signature bark hue, finished with handcrafted crochet stripes, anchors the clothing — alongside double-knit jersey tracksuits, pinstriped track sets, collared football jerseys, stirrup track pants, and neoprene bags embossed with crocodile panels. The campaign, shot by Harley Weir, draws on 1980s London youth culture.

Why it matters

• This is the partnership's archive era: six years in, Wales Bonner is remixing her own adidas work rather than just reworking the Three Stripes back catalog.

• The Karintha remains the one to watch — the only shoe in the line she drew from scratch, now dressed in its most premium material yet.

• The BW Army/GAT surge is real, and the pony-hair pairs are the collection's most wearable statement.

Sources: adidas and Wales Bonner Drop FW26 Collaboration (Hypebae); Wales Bonner adidas Fall 2026 Collection (Sneaker Bar Detroit); Wales Bonner x adidas Fall/Winter 2026: The Full Collection (Sneakerjagers).
