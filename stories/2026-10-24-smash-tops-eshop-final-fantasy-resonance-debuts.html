Smash Ultimate Back on Top of the eShop as Final Fantasy Resonance Debuts Twice in the Top 10

By Matt

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate retakes No. 1 on the Switch eShop for the week ending October 24, while Final Fantasy Resonance lands at No. 5 — and its Digital Deluxe Edition at No. 8.

Super Smash Bros. Ultimate is No. 1 on the Nintendo Switch eShop again. The latest weekly chart, for the week ending October 24, 2026, has Smash retaking the summit from Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, which slips to No. 2.

The top 10

1. Super Smash Bros. Ultimate

2. Castlevania: Belmont's Curse

3. Minecraft

4. Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream

5. Final Fantasy Resonance

6. Mario Kart 8 Deluxe

7. Super Mario Party Jamboree

8. Final Fantasy Resonance Digital Deluxe Edition

9. Warrior Cats: Clans of the Forest – Feral Edition

10. Pokemon FireRed

Resonance's split debut

The week's major new release, Final Fantasy Resonance, did something unusual: it charted twice. The standard edition debuted at No. 5, and the Digital Deluxe Edition landed at No. 8 — meaning a chunk of players paid up front for the premium version, giving the game a bigger launch-week footprint than a single top-10 spot suggests. The real test is next week, when launch-week pre-orders and day-one buyers are out of the picture.

The old guard holds

Smash on top, Minecraft at No. 3, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe at No. 6 — the Switch catalog's evergreens aren't going anywhere. Belmont's Curse falling to No. 2 isn't a collapse, either: its Midnight Edition also sits at No. 19, so it's still selling in two forms at once.
