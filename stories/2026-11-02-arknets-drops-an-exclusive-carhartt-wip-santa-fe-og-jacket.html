Piece: Carhartt WIP x ARKnets Santa Fe OG JKT

Availability: Exclusive — not in the mainline collection

Carhartt WIP's Santa Fe is already a workwear staple, so an exclusive colorway on it is a low-risk, high-appeal drop: the silhouette is proven, and the only way to get this gray is this release.
