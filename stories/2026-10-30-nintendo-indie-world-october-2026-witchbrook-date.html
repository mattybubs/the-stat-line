Nintendo's October Indie World Finally Dates Witchbrook — and Loads Up Switch 2's 2027

By Matt

Nintendo's 15-minute October Indie World showcase on Oct. 28 packed in 23 games: Witchbrook finally has a date (Jan. 28, 2027), Slime Rancher 2 and R.E.P.O. are coming to Switch 2, and four demos dropped the same day.

After years of "someday," Nintendo's October Indie World showcase finally gave the cozy crowd a date — and gave Switch 2 owners a 2027 calendar worth getting excited about.

The 15-minute presentation on Oct. 28 spotlighted 23 indie games for Switch and Switch 2. Here's what actually matters from it.

Witchbrook is real, and it's dated

The headline, by a mile: Witchbrook, Chucklefish's magical life sim that has been in development so long it became a running joke, launches January 28, 2027 on Switch 2 and Switch — plus PC, PS5 and Xbox, with Xbox Game Pass availability on day one, per Chucklefish.

You play the newest resident witch in the seaside town of Mossport, attend Witchbrook College, make friends, find love, and build a life — solo or with up to four players online. For a game that has lived in teaser trailers for years, a firm date is the news.

Switch 2's 2027 is stacking up

• Slime Rancher 2 arrives on Switch 2 in January 2027, continuing Beatrix LeBeau's adventures on Rainbow Island, with a Switch 2-exclusive in-game item.

• R.E.P.O., the physics-based co-op horror looter that took PC by surprise, is coming to Switch 2 in 2027 — procedurally generated haunted houses, fragile ceramics, heavy pianos, and teamwork that can collapse in seconds.

• Super Battle Golf — fairway sabotage, co-op chaos — lands March 25, 2027.

• Spirit Crossing, a multiplayer home-building game above the clouds from the makers of Cozy Grove, arrives November 16.

Out now, and free to try

Sword of the Sea, from the team behind Abzu and Journey, hit Switch 2 on October 29. And four demos dropped on the eShop the day of the showcase: the paranormal co-op puppy adventure Haunted Paws, the pixel-powered open-world RPG Petal Runner, the roguelike coin-pusher RACCOIN: Chaos Max Edition, and the newly revealed action-adventure Winds of Viridia (which also heads to PC in early 2027).

Thirteen of the 23 games also come to the original Switch; the other ten, including Tenjutsu and Slime Rancher 2, are Switch 2 only.

Why it matters

Indie World showcases are usually a pleasant blur. This one wasn't: it closed the book on one of indie gaming's longest-running "when?" questions, put two proven hits on Switch 2's 2027 slate, and gave players something to play — demos and a shadow-adjacent launch — the same week. Witchbrook's date alone makes this one of the better Indie Worlds in a while.
