Stage 2 is set at the PGL Singapore Major — 100 Thieves take the last spot as NAVI, G2 and Astralis enter

By Matt

Stage 1 is done in Singapore: 100 Thieves, Imperial and Lynn Vision won the last three deciders on Friday, and the eight survivors join NAVI, G2, Astralis and five more invited teams in Stage 2 starting Saturday.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Stage 1 of the PGL Major Singapore is over, and Stage 2 has its field. 100 Thieves took the last of the eight qualifying spots on Friday, beating M80 2-1 in a 2-2 decider — Dust2 13-6, Inferno 10-13, Mirage 13-5 — to extend their first-ever Major appearance into a second stage. They needed a title at the FiRe Last Chance qualifier just to secure Europe's final invitation to Singapore, then survived two elimination series in a row to get here.

Alex "poiii" Nyholm Sundgren led the decider with a 1.28 rating and a 50-27 K-D, and Nicolai "device" Reedtz added a 1.27. For M80, the loss ends a run of four straight Majors reaching Stage 2 — they had won their first two series in Singapore, over 9z and HEROIC, before dropping three in a row.

The last three deciders

Imperial were the first of the three to go through, beating Wildcard 2-1 after a series full of swings — Dust2 13-11 on Wildcard's pick, Mirage 11-13 after Wildcard led 11-1 at the half, then Cache 13-8 to close it. Kaiky "noway" Santos was the difference with a 1.34 rating and 57 kills, and Imperial salvaged a 2-0 start that had slipped away before rallying in the decider. Wildcard finish 25th to 27th.

Lynn Vision took the second spot with the cleanest result of the day, sweeping HEROIC 2-0 — Nuke 13-11, Dust2 13-6 — behind a 1.45 rating from Junjie "EmiliaQAQ" Tang. The Chinese squad are the second Asian team through after FlyQuest's surprise 3-0 run, and the win ends HEROIC's tournament one day after HEROIC ended FaZe's season.

The eight who made it

Ninjas in Pyjamas and FlyQuest led the way at 3-0. PARIVISION, Eternal Fire and Team Liquid went through at 3-1, and Imperial, Lynn Vision and 100 Thieves all finished 3-2 after going the distance in the final round.

The names Stage 1 already claimed are the story of the week so far: FaZe went out 1-3 to HEROIC, Oleksandr "s1mple" Kostyliev's BC.Game roster — playing as GOSH — finished 0-3, and 9z, who made the playoffs in Cologne, also went home at 1-3.

Who is waiting in Stage 2

Eight invited teams enter on Saturday: FUT, Nemiga, G2, 1win — competing as THE ONE — Natus Vincere, Astralis, The MongolZ and MIBR. The opening round is all best-of-ones, starting at 05:00 CET (11 p.m. ET Friday):

• FUT vs. FlyQuest

• The MongolZ vs. Lynn Vision

• Astralis vs. Imperial

• Nemiga vs. Ninjas in Pyjamas

• G2 vs. Team Liquid

• 1win vs. Eternal Fire

• Natus Vincere vs. PARIVISION

• MIBR vs. 100 Thieves

Stage 2 runs November 28 to December 1, and the top eight move on to Stage 3 from December 2 to 6, where MOUZ, Vitality, Aurora, Spirit, BETBOOM, Falcons, FURIA and Legacy are waiting. The playoffs follow from December 10 to 13 at the Singapore Indoor Stadium, with $500,000 of the $1.25 million prize pool going to the winner. It is the second Counter-Strike Major of 2026, the first ever held in Southeast Asia, and the 25th Counter-Strike Major overall.
