Two Bills, Two Spitting Suspensions, One Wild Week

By Matt

Sam Franklin Jr. became the second Bills defender suspended for spitting in the same Week 6 win over the Raiders — and unlike C.J. Gardner-Johnson, he's fighting it with a '#notthemcnuggets' defense.

The Buffalo Bills won Sunday 33-30 in Las Vegas. The league's discipline office had a busy week after it.

Sam Franklin Jr. was suspended one game without pay for spitting at a Raiders player — the second Bills defender punished for spitting in the same game. C.J. Gardner-Johnson had already been banned one game Monday after being ejected for spitting toward tight end Brock Bowers in the first half.

The two incidents

Per the NFL's release, Franklin — the Bills' special teams captain — spat at an opponent after Las Vegas converted an extra point with 1:32 left in the fourth quarter. Officials threw no flag on the play. Both suspensions cite Rule 12, Section 3, Article 1: "any act which is contrary to the generally understood principles of sportsmanship."

The suspensions begin Oct. 26, after Buffalo's Week 7 bye. Both players return to the active roster Nov. 2, following the Nov. 1 game against the Ravens.

The denial

Franklin is fighting it. He challenged the ruling within hours — a hearing is set for Thursday before one of the league's jointly appointed officers — and posted a slowed-down clip of the extra point to Instagram with the hashtag #notthemcnuggets.

"Still won't spit on anyone," he wrote. His agents "adamantly deny" the allegation and said they expect "the process fully exonerating him tomorrow at the hearing and the apology he deserves."

Gardner-Johnson took the other route: he apologized to his teammates and accepted the punishment. Coach Joe Brady called the act "inexcusable."
