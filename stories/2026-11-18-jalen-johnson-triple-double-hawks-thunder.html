Jalen Johnson's second triple-double in five days powers Hawks past Thunder 117-112

By Matt

Jalen Johnson put up 16 points, 12 rebounds and 11 assists — his second triple-double in five days — as the Hawks beat the Thunder 117-112 in Oklahoma City.

Jalen Johnson recorded a triple-double with 16 points, 12 rebounds and 11 assists as the Atlanta Hawks beat the Oklahoma City Thunder 117-112 on Wednesday night at Paycom Center. It was his second triple-double in five days — he put up 27 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists at Chicago on November 13 — and it handed the Thunder their first home loss of the season.

Both teams now sit at 9-6, but the night belonged to Atlanta's balance. Onyeka Okongwu led the Hawks with 28 points and nine rebounds, Nickeil Alexander-Walker added 19 points and seven assists, and Dyson Daniels chipped in 10 points and 10 rebounds. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led all scorers with 34 for Oklahoma City, and Jared McCain added 18 off the bench.

The homecoming stole the first half of the story

The game was circled on the calendar the moment the schedule came out: Lu Dort and Aaron Wiggins were back in Oklahoma City for the first time since being traded to Atlanta this past offseason. Both helped the Thunder win the 2024-25 NBA championship, and the home crowd gave them standing ovations and video tributes — Wiggins during the first media timeout, Dort after the first quarter.

They made sure the return was more than a ceremony. Dort finished with 16 points, three rebounds and two steals, knocking down three triples. Wiggins scored 14. And when the game swung in the fourth quarter, it was the two former Thunder players who swung it.

How it played out

Oklahoma City erased a 13-point halftime deficit to tie the game at 83 entering the fourth quarter. Then, in the first 1:15 of the final frame — with Gilgeous-Alexander on the bench — Atlanta retook control with three straight three-pointers: Wiggins, then Dort, then Wiggins again. The Hawks never gave the lead back, winning the fourth 34-29.

Atlanta's shooting carried the night. The Hawks hit 20 of 50 from three-point range and outrebounded the Thunder 50-43, while Oklahoma City went 9 of 26 from deep. Gilgeous-Alexander got his 34, but he missed critical late shots as the comeback fell short, including a mid-range jumper off the front of the rim and a pull-up three in the final seconds.

Johnson is stacking them now

Five days ago in Chicago, Johnson's triple-double came in an overtime win. This one came on the road against the defending champions' building, with the game still in the balance. The 16 points were the quietest part of the line — the 12 rebounds and 11 assists were the engine, with Atlanta's ball movement and second chances built around his playmaking.

The Hawks have now won back-to-back games and six of their last eight. The Thunder have dropped four of their last five.
