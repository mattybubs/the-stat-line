Batherson's Hat Trick Powers Senators Past Rangers 7-4

By Matt

Drake Batherson scored three goals and added an assist as Ottawa beat the New York Rangers 7-4 on Saturday night, with Jake Sanderson's first goal of the season standing as the winner.

Drake Batherson had the hat trick, and Ottawa had the answer every time New York pushed. Batherson scored three goals and added an assist as the Senators beat the Rangers 7-4 on Saturday night at Canadian Tire Centre.

How it went

Ottawa jumped out 3-0 early in the second period, then watched the Rangers claw back to 4-3 late in the second. Jake Sanderson's first goal of the season — a rebound off Ridly Greig's shot at 13:38 of the third — made it 5-3 and stood as the winner. Pavel Dorofeyev pulled New York within one again, but Michael Amadio's empty-netter and Batherson's empty-netter at 19:48 of the third sealed it.

Drake Batherson: 3 goals, 1 assist

Jake Sanderson: 1 goal (winner), 2 assists

Jonas Johansson: 24 saves in his Senators debut

Final score: Senators 7, Rangers 4

Tim Stutzle and Dylan Cozens also scored for Ottawa, which improved to 8-4-0. Will Cuylle, J.T. Miller, Alexis Lafreniere and Dorofeyev scored for the Rangers, who are 1-3-1 in their last five. Dylan Garand stopped 17 shots for New York.

Why it matters

Hat tricks are the trigger this blog watches for, and this one came with a game attached: Ottawa nearly gave away a three-goal lead, then closed it out anyway. Sanderson's winner and Johansson's debut win are the kind of details that make a hat-trick night a team night.
