Xbox Drops Nearly 40 Games This Week — and Phantom Blade Zero Lands Thursday

By Matt

Microsoft's late-October flood runs October 26–30 with Mouthwashing and The Wolf Among Us Remastered headlining October 29 — the same day S-GAME's wuxia action RPG Phantom Blade Zero arrives on PC and PS5.

It is a flood week on Xbox. Microsoft's store slate runs from October 26 to October 30 with nearly 40 new games, and the headliners stack up on a single day: Thursday, October 29.

The Xbox week

The Game Pass day-one additions lead the way:

• Monday, October 26: Scramble Knights Online (Game Preview) and Ayasa: Shadows of Silence

• Tuesday, October 27: The Séance of Blake Manor, a Victorian detective story

• Wednesday, October 28: SULFUR, a survival shooter

• Thursday, October 29: Mouthwashing, the first-person horror hit about a wrecked freighter crew, and The Wolf Among Us Remastered, Telltale's Fabletown noir with updated visuals

• Friday, October 30: Queen's Domain and a stack of smaller releases

Mouthwashing carries a $12.99 preorder price on the Xbox store, per Xbox Wire's roundup. The Wolf Among Us Remastered revisits Bigby Wolf's first investigation, drawn from DC's Fables comics.

The week's biggest single-player release

Thursday's other arrival is arguably the week's biggest game outright: Phantom Blade Zero, S-GAME's wuxia action RPG, launches October 29 on PC and PlayStation 5. It headlines a week that also brings The Lord of the Rings Online's The Wolves of Mordor expansion (October 28) and Monster Hunter Outlanders on mobile (October 29).

Why it matters

Late October is traditionally the year's busiest release window, and this week is a case study in volume versus event: dozens of small discoveries on Xbox, one genuine event game on Thursday, and a horror remaster double-header for the Halloween weekend. If you only play one thing this week, the calendar says Thursday.
