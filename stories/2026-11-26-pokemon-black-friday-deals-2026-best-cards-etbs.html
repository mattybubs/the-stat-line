Pokémon Black Friday Deals 2026: Best Cards & ETBs

By Matt

TCGplayer's Cyber Weekend pays 10% back in store credit from Black Friday through Cyber Monday — and the Delta Reign Booster Box is the best per-pack value in the set's lineup.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Black Friday for Pokémon collectors in 2026 isn't about slashed prices — it's about store credit. TCGplayer's Cyber Weekend promotion runs from Friday, November 27 at 9 AM ET through Monday, November 30 at 11 PM ET, and it pays 10% back in future store credit on eligible purchases. Subscribers get 12% under TCGplayer's official terms. And the best value in the newest set, Delta Reign, is the booster box — at the November 26 market snapshot it worked out to about $5.33 a pack, well under what the ETB or booster bundle cost per pack.

The TCGplayer Cyber Weekend deal

The promotion covers all eligible products — singles, sealed product, accessories — with a few exclusions: gift cards, taxes, shipping, and anything bought before the window opens don't count. Preorders do qualify.

The fine print matters here. The credit is not a discount at checkout; it lands in your TCGplayer account 3–5 business days after the purchase, and it's only good on a later order. Credit is capped at $50 per qualifying item and $1,500 per account for the whole promotion. So a single big-ticket item tops out at $50 of credit no matter the price, and you'd need $15,000 of eligible buying to hit the account cap.

Subscribers get the better rate, but only if you subscribe before you buy — the perk isn't retroactive.

Delta Reign, by the numbers

Delta Reign launched November 6, and it's still the set everyone is chasing — largely because of the Mega Rayquaza cards. Here's how the sealed products stacked up in the November 25–26 TCGplayer market snapshot:

Delta Reign Booster Box: 36; $191.93; $5.33; $161.64

Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box: 9; $74.41; $8.27; $49.99

Delta Reign Booster Bundle: 6; $42.92; $7.15; $26.94

The box is the clear winner on per-pack cost, and it's the only one of the three that comes close to its list price — it sits about 19% over MSRP, while the ETB is roughly 1.5x its $49.99 list price. At the box's market price, the Cyber Weekend promo would return roughly $19.19 in future store credit.

Prices have been falling since presale, which is the real story of this set's launch. The ETB was $106.29 on presale and the booster box $238.91; by the November 24 check they were down to $74.95 and $191.35. The trend has been steady, not a Black Friday cliff — if you're buying to open, the box at its current price is already the deal.

The honest context

Don't walk into the weekend expecting sealed Pokémon at or below sticker price. On November 26, zero of the 31 core sealed products tracked by The Lab TCG sold at or under MSRP on TCGplayer. That's the market you're shopping in.

The exception is at retail, not on the marketplace. Best Buy had the Delta Reign Booster Box at $160.99 — a hair under its $161.64 MSRP — through its invitation system. Walmart listed the ETB at $79.77, which is actually above the $74.41 market price, so it's only worth it if you'd rather buy from a retailer than the marketplace. Target has a Pokémon trading card drop scheduled for Saturday, November 28 as part of its Black Friday event. Best Buy is the spot where an actual below-market price shows up; everywhere else, the "deal" is the store credit coming back to you later.

The rule of thumb for the weekend: a booster bundle near $27, an ETB near $50, or a booster box near $162 is at MSRP — and for a current set, that's already a good price. Anything under those numbers is a genuine find.
