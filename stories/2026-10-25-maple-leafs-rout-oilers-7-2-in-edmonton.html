Maple Leafs Rout Oilers 7-2 in Edmonton

By Matt

William Nylander scored twice to reach 300 NHL goals as Toronto routed Edmonton 7-2 on Saturday night, extending the Maple Leafs' winning streak.

The Maple Leafs went into Edmonton and left with a statement win. Toronto routed the Oilers 7-2 on Saturday night, extending its winning streak behind a milestone night for William Nylander (NBC Sports).

Nylander reaches 300

Nylander scored twice on Saturday night to reach 300 NHL goals. The milestone goals powered a rout in which Toronto never looked threatened, turning a marquee road matchup into a comfortable win.

Stolarz stands tall

Anthony Stolarz backed the offensive outburst with 30 saves, keeping Edmonton from ever building momentum in its own building. When the Oilers pushed, Stolarz had the answer — and the Leafs kept pulling away.

A streak with momentum

The victory extended Toronto's winning streak, and the fashion of it matters: a 7-2 road rout against one of the league's heavyweights is the kind of win that builds belief. The Leafs are rolling, and Saturday night in Edmonton was their loudest statement yet.
