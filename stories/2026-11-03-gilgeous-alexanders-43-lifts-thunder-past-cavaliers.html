Gilgeous-Alexander's 43 Lifts Thunder Past Cavaliers

By Matt

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander buried the go-ahead three-pointer with 32.6 seconds left, capping a 43-point night as the short-handed Thunder beat the Cavaliers 117-112.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander delivered when the short-handed Thunder needed him most. He buried the go-ahead three-pointer with 32.6 seconds left, capping a 43-point night as Oklahoma City beat the Cleveland Cavaliers 117-112.

The clutch shot

The game came down to one possession, and Gilgeous-Alexander took it. His go-ahead three with 32.6 seconds remaining was the decisive blow in a game the Thunder had to have — and it came from the player they lean on most when the roster is thinned out.

A needed stop to the skid

This was not just another road win. The short-handed Thunder arrived in Cleveland needing a response after two straight road losses by 20-plus points. Instead of letting the skid stretch to three, they rode their star to the end and closed out a win over the Cavaliers.

Final score: Thunder 117, Cavaliers 112

Top scorer: Shai Gilgeous-Alexander — 43 points

Decisive play: Go-ahead three-pointer with 32.6 seconds left

Context: Short-handed Thunder snapped two straight road losses by 20-plus points

Why it matters

Road wins like this one steady a team. The Thunder had been blown out twice in a row on the road; answering that with a clutch win, built around a 43-point night from Gilgeous-Alexander, is the kind of result that resets the mood around a short-handed group.
