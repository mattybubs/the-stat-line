ARC Raiders 2.6 brings back Cold Snap

By Matt

Embark Studios launched Live Update 2.6.0 on November 17, bringing Cold Snap back to the map rotation, a new Outlaw Weapon Stencil Set, a 50% Black Friday discount, and a first look at Flickering Flames Part 1 arriving November 24.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Embark Studios launched Live Update 2.6.0 for ARC Raiders on November 17, 2026. The headline is the return of Cold Snap to the Major Map Condition rotation, just as the snow settles in — and the update pairs it with a new weapon stencil set, a long list of fixes, a 50% Black Friday discount, and a first look at the Flickering Flames project arriving November 24.

Cold Snap is back

Cold Snap rejoins the Major Map Condition rotation in 2.6.0. Embark's own note on it is short — "As the days get colder and snow settles in, Cold Snap is back!" — but the condition changes how every run plays: snow, reduced visibility, and the cold itself become part of the extraction, on top of the ARC machines and rival Raiders already competing for the same loot.

It is a return, not a debut. Cold Snap first arrived as the game's winter update in December 2025, and its comeback a year later lands alongside a fresh event built around the same season.

What's new in 2.6.0

Cold Snap: Back in the Major Map Condition rotation

Outlaw Weapon Stencil Set: Three weapon stencils — Taurus, Sundob, and Malocchio — now in the in-game store rotation

Buried items: The Dockmaster's Detector now finds buried items on all maps except Pendola Pass (which still needs the Mountaineer's Detector); the Beachcombing minor condition is gone from Riven Tides

50% discount: Black Friday sale on the base game and Deluxe Edition, dates vary by platform (below)

The buried-items change is the quiet quality-of-life win in the patch. What used to be a Riven Tides-only mechanic is now available almost everywhere, so detector runs are worth doing no matter which map the rotation serves up.

The 50% Black Friday sale

New players can join topside at half price, with the sale window depending on platform:

PlayStation: November 16–30; Base game only

Epic Games Store: November 17–December 1; Base game, Deluxe Edition, Deluxe Edition Upgrade

Xbox: November 19–December 3; Base game, Deluxe Edition, Deluxe Edition Upgrade

Steam: November 19–December 3; Base game, Deluxe Edition, Deluxe Edition Upgrade

The game normally sells for $40 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Epic, Steam, and Nvidia GeForce NOW).

Flickering Flames Part 1 lands November 24

Update 2.7 arrives next week, on November 24, with Part 1 of the two-part Flickering Flames Raider project. The event warms Toledo in a celebration of light: with hopes of a festive gathering of all four contradas, Celeste is preparing a special dinner, and Raiders complete three steps to help her get ready. Each step pays out weapons and weapon mods, with additional cosmetic rewards coming in Part 2. The Nessuno Set — outfit, backpack, and backpack charm — is also headed to the in-game store rotation with the update.

The fixes that matter

2.6.0 is mostly a fixes patch, and several of the fixes target the kind of bug that ends a run unfairly:

• Invisible players: fixed two separate cases where a Raider could appear invisible to others — one tied to entering and quickly exiting the Gondola, one that could happen mid-round.

• Revive protection: enemies could knock a player out immediately after a defibrillator revive; being revived now properly prevents knockouts.

• Road Warrior skill: it can now recycle Weapon Amplification Modules and Candleberries during raids as intended.

• Vaulting stamina: vaulting no longer drains jump stamina, and the "Vaults on Vaults on Vaults" skill no longer drains any stamina.

• Hydra vs. lures: the Hydra now only aims some of its weapons at Lure Grenades, still targeting players with the rest.

• Snap Hook: the description now states the correct 18m range (previously listed as 20m).

• Stiletto: weight reduced from 7 to 6.

• Scoped aiming: aiming down sights with scoped weapons could shift aim sideways; aim now stays aligned with the crosshair entering and exiting ADS.

• Inventory: items can now be dropped by dragging them outside the inventory, and a gamepad looting bug that could delete a stack when interrupted is fixed.

• Outpost: the Outpost can now be correctly upgraded to Level 4, fixing a case where it could not be fully upgraded.

• Codex: a new "Times Revived By A Stranger" stat is on the player stats page.

Lighting and exposure also got a pass on Spaceport and Buried City, aimed at clearer visibility looking from interiors to exteriors and in dark interiors, and collision fixes landed across Pendola Pass, Spaceport, Riven Tides, and Stella Montis.

A year after launch, the cadence is the story as much as any single patch: 2.6.0 lands, 2.7 follows a week later, and the game keeps getting seasonal events and systemic fixes rather than coasting on its launch audience.
