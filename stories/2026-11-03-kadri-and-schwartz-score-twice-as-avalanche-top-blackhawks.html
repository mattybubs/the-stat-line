Kadri and Schwartz Score Twice as Avalanche Top Blackhawks

By Matt

Nazem Kadri and Jaden Schwartz each scored twice as Colorado answered an early deficit with five straight goals to beat Chicago 5-2 on Monday night.

Colorado fell behind early in Chicago on Monday night — and then took the game over. The Avalanche answered with five straight goals, with Nazem Kadri and Jaden Schwartz each scoring twice, to beat the Blackhawks 5-2.

An early hole, then five straight

The Avalanche's comeback was a complete reversal. After falling behind early, Colorado scored five goals in a row to seize control of Monday's game in Chicago and never gave it back.

Two-goal nights from Kadri and Schwartz

Kadri and Schwartz did the damage. Each found the net twice, accounting for four of Colorado's five goals in the comeback win.

Final score: Avalanche 5, Blackhawks 2

Two-goal scorers: Nazem Kadri (2), Jaden Schwartz (2)

The run: Five straight Avalanche goals after falling behind early

Location: Chicago

Kane leaves with an injury

The night also brought bad news for Chicago. Blackhawks star Patrick Kane left the game with an upper-body injury and did not return.

Why it matters

A five-goal unanswered run on the road is a statement, and it came from Colorado's depth scoring rather than a single hot stick — two players with two goals each. For Chicago, the loss stings twice: a lead given away, and its star forward leaving hurt.
