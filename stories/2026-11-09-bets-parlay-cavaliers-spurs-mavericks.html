TSL Bets parlay: Cavaliers -8.5, Spurs -8.5, Mavericks -2.5 (about +550)

By Matt

Three legs, about +550: the Cavaliers laying 8.5 against a gutted Hornets team, the Spurs laying 8.5 against a Suns team with no answer for Wembanyama, and the Mavericks laying 2.5 against a Pacers team missing its star.

The games

Cleveland Cavaliers (5-5) at Charlotte Hornets (3-7) — 7:00 PM ET, Spectrum Center, DAZN CLE

Cavaliers -8.5 (-112), total 215.5, moneyline Cavaliers -335 / Hornets +250 (BetRivers). Cleveland lost to the Knicks on Saturday and is 1-3 on the road. Charlotte is 2-4 at home and skidding.

Injuries — Cleveland: Sam Merrill (elbow) is out, Jaylon Tyson (ankle) is out, Nae'Qwan Tomlin (back) is out, and Jarrett Allen (knee contusion) is questionable.

Injuries — Charlotte: Brandon Miller (hip/groin) is out, Kon Knueppel (hamstring, reaggravated, out roughly four weeks) is out, Dennis Schroder (hamstring) is out, Grant Williams (hamstring) is out, and Moussa Diabate (quad) is questionable. The Hornets are missing their leading scorer (Miller, 20.6 a game), their point guard (Schroder, a team-high 4.9 assists), and their rookie (Knueppel) all at once.

San Antonio Spurs (8-2) at Phoenix Suns (3-5) — 9:00 PM ET, Mortgage Matchup Center, DAZN-SAS

Spurs -8.5 (-115), total 227.5, moneyline Spurs -352 / Suns +282 (PickDawgz). San Antonio is unbeaten on the road at 4-0 with a +14.8 point differential away from home, and arrives on a five-game winning run. Phoenix has won three games all season.

Injuries — San Antonio: Tobias Harris (calf) is out, Jayden Quaintance (knee) is out, and Devin Vassell (adductor tightness) is questionable. Victor Wembanyama is averaging 29.1 points and 12.0 rebounds, and he scored 29 in just 24 minutes in his last game.

Injuries — Phoenix: Mark Williams is out after shoulder surgery, leaving undersized Oso Ighodaro and developing Khaman Maluach to cover all 48 minutes at center against Wembanyama. Jordan Goodwin (calf) is questionable, and Rasheer Fleming (ankle) is questionable.

Indiana Pacers (3-7) at Dallas Mavericks (6-4) — 8:00 PM ET, American Airlines Center, DAZN-IND

Mavericks -2.5 (-117), total 222.5, moneyline Mavericks -155 / Pacers +130 (PickDawgz). Dallas has won two straight after blowing out Brooklyn on Sunday. Indiana is 3-2 on the road but is one of the most injured teams in the league.

Injuries — Indiana: Tyrese Haliburton (hamstring) is out, Ben Sheppard (meniscus) is out, Johnny Furphy (ACL) is out, and T.J. McConnell (hamstring) is questionable. Haliburton averages 21.8 points a game and has played just four times this season.

Injuries — Dallas: Dereck Lively II (foot) is out, Sergio de Larrea (knee) is out, and Tarik Biberovic (heel) is out. Daniel Gafford is expected to be limited to 20-25 minutes, and Caleb Martin is available despite a thumb issue.

The legs

Leg 1 — Cavaliers -8.5 (-112). Charlotte is missing four rotation players, including its best scorer, its starting point guard, and its rookie — and Cleveland's net rating (+2.6) sits against Charlotte's (-14.2). The Hornets are converting just 28.8 percent from three, one of the worst marks in the league, and they have nobody left to create offense. Cleveland has to win by nine. Against this version of Charlotte, that is the right side.

Leg 2 — Spurs -8.5 (-115). The matchup is the whole bet: Phoenix has no center. Williams is out after shoulder surgery, and the Suns have to cover 48 minutes at the five against Wembanyama — 29.1 points and 12 rebounds a game, first in the league in blocks at 3.9 a night — with Ighodaro and a rookie. San Antonio is 4-0 on the road and has covered this number in four of its last five. The Spurs' three guards break down Phoenix's perimeter defense, and nobody on the Suns can challenge Wembanyama at the rim.

Leg 3 — Mavericks -2.5 (-117). The Pacers are missing their star, their backup point guard is questionable, and they just flew home from a loss in Mexico City on Saturday. Dallas is at home, has won two straight, and just blew out Brooklyn by 37 on Sunday — pulling its starters early, which matters on the second night of a back-to-back. The Mavericks have covered in seven of their last eight as favorites against Eastern Conference opponents. Two and a half points, at home, against a team that cannot score enough without Haliburton.

The honest caveat

Every leg has a real reason to lose, and the record says so. The Cavaliers are 3-7 against the spread this season and have failed to cover in nine of their last ten as road favorites against Eastern Conference opponents — and the Hornets have covered seven of their last eight against Cleveland following a loss. The Spurs are 4-6 ATS, the home team has covered seven of the last eight in the Spurs-Suns matchup, and San Antonio has failed to cover in each of its last four games at the Mortgage Matchup Center. The Mavericks are on the second night of a back-to-back, Gafford is limited, and the road team has covered the last six games between these two. Three favorites, three real risks. Nothing here is a lock — that is why it pays about +550 instead of -110.

The pick

3-leg parlay, about +550: Cavaliers -8.5 (-112) + Spurs -8.5 (-115) + Mavericks -2.5 (-117). A $10 ticket returns about $65. Three favorites, three injury-ravaged opponents, three different reasons to believe. That is the bet.
