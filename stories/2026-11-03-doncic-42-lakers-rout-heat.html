Doncic Drops 42 and 13 as the Lakers Rout the Heat 138-117 — His Fourth 40-Piece in Five Games

By Matt

One night after 45 and 15 against Toronto, Luka Doncic went for 42 points, 13 assists and seven rebounds as the Lakers blew out Miami 138-117. It is his fourth 40-point game in five outings — and his fifth straight game with at least 37.

Luka Doncic is not cooling off. One night after dropping 45 points and 15 assists on Toronto, Doncic went for 42 points, 13 assists and seven rebounds as the Los Angeles Lakers routed the Miami Heat 138-117 on Monday night at Crypto.com Arena — and this one was never really a game.

Doncic scored 16 points in the first quarter, had 24 by halftime, and added nine more in the first four and a half minutes after the break to balloon the lead to 93-74. He finished 14-of-24 from the field, 6-of-15 from three, and reached 40 on a three-pointer that made it 128-106 with 5:37 left. The Lakers' largest lead was 24.

A heater with history attached

The numbers are starting to stack into something historic. This was Doncic's fourth 40-point game in his last five outings, his fifth consecutive game with at least 37 points, and his 22nd 40-point game in 99 games since joining the Lakers — the sixth-most in franchise history already. His 13 assists were his highest of the season.

The Lakers, as a team, were just as hot: 57.3 percent from the field, 19-of-43 from three, and a season-high 138 points. They shot 67.4 percent in the first half and still led by only seven at the break, 79-72 — Miami shot 59.5 percent in the half themselves — before head coach JJ Redick's halftime challenge on defense turned the game. The Lakers held Miami to 45 second-half points while scoring 59 of their own, opening the third quarter on an 11-0 run.

The supporting cast, and the other star

Austin Reaves broke out of a mini-slump with 27 points and five assists, and Walker Kessler had a 15-point, 15-rebound double-double — the kind of interior work that let Doncic play downhill all night. Sandro Mamukelashvili added 15 points and four assists off the bench. Doncic and Reaves combined for 69 points, exactly half of the Lakers' total.

On the other side, Giannis Antetokounmpo — now in a Heat uniform — put up a quiet 30 points, 12 rebounds and seven assists, but also seven turnovers, and most of his damage came after the game was out of reach. Klay Thompson scored 19, Bam Adebayo had 16 and nine rebounds, and Davion Mitchell added 15 and seven assists. Miami's two-game winning streak is over, and the Heat never led in a game the Lakers controlled from the opening tip.

At 6-2, the Lakers have now answered a disappointing loss to Golden State last week with back-to-back offensive showcases — 131 against Toronto, 138 against Miami. The question early in the season was whether the offense would flow. Right now, it flows through one man, and nobody has slowed him down yet.
