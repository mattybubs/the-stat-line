# Brewers take 2-0 NLCS lead over Dodgers

Milwaukee did it again. The Brewers took Monday night's NLCS Game 2 over the Dodgers 7-4, and now head home with a 2-0 series lead — after Los Angeles threw both of its aces at the first two games and lost both.

Tarik Skubal started Game 1 for the Dodgers. Yoshinobu Yamamoto started Game 2. Zero wins to show for it.

Source: Reuters — William Contreras sparks Brewers' Game 2 rally against Dodgers

The night in numbers

Final score: Brewers 7, Dodgers 4

William Contreras: 3 hits, 3 RBI

Brewers bullpen: 15 up, 15 down

Dodgers team hits: 3

That bullpen line is the story of the night. Fifteen batters faced, fifteen outs recorded. When your starter hands you a lead and you need nothing else, that's how a series lead gets built.

What it means

The Dodgers burned their two best arms to go down 0-2. That's not a rotation problem you fix with a day off — that's the kind of hole that turns a series. Milwaukee now needs two more wins out of five games, and the Dodgers have to win four of the next five without the ace matchups they already spent.
