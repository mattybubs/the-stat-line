Anton Lundell's First Hat Trick Buries the Jets 5-2 — Winnipeg's Eighth Straight Loss

By Matt

Anton Lundell waited 372 games for his first NHL hat trick, then delivered it in style — two goals in the final seven minutes — as Florida beat Winnipeg 5-2 and handed the Jets their eighth straight loss.

Anton Lundell picked quite a night to end the wait.

The Florida Panthers center scored his first career hat trick — in his 372nd game — closing out a 5-2 win over the Winnipeg Jets on Friday night at Amerant Bank Arena. Lundell entered the night with one goal in 13 games this season. He left with three, and the Jets left with their eighth straight loss.

How it happened

Sam Reinhart opened the scoring on the power play with 1:45 left in the first period, and Lundell doubled the lead 2:38 into the second, recovering his own blocked shot in the left circle and beating Nico Daws glove side.

Winnipeg kept pushing back. Kyle Connor scored on a 5-on-3 power play to make it 2-1, Seth Jones restored Florida's two-goal lead with a power-play one-timer at 15:24 of the second, and Morgan Barron's fourth-line goal with 11:14 left in the third pulled the Jets within one at 3-2.

Then Lundell took the game over. He poked in a rebound at the edge of the crease with 6:27 left to make it 4-2, and added an empty-netter with 1:39 remaining to complete the hat trick. Jones finished with a goal and two assists, Reinhart had a goal and an assist, and Akira Schmid made 22 saves for his third consecutive win. Jones's 18 October points (two goals, 16 assists) also tied Keith Yandle's franchise record for most points in a month by a Florida defenseman.

The bigger picture

Lundell's hat trick is Florida's fifth of the season — already tied for the second-most in a single season in franchise history, per NHL.com. The Panthers (8-4-2) have won three straight and beaten Winnipeg twice in four days, outscoring the Jets 10-3 across the home-and-home after Tuesday's 5-1 win.

Winnipeg (4-7-3) is now 0-6-2 over its last eight. The franchise record for consecutive losses is 10, set three times (2000, 1994 and 1980) — a mark this Jets team is now uncomfortably close to. Daws, acquired from New Jersey last week, made 33 saves in his first start as a Jet and deserved better.

For Lundell, a sixth-year veteran who has built his reputation on two-way reliability rather than highlight reels, the first hat trick is the kind of milestone that tends to stick. He waited 373 games for it. The last two goals took about five minutes.
