TNF Recap: Bears Hold Off Patriots 30-24 in Chicago Shootout

By Matt

Caleb Williams outduels Drake Maye as Chicago covers and the over cashes. Final: Bears 30, Patriots 24.

Bears 30, Patriots 24 — Final. Thursday Night Football delivered exactly what the 50.5 total promised: a shootout in Chicago between the top two quarterbacks from the 2024 draft.

Williams Gets the Last Word

Caleb Williams went 22-of-33 for 308 yards, 2 TD, 0 INT — clean, efficient, and just enough. His top target was Luther Burden III: 4 catches for 130 yards and a TD, including a 58-yard bomb that broke the game open.

Maye's Big Night Not Enough

Drake Maye actually outgained Williams through the air: 24-of-31 for 321 yards, 2 TD, 1 INT, plus 26 rushing yards and a rushing TD. Rhamondre Stevenson was a dual threat — 5 catches for 93 yards and a TD to go with his ground work. But the one interception proved costly, and the Patriots' depleted offensive line (two starting guards out) couldn't protect Maye late.

The Slip: 2-0 ✅

The TNF slip cashed both legs:

• Bears -3 (-105) ✅ — Chicago won by 6

• Over 50.5 (-110) ✅ — 54 combined points

Both teams came in 4-2. Chicago moves to 5-2; New England falls to 4-3.
