Deborah Ann Woll will play Faye in Amazon's God of War series — the same role she plays in the games

By Matt

Amazon and Sony confirmed Monday that Deborah Ann Woll will reprise her role as Faye in Prime Video's live-action God of War series — the same character she voices in Ragnarök and stars as in next year's God of War Laufey.

Prime Video's God of War series just made one of its smartest casting calls yet. On Monday, Amazon and Sony Pictures Television confirmed that Deborah Ann Woll will play Faye — Kratos' wife and Atreus' mother — in the live-action adaptation, reprising the role she already plays in the games. Deadline first reported the casting, and the studios confirmed it in a press release the same day.

Woll is one of the few performers carrying a game role straight into live action. She voiced Faye in God of War Ragnarök and provides the voice and motion capture for the character in the upcoming God of War Laufey, where Faye takes the lead for the first time. Now she will play her on screen, opposite Dave Bautista's Kratos.

Why this one lands differently

Faye is a strange role to cast, because in the story the show is adapting, she is already gone. She dies right before the 2018 God of War begins, and the whole journey — Kratos and Atreus carrying her ashes to the highest peak in the Nine Realms — is a farewell to her. In the games she appears only in flashback and dream sequences, and briefly at that.

That is exactly why Woll's casting is interesting. Bringing back the actor who already plays Faye, rather than recasting her for live action, signals that the show plans to give her more presence than the first game ever did — flashback sequences, at minimum, and possibly an expanded role built while production was paused. Deadline reports Woll was always the producers' choice for the part, and the casting is only surfacing now because Faye does not appear in the episodes that were already shot.

There is a longer game here, too. God of War Laufey shifts the focus from Kratos to Faye entirely, and Woll stars in it. If the series runs past its current two-season order, the show already has its Faye in place for stories that follow her — not just the ones that remember her.

The cast so far

Woll joins a cast that has already been through one major shake-up. Bautista took over as Kratos after Ryan Hurst, the original choice, injured his shoulder on set and had to step away; filming restarted with Bautista in the role. The rest of the ensemble, as confirmed across the studios' announcements:

• Dave Bautista as Kratos

• Callum Vinson as Atreus

• Deborah Ann Woll as Faye

• Mandy Patinkin as Odin

• Ed Skrein as Baldur

• Ólafur Darri Ólafsson as Thor

• Alastair Duncan as Mimir — like Woll, reprising his game role

Ronald D. Moore serves as showrunner, executive producer and writer. Amazon has ordered two seasons, filming back to back.

The show's road so far

This adaptation has been in the works since March 2022, when it was first reported in negotiations with Amazon Prime Video, and it has already survived a full creative reset — a change of showrunner, a recast of its lead, and a pause in filming. Production is now underway again with Bautista, and the series still has no release date.

The timing puts Woll in the middle of a big year for the character. God of War Laufey was revealed in June at PlayStation's State of Play, with Faye waking after her own funeral in the Everywhen, an afterlife where gods from different mythologies cross paths. The game was delayed earlier this month from February 16 to May 20, 2027. Woll's Faye is the thread connecting the two projects — the game that finally lets her lead, and now the show that refused to recast her.
