Frozen Trail pushes ARC Raiders to a six-month player peak

By Matt

ARC Raiders hit 131,388 concurrent Steam players on the back of the Frozen Trail update — its biggest day since April's Riven Tides release — and cracked the top five on Steam's best-seller chart.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The numbers

ARC Raiders posted a 24-hour Steam peak of 131,388 concurrent players after the Frozen Trail update went live — the game's highest player count since April's Riven Tides release, a six-month high. The comeback also pushed it to fifth on Steam's best-seller chart.

Why it surged

Frozen Trail is the game's fifth major update, and it's a big one: a new frozen map (Pendola Pass), new ARC enemy types, and a free weekend that let anyone jump in through October 12. Big updates have historically been ARC Raiders' strongest lever for bringing lapsed players back — and this one landed exactly how Embark planned.

The takeaway

Hitting a six-month player peak five updates deep into a live-service lifecycle is no small feat. The question now is retention: how many of those 131,000 stick around once the free weekend ends and the Pendola Pass novelty wears off. If even a fraction converts, Embark has a real second act on its hands.
