Jahmyr Gibbs just tied Barry Sanders — 52 rushing touchdowns before 25, and the record is one score away

By Matt

Two touchdowns against Tampa Bay gave Jahmyr Gibbs 52 career rushing touchdowns, tying Barry Sanders for the most by a player before his 25th birthday — and Gibbs doesn't turn 25 until March.

The tie

Jahmyr Gibbs needed two rushing touchdowns on Sunday to catch Barry Sanders. He got both — in a 31-21 Lions win over the Tampa Bay Buccaneers at Ford Field — and now the two Detroit running backs share the NFL record for the most rushing touchdowns by a player before turning 25.

Gibbs walked into the game with 50 career rushing scores, level with Emmitt Smith. The brace on Sunday passed Smith and put him alongside Sanders, the greatest Lion of them all.

He finished the afternoon with 23 carries for 98 yards and the two touchdowns, plus four catches for 20 yards — 118 scrimmage yards in a win that pushed Detroit to 8-2 ahead of Thursday's Thanksgiving showdown with the Bears.

One more and it's his alone

Here is the part that makes this more than a tie: Gibbs does not turn 25 until March 20, 2027. He has the rest of this season — and the whole of next season's opening stretch before his birthday — to score one more rushing touchdown and own the record outright.

He has already done the same thing in the combined category. Back on October 11 against Arizona, Gibbs passed Sanders for the most total touchdowns by a player before 25, reaching 58 career scores in that game. Now he shares the rushing-only mark too, and the next one breaks the tie.

The season he is putting together makes it a matter of when, not if. Through ten games he has 1,502 scrimmage yards and 15 scrimmage touchdowns, and he entered Sunday tied for the league lead in touchdowns from scrimmage.

The company he keeps

The record book keeps running out of room for Gibbs. His 64 total touchdowns in 59 career games moved him past Jim Brown for the second-most by a player in his first 60 games. He is the fourth player ever to score a touchdown in 40 games within his first four seasons, joining LaDainian Tomlinson, Terrell Davis, and Maurice Jones-Drew.

And it is not just a solo act. Gibbs and Amon-Ra St. Brown have now each scored a touchdown in the same game 25 times, tying Marvin Harrison and Edgerrin James for the third-most by a running back and wide receiver duo.

What it means

Tying Barry Sanders in anything is a career-defining line on a resume. Gibbs did it at 24, in his fourth season, on a team chasing the NFC's top seed. The Lions are 8-2, the record is one touchdown away, and the player ahead of him on the list is the one whose name is on the standard itself.

Sanders congratulated Gibbs personally when he passed him for the combined-touchdowns mark in October. The next one — the one that puts Gibbs alone at the top of the rushing list — is coming, and it will not take long.
