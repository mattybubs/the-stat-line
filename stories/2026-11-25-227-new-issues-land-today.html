227 new issues land today

By Matt

It's the last New Comic Book Day of November: 227 new issues hit shops today, led by Amazing Spider-Man #1004 — and Jean Grey #1 starts a new five-issue series as the Phoenix comes back to Earth.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

It's the last New Comic Book Day of November, and it's a big one: 227 new issues land in shops today, with 591 new variants across them. The Christmas comics are starting to arrive, and the week's haul is led by a familiar name at the top of the list.

The week's headliner: Amazing Spider-Man #1004

The Amazing Spider-Man #1004 takes the number one spot on the week's list again. The series is still going strong after its big #1000 milestone, and the demand hasn't let up.

This week's issue comes from Joe Kelly, Francesco Manna, and Luca Maresca. Itsy Bitsy has some feedback for J. Jonah Jameson — shut up and shove off this mortal coil — and while Spidey leaps into action to save JJJ, another friend flips and puts the wall-crawler between him and his revenge. Cover A is by Ed McGuinness, with variants from Peach Momoko, Mark Spears, Sara Pichelli, and Ron Lim, among others. Cover price is $4.99.

Elsewhere in the Spider-corner of the Marvel list, Dan Slott and Stefano Caselli's Spectacular Spider-Man #6 also hits shelves today.

The big debut: Jean Grey #1

The week's biggest launch is Jean Grey #1, the start of a new five-issue series from Jed MacKay and Rogê Antônio. It's the first of eight variants for the issue, and the timing is no accident — with the X-Men heading back to the big screen in Avengers: Doomsday, it's a good moment to make room for Marvel's mutants on the reading pile.

The setup: after DNX, the Phoenix is coming home — and Jean Grey is burning with it. The question hanging over the issue is whether she can hold onto a power that gives life and takes it in the same breath, or whether it burns through everything she touches when she lands. It's a new chapter for Jean and the X-Men, and the early word is strong: ComicBook.com gave the debut 4 out of 5, calling it a character study that bridges her cosmic era as Phoenix with her return to Earth.

The full creative team is MacKay on writing, Antônio on art, Edgar Delgado on colors, and Arianna Maher on letters. Cover A is by Joëlle Jones, with variants from Ito, Nogi San, Peach Momoko, and Miguel Mercado. It's 32 pages for $4.99.

The rest of the stack

Jean Grey isn't the only new series launching today. The week's debutants also include Robin Hood #1, Leaded Gasoline #1, Rampant Swine #1, and DC's Naughty List #1, plus the Batman 2026 Annual.

The rest of Marvel's list includes Alien vs. X-Men #3, Amazing Venom #3, Gambit Gone #3, Marvel Zombies: War Zone #3, Predator vs. Planet of the Apes #5, and Spider-Man / Hulk: Fire and Brimstone #4. All told, Comics Bulletin counts 83 new releases for the week — and once every variant edition is tallied, CLZ's count runs to 227 new issues.

If you're heading to the shop before the holiday, Jean Grey #1 is the one to grab first. Spider-Man #1004 is the one to grab second. Everything after that is gravy.
