420 million hours: GTA 6 is the most-watched game launch in livestreaming history

By Matt

GTA 6 didn't just break sales records. In its first seven days, people watched 420 million hours of it — nearly triple the old record, and more than Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo IV and Lost Ark drew in their opening weeks combined.

GTA 6 didn't just sell. It got watched — at a scale livestreaming has never seen. Streams Charts closed the book on the game's first seven days on Tuesday, and the final tally is 420 million hours watched across Twitch, YouTube, Kick and the rest. That is nearly triple the previous first-week record, and it puts the launch in a category of its own.

The old record belonged to Valorant's closed beta in April 2020, at 150.48 million hours. GTA 6 cleared that mark in about two days and kept going. Its seven-day total also beat the combined opening weeks of Valorant, Elden Ring, Diablo IV and Lost Ark — four of the biggest launches of the last six years, added together, still came up short of one week of GTA 6.

The peak was just as absurd

At the busiest point in the first 24 hours, 7.91 million people were watching GTA 6 streams at the same time across platforms. Twitch alone peaked at 3.31 million simultaneous viewers, passing League of Legends' all-time peak for a game category on the platform. Twitch carried the largest share of total watch time, with YouTube and Kick finishing remarkably close to each other behind it.

The two-day checkpoint told the story early. After 48 hours, GTA 6 had already drawn 214.63 million hours watched — more than the combined opening weeks of Elden Ring, Hogwarts Legacy and Cyberpunk 2077, which totaled 186.98 million. The record wasn't close. It was over before the week was half done.

The streamers rode it too

TheBurntPeanut was the most-watched GTA 6 streamer of launch week, generating 11.43 million hours watched across his broadcasts, mostly on YouTube. IShowSpeed drew the most concurrent viewers on a single channel, near the end of a 52-hour marathon that ended with him finishing the story without sleeping, in tears on camera. Spanish streamer IlloJuan recorded the highest combined peak viewership across multiple platforms.

It wasn't just the big names. A total of 1,056 channels broke their all-time peak viewership records while playing GTA 6, and 193 of them pulled at least ten times their previous record audience. The launch didn't just set a record for the game — it reset the ceiling for more than a thousand streamers at once.

The context that makes it land

The viewing record sits alongside the sales records we already covered: GTA 6 cleared $4 billion in its first four days. But the streaming number measures something different. Sales say how many people bought it. Hours watched say how many people couldn't look away from someone else playing it — including millions who don't own a console that can run it yet.

Every launch-week record in the book now has the same name on it: biggest sales, biggest PlayStation launch, and now the most-watched game launch in livestreaming history. The week is over. The numbers are final. And the gap between GTA 6 and everything that came before it is not small.
