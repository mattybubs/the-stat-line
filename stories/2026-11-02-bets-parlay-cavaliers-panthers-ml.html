2-Leg Parlay (~+141): Cavaliers ML + Panthers ML

By Matt

Two home favorites in the best spots on Monday's board — Cleveland against a six-man injury list, and Florida against a leaky Detroit penalty kill. Together they pay about +141.

The legs

Leg 1 — Cleveland Cavaliers moneyline (-180, BetMGM). Oklahoma City is down six players, including Chet Holmgren and Jalen Williams, and has lost its last two games by an average of 22.5 points. Cleveland's core — Harden, Mitchell, Mobley, Allen — is intact at home. The Cavs do not need to cover 4.5 here; they just need to win, and everything about the injury report says they will.

Leg 2 — Florida Panthers moneyline (-182, FanDuel). Detroit is missing its captain (Dylan Larkin) and a chunk of its lineup, its penalty kill has allowed 14 goals in 45 chances, and its goaltending has been shaky. Florida has won three of four at home despite its own injury list. The Panthers are the better, healthier team in this building.

Combined price: about +141. Two favorites, no lottery tickets, no ten-leg dreams. Each leg has to clear on its own — and each one does, on the injury report and the form, before you ever combine them.

The honest caveat

Parlays are still parlays: one bad night from either favorite sinks the whole ticket, and Shai Gilgeous-Alexander can ruin the Cleveland leg by himself if he goes for 40. That is why the legs are moneylines, not spreads — you are not asking either team to win by a margin, just to win. If either number moves against you before tipoff, or if a late injury report changes the picture, pass and take the single bets instead.
