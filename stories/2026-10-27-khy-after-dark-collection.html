Kylie Jenner's Khy After Dark Winter Collection Launches Today

By Matt

Kylie Jenner's Khy drops its 16-piece After Dark winter collection today — statement outerwear, sculptural silhouettes, faux fur and animal prints, following her Victoria's Secret runway debut earlier this month.

The drop

Kylie Jenner's clothing label Khy releases its winter collection, Khy After Dark, today, October 27. The 16-piece lineup leans dark: statement outerwear, sculptural silhouettes, faux-fur details, oversized bomber jackets, knitwear and animal prints.

The campaign

Jenner stars in the campaign herself, photographed by Quentin de Briey in the hills of Los Angeles. Outerwear is the center of the collection, per ELLE's preview — a darker aesthetic for the brand's latest winter release.

The timing

The drop follows Jenner's runway debut at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show earlier this month — Khy's founder stepping onto the runway herself, then immediately putting a winter collection behind it.

Why it matters

• Khy After Dark is the brand's winter statement piece — outerwear-led, texture-heavy, and timed to the cold-weather buy.

• The Victoria's Secret debut-to-drop pipeline shows how fast Jenner is moving the brand from label to runway presence.

• Sixteen pieces keeps it tight: a focused winter edit, not a sprawling catalog.

Sources: Kylie Jenner Reveals Khy After Dark Collection Following Victoria's Secret Debut (Fashion Times, citing ELLE's preview).
