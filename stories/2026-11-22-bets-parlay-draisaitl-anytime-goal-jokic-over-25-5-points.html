TSL Bets: The Sunday night parlay (~+318) — Draisaitl anytime goal, Jokić OVER 25.5 points

By Matt

Leon Draisaitl leads the NHL with 22 goals and is plus money to score again in St. Louis, and Nikola Jokić has cleared 25.5 points in 4 of his last 5 against the league's second-worst scoring defense. Two legs, about +318.

Leg 1 — the game

Edmonton Oilers (17-5-3) at St. Louis Blues (12-9-2) — 7:00 PM ET, Enterprise Center, St. Louis, BN+ / STLNHL / SNW / SN1

Oilers -110 moneyline (consensus; -118 DraftKings), total 6 (lines via BetWatch).

Injuries — Edmonton: C Ryan Nugent-Hopkins OUT (undisclosed — expected back Tuesday, November 24). LW Zach Hyman day-to-day (migraine/illness — left Saturday's game in Carolina early). C Mattias Janmark, D Alec Regula, F Matt Savoie and G Frederik Andersen on IR.

Injuries — St. Louis: LW Connor McMichael OUT (undisclosed). D Torey Krug OUT (ankle, out for the season). D Tyler Tucker OUT (knee). LW Dillon Dubé OUT (neck). F Ross Johnston day-to-day (upper body).

Storylines: The Oilers close a six-game road trip after back-to-back losses — 7-4 at Carolina on Saturday, 3-1 at Tampa Bay on Thursday — ended a six-game win streak, but they can still finish the trip 4-2. Draisaitl leads the NHL with 22 goals, has scored in 7 of his last 10 games with 10 goals over that stretch, and nine of his 22 have come on a power play converting at 34.1%, second in the league. He skates with Connor McDavid (57 points) and Vasily Podkolzin, a line that has outscored opponents 23-6 at five-on-five. The Blues are no pushover: they have won three straight and scored 22 goals in their last three games. The wrinkle is in net — Joel Hofer is the projected Blues starter, and he has saved 9.7 goals above expected, sixth-most in the league. Both teams are on the second night of a back-to-back.

Leg 2 — the game

Denver Nuggets (9-5) at Chicago Bulls (4-11) — 7:00 PM ET, United Center, Chicago, ALT / CHSN

Nuggets -8.5, total 237.5, moneyline -366 / +298 (lines via BetWatch).

Injuries — Denver: G Julian Strawther OUT (knee) — the only Nugget on the report. Jokić and Jamal Murray (24.9 points a game) are both healthy, no designation.

Injuries — Chicago: F Caleb Wilson OUT (hand). G Norman Powell day-to-day (hamstring — the Bulls' leading scorer at 20.8 points a game). C Jalen Smith day-to-day (illness). F Patrick Williams probable (thumb). F Tobe Awaka OUT (back).

Storylines: The Bulls allow 122.8 points a game, second-worst in the NBA, with a defensive rating ranked 26th — and their top scorer and starting center are both questionable. Jokić has cleared 25.5 points in 4 of his last 5 games — 26, 24, 28, 26, 27, newest first — and 6 of his last 10, averaging 25.9 points in November. Denver has won seven of its last ten and two straight, including a 133-124 win over Houston on Friday; Chicago blew a 15-point lead in a 94-98 loss to Washington the same night and is 1-5 at home.

The bet

Leon Draisaitl anytime goal (+120, FanDuel) + Nikola Jokić OVER 25.5 points (-111, Novig best price via BetWatch) — about +318. A $10 ticket returns about $41.80.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Draisaitl is the league's leading goal scorer at plus money, on the league's second-best power play, against a Blues team that has allowed 18 goals over its last four games. Jokić is the defining matchup problem in the league against the NBA's second-worst scoring defense, with Chicago's frontcourt banged up. Neither leg depends on the other — different sport, different building, same 7:00 PM window. That is the juice rule working as intended: a plus-money leg plus a near-even leg, about +318 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

Edmonton is on the second night of a back-to-back after being outshot 36-15 in Carolina, and Hofer is a real goalie — 9.7 goals saved above expected is not a soft matchup. On the other leg, the blowout risk is real: Denver is an 8.5-point favorite, and Jokić could sit much of the fourth quarter in a rout, and his last-10 hit rate on this line is only 60%. A parlay needs both legs, and either one can sink it alone.

The record

TSL Bets is 21-37 in November, 25-58 all-time.
