2-Leg Parlay (about +261): Derrick Henry Under 70.5 + Jaguars -10

By Matt

Two plays, one story: the Ravens' offense is broken at quarterback, and Jacksonville is the wrong team to be broken against. Henry under 70.5 rushing yards plus the Jaguars laying 10 pays about +261.

Game slip

Jacksonville Jaguars (5-2) at Baltimore Ravens (4-4)

Thursday, November 5, 2026 — 8:15 PM ET — M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore, MD — Prime Video

Spread: Jaguars -10 (-110)

Total: 39.5

Moneyline: Jaguars -535 / Ravens +415

Odds source: DraftKings, via Fantasy Alarm, updated Nov. 5, 5:40 AM ET; consensus via The Sport Stack, Nov. 5, 6:40 AM ET

### Injury report

Jacksonville: RB Bhayshul Tuten (ankle/foot) — questionable, game-time decision. DE Travon Walker (rib) — questionable. S Eric Murray and DT DaVon Hamilton — season-ending IR. S Antonio Johnson — cleared, available.

Baltimore: QB Lamar Jackson (thumb) — OUT, surgery, injured reserve, possibly season-ending. QB Tyler Huntley (toe) — OUT for the season, turf toe surgery. S Jaylinn Hawkins (knee) — OUT. CB Nate Wiggins (stinger/illness) — questionable. QB Joe Fagnano (ankle) — full participant, no designation, starting his first NFL game.

### Storylines

• Baltimore is on its third quarterback: undrafted rookie Joe Fagnano, who went 10 of 21 for 91 yards with two turnovers in relief against Buffalo.

• Jacksonville just won 42-7 at Indianapolis and sits second in the NFL in points allowed per game.

• The Ravens have not topped 13 points in three straight games. The line has moved 13 points from its open, Baltimore -3 to Jacksonville -10.

The parlay

Leg 1: Derrick Henry Under 70.5 rushing yards (-109, DraftKings)

Leg 2: Jacksonville Jaguars -10 (-110, DraftKings)

Combined: about +261

Why these two legs belong together

This is not two random bets stapled together. It is one thesis, expressed twice: the Ravens' offense cannot function tonight, and Jacksonville is built to punish exactly that.

Henry has topped 70 rushing yards just four times all season and has gone 53, 45, and 58 in his last three games. He now faces the NFL's No. 1 run defense by EPA — fewest yards per carry (3.4) and fewest total rushing yards (542) allowed. And if the Jaguars jump ahead, as a 10-point favorite should, Baltimore has to throw with a rookie quarterback, which takes carries away from Henry in the second half.

The spread leg is the same story from the other side. A defense that just held Indianapolis to seven points, against a quarterback making his first NFL start on a short week, with an offense that has not scored more than 13 points in three games. Jacksonville only needs to be competent to cover ten.

Each leg clears on its own merits. Together they pay about +261 — a fair price for two plays that point the same direction without depending on each other to happen.

The honest caveat

Both legs live and die with the same game, so the correlation cuts both ways. If Baltimore somehow keeps this close — Fagnano plays clean, Henry grinds out first downs, the Jaguars play conservative with a lead — both legs can lose together. And Henry owns this matchup historically, with 84 or more rushing yards in seven of his last eight against Jacksonville. This is a two-leg parlay, not a lottery ticket — but it is still one game, and one game can always surprise you.

The pick

2-leg parlay, about +261: Henry Under 70.5 + Jaguars -10. One broken offense, two ways to play it.
