GTA 6 is PlayStation's biggest launch ever — 39 million players on PS5 in four days

By Matt

Sony says more than 39 million unique players jumped into Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 in its first four days — the biggest launch in PlayStation history, with 683 million hours played.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Sony has now put its own number on the launch, and it is the one that settles the argument about scale. More than 39 million unique players experienced Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5 between launch on Thursday, November 19, and Sunday, November 22 — making it the biggest game launch in PlayStation history, across three decades and five console generations.

That is Sony's own announcement, published Monday on the PlayStation Blog. It is not a third-party estimate, and it is not a sales figure. It is a count of people who actually played the game on PS5 — and it lands on top of a weekend that already produced the biggest entertainment launch ever by revenue.

The launch window by the numbers

Unique players on PS5, Nov 19–22: More than 39 million

Hours played on PS5 in the window: 683 million

Average playtime per player: 17.4 hours

First game played on new PS5 consoles: 70%

The 683 million hours is the number that stops you. Four days after launch, the average player had already spent 17.4 hours in Leonida — more than four hours a day, every day, since the game unlocked. That is not a launch-weekend spike of people downloading a game and checking the menu. That is a player base living in the game.

Sony also confirmed two records that go beyond the player count. GTA VI set a new record for the highest number of concurrent players for any game on PlayStation — passing every multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player title before it, though Sony did not publish the concurrent figure itself. And the launch drove the largest number of newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day since the console launched, outside of end-of-year holidays. Since launch, GTA VI was the first game played on 70% of new PS5 consoles.

Players are not copies — and the gap is the story

One distinction matters here, and Sony's own framing invites it. Unique players and copies sold are different measures. Alinea Analytics estimated 33.7 million copies sold on PS5 across the same weekend — while Sony counts more than 39 million unique players on the platform. The gap is shared consoles, second profiles, households passing the controller around. The game is reaching more people than it has buyers, four days in.

The sales side of the weekend is already on the record. Take-Two announced 32.5 million pre-orders and $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales, calling it the biggest entertainment launch in history. Alinea's weekend estimate put the total at 44.9 million copies and more than $4 billion in four days. Sony's numbers are the other half of the same weekend: not what the game sold, but how many people showed up — and how long they stayed.

What "biggest in PlayStation history" actually means

PlayStation has been launching games for 30 years. The claim covers five console generations, from the original PlayStation through the PS5 — every exclusive, every third-party blockbuster, every free-to-play phenomenon that has ever run on the platform. No single game has ever drawn this many players this fast.

Sony's post, written by content communications manager Tim Turi, also nodded to the partnership behind it — Rockstar and PlayStation go back to Grand Theft Auto III on the PS2 — and to the launch-weekend events around the game, including the two limited-edition DualSense controllers inspired by Vice City and the GTA VI festival in Miami. The message of the post is a thank-you to the PlayStation community. The numbers in it are the story.

All figures are based on Sony Interactive Entertainment internal data collected from November 19 to November 22, 2026.
