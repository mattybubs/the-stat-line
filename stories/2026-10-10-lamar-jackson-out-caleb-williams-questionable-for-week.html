# Lamar Jackson out, Caleb Williams questionable for Week 5

The final injury calls are in for Week 5, and they're headlined by one of the league's biggest names missing a primetime game.

Lamar Jackson — OUT

The Ravens ruled out Lamar Jackson with a sprained ankle, so he's out of Sunday night's game in Atlanta. Losing your starting quarterback ahead of Sunday Night Football is the kind of news that reshapes an entire slate — and Baltimore will have to win without him.

Caleb Williams — QUESTIONABLE

The Bears held the door open for Caleb Williams instead, listing him questionable at Green Bay after a limited Friday practice. That's a game-time decision to watch — limited Friday activity usually means the team wants to see how the player responds before making the final call.
