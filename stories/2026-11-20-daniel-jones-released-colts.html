Colts release Daniel Jones after 26-3 loss in Houston, hand the job back to Anthony Richardson

By Matt

The Colts released quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday, one day after a 26-3 loss in Houston, and named Anthony Richardson the starter for the November 29 game against the Giants.

The move

The Indianapolis Colts released quarterback Daniel Jones on Friday, head coach Shane Steichen announced, ending an 11-game stint that started with an $88 million contract and ended with the worst passing numbers of Jones's career.

Anthony Richardson is the starter now. His first start since the 2024 season comes on Sunday, November 29, when the Colts host the New York Giants — the team that drafted Jones and where he spent his first six seasons. That game was supposed to be Jones's revenge game. Instead he'll be watching it from waivers.

Jones is 29. He signed that two-year deal in March, with $50 million fully guaranteed — $100 million with incentives, the largest two-year contract in NFL history, per the New York Post. The Colts paid $49.79 million, per Over The Cap via the Post.

The final game

Thursday night in Houston was the end of it, even if nobody said so at the time. The Texans beat the Colts 26-3, and Jones went 12-of-27 for 144 yards with an interception before Steichen pulled him in the fourth quarter for Richardson, who went 0-for-2 with one carry for two yards.

Steichen was noncommittal about Jones's job after the loss. By Friday, there was no job to be noncommittal about.

The season line tells the story on its own: a 3-8 record, 1,974 passing yards, 7 touchdowns and 13 interceptions — the most interceptions in the league and a career high for Jones — with a career-low 58.4 percent completion rate.

Why now

The timing is about money as much as football. Jones carried a $10.51 million injury guarantee for 2027 that would have become fully guaranteed if he got hurt, so a benching was coming either way. The Colts skipped the benching and went straight to the release.

Jones was set to be a $46.8 million cap hit next season. The release saves Indianapolis about $11.6 million, with dead cap of $14,889,520 in 2026 and $35,200,000 in 2027, per Spotrac via USA Today.

The deadline blunder

The part that will sting in Indianapolis is what happened 10 days ago. Baltimore and Minnesota were both interested in trading for Jones at the deadline, and the Colts kept him off the market. Now they get nothing for him. He is on waivers through Monday at 4 p.m.

A team that wanted him at the deadline can now have him for the cost of a waiver claim. That is the whole story of this Colts season in one paragraph.

The backstory

This is the second time in three years that Jones has been released in November. The first was the Giants in 2024 — but that time, he sought it. This time, Indianapolis made the call.

It is a strange ending to a stint that started so well. Jones's Colts run opened 8-2 in 2025 before a torn Achilles cut his season short. He tried to play last season on a broken leg until the Achilles snapped — the kind of toughness that made Steichen's praise on Friday land as more than a courtesy.

"Can't say enough good things about Daniel, just the ultimate competitor, ultimate teammate, toughness that he brought to the football team," Steichen said.

And on the release itself, Steichen was blunt about who made the decision: "No. This was a decision that was just made for the best interest of both parties." Jones did not ask for the release. "It's just where we're at in our season," Steichen said.

What it means

Their general manager Chris Ballard was fired on October 19, and they already moved captains Quenton Nelson and DeForest Buckner at the deadline. This is a teardown, and Jones was the most expensive piece left to move.

For Richardson, it is a second chance that nobody saw coming a month ago. His first one back is against the Giants — the same franchise Jones was supposed to face with a point to prove. The revenge game still happens. It just has a different quarterback, and a different kind of point to prove.

Up next: the Colts host the Giants on Sunday, November 29.
