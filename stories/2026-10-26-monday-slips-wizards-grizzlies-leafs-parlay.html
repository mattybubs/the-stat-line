Monday's Slips: Wizards +2.5 in Charlotte, Grizzlies +1.5 in Utah, Leafs on the Moneyline — Plus a 2-Leg Parlay (~+260)

By Matt

Three clean plays for Monday night: Washington catching points against a Hornets team missing both starting guards, Memphis catching a point and a half as the NBA's surprise 3-0 team, and Toronto's rolling Leafs in Calgary. Skipped on purpose: MNF and the Thunder game, both on fresh injury news.

Three games, three picks, one parlay. Skipped on purpose: Cowboys at Eagles (Jalen Hurts on IR, Saquon Barkley questionable, DeVonta Smith out), Suns at Thunder (Jalen Williams left Sunday's game on a stretcher), Mavericks at Heat (Cooper Flagg questionable) and Pacers at Bucks (Ivica Zubac doubtful). No guessing on injury news — those four wait for another day.

GAME SLIP 1 — Washington Wizards (2-1) at Charlotte Hornets (2-1)

Tip: 7:00 PM ET, Spectrum Center, Charlotte — NBA League Pass, Monumental Sports Network, TV64.

The lines: Hornets -2.5 (-105), Wizards +2.5 (-115), total 229.5, moneyline Hornets -130 / Wizards +105 (BetMGM via SportsbookWire, updated 6:11 AM ET).

Injury report:

• Wizards: OUT — AJ Dybantsa (ankle), Tristan Vukcevic (hip), Jamir Watkins (ACL surgery). Dybantsa, the No. 1 pick, has yet to debut, so this is the team Washington has actually been all season.

• Hornets: OUT — Kon Knueppel (hamstring), Coby White (calf), Grant Williams (hamstring). Both starting guards, again.

Storylines:

1. The health gap is the whole bet. Charlotte's two starting guards are out, and the Hornets' 128-81 home loss to Atlanta on Friday showed what this roster looks like without them. Washington's net rating (+1.9) sits well above Charlotte's (-11.6).

2. Washington just passed a road test: a 124-120 win at Chicago on Saturday, with Trae Young posting 25 points and 9 assists and Kyshawn George adding 22 and 9 rebounds.

3. The case against: Charlotte just beat Miami 115-110 on Saturday behind Brandon Miller's 34, and the Hornets swept last season's series 4-0. This is a bet on the current injury report, not the rivalry history.

PICK: Wizards +2.5 (-115). Getting more than a possession with the healthier backcourt.

GAME SLIP 2 — Memphis Grizzlies (3-0) at Utah Jazz (1-2)

Tip: 9:00 PM ET, Delta Center, Salt Lake City — KJZZ, Jazz+, DAZN-MEM.

The lines: Jazz -1.5 (-120), Grizzlies +1.5, total 238.5, moneyline Jazz -120 / Grizzlies +102 (FanDuel).

Injury report:

• Grizzlies: QUESTIONABLE — Zach Edey (ankle), Karim Lopez (knee); OUT — Richie Saunders (knee). Edey and Lopez were both ruled out Sunday, so check the final report before tip.

• Jazz: OUT — Jusuf Nurkic (foot). Utah's interior takes the hit here.

Storylines:

1. Ty Jerome is the story of the NBA's first week. He is averaging 36.5 points per game, dropped 45 on Utah in Memphis' 123-103 win on October 21, and went 14-for-14 from the line in Sunday's 123-112 win at Sacramento.

2. The trends back the dog: Memphis is 3-0 straight up and 3-0 against the spread; Utah is 0-3 against the spread and just blew an 18-point lead in a 132-123 home loss to the Lakers on Sunday.

3. The case against: both teams are on the second night of a back-to-back, and if Edey sits again, Memphis' interior defense gets thinner against a Jazz team that can score in bunches.

PICK: Grizzlies +1.5. The 3-0 team catching points against a 1-2 team that hasn't covered yet.

GAME SLIP 3 — Toronto Maple Leafs (7-4-2) at Calgary Flames (4-6)

Puck drop: 8:30 PM ET, Scotiabank Saddledome, Calgary — ESPN+.

The lines: Maple Leafs -164, Flames +136, total 6.5, puck line Maple Leafs -1.5 (FanDuel).

Injury report:

• Maple Leafs: OUT — John Tavares (lower body), Chris Tanev, Max Domi, Dakota Joshua and others. The absences are real, but they are also priced in: Toronto has won four straight without them.

• Flames: OUT — Jonathan Huberdeau (hip), Samuel Honzek; QUESTIONABLE — captain Mikael Backlund (upper body), who has missed the last two games.

Storylines:

1. Toronto is rolling: 18 goals over its last three games, capped by a 7-2 battering of Edmonton on Saturday in which 13 different Leafs recorded a point. Auston Matthews has 9 goals in 13 games; William Nylander has 8.

2. The head-to-head is lopsided: Toronto has won eight straight against Calgary, a run stretching back to February 2022.

3. The case against: Calgary has won three straight itself, thumping Detroit 4-1 on Saturday, and its power play is 6-for-12 over its last four games. The Flames are live dogs at +136 — this is a bet on Toronto's form, not a fade of Calgary's.

PICK: Maple Leafs moneyline (-164). Paying chalk for the hotter team with the head-to-head edge.

THE PARLAY — 2 legs, roughly +260

• Wizards +2.5

• Grizzlies +1.5

Two prices, both sourced above, no lottery ticket. Roughly +260 at those prices — check your book, since the Wizards line has already moved between -1.5 and -2.5 this morning. If either leg scares you, play them straight — that's the whole point of keeping it to two.
