Sol de Janeiro drops longer-lasting Intense Perfume Mists

Sol de Janeiro's new Intense Perfume Mists promise up to 10 hours of wear on Cheirosa 62 and 59 — plus the all-new Cheirosa 74.

# Sol de Janeiro drops longer-lasting Intense Perfume Mists

The number-one request Sol de Janeiro heard from its community — longer wear — finally gets an answer. The Intense Perfume Mists go on sale today, October 12, online and in stores at Sephora, Boots, Lookfantastic, and Space NK.

What makes them "intense"

The new format delivers a richer, deeper expression of the brand's cult scents, built for up to 10 hours of scent — a direct answer to the community's top complaint about the original mists.

The lineup

Cheirosa 74 (all-new): Ripe pineapple · orange flower · solar ambery accord

Cheirosa 62 (intense): The cult classic, richer

Cheirosa 59 (intense): The cult favorite, richer

The headliner is Cheirosa 74, a brand-new golden gourmand built on ripe pineapple, orange flower, and the signature solar ambery accord.
