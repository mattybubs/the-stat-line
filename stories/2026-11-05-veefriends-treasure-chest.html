VeeFriends goes luxury: the Treasure Chest packs gemstone pins and graded coins into 5,555 boxes for Black Friday

By Matt

Gary Vaynerchuk's VeeFriends is going physical in the biggest way yet — a premium Treasure Chest with sapphire gemstone pins, NGC graded coins, and Super Stickers, limited to 5,555 boxes and launching Black Friday.

What happened

VeeFriends is ushering in a new era of premium collecting. The Gary Vaynerchuk-founded brand has unveiled the all-new physical Treasure Chest, a luxury collectible box that launches on Black Friday — and with only 5,555 chests in existence, it is the most exclusive VeeFriends Super Stickers and pin release yet.

Each Treasure Chest holds a randomized assortment of four premium collectibles, built around 21 VeeFriends characters. This is not another sticker drop. This is VeeFriends dressing up for the high-end shelf.

What's inside

Every chest contains four treasures, each featuring one of 21 possible VeeFriends characters:

• Sapphire gemstone pins framed in 18K gold plating

• NGC graded coins crafted in various metals, including pure silver and gold

• Debut Super Stickers in new sapphire colorways

• Classic pins

The scarcity is the point. Every collectible inside the chest — except the coins — is limited to 99 or fewer. The 5,555 chests break down into 555 sealed cases of 10 boxes each, and the sealed-case sale is the only chance to buy a case as a case.

How the sale works

The release rolls out in five waves, and the order matters — holders get first crack, the public gets last:

• Wave 1 — Sealed case sale: Monday, November 24 at 12 p.m. ET through Tuesday, November 25. Exclusive to select Series 1 holders, based on an account snapshot taken Monday, November 24 at 9 a.m. ET. One case per account. Price: $1,999.99 (43% off). Cases ship starting Friday, November 28.

• Wave 2 — Series 1 holder sale: Tuesday, November 25 at 12 p.m. ET through Wednesday, November 26, if inventory remains.

• Wave 3 — Series 2 and Iconics holder sale: Wednesday, November 26 through Friday, November 28 at 12 p.m. ET, if inventory remains. Price: $249.99 (29% off). Boxes ship the week of December 3.

• Wave 4 — Black Friday weekend live breaks: Friday, November 28 through Monday, December 1. VeeFriends goes live to break 55 reserved cases on Whatnot.

• Wave 5 — Public sale via EQL: Week of December 3, if inventory remains.

Series 1 Spectacular holders of the 21 featured characters also get a two-week window to claim one Treasure Chest. Hobby retailers have the box listed for preorder at $399.95, with a 10-box case at $3,989.95.

Why this one matters

VeeFriends started as an NFT project, but its physical collecting arm has been building for years. The Super Stickers line debuted at The National Sports Collectors Convention in July 2023, and the brand has been steadily turning its characters into real, hold-in-your-hand collectibles ever since — including a Spectacular Series boxed sticker set whose public pre-order draw ran November 4–5 at $99 a box, and a Monster Squad collaboration with Crocs for Halloween.

The Treasure Chest is the loudest statement of that direction yet: gemstones, gold plating, graded coins, and a print run smaller than most card-shop allocations. It lands as VeeFriends approaches its five-year anniversary in 2026, and as the brand takes a bigger live role in the hobby — VeeFriends joins Goldin, TikTok, and AFA at the Coins, Cards & CHAOS live shopping event at Valley Forge Casino Resort in King of Prussia, Pennsylvania, on November 14–15.

For collectors, the math is simple. There are 5,555 boxes, a holder-first sale ladder, and a public window that only opens if anything survives. If the Treasure Chest sells the way VeeFriends' last few drops have, the week of December 3 may not come at all.
