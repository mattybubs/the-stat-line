Sunday Afternoon Card: Lions -3.5 in the NFC North Shootout, Under 42.5 in Houston

By Matt

Two more for the afternoon window: Detroit laying a field goal and a hook at home in the week's biggest total, and the under in a Houston game where both offences are missing pieces.

The morning slip covered the 1:00 PM Vikings game and tonight's Raptors game. This is the afternoon card: two plays, one for the late window and one for the early window that still has time before kickoff.

GAME SLIP 1 — Green Bay Packers (4-2) at Detroit Lions (3-2)

Kickoff: 4:25 PM ET, Ford Field, Detroit — FOX.

The lines: Lions -3.5 (-105), Packers +3.5 (-115), total 54.5, moneyline Packers +160 / Lions -190 (consensus: Sharp Football Analysis, updated Saturday 7:51 PM ET; Vegas Insider, October 24)

Injury report:

• Packers: LB Zaire Franklin (shin) is out. RB Josh Jacobs is expected to get a normal starter's workload — NFL Network's Mike Garafolo reports Jacobs looked "great" in practice this week

• Lions: C Cade Mays (wrist) and S Brian Branch (Achilles) are questionable

Storylines:

1. This is the marquee game of the day: an NFC North showdown with the highest total on the board (54.5). Both offences can score, and Ford Field is where Detroit's offence plays its fastest

2. Jacobs back to a full workload matters more than the total suggests: Green Bay's run game is what keeps Detroit's pass rush honest, and a healthy Jacobs makes the Packers' play-action real

3. The case against: the dog has the better record. Green Bay is 4-2, Detroit is 3-2, and laying points against a divisional opponent with the better record is how favourites get burned — a Packers outright win is very live here

PICK: Lions -3.5 (-105). Honest caveat: this is a field-goal-and-a-hook spread in a game both teams can win. The bet is Detroit at home in a shootout, not a belief that Green Bay can't score.

GAME SLIP 2 — New York Giants (3-2-1) at Houston Texans (2-4)

Kickoff: 1:00 PM ET, NRG Stadium, Houston — FOX.

The lines: Texans -10.5 (-108), Giants +10.5 (-112), total 42.5, moneyline Giants +390 / Texans -520 (Vegas Insider, October 24)

Injury report:

• Texans: OUT — RB David Montgomery (groin), OT Blake Fisher (thumb), S Reed Blankenship (concussion/hand), S Jalen Pitre (shoulder). WR Tank Dell (knee) is questionable

• Giants: OUT — LB Tremaine Edmunds (shoulder), CB Greg Newsome II (concussion)

Storylines:

1. The number to play here isn't the 10.5-point spread — it's the total. Houston's offence is missing its starting running back and possibly its top receiver, and the Giants' offence has not shown it can win a shootout on the road

2. Both teams' recent form points the same way: low-scoring, field-position football, with Houston's defence — even banged up in the secondary — good enough to make the Giants earn every point

3. The case against: two starting safeties out for Houston is a real crack in the secondary, and if Dell plays, the Texans' offence has a gear the Giants' defence may not match. A Houston explosion sinks this

PICK: Under 42.5 (-108). Honest caveat: 42.5 is a low number, and one defensive touchdown or a short field can wreck an under. The bet is two limited offences, not a belief that nobody scores.

THE PARLAY — 2 legs, ~+276

• Lions -3.5 (-105)

• Under 42.5, Giants at Texans (-108)

Detroit at home in the shootout, Houston in the grinder. If either leg scares you, play them straight.
