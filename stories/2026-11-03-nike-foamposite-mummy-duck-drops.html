Nike's 'Mummy Duck' Foamposite Pro Hits Retail Today — Oregon's Spooky PE, Finally for Everyone

By Matt

The Nike Air Foamposite Pro 'Mummy Duck' — born as a University of Oregon player exclusive in 2025 — gets its first retail release today, November 3, at 10 a.m. ET on SNKRS and select retailers, listed at $230 in men's sizing.

Oregon's spookiest player exclusive is finally for sale. The Nike Air Foamposite Pro "Mummy Duck" — a colorway built for the Ducks' football program in 2025 and worn by Oregon players last September — gets its first-ever retail release today, Tuesday, November 3, at 10 a.m. ET on the Nike SNKRS app and at select retailers.

What you're actually buying

The shoe is a Halloween concept that missed Halloween by a few days, and it's better for it — this isn't a costume shoe, it's a Foamposite. The deep iridescent shell carries the mummy treatment, with Volt accents and glow-in-the-dark Swooshes wrapped in tattered, mummy-like fabric detailing, all on the classic Foamposite Pro silhouette. The colorway is listed as Black/White/Barely Volt, style code IZ7806-001, in men's sizing.

The price is listed at $230 in most release calendars — Sports Illustrated, JustFreshKicks and Sneaker Bar Detroit all list $230 — though a couple of outlets list $240, so check the SNKRS listing at drop time rather than trusting any single calendar.

Why this one matters

Player exclusives used to stay player exclusives. The "Mummy Duck" is part of a bigger Nike push around college football releases this season — the Foamposite Pro "NCAA Pack" pairs four styles with matching cleat counterparts — and the Mummy Duck story doesn't even end today: a matching Vaporposite Pro football cleat in the same theme drops December 15 on SNKRS for $200.

For collectors, the timing is the thing. This week is stacked — the Space Jam 30th anniversary Jordan pack lands Thursday, November 5, the New Balance 990v7 arrives Friday, November 6, and the Air Jordan 6 "White Infrared" closes the week Saturday, November 7. The Mummy Duck kicks the week off, and as a first-time retail release of a real PE, it's the one from this week most likely to be talked about after the week is over.
