ARC Raiders hotfix 2.1.1 patches locked-room, duplication exploits

By Matt

Embark is deploying ARC Raiders hotfix 2.1.1 on Steam to close locked-room access and item-duplication exploits — three days after update 2.1.0 opened the PvE-toggle beta.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Embark Studios is rolling out ARC Raiders hotfix 2.1.1 today, a targeted patch aimed at the exploits that have been ruining runs for the last few days. Deployment is underway via Steam, per the studio's announcement.

What's being fixed

The hotfix addresses two categories of issues:

• Locked-room access — methods that let players get into locked rooms (the maps' high-tier loot rooms, normally gated behind ultra-rare keycards) without the key.

• Item duplication — exploits that duplicated items, the kind of economy-breaking trick that wrecks an extraction shooter where every piece of gear has real stakes.

No details yet on whether this is the fiery "very hot fix" treatment from previous locked-room patches or a quieter close of the holes — but given Embark's history with this exact exploit, expect more punishment for repeat offenders in the notes.

Report, don't spread

Embark's ask is pointed: report exploits through the Discord support inbox or @ScrappyMail rather than spreading the methods publicly. Private channels let the team act on issues without turning them into tutorials — and keep the duped loot economy from spiraling before the fix lands.

Context: three days after 2.1.0

The hotfix lands just three days after update 2.1.0, which opened the PvE-toggle beta alongside broad gameplay, map, and stability fixes. Fast follow-up on exploit reports this early in the beta window is a good sign — Embark is watching the PvE beta's new-player economy closely, and this is the kind of swift response that keeps a trial wave (remember the 166k Steam peak during the free window) from turning into a "don't bother, it's full of cheaters" reputation.
