Cutter Gauthier's goal streak hits eight games — 15 goals in eight games, and the Ducks still lost

By Matt

Cutter Gauthier scored his 17th goal of the season in Anaheim's 6-2 loss to the Islanders, extending his goal streak to eight games and his point streak to ten. Fifteen goals in eight games, three hat tricks in the run — and the Ducks lost by four anyway.

The night

Cutter Gauthier cannot stop scoring. The Anaheim Ducks cannot stop losing anyway.

Gauthier scored his 17th goal of the season on Saturday night in a 6-2 loss to the New York Islanders, extending his goal streak to eight games and his point streak to ten. He has scored 15 goals during the eight-game streak — including three hat tricks — which means he has scored nearly as many goals in the last eight games as most players score in a season.

The goal came with 3:23 left in the second period, a small bit of light in a game that was already getting away from Anaheim. Simon Holmstrom recorded his first career hat trick for the Islanders, Matthew Schaefer added three assists, and New York pulled away to earn at least a point for the fourth consecutive game.

The run

This is the second time in a week Gauthier has forced his way into this recap. Last Sunday he scored his third hat trick in five games — the first player in NHL history with three hat tricks in the first 10 days of a calendar month — and had 13 goals in a six-game goal streak. A week later the numbers are somehow better: 15 goals in eight games, 17 on the season, and a point streak that's now at ten.

The context that makes it stranger is the team around him. Anaheim lost 6-2 on a night its star scored again, and the Ducks are still just trying to keep up with him.

The Ducks host Calgary on Sunday. Gauthier goes for nine straight.
