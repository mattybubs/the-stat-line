Mikko Rantanen factored into all four Stars goals — the tying goal with five seconds left, then the OT-winner assist — in a 4-3 stunner over Vegas

By Matt

Mikko Rantanen had a goal and three assists — a point on every Dallas goal — including the tying goal with five seconds left in regulation and the assist on Miro Heiskanen's overtime winner, as the Stars erased a 3-1 deficit in the final 1:18 to beat the Golden Knights 4-3.

The night

The Dallas Stars were dead. Down 3-1 to the Vegas Golden Knights with less than two minutes to play in regulation at T-Mobile Arena on Tuesday night, goalie pulled, season's road trip hanging in the balance. Then Mikko Rantanen took the game over.

Rantanen finished with a goal and three assists — a point on all four Dallas goals — as the Stars scored twice in the final 1:18 of regulation and won it 4-3 in overtime.

Wyatt Johnston started the comeback at 18:42 of the third, scoring 6-on-5 from Roope Hintz and Rantanen to make it 3-2. Then, with five seconds left, Rantanen threw a shot toward the net from along the right boards. It deflected in off Vegas defenseman Rasmus Andersson. Tie game, 3-3.

In overtime, Rantanen did it again — feeding Miro Heiskanen for the winner at 3:36, a tap-in from the bottom of the left circle into an open net after a scramble near the crease.

The return

Heiskanen's goal was a story on its own. The defenseman was playing his first game back after missing five with an upper-body injury, and the overtime winner was the fourth of his career. He logged 26:44 over 25 shifts, blocked three shots, and scored on his only shot of the night.

Vegas had controlled the game until the collapse. William Karlsson scored twice — his first two-goal game since October 16, 2025 — and Brett Howden added a goal as the Golden Knights built a 3-1 third-period lead. Nils Lundkvist had pulled Dallas within one at 10:59 of the third, finishing a rush off a Rantanen feed, but Howden answered 32 seconds later to restore the two-goal cushion.

Then the final 1:18 happened. Two 6-on-5 goals, 73 seconds apart, both with Jake Oettinger on the bench for the extra attacker. Oettinger finished with 24 saves.

The context

The win was Dallas' third straight and moved the Stars to 13-6-2 — and within two points of Vegas in the Western standings. It was also their fifth overtime win in 21 games. Rantanen's line — one goal, three assists, plus-4, five shots in 24:24 — is the kind of night that defines a player's value to a team: he did not just produce, he touched every goal that mattered.

Vegas, meanwhile, lost its third straight (0-1-2) and fell to 13-6-4. The two teams do it all over again Thursday night in the same building.

What it means

A two-goal lead with 1:18 left is supposed to be over. Rantanen made it a footnote. When one player factors into every goal in a comeback win — the setup, the equalizer, and the overtime winner — that is not a hot streak, that is a takeover.
