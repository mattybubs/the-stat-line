Panthers -1.5 (+132): Detroit's Penalty Kill Meets a Florida Team That Scores at Home

By Matt

Detroit's penalty kill has allowed 14 goals in 45 chances and its goaltending has been shaky. Florida, at home and laying 1.5 at plus money, is the value side.

The game slip

Matchup: Detroit Red Wings (6-5-3) at Florida Panthers (8-4-2)

Puck drop: Monday, November 2, 7:00 PM ET — ESPN+, Amerant Bank Arena, Sunrise

Line: Panthers -182 | Puck line: Panthers -1.5 (+132) | Total 5.5 (via FanDuel, Monday morning)

Red Wings injury report: On injured reserve — Dylan Larkin, Mason Appleton, Marco Kasper, Simon Edvinsson. Albert Johansson is out. Detroit is missing its captain and a chunk of its top six and blue line.

Panthers injury report: On injured reserve — Brad Marchand, Aleksander Barkov, Jacob Markstrom, Jonah Gadjovich. Florida is not healthy either — Barkov and Marchand are massive absences — but the Panthers are 8-4-2 anyway and have won three of four at Amerant Bank Arena.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. Detroit's penalty kill is a problem: 14 goals allowed in 45 chances. Florida's power play does not need many invitations, and Detroit keeps handing them out.

2. The goaltending gap is real. John Gibson's .860 save percentage has not held up, while Daniil Tarasov has looked much steadier at .940 with a shutout. If Gibson starts, Detroit needs its offense to win a shootout; if Tarasov starts, the number still works because Florida is the better team at even strength too.

3. Detroit arrives off a 5-4 loss to St. Louis on Saturday, while Florida just beat Winnipeg 5-2 on Friday and has won three of four at home. Viktor Arvidsson (eight goals, four on the power play), Alex DeBrincat (six goals, nine assists) and Lucas Raymond (14 points) keep Detroit dangerous — but dangerous is not the same as reliable right now.

The bet

Panthers -1.5 at +132. Getting plus money on a home favorite against a team missing its captain, with a leaky penalty kill and shaky goaltending, is the kind of number you take and do not overthink. Florida does not need a blowout to cover this — a 4-2 or 5-3 win, with an empty-netter doing the final damage, gets it done.

The honest caveat: Florida's own injury list is long, and if Barkov's absence finally catches up with the Panthers' center depth, Detroit's top scorers can keep this within a goal. And puck-line bets always carry the empty-net lottery ticket in both directions. But at +132, the price pays for the risk.
