TSL Bets: Wild at Canadiens Over 6.5 (-110) — two hot offenses, depleted blue lines, backup goalies

By Matt

Montreal has a point in ten straight and is scoring 3.8 a game in that stretch. Minnesota's offense runs through Kaprizov. Both teams are down key defensemen and goalies. Over 6.5 (-110) is the play in Montreal.

The game

Minnesota Wild (12-3-4, 28 points) at Montreal Canadiens (11-3-3, 25 points) — 7:00 PM ET, Centre Bell

Wild -125 moneyline, total 6.5 (DraftKings, as of November 10, 04:04 ET). Two of the best records in the league, and two offenses that are rolling.

Injuries — Minnesota: Filip Gustavsson is on injured reserve with a hip injury, Jared Spurgeon is on injured reserve with a lower-body injury, Brock Faber is on injured reserve with a skull injury, and Michael McCarron is day-to-day with an upper-body injury. The Wild are down their starting goalie and two of their top defensemen.

Injuries — Montreal: Ivan Demidov is on injured reserve with an ankle injury, Mike Matheson is out with a lower-body injury, Samuel Montembeault is day-to-day with a lower-body injury, Jake Evans is day-to-day with a hand injury, and Josh Anderson is day-to-day for personal reasons. The Canadiens are down a top defenseman and their starting goalie is questionable.

Storylines: Montreal has a point in ten straight games and is averaging 3.8 goals a game in that stretch. Minnesota's offense runs through Kirill Kaprizov, and the Wild have been scoring with anyone. Both teams are missing key defensemen and goalies — the blue lines are depleted and the creases are backup territory. When two hot offenses meet two patched-up defenses, the total is where the value is.

The bet

Wild at Canadiens Over 6.5 (-110). Two top offenses, depleted blue lines, backup goalies on both sides, and a Montreal team that has scored 3.8 a game for ten straight. The total is 6.5 for a reason — it's a high number — but the conditions are exactly the ones that produce seven-goal games. Take the over.

The honest caveat

Six and a half is a high number, and high numbers lose more often than people think. If either backup goalie gets hot, or if the game tightens up in the third period with both teams protecting a lead, this can land on six and burn the ticket. And both teams are good enough defensively at full strength that the depleted versions still aren't pushovers. The over is the right side, but it's a sweat — expect the seventh goal to come late, if it comes at all.

The pick

Wild at Canadiens Over 6.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $19.09. Two hot offenses, two patched-up defenses, and a total that the game script supports.
