Costco's Pokémon 30th Anniversary drop sold out in minutes — the ETB bundle went in under 40

By Matt

Costco put a 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box + Booster Bundle 2-pack online Friday for $125.99 and it was gone in under 40 minutes; the Ascended Heroes mini tins lasted about eight. Membership, a queue, and a price under combined MSRP — the hobby's hottest chase keeps finding new ways to sell out.

The drop

Costco ran two Pokémon drops online on Friday, November 13, and both vanished fast. The headline item was a 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box + Booster Bundle 2-pack — two ETBs and two booster bundles, 30 packs in all — for $125.99, limit one per membership. It sold out in under 40 minutes. The Ascended Heroes mini tins bundle — 10 tins and 10 promo cards for $86.99 — lasted about eight.

Both drops ran through an online queue, with shoppers placed at random once it went live, and both required a Costco membership. The price is the tell: an ETB alone carries a $49.99 MSRP and a booster bundle runs about $26.99, so two sets at retail would be roughly $154. Costco's $125.99 undercut that by nearly $30 — and the hobby noticed.

The rest of the day

Costco was not the only register ringing. Macy's put up the 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection, the Ditto Premium Collection and the Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box, limit two. Target cycled the 30th Celebration Binder Collection and the Unova Heavy Hitters Premium Collection in and out of stock through the afternoon. Walmart started sending out results from its November 12 drawings for the 30th Celebration UPC, and Cynthia's Garchomp ex Premium Collection was checking out there for $49.97.

Why it keeps happening

The 30th Anniversary line has been the hobby's white whale since the Day and Night Ultra-Premium Collections launched November 6 at $179.99 and started trading at double retail. A week later, Delta Reign singles are spiking while the Star Trek set crashes on release day — and every restock, at every retailer, still clears in minutes. The demand is not cooling. The supply just keeps arriving in drops small enough to disappear.
