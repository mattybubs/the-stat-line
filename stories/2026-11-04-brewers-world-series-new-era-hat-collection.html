New Era's Brewers World Series Collection Gives a First Title Three Distinct Looks

By Matt

New Era's Brewers World Series collection covers Milwaukee's first championship three ways: a black Locker Room set for the celebration, team-colored side-patch caps in six silhouettes, and a white Parade cap — already sold out in one style.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Milwaukee waited a long time for a first World Series title, and New Era is marking it the way only New Era can: with a lot of caps. The Brewers World Series collection, now listed at New Era's official store, gives the same milestone three distinct treatments — one for the celebration itself, one for everyday team colors, and one for the parade.

The three approaches

The Locker Room Collection is built around the championship's first moments. Its black 9FORTY A-Frame and 9TWENTY adjustable caps put "World Series Champions" across the front, with the Brewers logo centered in the composition — the look of the clubhouse celebration, made wearable.

The Side Patch designs take the opposite route: team-colored caps carrying commemorative side patches. Six silhouettes carry the treatment — the 59FIFTY, Low Profile 59FIFTY, 9FIFTY, 9SEVENTY Stretch Snap, 9FORTY M-Crown, and 9TWENTY — so the choice is about fit and format (fitted, snapback, or adjustable) without changing the central design idea.

Then there is the Parade cap, and it breaks away from both. The Parade 9FIFTY A-Frame uses a white crown and gray visor as a lighter setting for the Commissioner's Trophy, positioned behind an embroidered waving flag reading "World Champions '26." It is the most distinct piece in the collection — and, per the current listing, the one already marked sold out.

Why the hat story is the story here

Championship merch usually collapses into one design stamped on everything. What makes this collection worth noting is that it doesn't. The Locker Room set is for the night it happened. The side-patch set is for wearing a Brewers cap that happens to carry a World Series patch, long after the celebration. The Parade cap is for the day the city actually celebrates together. Three different jobs, three different caps, one title.

That matters more than usual for Milwaukee because this is a first. The Brewers beat the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 to win the franchise's first World Series, and "2026" on a side patch is not a generic anniversary number — it is the year it finally happened. Availability varies by style, and the Parade cap's sold-out listing is a snapshot of retail status at a particular moment, not a claim about permanent rarity or future value. Check New Era's official store for current stock, pricing, and sizing before assuming any style is still there.

The bottom line for collectors

If you want the celebration look, the Locker Room set is the obvious pick. If you want a cap you will actually wear every week, the side-patch range — especially the 59FIFTY fitted — is the one built for that. And if you wanted the Parade cap, you may already be late: that white-and-gray A-Frame was the first to go. For a first championship, that is probably the most Milwaukee outcome of all — the parade cap selling out before the parade feeling has even faded.
