TSL Bets parlay: Higgins over 73.5, Lions -6.5, Broncos -2.5 (about +560)

By Matt

Three legs, about +560: Tee Higgins over 73.5 receiving yards in Madrid (-122), the Lions -6.5 in Miami (-110), and the Broncos -2.5 in Carolina (-110).

The games

Cincinnati Bengals (5-2) at Atlanta Falcons (3-5) — 9:30 AM ET, Santiago Bernabéu, Madrid, NFL Network

Bengals -3.5, total 52.5, moneyline Bengals -163 / Falcons +160 (optimal-bet). The highest total on the board this week. Injuries: Atlanta is without Brian Robinson Jr. (hamstring) and Zachariah Branch (hamstring). Cincinnati is without safety Kyle Dugger (quad). Storylines: the Bengals gave up 36 points to a Titans team that had been averaging 14.5 a game, and the Falcons lost 16-0 to the Buccaneers in a game played through heavy rain. Both teams are coming off ugly weeks, and the total of 52.5 says the market expects points in Madrid.

Detroit Lions (5-2) at Miami Dolphins (1-6) — 1:00 PM ET, Hard Rock Stadium

Lions -6.5 (-110), total 49.5, moneyline Lions -310 / Dolphins +250 (bet365). Detroit scores the third-most points in the league at 29.0 per game and ranks eighth in offensive DVOA. Miami allows the ninth-most points at 25.9 per game. Injuries: Detroit is without Jack Campbell (neck, IR), Cade Mays (ankle), D.J. Reed (hip), Kerby Joseph (knee), and D.J. Wonnum (concussion). Amon-Ra St. Brown (ribs) is questionable but is good to go. Miami's questionable list is mostly depth: Kaytron Allen (quad), Jaylen Wright (quad), and Malik Herring (neck). Storylines: the Lions' offense against one of the league's worst defenses, and a Dolphins team that has covered three of its last four — but covering and winning are different things.

Denver Broncos (6-2) at Carolina Panthers (3-4) — 1:00 PM ET, Bank of America Stadium, CBS

Broncos -2.5 (-120), total 44.5, moneyline Broncos -146 / Panthers +124 (FanDuel via Sportsbook Wire). Denver is 6-2 against the spread this season. Injuries: Denver is without Kris Abrams-Draine (ankle), Brandon Jones (hamstring), and Eyioma Uwazurike (shoulder), with Talanoa Hufanga (ankle) questionable. Carolina's offensive line is the story — Taylor Moton is on IR, Ikem Ekwonu is on PUP, Damien Lewis (knee), Jaelan Phillips (hamstring), Bobby Okereke (foot), and Will Lee III (hamstring) are all out. Bryce Young is on the injury report with an ankle issue but was a full participant. Storylines: Carolina's offense managed just 266 and 258 total yards in its last two losses, Young was sacked six times at Green Bay, and Denver's pass rush (2.9 sacks per game) faces a line that allows 3.3 sacks per game, the second-most in the league. The Broncos have won and covered both of their road games this season.

The legs

Leg 1 — Tee Higgins over 73.5 receiving yards (-122, BetRivers)

Higgins has 43 catches for 726 yards and four touchdowns in seven games. He had 10 catches for 155 yards last week against Tennessee, and 303 yards in the two games since the bye. He has topped 90 yards in all but two games this season. The Falcons rank 24th in DVOA against No. 2 wide receivers and allow 148.2 yards per game to the position. This is the highest total on the board, in a dome-like atmosphere in Madrid, against a defense that gives up yards to receivers. Higgins over 73.5 is the cleanest receiving prop on the slate.

Leg 2 — Detroit Lions -6.5 (-110)

The Lions score 29 points a game. The Dolphins give up 25.9. Detroit is the better team by a touchdown on paper, and the spread is 6.5. Miami has covered three of its last four, but the Lions' offense is the kind that turns a cover into a blowout — and the Dolphins' injury list is mostly on the defensive side of the ball. This is the chalk leg, and it is chalk for a reason.

Leg 3 — Denver Broncos -2.5 (-110)

The Broncos are 6-2 against the spread, they have won and covered both road games, and they are playing a Panthers team whose offensive line is held together with tape and whose quarterback was sacked six times last week. Denver's pass rush against Carolina's line is the biggest mismatch in this game, and a short road number of 2.5 does not reflect how far apart these teams are right now. Covers' top pick is the Broncos.

Why these three together

Three different games, three different kinds of bet, one story: the better team, the better receiver, and the better matchup, all at fair prices. The parlay pays about +560 because each leg is a real bet on its own, and putting them together is how you get paid for being right three times.

The honest caveat

Parlays multiply risk, not just payout. Higgins has to clear 73.5 against a Falcons defense that, for all its flaws, just shut out Tampa Bay — even if that was in the rain. The Lions are laying 6.5 on the road against a Dolphins team that has covered three of four, and Detroit's defense is missing four starters. The Broncos are laying points on the road with a banged-up secondary of their own. Any one of these legs can lose on its own. That is why the price is +560 and not -560. Bet it as a fun ticket on three good reads, not as a mortgage payment.

The pick

3-leg parlay, about +560: Tee Higgins over 73.5 receiving yards (-122) + Detroit Lions -6.5 (-110) + Denver Broncos -2.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $66.30. A morning prop in Madrid, a road favorite in Miami, and a road favorite in Carolina. That is the bet.
