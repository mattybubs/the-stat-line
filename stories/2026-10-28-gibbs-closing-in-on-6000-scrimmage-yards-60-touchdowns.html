Gibbs Closing in on 6,000 Scrimmage Yards, 60 Touchdowns in Four Seasons

By Matt

Jahmyr Gibbs enters Sunday against Minnesota with 5,919 scrimmage yards since Detroit drafted him in 2023 — 81 yards short of 6,000 — and on pace for a single-season record 2,522.

The numbers

Jahmyr Gibbs enters Sunday's game against Minnesota closing in on two milestones in just his fourth NFL season since Detroit drafted him in 2023:

Scrimmage yards since 2023: 5,919

Yards short of 6,000: 81

Touchdowns in four seasons: Closing in on 60

17-game pace this season: 2,522 scrimmage yards

The pace is the story

The raw total is impressive on its own — 5,919 scrimmage yards in under four seasons. But the pace is what turns this into a record watch: at 2,522 scrimmage yards over 17 games, Gibbs would break the all-time single-season record.

What to watch Sunday

Gibbs needs 81 scrimmage yards against Minnesota to reach 6,000 for his career — a mark well within a single game's work at his current pace. The 60-touchdown milestone is next in line, and the single-season pace gives every remaining game this season record-chase stakes.
