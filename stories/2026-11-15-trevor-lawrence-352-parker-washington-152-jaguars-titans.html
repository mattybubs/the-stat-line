Trevor Lawrence throws for 352 and three scores as the Jaguars hang 40 on Tennessee

By Matt

Trevor Lawrence and Parker Washington took over in Nashville — a career day for Washington, a 20,000-yard milestone for Lawrence, and a 40-23 Jaguars win that keeps Jacksonville alone atop the AFC South.

The day

The Jacksonville Jaguars beat the Tennessee Titans 40-23 at Nissan Stadium on Sunday, and the passing game did almost all of the damage. Trevor Lawrence finished 25-of-35 for 352 yards with three touchdowns and one interception (a 120.2 passer rating), adding 27 yards on four carries. Parker Washington caught seven passes for 152 yards and a touchdown on nine targets, with a long gain of 50. Jacksonville never trailed.

A first half for the record books

Washington did nearly all of his damage before halftime. He had six catches for 148 yards in the first half alone — a career high for a half, and more than Tennessee's entire passing offense had managed by the break. His two-yard touchdown catch on the game's opening drive made it 7-0, and he had a direct hand in all 16 of Jacksonville's first-half points: a 20-yard toe-tap catch to set up a Cam Little field goal, a 50-yard catch-and-run to set up another, and a 32-yard grab on the final drive of the half to set up a third.

Lawrence was just as hot early, throwing for 234 yards in the first half — a career high for a half. Jacksonville led 16-10 at the break, and the lead never really felt threatened again.

A milestone and a small club

The yardage pushed Lawrence past 20,000 career passing yards. It also put him in select company for the season: he is only the fifth quarterback this year to throw for 300-plus yards and three touchdowns while completing at least 70 percent of his passes. The one blemish was a tipped-ball interception.

The supporting cast

Bhayshul Tuten ran for 88 yards on 26 carries and scored the game-sealing touchdown, adding 25 receiving yards for 113 scrimmage yards. Cam Little kicked four field goals, from 46, 48, 36 and 40 yards. The other two touchdown passes went to Brenton Strange, a three-yarder on fourth-and-2 that stretched the lead to 23-13, and rookie Josh Cameron, a 25-yarder set up by Lawrence's 15-yard scramble on third-and-11.

Tennessee got a solid day from Cam Ward, who completed 28 of 39 passes for 231 yards and two touchdowns with an interception, but the Titans were outgained 459-327 and never got the stops they needed. The win is Jacksonville's third straight, and at 7-2 the Jaguars sit alone on top of the AFC South. Tennessee fell to 1-8 and 0-5 at home.
