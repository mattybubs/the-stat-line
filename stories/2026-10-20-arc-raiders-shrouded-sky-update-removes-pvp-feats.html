ARC Raiders' Shrouded Sky update removes PvP feats

By Matt

Embark's Shrouded Sky update went live overnight in ARC Raiders, cutting PvP feats entirely and doubling down on PvE — plus a new Hurricane map condition and the Dam Battlegrounds area.

Embark pushed the Shrouded Sky update live overnight in ARC Raiders, and it's the biggest philosophical shift the game has made in a while: PvP feats are gone.

What's gone

Feats — the challenge-style objectives that pay out for completing specific tasks — no longer reward fighting other raiders. The removed PvP feats specifically rewarded:

• Damaging other raiders (with certain ammo types and weapons)

• Searching other raiders' bodies after taking them down

That's the whole PvP-feat category wiped out in one patch.

What's new

• Expanded PvE feats take the place of the removed PvP ones — more challenges oriented around fighting the environment instead of each other.

• Free feat rerolls, up to three times per day. If you don't like the feat you rolled, you can swap it without spending anything.

• Hurricane map condition — a new weather state for expeditions.

• Dam Battlegrounds area — a brand-new playable area.

What it means

Feats were one of the main systems nudging players toward PvP: if your daily challenge said "damage raiders with X ammo," you had a reason to hunt other players. Removing those feats outright takes the incentive off the table, and pairing the removal with an expansion of PvE feats makes Embark's direction unmistakable — the Rust Belt is being pushed toward player-vs-environment play, with PvP left as something players choose for its own sake rather than something the game pays them to do.
