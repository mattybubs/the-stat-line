Palace Winter 2026 Drop 4 Lands Today — Plus Patta x Carhartt WIP Drops Tomorrow

By Matt

Palace's Winter 2026 Drop 4 goes live today in the UK, EU, and US with weather-ready GORE-TEX shells and puffers — and Patta x Carhartt WIP's 'Hartt and Soul' capsule lands tomorrow.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Palace Skateboards keeps its Friday rhythm rolling. Winter 2026 Drop 4 lands today across the UK, EU, and US, built around weather-ready GORE-TEX shells and insulated puffers — and tomorrow brings Patta x Carhartt WIP's "Hartt and Soul" capsule, a workwear rework of washed chore coats and double-knee pants with co-branded insignias. (Hypebeast)

What Drop 4 brings

This week's drop centers on the Dascar range, cut across a spread of Realtree camo patterns. The Dascar Jacket anchors the week in three versions — Realtree Cherry, Realtree Blue, and a straight black option — while the Dascar Hockey Jersey takes the theme into sportswear in Realtree Shadows and Realtree Blue, and the Dascar Shirt narrows it to Realtree Edge Blue. (Hypebeast)

Balancing the camo, the On Ya Bike jersey and hoodie bring solid colorways — jersey in blue and navy, hoodie in Chocco Brown, black, and blue — layerable staples under or alongside the heavier outerwear.

Release timing

The drop goes live Friday, October 23 at 11:00 a.m. BST in the UK, Europe, and the US, with Japan, China, Korea, and Australia following Saturday, October 24 at 11:00 a.m. local time.

Tomorrow: Patta x Carhartt WIP "Hartt and Soul"

The second headline of the weekend is the Patta x Carhartt WIP capsule — washed chore coats and double-knee pants reworked in workwear form with co-branded insignias. Two serious drops, two days, one loud weekend for streetwear.
