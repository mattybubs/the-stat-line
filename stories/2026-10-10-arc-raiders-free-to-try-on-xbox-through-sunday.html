# ARC Raiders is free to try on Xbox through Sunday

If you've been waiting for a reason to jump into Embark's extraction shooter, this is it: ARC Raiders is free to try on Xbox this weekend — for every member, Game Pass or not.

The deal

• What's free: ARC Raiders, full game access

• Who: Every Xbox member — no Game Pass subscription required

• Window: Free Play Days runs through Sunday, October 11

• Bonus for Game Pass members: Screamer and Family Man are thrown in too

Why now

The free weekend lines up with Frozen Trail, the game's biggest update yet — a frozen Pendola Pass map, new enemies, and a player-count surge that just hit a six-month peak. It's a no-risk way to see whether the extraction loop clicks for you before spending a dime.
