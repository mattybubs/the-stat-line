Friday's Slips: Celtics -2.5, Timberwolves -1.5, Pistons PK — Plus a 3-Leg Parlay (~+500)

By Matt

Friday night NBA: Knicks at Celtics, Timberwolves at Magic, Pistons at Heat. Full verified breakdowns — lines, injuries, storylines — three best bets plus a 3-leg parlay.

Three games, three picks, one parlay. No NFL tonight — TNF was last night. All lines below are Friday-morning consensus across 11 US sportsbooks (moneymavericksports.com odds board; corroborated by pickdawgz.com and Covers for the Celtics and Pistons). Shop the numbers — lines move.

GAME SLIP 1 — New York Knicks (0-1) at Boston Celtics (0-1)

Tip: 7:00 PM ET, TD Garden (Boston home opener) — Prime Video (national)

The lines: Celtics -2.5, O/U 223.5, ML Knicks +115 / Celtics -135 (consensus Friday morning)

Injury report:

• Knicks: No rotation player listed — Brunson, Towns, Bridges, Anunoby, Hart all clear (two-way G-Leaguers only, non-injury)

• Celtics: Devin Carter OUT (mononucleosis, no timetable — doesn't affect rotation); Mitchell Robinson (ankle) practiced Thursday, off the report, starting; everyone else available

Storylines:

1. Both 0-1 — the Knicks spoiled ring night losing 106-100 to the Sixers (Brunson 6-of-19, Towns in foul trouble); the Celtics blew a 22-point lead in Detroit on a 23-0 fourth-quarter Pistons run

2. Paul George dropped 38 in his Celtics debut — a franchise record for a debut — and Knicks wings Bridges/Anunoby have to cool him

3. Mitchell Robinson faces his old team in his first game as a Celtic, in Boston's home opener

PICK: Celtics -2.5. Honest caveat: Brunson shot 6-19 on Tuesday and won't often be that bad; it's game 2 and Boston's fourth-quarter defense was a genuine disaster. ML -139 is the safer route if the spread scares you.

GAME SLIP 2 — Minnesota Timberwolves (1-0) at Orlando Magic (0-1)

Tip: 7:00 PM ET, Kia Center, Orlando — local TV / League Pass

The lines: Timberwolves -1.5, O/U 235.5, ML Wolves -125 / Magic +105

Injury report:

• Wolves: Donte DiVincenzo OUT (Achilles). Anthony Edwards: 45 points + franchise opening-night-record nine threes in Wednesday's 131-106 win in Miami; LaMelo Ball added 21

• Magic: Anthony Black (right ankle) OUT, Jonathan Isaac (knee) OUT — both ruled out again Friday; Noah Penda (hamstring) out, Izaiyah Nelson (ankle surgery) out; Jamal Cain limped off Wednesday but is NOT on the report. Paolo Banchero: 35/10 in the 132-125 home loss to Atlanta

Storylines:

1. The hottest scorer in the league (Ant's 45) against a Magic backcourt missing its two best perimeter defenders

2. Orlando already gave up 132 at home to the Hawks; Minnesota just hung 131 in Miami — this total (234.5) says shootout

3. No back-to-back angle tonight; both rested since Wednesday

PICK: Timberwolves -1.5. Honest caveat: Banchero can keep any game within a bucket by himself, and Orlando's home crowd + 0-1 desperation makes -1.5 a sweat. Wolves by a possession.

GAME SLIP 3 — Detroit Pistons (1-0) at Miami Heat (0-1)

Tip: 8:00 PM ET, Kaseya Center, Miami — local TV

The lines: Pick'em — Pistons +1.5 / Heat -1.5, O/U 236.5, ML Pistons -110 / Heat -110 (consensus Friday morning). Some books still show Heat -1.5/-110; the market has moved toward Detroit overnight — shop the number.

Injury report:

• Pistons: Taurean Prince probable (personal). Last year's 59-22 East top seed; opened with a 23-0 fourth-quarter run to beat Boston (Cade Cunningham and Jalen Duren 22 each)

• Heat: NOBODY — official report fully clean. Giannis: 35 pts on 13-of-19 in his Heat debut (a franchise debut record) — and a 131-106 loss. His quote: "Our effort has to be better. It starts with me."

Storylines:

1. The team that finished as the East's #1 seed last season is basically a pick'em against a team that just lost by 25

2. Detroit's defense (led the league in opponent true-shooting %, 1st in steals) vs. a Heat offense whose own superstar says it doesn't trust each other yet

3. Spoelstra bounce-back: full-strength Heat at home after a public embarrassment is exactly the spot Miami usually answers in

PICK: Pistons PK (ML -110 fine; +1.5 widely available). Honest caveat — this is the shakiest leg of the three: Giannis at home off a humbling loss with a full roster is a real bounce-back spot, and some books still have Miami -1.5. Bet it smallest of the three.

Three legs, realistic only:

• Timberwolves ML (-125) + Celtics ML (-135) + Pistons ML (-110) = ≈ 5.98-to-1 → ~+500
