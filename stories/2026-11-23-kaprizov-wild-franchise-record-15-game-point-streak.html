Kaprizov sets the Wild's record — 15 straight games with a point, and Minnesota just won't lose

By Matt

Kirill Kaprizov broke his own franchise record with a point in his 15th straight game, and the Wild stretched their own record point streak to 15 with a 5-2 win in San Jose — 12-0-3 in that span, and alone at the top of the NHL.

The night

Kirill Kaprizov keeps rewriting the Minnesota record book — and this time he broke his own record to do it.

Kaprizov scored a goal and added three assists in the Wild's 5-2 win over the San Jose Sharks on Monday night at SAP Center, extending his point streak to 15 games. That passes the 14-game mark he set himself in November and December of 2022, and it is the longest point streak in Wild franchise history.

The team streak matched it. Minnesota has points in 15 straight games — 12 wins, no regulation losses, three overtime defeats — the longest point streak in franchise history, passing the 14-game run from the 2022-23 season. The Wild have not lost in regulation since October 22.

The numbers

The production inside the streak is absurd. Kaprizov has 32 points across the 15 games — eight goals and 24 assists — and he opened the scoring Monday on the power play 7:37 into the first period, his 19th goal of the season.

He was not alone. Joel Eriksson Ek scored twice and added an assist, Matt Boldy had three assists of his own, and Filip Gustavsson made 22 saves in his season debut after offseason hip surgery.

The run has carried Minnesota to the top of the entire league. The Wild are 18-3-5 with a league-best 41 points.

The other record

One more piece of franchise history got rewritten Monday, quietly. Defenseman Jared Spurgeon played his 1,029th game as a member of the Wild, passing Mikko Koivu for the most games played in franchise history.

What it means

Fifteen games is a long time to never leave the rink empty-handed, and the Wild are doing it with the whole roster contributing — Kaprizov driving it, Eriksson Ek and Boldy piling up points behind him, and a goalie coming off hip surgery winning his season debut.

The record Kaprizov broke was his own. The next names on the all-time list belong to the league's greats — and at this pace, it is a matter of when, not if, that conversation starts.
