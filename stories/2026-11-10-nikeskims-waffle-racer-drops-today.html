NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer drops today — three colorways, $130, and the archive treatment

By Elijah

The NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer drops today at $130 in three colorways — Baroque Brown, Light Bone/Varsity Red and Thunder Blue — the first Nike archive running silhouette of the partnership.

What happened

The NikeSKIMS Waffle Racer drops today, Tuesday, November 10, at $130 USD. It's a women's exclusive, available at nike.com/NikeSKIMS and SKIMS.com, with select retail to follow. Three colorways are on the board: Baroque Brown (IW3882-200), Light Bone/Varsity Red (IW3882-002) and Thunder Blue (IW3882-400).

The shoe is built on the 1977 Waffle Racer silhouette — the original waffle-sole runner — and it's the first Nike archive running silhouette the partnership has touched, after the Air Rift. Retro Sport apparel from the collection follows on November 17.

The shoes

This is the archive lane done properly: a heritage runner, kept close to the original, with the SKIMS treatment in the materials and the colorways rather than a redesign. The Thunder Blue pair is the loudest of the three — navy suede and nylon with a gum waffle sole — while Baroque Brown and Light Bone/Varsity Red keep it closer to the vintage palette. The waffle outsole, the low profile, the suede overlays: it's a 1977 shoe that happens to be new.

At $130, it's sitting in the same range as the partnership's earlier drops, and the women's-exclusive positioning has been the pattern for NikeSKIMS footwear so far. Expect the usual story — the popular sizes in the popular colorway will not last the day.
