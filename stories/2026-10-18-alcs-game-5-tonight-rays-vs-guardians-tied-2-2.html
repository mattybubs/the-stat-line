How We Got Here

The Guardians struck first in the series, taking ALCS Game 1 in Cleveland, and the Rays answered back with Freddy Peralta's six shutout innings to even things at 1-1. After a rain delay pushed Game 4 back a night, Tampa Bay won it 7-3 to knot the series at 2-2 — turning the ALCS into a best-of-three.

What's on the Line

The winner of tonight's game takes a 3-2 series lead — and momentum heading into the back half of the series. With the ALCS tied 2-2, Game 5 is the swing game: take it and you need just one win to reach the World Series; lose it and you're facing elimination.
