Captain Price is coming to the big screen — probably: Peter Berg teases the Call of Duty movie's most famous face

By Matt

Director Peter Berg told Variety the Call of Duty movie is set in the Modern Warfare universe — and that fans can 'probably expect to see Captain Price.' Casting is just beginning, and the film hits theaters June 30, 2028.

The most famous face in Call of Duty is probably headed to the movies. Director Peter Berg, speaking to Variety while promoting his new World War II film The Mosquito Bowl, said the long-awaited Call of Duty movie is set in the Modern Warfare universe — and dropped the tease fans have been waiting for.

"We've already said that we're basing it on the 'Modern Warfare' universe," Berg said. "So you can probably expect to see Captain Price, I would think."

That is not a confirmation. It is the closest thing to one anyone has gotten. Price has been the spine of Modern Warfare since the original Call of Duty 4 in 2007, when he debuted as captain of the 22nd SAS Regiment, and he has appeared in every installment of the original trilogy and the reboot series since. He is the one character a movie version of Modern Warfare simply has to get right — and Berg just told the world he knows it.

Where the movie stands

Berg is directing and co-writing the film with Taylor Sheridan, the Yellowstone creator and Sicario writer, who is also producing. Berg called Sheridan "the best possible partner I could have in the whole world," and said Paramount chief David Ellison and Activision have been "really smart about how they want to do it."

Casting is "beginning," Berg said, with no actors announced yet. The film is slated to open in theaters on June 30, 2028. As for when fans will hear more — casting, plot, the rest of the Task Force 141 lineup — Berg put a rough clock on it: "Check in with me, I'd say, in four months."

The casting question is the whole movie

Price's most recent incarnation is Barry Sloane, who has played him through the reboot series and again in this year's Modern Warfare 4 — including the voice, the motion capture, and a face modeled closely on the actor. Fans have already been campaigning for Sloane to reprise the role on screen, and it is easy to see why: he is the version of Price an entire generation of players knows.

But nothing is settled, and Berg's wording leaves the door open for a different choice. That is the gamble of the whole project. Modern Warfare's characters are so defined by their voices and faces that recasting Price with a movie star would be felt instantly — for better or worse. Get him right and the film has instant credibility with the franchise's core audience. Get him wrong and it spends its opening weekend apologizing.

Why the timing is perfect

The movie arrives at the strongest moment the franchise has had in years. Modern Warfare 4, which released October 23, is the highest-rated Call of Duty in 15 years, according to Metacritic. The game puts Price front and center too — a vengeful captain waging a personal war from the shadows while a North Korea–South Korea conflict erupts around him. The film and the game are now selling the same character from opposite directions, and the audience for both is the same.

The adaptation was announced in late 2025, and details have been scarce ever since — the Modern Warfare setting was the main thing anyone knew for certain. Now there is a character, a casting window, and a timeline for real news. Four months from now, we should know who is wearing the boonie hat.
