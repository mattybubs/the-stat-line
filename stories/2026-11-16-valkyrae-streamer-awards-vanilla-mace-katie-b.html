Valkyrae calls out the critics of Vanilla Mace and Katie B's Streamer Awards wins: 'I don't get why some people are so closed-minded'

By Matt

During a livestream on Sunday, Valkyrae pushed back on people discrediting Vanilla Mace and Katie B after their wins at the 2026 Streamer Awards — the latest fallout from Thursday's ceremony in Los Angeles.

The moment

The Streamer Awards were Thursday. The arguments about them are still going.

During a livestream on Sunday, November 15, YouTube and Twitch star Rachell "Valkyrae" Hofstetter called out people criticizing Vanilla Mace and Katie B for winning at the 2026 Streamer Awards, saying she doesn't understand why some people are so closed-minded about the two creators being honored.

Valkyrae — the co-owner of 100 Thieves — was discussing her experience at the annual awards ceremony, hosted by QTCinderella and Caroline Kwan in Los Angeles, when she turned to the criticism the two winners had drawn since Thursday night. Her point was simple: the wins were earned, the discrediting was unwarranted, and she was delighted both creators were honored.

The weekend around it

The sixth annual Streamer Awards, held November 12 in Los Angeles with 37 publicly voted categories, has generated a weekend of fallout. IShowSpeed won Streamer of the Year for the third consecutive year — the first creator ever to win the title three straight times — delivering his entire acceptance speech in Bahasa Indonesia, a nod to his connection with the country since his 2024 visit. He beat fellow nominees Jynxzi, Stable Ronaldo, Joe Bartolozzi and CaseOh.

Elsewhere, Indian creator Ashswag won Best International Streamer over Nigerian streamers Peller and Carter Efe, a result that drew its own debate — former Big Brother Naija winner Phyna alleged the organizers used Nigerian nominees to promote the event, a claim that is her allegation and not a finding, with no voting breakdown published.

Valkyrae's defense of Vanilla Mace and Katie B lands in that same churn: a weekend where every result got re-litigated in clips and streams. The difference is that Valkyrae wasn't arguing about a result. She was arguing about the impulse to tear one down.
