Colts Eyeing 3-4 More Trades, Seeking Second-Rounder for Taylor

By Matt

Indianapolis is expected to make three to four more trades ahead of the November 10 deadline, with a second-round pick the believed asking price for Jonathan Taylor.

Indianapolis is not done dealing. The Colts are expected to make three to four more trades ahead of the trade deadline, according to Jason La Canfora — and the biggest name in that conversation is running back Jonathan Taylor.

The report

Per La Canfora, Indianapolis is expected to make three to four more trades before the deadline closes. That is a busy final stretch for a team already in the middle of deadline season, and it puts the Colts squarely at the center of the league's trade chatter.

Expected additional trades: 3–4

Trade deadline: November 10

Player at the center of it: Jonathan Taylor

Reported by: Jason La Canfora

The asking price for Taylor

The believed asking price for Taylor is a second-round pick. That sets a clear bar for any team hoping to land him: this is not a throw-in deal, it is a premium price for a featured back, and any suitor has to be willing to pay it.

The clock

The deadline is November 10, so the window for those three to four moves is short. Between now and then, every call Indianapolis takes — and every offer it turns down — will shape how aggressive this final stretch gets.
