DIIV Share 'Dare Me' and Announce a 2027 World Tour Ahead of 'ZIRP!' Out Friday

By Matt

DIIV dropped 'Dare Me,' the final single before their fifth album 'ZIRP!' arrives Friday, October 30 — and announced a 47-date headline world tour for 2027, with presales Thursday and general sale Friday.

DIIV are not easing into album week. The New York band shared "Dare Me" on Wednesday — the fourth and final single before their fifth album, ZIRP!, arrives this Friday, October 30 on Fantasy Recordings — and used the moment to announce a 47-date headline world tour for 2027.

"Dare Me" premiered as BBC Radio 1's "Hottest Record" and comes with a music video directed by Louie Kovatch, shot in a vacant office space with the band in their now-signature business wear. It follows previously shared singles "Trillions," "Kid" and "The Fountain," and lands with ZIRP! already named Stereogum's Album of the Week, which called it "the most stylish, dynamic, endlessly replayable album of DIIV's career."

The tour

The 2027 headline run spans festivals in Australia and Japan plus full legs across North America, Europe and the United Kingdom. Presales begin Thursday, October 29, with general sale Friday, October 30 at 10 a.m. local time. In the nearer term, DIIV play Pitchfork Music Festival in both Paris and London this November, plus Belgium's Sonic City Festival.

If "Dare Me" is the last taste, Friday is the main course — and 2027 is when it hits a stage near you.
