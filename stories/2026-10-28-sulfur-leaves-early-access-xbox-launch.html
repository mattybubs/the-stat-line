SULFUR Leaves Early Access for Xbox Today

By Matt

SULFUR's 1.0 release lands October 28 on Xbox, ending its Early Access run — a roguelike first-person shooter built on fast gunplay and hard choices, already rated Very Positive on Steam from over 6,000 reviews.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The launch

SULFUR leaves Early Access on October 28 with its 1.0 release, launching on Xbox alongside the full release on Steam and the other consoles — PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 — from developer Perfect Random.

What kind of game it is

SULFUR is a roguelike first-person shooter where runs hinge on fast gunplay and hard choices. Every expedition is a risk-reward calculation: what you bring in, what you stand to lose, and how deep you're willing to push for better loot.

It already holds a Very Positive rating on Steam from over 6,000 reviews, earned across its Early Access run.

What 1.0 brings

The 1.0 release completes the game, with the full Enchantments system — including five new combined enchantments — a quest log tracking current quests, the complete progression from beginning to end, and balancing, polish, performance and quality-of-life updates.

Sources: The Guide Hall, Steam News, and PlayStation Lifestyle.
