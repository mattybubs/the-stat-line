TSL Bets: 1pm window slip — Kyren TD, Puka over 92.5, Cowboys-Colts over 51.5 (about +500)

By Matt

The 1pm window slip leans on the Rams' run game in Washington and a shootout in Indianapolis: Kyren Williams to score (-145), Puka Nacua over 92.5 receiving yards (-114), and Cowboys-Colts over 51.5 (-110). Three legs, about +500.

The games

Los Angeles Rams (4-4) at Washington Commanders (2-4-1) — 1:00 PM ET, Northwest Stadium, FOX

Rams -4.5 (-110), total 47.5, moneyline Rams -235 / Commanders +191 (Action Network). The Rams have won two straight, 24-9 over the Chargers last week, and are 4-4 against the spread. Washington is coming off a convincing win over Philadelphia and is 4-3 ATS. The injury flag: Matthew Stafford was icing his shoulder after the Chargers win, and there is a chance he is not at 100%. That matters for the passing game — it does not matter much for the leg we are playing. On the Washington side, the defensive front is injury-depleted, which is exactly why the Rams' run game is the play. Storylines: the Rams' offense has found its identity on the ground, with Kyren Williams and Blake Corum becoming the keys to the attack; Washington's defense ranks as the second-leakiest in the league in passes to running backs relative to expectation; and the spread is close enough to keep Stafford honest, which keeps the Rams in scoring position all afternoon.

Dallas Cowboys (3-5) at Indianapolis Colts (2-6) — 1:00 PM ET, Lucas Oil Stadium, FOX

Cowboys -4.5, total 51.5, moneyline Cowboys -212 / Colts +208 (optimal-bet). Dallas is 5-2 to the over this season; Indianapolis is 4-3 to the over. The Colts have lost four straight, including a 42-7 loss to Jacksonville last week. Injuries: Dallas is without KaVontae Turpin (ribs) and Malachi Lawrence (ankle), and George Pickens (hamstring), Jake Ferguson (ankle), DeMarvion Overshown (hamstring/back), and Joey Porter Jr. (ankle) are all questionable. Indianapolis is without Akeem Davis-Gaither (knee) and Justin Walley (knee), with Jalen Travis (thumb) questionable and Cam Taylor-Britt (groin) on injured reserve. Storylines: both defenses are rotten — Dallas plays at the fastest pace in the league, the Colts have allowed 30 or more points in four games, and Indianapolis ranks 27th in defensive DVOA. The total has held at 51.5 all week, and the market expects points.

The legs

Leg 1 — Kyren Williams anytime touchdown (-145, Fanatics)

Williams has 112 carries for 548 yards and six touchdowns, averaging 4.9 yards per carry, and he has found the end zone in consecutive games. He is the most likely scorer in this game at Fanatics, and the matchup is right: Washington's defensive front is depleted, the Rams are 4.5-point favorites, and Williams is the goal-line back in an offense that is leaning on the run. The Stafford shoulder flag does not touch this leg — if anything, a limited Stafford means more Williams near the goal line.

Leg 2 — Puka Nacua over 92.5 receiving yards (-114, FanDuel)

Nacua is healthy with no injury designation, and he is the Rams' primary target with Davante Adams on injured reserve — 19 catches and two touchdowns in his last two games. The projection is roughly 9-10 receptions for about 96 yards. Washington's secondary has been leaky all season, and Nacua is the kind of receiver who gets there on volume alone. Even if Stafford is not at 100%, Nacua is the first read on nearly every dropback.

Leg 3 — Cowboys at Colts over 51.5 (-110)

Two bad defenses, one fast-paced offense, and a total that has not moved off 51.5. Dallas is 5-2 to the over this season. The Colts have allowed 30-plus in four games. Both teams can score — the question is whether either can stop the other, and the answer this season has been no. This is the shootout leg of the slip, and it is the one with the most margin for error: 52 points between two teams that both play fast and neither of which tackles well.

Why this slip

Two legs ride the Rams' offense in a game they should control, and the third rides a total that two bad defenses should clear. The legs are correlated in a good way — if the Rams score, Williams and Nacua are the two most likely reasons — but they are not the same bet three times. Williams needs one trip to the end zone. Nacua needs a big receiving day. The Cowboys and Colts need to do what they have done all season: score, and let the other team score.

The honest caveat

The Stafford shoulder is the real risk in this slip. If he is limited, the Rams' passing game shrinks, and Nacua's over gets harder — though Williams' leg gets easier. The Cowboys-Colts over is the other risk: 51.5 is a big number, and if either team has an off day, or if the questionable tags on Pickens and Ferguson turn into absences, the scoring dries up. And Williams at -145 is a short price for a reason — he has to actually get in. Nothing here is a lock. The slip pays about +500 because all three legs have to hit.

The pick

3-leg 1pm window slip, about +500: Kyren Williams anytime TD (-145) + Puka Nacua over 92.5 receiving yards (-114) + Cowboys-Colts over 51.5 (-110). A $10 ticket returns about $60.60. The Rams' run game, the Rams' No. 1 receiver, and two defenses that cannot stop anyone. That is the bet.
