The Blood of Dawnwalker just beat GTA 6 for the AP's game of the year

By Matt

The Associated Press put The Blood of Dawnwalker at No. 1 on its top 10 games of 2026 — ahead of Grand Theft Auto VI, the biggest entertainment launch in history. The year's best-selling game didn't win the year's best-game argument.

Grand Theft Auto VI has spent the last week breaking every record in the book. It did not win the Associated Press's game of the year.

The AP's top 10 games of 2026, published Monday by critic Lou Kesten, puts The Blood of Dawnwalker — the medieval vampire RPG from Rebel Wolves — at No. 1, with GTA VI second. It is the clearest split yet between the year's commercial story and its critical one: the game that sold more than anything ever made sits behind a dark fantasy RPG that most of the mainstream audience only discovered this fall.

The list

Here is the AP's full top 10:

1. The Blood of Dawnwalker

2. Grand Theft Auto VI

3. Star Wars Zero Company

4. Mina the Hollower

5. Resident Evil Requiem

6. Pragmata

7. Crimson Desert

8. Zero Parades: For Dead Spies

9. Control Resonant

10. Pokémon Pokopia

The list mixes blockbusters with smaller swings. Star Wars Zero Company, a tactical strategy game, takes third; Mina the Hollower, the retro-inspired indie, is fourth; and Resident Evil Requiem — the horror entry that lost out to Hellraiser: Revival at the Horror Game Awards — rounds out the top five.

Why Dawnwalker won

Dawnwalker is Rebel Wolves' first game, published by Bandai Namco, and it launched September 2 on PC and consoles. You play Coen, a young man turned into a Dawnwalker — human by day, vampire after dark — in 14th-century Europe, with a ticking clock: every quest you accept advances time and eats into the days your family has left.

The AP praised it as a cleverly constructed role-playing epic, and the awards season has agreed so far: the game is nominated for Best Narrative and Best Role Playing Game at The Game Awards, which hand out their prizes December 10 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. GTA VI leads that show with eight nominations, including Game of the Year — so the rematch is two weeks away.

If the ranking sends you to the store, the timing is kind: Dawnwalker is 20 percent off on Steam for the first time, down to $55.99 from its $69.99 list price, with the sale running until December 7.

What second place says about GTA 6

The AP was not down on GTA VI — it called the game a landmark in interactive storytelling, praised its central romance between Jason and Lucia, and noted that other publishers spent the year steering clear of its launch window entirely. Second place on a top-10 list is not a snub.

But the gap between the two lenses is the story. Take-Two announced day-one retail sales of $3.79 billion for GTA VI, a record for any entertainment product, with 32.5 million copies sold before release. No game has ever been bigger. The AP's list says biggest and best are different questions — and this year, a vampire RPG from a first-time studio answered the second one better.
