TSL Bets: Oilers -1.5 (+124) vs Flyers — McDavid and Draisaitl against a tired Flyers team

By Matt

The Oilers are 13-3-3 with the league's hottest two players, and the Flyers are 5-10-5 with two defensemen out. Edmonton on the puck line at plus money is the play tonight.

The game

Philadelphia Flyers (5-10-5) at Edmonton Oilers (13-3-3) — 9:00 PM ET, Rogers Place, Edmonton, ESPN+

Oilers -1.5 (+124), moneyline Oilers -218, total 6.5 (DraftKings via ESPN). Projected goalies: Dan Vladar (PHI) vs Devon Levi (EDM).

Injuries — Edmonton: Depth only. Matthew Savoie is on IR with a lower-body injury, Andersen is on IR with a knee injury, and Mattias Janmark, Isaac Howard and Spencer Regula are all on IR as well. None of them are core skaters — the rotation is intact.

Injuries — Philadelphia: Rasmus Ristolainen is out with a hand injury and Nikita Grebenkin is out with a back injury. The Flyers are thinner on the blue line against the best offense in hockey.

Storylines: Connor McDavid has 48 points (11 goals, 37 assists) and a season-opening point streak of 19 games — the fourth player ever to start a season with a 19-game point streak. Leon Draisaitl leads the league with 18 goals and has scored in each of his last four games, six goals in that stretch, including the last goal in each of the last four. The Oilers are first in the Pacific at 13-3-3. The Flyers, meanwhile, lost 4-3 in overtime at Calgary on Sunday and are 5-10-5. Devon Levi, getting the start for Edmonton, stopped 29 of 30 on Monday against the Rangers.

The bet

Oilers -1.5 (+124). This is the best offense in the league, at home, against a Flyers team that is 5-10-5 and missing two defensemen. The puck line at plus money is the play — you are getting a two-goal margin on a team that wins by two or more more often than not, and the price says the market thinks this is closer than it is. McDavid and Draisaitl are playing at a level where the Flyers' depleted blue line has no answer.

The honest caveat

The puck line is the risk. Edmonton can win this game 4-3 and the ticket loses — the -1.5 needs a two-goal margin, not just a win. The Flyers have kept games close even while losing, and Levi is a young goalie getting a second start in three nights. And McDavid's 19-game point streak has to end sometime — if tonight is the night, the offense has to find another way. This is a plus-money bet for a reason. But the Oilers are the better team by a wide margin, and the price is right.

The pick

Oilers -1.5 (+124). A $10 ticket returns about $22.40. The best offense in hockey, at home, against a 5-10-5 team, at plus money.
