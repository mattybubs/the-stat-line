Tiny Bookshop just sold its millionth copy — and its first DLC lands December 5

By Matt

The cozy bookshop sim's first expansion, Arts & Crafts, arrives December 5 with two new locations, new wagon parts, and a stolen-painting mystery — after a sales milestone most indie games never reach.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Tiny Bookshop has officially sold more than one million copies worldwide. Publisher Skystone Games and developer neoludic announced the milestone on Monday, November 23, covering every platform the game is on — PC, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation 5, Xbox, iOS and Android — since its launch in August 2025. For a cozy sim about running a second-hand bookshop out of a wagon in a seaside town, that is a serious number.

The announcement came with a second piece of news: the game's first expansion, Arts & Crafts, launches on December 5, 2026.

What Arts & Crafts adds

The expansion opens up two new locations in Bookstonbury-by-the-Sea. Puffin Primary is the town's primary school, where players can finally sell their kids' books. The Artist Quarter is a neighborhood built around the town's creative scene, where trendsetters browse and a shopkeeper named Tomasz sells antique wares.

Beyond the new stops, the DLC adds 24 new wagon parts for rebuilding the shop on wheels, more than 30 decor items, new quests, and a mystery built around a stolen painting. The official overview also teases a new friendly inhabitant of Bookstonbury, kept behind a spoiler tag for now. The new content kicks in during Autumn, in the fifth in-game week.

A cozy hit that kept growing

Tiny Bookshop launched on August 7, 2025, and its slow-burn success has been one of the quieter stories in gaming this year — a gentle shopkeeping sim with no combat and no fail state that found an audience on every platform at once. Nintendo Life gave it an 8/10 in its review, calling it "a fun little experience whether you're into reading or not."

The expansion's price has not been announced yet, and Skystone says more details are coming soon — which is cutting it close, with the release date under two weeks away. What is confirmed is the launch itself, landing on Steam and Nintendo Switch, with other platforms to be confirmed.
