Stars blank Sharks 5-0 for first win

By Matt

Jake Oettinger's 17th career shutout, and a Sharks captain scratched minutes before faceoff.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The shutout

Jake Oettinger stopped all 13 shots for his 17th career shutout in Dallas's Monday win. Final: Stars 5, Sharks 0 — Dallas's first win of the season.

The late scratch

San Jose scratched captain Macklin Celebrini minutes before faceoff with a lower-body injury — after he took warmups. That's the kind of lineup news that moves a betting board, and it showed: without their captain, the Sharks never threatened.
