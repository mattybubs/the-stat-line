Max Verstappen played 40 hours of GTA 6 during the Las Vegas GP weekend — and still won the race

By Matt

Four-time F1 world champion Max Verstappen revealed he logged about 40 hours of GTA 6 across the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend — playing mornings and evenings around practice, qualifying and the race — then went out and won it anyway, his fourth victory of the 2026 season.

Max Verstappen spent the Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend doing what most of the rest of the world was doing: playing GTA 6. The difference is that Verstappen squeezed it in around driving a Formula 1 car at 200 miles per hour — and still won the race.

The four-time world champion revealed on a livestream after the race that he logged about 40 hours on Grand Theft Auto VI across the weekend in Vegas, playing in the mornings and evenings between his track duties. "I played a lot in the evening and in the morning in Vegas," he said. "I mean, I can't go out anyways and walk around." He did not play on the flight home — that time was for sleep.

GTA 6 launched on Thursday, November 19, the same week Formula 1 arrived in Las Vegas, so the timing was almost designed for it. Verstappen had talked before release about wanting to play the game, and once it unlocked, he went all in — by his own account, he is already about 80 percent through the story.

Pole, win, and a 40-hour side quest

The gaming marathon did not slow him down. Verstappen converted pole position into victory in the Las Vegas Grand Prix, his third win in Vegas and his fourth of the 2026 season, finishing ahead of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri in a 50-lap race that featured 35 lead changes.

It is the kind of stat that sounds made up until you remember who it is about. Verstappen is a keen gamer who streams on Twitch between races, mostly sim racing with his Team Redline outfit, and he dips in and out of other games too — including ARC Raiders. But he admits he cannot juggle too many at once. "I'm so bad when I play too many games at the same time. I can't, I just can't," he said on the stream. GTA 6, apparently, got the full focus.

He is not the only athlete who cleared the schedule for Vice City. Lewis Hamilton said before launch that he was hoping to take the day off to play it, and NBA center Steven Adams joked that sports leagues would "plummet" in quality while everyone was locked into Leonida. Verstappen just took the joke one step further: he played the game for the equivalent of a full work week and won a Grand Prix in the middle of it.

The next stop on the F1 calendar is Qatar, and Verstappen has a stream scheduled to play Minecraft ahead of it — though he admitted on the same livestream that after all that time in Vice City, he has "completely forgotten" how to play it.
