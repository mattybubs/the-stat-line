TSL Bets: The Saturday parlay (~+390) — Draisaitl anytime goal, McDavid anytime goal

By Matt

Leon Draisaitl leads the league with 24 goals and scored against Seattle last night. Connor McDavid scored on the power play against the same Kraken team last night too. Two legs, about +390, in the home-and-home rematch.

The game

Edmonton Oilers (17-7-4) at Seattle Kraken (12-10-1) — 10:00 PM ET, Climate Pledge Arena, Seattle, WA

Oilers moneyline -170 (DraftKings via ESPN). This is the second half of a home-and-home: Seattle won the opener 4-2 in Edmonton last night, and both of tonight's legs scored in that game.

Injuries — Edmonton: F Zach Hyman OUT (vision). F Filip Hallander OUT (knee). Matt Savoie, Mattias Janmark, Alec Regula and Frederik Andersen on injured reserve.

Injuries — Seattle: G Philipp Grubauer DAY-TO-DAY (upper body — left last night's game, Joey Daccord finished). F Jared McCann on injured reserve (lower body). F Jake O'Brien on injured reserve.

Storylines: Edmonton has lost five straight and gets the same opponent 24 hours later — a revenge spot with the two best scorers in the league both already on the board against this team. Draisaitl leads the NHL with 24 goals in 28 games. McDavid hit 60 points in 28 games last night, the fastest to that mark since Mario Lemieux in 1995-96. And Seattle's penalty kill entered last night second-worst in the league at 61.7% — against Edmonton's power play, that is where both of these legs can cash.

Leg 1 — Leon Draisaitl anytime goal

The league's leading scorer, at even money. Draisaitl has 24 goals in 28 games, he scored against this same Seattle team last night, and he has scored in six of his last 10 — a 60% hit rate against the roughly 50% implied by the price. Line: anytime goal (+100, FanDuel).

Leg 2 — Connor McDavid anytime goal

McDavid has 15 goals in 28 games and scored on the power play against Seattle last night. The OPTIMAL projection puts him at 0.6 goals tonight with a fair price of +144 — BetMGM is hanging +145, right at fair, in a matchup against one of the league's worst penalty kills. Line: anytime goal (+145, BetMGM).

The bet

Leon Draisaitl anytime goal (+100, FanDuel) + Connor McDavid anytime goal (+145, BetMGM) — about +390. A $10 ticket returns about $49.00.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own — that is the rule, and both do. Draisaitl is the league's top goal scorer in a plus-matchup at even money, and McDavid is a form-and-matchup play at a fair price against a penalty kill that cannot stay out of trouble. Two anytime-goal props on the two most dangerous finishers alive, about +390 instead of the chalky price you get from stacking moneyline favorites.

The honest caveats

Both legs live in the same game, on the same team — one dead night for Edmonton's offense sinks both, and the Oilers have lost five straight, so the team form is a real warning even if the individuals keep scoring. McDavid's hit rate is the weaker leg: five of his last 10. And a parlay needs both legs — either one can sink it alone. The edge is real, but it is an edge, not a lock.

The record

TSL Bets is 23-47 in November, 27-68 all-time.
