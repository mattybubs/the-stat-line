Pokémon Is Back at McDonald's — and the Promo Accidentally Spoiled Next Year's Cards Too

By Matt

McDonald's confirmed its Pokémon Happy Meal returns in the US on November 10 with 15 toys and 15 trading cards — and, in announcing it, reportedly showed off next year's M27 set a full year early.

The Pokémon Happy Meal is back — and this time the announcement itself became a collectible story.

McDonald's has confirmed the return of its Pokémon Happy Meal, launching in the US on November 10 with a 15-piece toy set and a 15-card trading card collection, plus a digital game built into the packaging. The international rollout follows through February 2027, starting with Canada, Australia and several other markets in November.

What's in the box

The toys are miniature Happy Meal boxes, each carrying the face of a different Pokémon — and they do more than sit on a shelf. McDonald's executive Guillaume Huin showed that each box-shaped toy converts into a bag charm you can clip onto keys, bags, or just about anything.

Confirmed headliners so far: Pikachu, Eevee, Charmander, Squirtle and Umbreon, with promotional artwork also showing Piplup. The card side includes holofoil cards, with Jirachi, Pikachu and Absol among the previews — though McDonald's has not detailed the complete 15-card checklist yet. Fifteen cards to collect, fifteen toys to chase: the classic Happy Meal math, with a charm twist collectors didn't see coming.

The accidental spoiler

Here's the part collectors are actually talking about: when Pokémon officially confirmed this year's cards, the post reportedly showed an entirely different set — stamped M27, not M26 — before it was quickly taken down.

That M27 set, per reporting on the since-removed post, includes Raichu and Rowlet among its 15 cards, with a new Squirtle card as one of the notable fresh designs. If those really are next year's Happy Meal cards, they were revealed roughly a year early — and they carry an interesting implication: with no sign of new Pokémon in the set, the next Pokémon games (Winds & Waves) may not arrive until later in 2027, since McDonald's sets historically include the newest starters.

Treat the M27 details as reported, not officially confirmed — the post came down fast, and McDonald's hasn't confirmed next year's set. But the M26 set itself is locked: November 10 in the US, 15 toys, 15 cards, and a charm for your bag.

Why collectors care

McDonald's Pokémon promos are one of the hobby's great equalizers — cheap, everywhere, and genuinely fun to chase. A 15-and-15 set with a usable charm gimmick is already a strong one. An accidental year-early leak of the next set on top of it? That's the kind of story the hobby talks about for months.
