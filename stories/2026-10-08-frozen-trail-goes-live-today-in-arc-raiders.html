Frozen Trail goes live today in ARC Raiders

By Matt

ARC Raiders' fifth major update is live now — new frozen map, new enemies, amplified weapons, and a free weekend through October 12.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The update is live

Frozen Trail, ARC Raiders' fifth major update, went live this morning at 4:00 AM EDT on PC, PS5, and Xbox Series S|X. The headline addition is the frozen Pendola Pass map, a new zone set beyond the Rust Belt (launch times).

What's new in Frozen Trail

• New map: Pendola Pass — a frozen zone beyond the Rust Belt, the biggest environmental shift the game has added

• New ARC enemies: the Bully, the Skulker, and the Hydra

• Weapon progression: 15 amplified weapons can now reach a 5th tier

Free weekend: October 8–12

Embark is pairing the update with a free-play weekend — the full game is free across Steam, Xbox, and PlayStation from October 8 through October 12 (update breakdown). If your squad has been on the fence, this is the window: the update's arrival plus zero buy-in makes it the easiest possible time to pull people in.
