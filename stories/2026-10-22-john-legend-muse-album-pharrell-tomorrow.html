John Legend's 'Muse' Arrives Tomorrow — Every Track Produced by Pharrell

By Matt

16 tracks, one producer, 20+ years of friendship. John Legend's 11th album 'Muse' — the 'Black James Bond aesthetic' project — drops Friday.

John Legend's 11th studio album, Muse, drops Friday, October 23 — and every one of its 16 tracks was produced by Pharrell Williams. It's the first full album the two have made together in more than 20 years of friendship.

The sound

Pharrell's concept: a "Black James Bond aesthetic" — 808s and hip-hop drums under classic, cinematic elements: strings, horns, a modern backbeat at the foundation. The record pulls from gospel, doo-wop, classic soul, Afrobeat, hip-hop and traditional standards, with Nina Simone, Nat King Cole and Marvin Gaye as reference points.

Most of it was built in Pharrell's studio inside the Louis Vuitton headquarters in Paris — where he works as men's creative director. Legend recorded the lead single "Daylight" at home in L.A. around Christmas 2024.

The tracklist

Two singles are already out: "Daylight" and "Fireflies." Pharrell appears as a featured artist on several songs including the title track, and Clipse shows up on "Bodies On The Floor." Other tracks include "Pray For Ya," "Her," "Get Back" and "Sail Off."

The whole thing started when Legend sang on Clipse's comeback album Let God Sort 'Em Out — Pharrell heard it and decided to build an entire project around Legend's voice. He also pushed the singer out of his comfort zone: "My instinct is to sing big," Legend said. "Pharrell wanted something softer and closer to talking."

Legend celebrates the release with a promotional appearance at the Mall of America the day after it drops.
