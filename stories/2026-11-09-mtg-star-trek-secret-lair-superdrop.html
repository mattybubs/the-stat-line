Magic's Star Trek Secret Lair superdrop beams in today — five drops, $29.99 each, and a sixth still to come

By Matt

Secret Lair x Star Trek: Set Phasers to Superdrop goes on sale today at noon ET on MagicSecretLair.com — five drops at $29.99 non-foil or $39.99 foil, from full-art lands to a full Klingon-language set, with a sixth drop still unrevealed and the main set landing Friday.

What happened

The transporters are warmed up. Secret Lair x Star Trek: Set Phasers to Superdrop goes on sale today, Monday, November 9, at 9 a.m. PT / noon ET on MagicSecretLair.com, with a pre-queue opening an hour earlier. Wizards of the Coast revealed five of the six drops on October 27. The sixth is still to be revealed.

Each drop costs $29.99 in non-foil or $39.99 in foil, while supplies last. All five revealed drops run $149.95 non-foil or $199.95 foil before tax and shipping, and spending $199.99 earns a foil copy of Return of the Wildspeaker, reskinned as "Transporter Boondoggle." Orders over $99 ship free.

The five drops

New Worlds — full-art basic lands from new worlds, the kind of drop that ends up in decks for years after the set itself is forgotten.

Beam Me Up — the transporter drop, and the cleverest of the five: four reversible cards. Eerie Interlude becomes Beam Us Up, Momentary Blink, Essence Flux becomes Materialize, and Conjurer's Closet becomes The Transporter. Every card in the drop is reversible.

tlhIngan maH — a full Klingon-language set, with Klingon-script cards developed with help from Marc Okrand, the linguist who created the language for the films. This is the one collectors will be talking about longest.

KHAAAAAAAAN — the Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan drop, which Wizards itself notes knows exactly how many A's belong in the title.

Lower Decks — the chaos of Star Trek: Lower Decks, for the people who want their Magic cards to be funny.

Why the timing matters

The superdrop lands four days before the main event: Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek hits tabletop on Friday, November 13, with prereleases running November 6–12 and the MTG Arena release on November 10. All four Commander decks have been revealed, each a ready-to-play 100-card deck with 32 new-to-Magic cards. The set is the next Universes Beyond release, and Wizards is treating it like one — a Secret Lair superdrop, a full Commander lineup, and a set code (TRK) collectors already know by heart.

The business behind it is worth a line, too. Hasbro reported that Magic: The Gathering set a quarterly revenue record of $616.1 million in Q3 2026, up 34 percent year over year, with The Hobbit as the fourth-best-selling set in Magic history and the highest prerelease participation ever for a Universes Beyond set. Star Trek is walking into the biggest moment the game has ever had.

What to watch

Secret Lair sales are queue-based and while-supplies-last, and the pre-queue opens at 8 a.m. PT — an hour before the sale. Adding a drop to your basket does not reserve it, so the drops that sell out will sell out fast, and the Klingon set is the obvious candidate. The unrevealed sixth drop is the other thing to watch: Wizards has not said what it is or when it appears, and a surprise sixth drop in a superdrop like this has a way of becoming the chase card of the whole release.

Set phasers, indeed.

The details
