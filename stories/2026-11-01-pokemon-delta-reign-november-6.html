Pokémon Returns to Hoenn on November 6 With Mega Evolution: Delta Reign

By Matt

Pokémon TCG: Mega Evolution — Delta Reign launches November 6 with 135+ cards, headlined by Mega Rayquaza ex's first appearance since 2015, plus a new Legendary Stadium mechanic and retailer-exclusive Hoenn promos.

Pokémon is going back to Hoenn. On Friday, November 6, the Pokémon TCG launches Mega Evolution — Delta Reign, the set that brings Generation 3's most iconic Legendaries back to the card table as part of the franchise's 30th anniversary celebrations — and it is shaping up to be the biggest TCG launch of the month.

The set

Delta Reign packs over 135 cards, led by Mega Rayquaza ex — appearing in the TCG for the first time since 2015's XY—Ancient Origins expansion. Rayquaza is joined by Mega Golurk ex, Mega Malamar ex and Mega Golisopod ex, plus 14 illustration rares and six special illustration rare Pokémon and Supporter cards.

The set's headline mechanic is Legendary Stadium: three Legendary Stadiums that can be combined into a single connected illustration, adding a new strategic wrinkle for players and a display-piece chase for collectors. Booster packs, Elite Trainer Boxes and other collections arrive through participating retailers, and the set hits Pokémon TCG Live a day early, on November 5.

The Hoenn promos

The Hoenn homecoming extends beyond the packs. Starting November 6, fans who spend $15 or more on Pokémon TCG products at participating retailers receive a promotional card, and the Legendary you get depends on where you shop:

• Kyogre — Hot Topic, Barnes & Noble, Five Below and Macy's

• Groudon — GameStop

• Giratina — Best Buy

All three promos carry the exclusive Pokémon 30 treatment, tying them to the anniversary year. For completionists, that means at least three stops — and for everyone else, a good excuse to pick a favorite Legendary and plan accordingly.

The same-day competition

Delta Reign doesn't arrive alone. November 6 also brings the Pokémon 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collections — one built around Espeon ex, one around Umbreon ex — each packing 29 30th Celebration packs, a Classic Collection pack, a Pikachu ex promo, the Espeon or Umbreon ex promo, a playmat and a metal coin. Same day, same anniversary, same crowded shelf: it is the biggest single Friday the hobby has seen in a while, and it lands right as the McDonald's Pokémon Happy Meal promotion (November 10) is about to send the whole franchise into overdrive.

If you're planning to hunt Delta Reign on release day, get there early. The Hoenn nostalgia is real, the Rayquaza chase is real, and November's card calendar only gets busier from here.
