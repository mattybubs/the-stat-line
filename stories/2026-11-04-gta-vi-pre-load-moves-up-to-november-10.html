GTA VI Pre-Load Moves Up to November 10

By Matt

Rockstar has moved GTA VI's digital pre-load up to Tuesday, November 10 — two days earlier than planned — and the download runs up to 212 GB depending on your device.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Rockstar has pulled GTA VI's digital pre-load forward. Instead of waiting until November 12, players can start downloading the game on Tuesday, November 10 — two extra days to get one of the biggest downloads in gaming history onto your console before launch.

What changed

When pre-orders opened, Rockstar gave November 12 as the pre-load date. That date has now moved up to Tuesday, November 10 — two days earlier than originally planned. The launch date itself is unchanged: GTA VI still arrives November 19, 2026.

The download: up to 212 GB

The pre-load needs up to 212 GB, depending on your device. That is a serious chunk of storage, so if your drive is close to full, now is the time to clear space — the earlier pre-load window gives you nine days between download and launch day to sort it out rather than scrambling at the last minute.

Pre-load date: Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Previous pre-load date: November 12, 2026

Download size: Up to 212 GB (varies by device)

Launch date: November 19, 2026
