Rays take 3-2 ALCS lead with 3-1 Game 5 win

By Matt

Drew Rasmussen dealt seven innings of one-run ball as Tampa Bay beat Cleveland 3-1 in ALCS Game 5 — one win away from a third pennant and a World Series date with Milwaukee.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Rasmussen deals the Rays to the brink

Drew Rasmussen was the whole story Sunday night in Cleveland. The Rays right-hander allowed one run on four hits over seven innings with six strikeouts in Tampa Bay's 3-1 win over the Guardians in ALCS Game 5, lowering his postseason ERA to 1.29 and pushing the Rays to a 3-2 series lead.

### Game 5 snapshot

Final: 3; 1

Series: 3-2; 2-3

Rasmussen's line: 7 IP, 4 H, 1 R, 6 K — postseason ERA down to 1.29.

One win from the pennant

Tampa Bay can clinch its third pennant in franchise history tonight in St. Petersburg, where the series shifts back for Game 6. Milwaukee is already awaiting the winner — the Brewers swept the Dodgers out of the NLCS, and the World Series starts Friday.
