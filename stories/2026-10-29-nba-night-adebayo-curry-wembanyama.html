Wednesday Night in the NBA: Adebayo Passes Haslem as the Heat's Rebound King, Curry Drops 44, Wemby Wins One in OT

By Matt

Bam Adebayo passed Udonis Haslem for the most rebounds in Heat history in Miami's first win, Stephen Curry poured in 44 in a Warriors win at Minnesota, and Victor Wembanyama went for 36 and 14 in an overtime win at Utah.

Wednesday night in the NBA had a little bit of everything: a franchise record, a first win, a 44-point night, and an overtime finish.

Adebayo passes Haslem — and the Heat finally win

Bam Adebayo grabbed nine rebounds in Miami's 129-117 win at Atlanta, pushing his career total to 5,794 and past Udonis Haslem's franchise record of 5,791 — a mark that had stood since November 2012. Haslem, the Heat's longtime captain and Adebayo's mentor, held the record across a 20-season career, all in Miami.

The win mattered just as much as the record: it was Miami's first of the season after an 0-4 start following the offseason acquisition of Giannis Antetokounmpo. Giannis had 24 points and a season-high 18 rebounds in the win, his first victory in a Heat uniform, and his 138 points through five games are the most by any player in his first five games with the franchise. Adebayo added a season-high 24 points of his own, and the Heat closed on an 11-5 run after Atlanta had cut an 18-point third-quarter lead to six.

"I can't really put it into words," Adebayo said on the court afterward, with the game ball already secured for him. "The person I'm passing has been so close to me throughout my 10 years in this career, from the time I walked in until now."

Curry's 44 sinks the Wolves

Stephen Curry scored 44 points — including a perfect 16-for-16 from the free-throw line — as Golden State beat Minnesota 119-114 at Target Center. Rookie Yaxel Lendeborg added 26 points and 9 rebounds for the Warriors, who improved to 2-3. Jonathan Kuminga led Minnesota with 20 in the loss; the Timberwolves, playing without Anthony Edwards after his knee surgery announcement, fell to 3-2.

Wemby takes over in overtime

Victor Wembanyama had 36 points and 14 rebounds as San Antonio beat Utah 117-111 in overtime, outscoring the Jazz 12-6 in the extra period after regulation ended tied at 105. Keyonte George led Utah with 31. The Spurs' win keeps them perfect on the young season's road swing; the Jazz fell despite 19 points from rookie Ace Bailey.

Elsewhere

• Mavericks 120, Thunder 96: Cooper Flagg had 26 points, 8 assists and 5 rebounds as Dallas handed Oklahoma City its first loss of the season. Ajay Mitchell led the Thunder with 29.

• Knicks 133, Bulls 127: Jalen Brunson scored 38 and New York outscored Chicago 43-31 in the fourth quarter at the United Center. Norman Powell led the Bulls with 30.

• Raptors 129, Magic 99: Scottie Barnes had 28 points in Toronto's rout; the Raptors improved to 3-1.

• Lakers 103, Clippers 87: The Lakers rolled at Intuit Dome to move to 4-1; the Clippers fell to 1-3.

• Cavaliers 121, Wizards 111 and Rockets 132, Bucks 100 rounded out the 10-game slate.
