Three Red Sox Earn Gold Gloves for Defensive Excellence

By Matt

Caleb Durbin, Ceddanne Rafaela, and Wilyer Abreu took home American League Gold Gloves on Sunday night — the first time since 2018 that Boston has had three winners in a single year.

Boston's defense got its night. Three Red Sox — Caleb Durbin, Ceddanne Rafaela, and Wilyer Abreu — took home American League Gold Glove honors on Sunday night for their defensive work in the 2026 season, giving the club three winners in a single year for the first time since 2018.

The trio

The three winners cover the left side of the infield and two-thirds of the outfield — a big chunk of the ground Boston has to defend every night.

Caleb Durbin: Third base; American League Gold Glove

Ceddanne Rafaela: Center field; American League Gold Glove

Wilyer Abreu: Right field; American League Gold Glove

Each award recognizes the season's defensive work at the position — the plays made, the ground covered, and the runs kept off the board with the glove rather than the bat.

First time since 2018

It is the first time since 2018 that Boston has had three Gold Glove winners in a single year, according to Talk Sox. That is a long gap for a franchise that has always prided itself on defense, and it says something about how this roster is built: up the middle and in the outfield, the Red Sox can catch it.

Three winners in one year also puts Boston at the top of the league's defensive class for 2026 — no American League club had more.

Why it matters

Gold Gloves are voted on the full season, not a hot month, so a trio like this is a statement about the whole year. Durbin locking down third, Rafaela patrolling center, and Abreu holding right field gave Boston a defense it could lean on all season — and now all three have the hardware to show for it.
