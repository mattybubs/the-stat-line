Pokémon UNITE Shuts Down March 23, 2027 After Nearly Six Years

By Matt

The Pokémon Company and TiMi Studio Group announced October 27 that Pokémon UNITE ends service March 23, 2027 on Switch and mobile — with gem sales already suspended and a farewell event coming November 12.

The announcement

Pokémon UNITE is ending. The Pokémon Company and developer TiMi Studio Group announced October 27 that the free-to-play team battler will end service on Tuesday, March 23, 2027, at 00:00 UTC, on both Nintendo Switch and mobile.

The game first launched on Switch on July 21, 2021, followed by iOS and Android on September 22, 2021 — so the shutdown lands just short of six years after launch. The announcement had been telegraphed since July, when UNITE was dropped from the 2027 Pokémon World Championships in favor of a standalone tournament.

What happens between now and March

• Aeos gem sales are already suspended as of October 27 at 05:00 UTC. Gems players already own can still be spent until service ends.

• New UNITE Club memberships and automatic renewals are suspended at the same time; current members keep their benefits until their current period runs out.

• A special farewell event and update arrives November 12, letting players earn Unite Coins through daily logins and missions and spend them in a Unite Coin Exchange on previously released Unite licenses, Holowear, Trainer fashion and snapshot items.

• A new Library feature will let players view battle records, acquired licenses, Holowear and other content after service ends — but only if they download the required library data before March 23. Miss the download, and the library is gone too.

One last tournament

The shutdown does not cancel the competitive farewell. The Pokémon UNITE Aeos Crown 2027 Tokyo tournament goes ahead as previously announced, with regional qualifiers starting December 5 and the finals in Tokyo on March 20-21, 2027 — two days before the servers go dark.

Why it matters

• A live-service Pokémon game with a real competitive scene is getting a clean, dated ending rather than a slow fade — and players have five months to spend gems, grab old cosmetics, and save their collections.

• The Library caveat is the one to act on: download it before March 23 or lose the record of what you unlocked.

• UNITE's exit also closes the book on its run as an official Asian Games medal event — a rare feat for a MOBA that never quite became a mainstay.
