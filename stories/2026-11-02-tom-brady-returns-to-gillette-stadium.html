Brady Returns: Tom Brady Calls His First Game at Gillette Stadium as a Broadcaster

By Matt

FOX's teaser says it all — Brady returns to Foxborough on Sunday, Nov. 8, calling Packers-Patriots as the network's lead analyst, his first game at Gillette Stadium in the booth.

Brady returns. FOX Sports dropped the teaser, and the message is exactly what it sounds like: Tom Brady is coming back to Foxborough — this time with a headset on.

On Sunday, Nov. 8, Brady will serve as FOX's lead analyst when the Green Bay Packers visit the New England Patriots at Gillette Stadium. It is his first Patriots game as a broadcaster, and his first time calling a game at Gillette since he left the field for the booth.

The assignment

• Game: Green Bay Packers at New England Patriots

• When: Sunday, Nov. 8, 4:25 p.m. ET

• Where: Gillette Stadium, Foxborough

• On: FOX's "America's Game of the Week" (also streaming on FOX One)

• Crew: Kevin Burkhardt (play-by-play), Tom Brady (analyst), Erin Andrews and Tom Rinaldi (sidelines)

The group is in its third season together as FOX's leading NFL broadcast team, and this is the assignment the schedule was always building toward: Brady, back in the building where he built the whole thing, calling his old team's game.

Why this one hits different

Brady spent 20 seasons with the Patriots, from his first start in 2001 through his sixth and final Super Bowl title with New England at the end of the 2018 season. FOX's teaser leans all the way into that history — two decades of memories, cut to the return.

And Sunday carries one more layer for Patriots fans: it also marks Rob Gronkowski's induction into the Patriots Hall of Fame. Two of the defining figures of the dynasty, back in Foxborough on the same day, one in the booth and one into the Hall.

The game itself

None of this is ceremony-only. Jordan Love's Packers visit Drake Maye's Patriots in a real Week 9 matchup between two of the game's top young quarterbacks — exactly the kind of game Brady's booth was built for. The return is the story, but the football underneath it is live.
