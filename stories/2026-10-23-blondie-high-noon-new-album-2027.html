Blondie Announce 'High Noon' — First New Album in Nine Years, Arriving Feb. 26, 2027

By Matt

The band's 12th studio album via BMG is the follow-up to 2017's 'Pollinator' — and poignantly, the final Blondie recording featuring co-founding drummer Clem Burke, who died in April 2025. Lead single 'Spectral Eyes' is out now.

Blondie are back. The band announced "High Noon" — their 12th studio album, arriving February 26, 2027 via BMG, their first new record since 2017's Pollinator.

The Details

• Lead single "Spectral Eyes" is out now — written by Johnny Marr, who also plays guitar

• Collaborators include Sex Pistols' Glen Matlock, Beirut's Zach Condon, and Lucius' Jess Wolfe and Holly Laessig

• Sessions began in NYC in 2022 with producer John Congleton, with additional production/mixing by David Wrench

The Poignant Part

This is the final Blondie recording featuring co-founding drummer Clem Burke, who died in April 2025. Nine years between albums, and one last record with the original heartbeat.
