Target Pokémon drops: what they resell for

By Matt

Target puts four Pokémon 30th Celebration products in stores Saturday, November 28 — and the $31.99 Booster Bundle is the one to queue for: it resells for about $59 on eBay, roughly $19 clear after fees.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Target's Black Friday Pokémon drop lands in stores on Saturday, November 28 — the day after Black Friday — and there are four 30th Celebration products on the shelf. One of them is worth lining up for. The Booster Bundle costs $31.99 at Target and resells for about $59 on eBay, which works out to roughly $19 clear after eBay's fees. The other three are a thinner play, and one of them loses money outright.

The drop is part of Target's Black Friday sale event, which runs November 22–29. Target's own announcement confirms a Pokémon trading card game drop on November 28, with no start time given — and these are in-store items, not a midnight online restock.

The four products, ranked by resale

CardWire tracked median prices from eBay sold listings between November 14 and 24, then subtracted eBay's 13.25% selling fee plus $0.40 per order. Here's how the four Saturday items stack up:

30th Celebration Booster Bundle (6 packs): $31.99; $59; ~$19 profit

30th Celebration Figure Collection (Mew or Mewtwo): $39.99; $61; ~$13 profit

30th Celebration Tin (Sylveon ex or Greninja ex): $29.99; $43; ~$7 profit

30th Celebration Battle Deck (Espeon ex or Umbreon ex): $24.99; $25; ~$4 loss

The Booster Bundle is the clear pick: six packs for $31.99, and the only item in the drop with a comfortable margin. The Figure Collection is the runner-up — Mew and Mewtwo versions both go for $39.99, with about $13 left after fees. The tin clears a modest $7. The Battle Deck is the one to skip: it sells on eBay for essentially what Target charges, so fees eat the whole thing.

The deal is the resale, not the shelf price

None of these are discounted. Target is charging its standard prices — in some cases more than other retailers. The Booster Bundle lists at $31.99, which is $5.05 above its reported $26.94 MSRP. The Figure Collection is $39.99 at Target against $29.99 at Best Buy, and the Battle Deck is $24.99 against $19.99 there. LV1 Gaming's price comparison put it bluntly: the savings on this drop are $0.

That is the whole story of the 30th Celebration set this year. Demand is doing the work, not discounts. A November 10 TCGplayer snapshot had the Booster Bundle at $67.08 and the Mew and Mewtwo Figure Collections at $72.69 and $78.55 — higher than CardWire's eBay medians from two weeks later, but the same direction: every one of these boxes trades above what Target charges for it.

The same pattern showed up across the wider market this week. NerdSnack's Black Friday guide, updated November 24, has the standard 30th Celebration Elite Trainer Box at $113.99 against a $49.99 MSRP — more than double list — while Magic booster boxes sell for about half of theirs. Pokémon is the premium side of the hobby right now, and Target's Saturday shelf is one of the few places to buy in at retail.

If you miss Saturday

A leaked notice tracked by TCGTracker points to a second date: December 4. Nothing is confirmed beyond that, and the December 4 tin is a different item — a $21.99 tin, not the $29.99 Sylveon ex or Greninja ex version in Saturday's drop — so don't count on a straight rerun. Saturday is the shot.
