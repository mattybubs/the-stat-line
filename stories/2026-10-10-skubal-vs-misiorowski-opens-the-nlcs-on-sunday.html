# Skubal vs. Misiorowski opens the NLCS on Sunday

Dave Roberts named Tarik Skubal to open the NLCS for the Dodgers on Sunday in Milwaukee, where he'll duel Brewers ace Jacob Misiorowski, with Yoshinobu Yamamoto set for Game 2 (Fox Sports).

Ace vs. ace

This is the pitching matchup the whole series will be measured against:

Tarik Skubal: Dodgers (LHP); Six innings of one-run ball in his NLDS Game 1 win

Jacob Misiorowski: Brewers (RHP); Owns a 1.59 ERA against the Dodgers this season

The matchup numbers all point one way: Misiorowski has held the Dodgers' lineup to .155/.210/.259 lifetime. That's not just good — it's outright ownership. The two-time defending champions open their three-peat bid against the one pitcher who's consistently made them look mortal (Sportsnaut).

The rotation picture

• Game 1 (Sunday, Milwaukee): Skubal vs. Misiorowski

• Game 2: Yamamoto for the Dodgers

• The stakes: The Dodgers chase a third straight pennant; the Brewers — NL-best 103 wins — want revenge for last year's NLCS sweep.
