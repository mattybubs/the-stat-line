TSL Bets: The Saturday parlay (~+377) — Towns OVER 37.5 PRA, Procházka moneyline at UFC Qatar

By Matt

Karl-Anthony Towns has cleared 37.5 points, rebounds and assists in 7 of his last 10, and Jiří Procházka is a live +160 underdog with championship experience against an unbeaten contender in his first main event. Two legs, about +377.

Leg 1 — the game

Portland Trail Blazers (10-5) at New York Knicks (9-6) — 7:30 PM ET, Madison Square Garden, New York, NBA TV

Knicks -5.5, total 217.5 (lines via FanDuel).

Injuries — Portland: F Deni Avdija QUESTIONABLE (left foot contusion — he has missed the last two games, and he is Portland's leading scorer at 24.2 points a game). G Ja Morant OUT (left hamstring strain, Grade 2 — out until around December 1). G/F Shaedon Sharpe OUT (right meniscus surgery — about six months). F Vit Krejci OUT (left ankle fracture). G Chris Youngblood OUT (right heel/foot bone bruise). C Robert Williams III day-to-day (rest) — expected to be available.

Injuries — New York: F OG Anunoby questionable (left calf tightness). G Jordan Clarkson OUT (right plantar fasciitis). Other reports list the Knicks as fully healthy, so the Anunoby designation is the one to watch.

Storylines: The Knicks are the defending champions, 9-6, and winners of three straight — twice over Toronto and a 102-99 win at Minnesota on Thursday after leading by 19 in the third quarter. Jalen Brunson leads them at 26.6 points a game, sixth in the league, and Karl-Anthony Towns is the second option. Portland is 10-5 under first-year coach Micah Nori with Damian Lillard back in the fold (20.9 points a game), on the fifth stop of a six-game road trip, chasing its first win in this series since November 2022. The frontcourt matchup is the edge: Portland's only true big is Donovan Clingan, with Williams day-to-day and Avdija questionable, and Towns has cleared 37.5 points, rebounds and assists in 7 of his last 10 while averaging 39.3. He landed on 38 on Thursday at Minnesota (22 points, 12 rebounds, 4 assists).

Leg 2 — the fight

Jiří Procházka (32-6-1) vs. Navajo Stirling (11-0) — UFC light heavyweight, 5 rounds, ~3:00 PM ET, ABHA Arena, Doha, main card 1:00 PM ET on Paramount+

Procházka +160, Stirling -192 — DraftKings via Wise Guy Team, prices checked November 21, 6:55 AM ET.

Weigh-ins: Both men hit 205 pounds on Friday, and the main event is official. The only miss on the 12-fight card was Tahir Abdullayev at 172 pounds, one over for his prelim against Jake Matthews — he forfeits 20 percent of his purse and the fight goes ahead at catchweight.

Camp/availability: Procházka returns for the first time since Carlos Ulberg knocked him out in the first round at UFC 327 in April, in a fight for the division's vacant title. Before that setback he knocked out Khalil Rountree Jr. in the third round and Jamahal Hill. Stirling trains at City Kickboxing alongside Ulberg — the man who just beat Procházka — and Procházka has said openly he didn't want this matchup at first, because Stirling is the lower-ranked fighter from the same camp. Stirling is 29 and has finished three straight opponents by knockout in 2026: Bruno Lopes, Ion Cuțelaba, and former champion Jan Błachowicz in the first round at UFC Belgrade in August. Saturday is his first UFC main event.

Storylines: This is a former champion as a +160 underdog. Procházka owns the championship experience — five-round fights, title-fight pressure, the walk to the cage as the hunted — and Stirling is in his first main event against his first former champion. The MMA Junkie staff picks back the dog, 7-4 for Procházka. The market disagrees: Stirling is a 62% favorite on the exchanges, and he has finished three straight by knockout this year, including a former champion in round one. The case for the dog is experience and proven five-round pedigree; the case for the favorite is that he just starched Błachowicz in a round.

The bet

Karl-Anthony Towns OVER 37.5 points + rebounds + assists (-120, FanDuel) + Jiří Procházka moneyline (+160, DraftKings) — about +377. A $10 ticket returns about $47.70.

Why these legs

Both legs clear on their own. Towns has cleared 37.5 PRA in 7 of his last 10 while averaging 39.3, and he just cleared it on Thursday with 38 — against a Portland frontcourt that is down to Clingan with Williams day-to-day and its leading scorer questionable. The line is 37.5 at -120, and the consensus line across books is 37.5. Procházka is the upside leg: a plus-money dog with a real path, not a lottery ticket — former champion, championship rounds on his record, staff picks 7-4 his way, and an opponent in his first main event. Neither leg depends on the other — different sport, different building, different afternoon. That is the juice rule working as intended: two real edges, one ticket, about +377 instead of the chalky +150 you get from stacking favorites.

The honest caveats

Towns landed on 38 on Thursday, clearing by half a point, and one model puts the over at 37.5 at about 50% — the trend is the case here, not a model consensus. Portland's defense is seventh in the league, and if Avdija does play, the Blazers' offense stays dangerous enough to keep the Knicks honest late. On the fight side, Procházka was knocked out in the first round in April, and Stirling has finished three straight by knockout, including a former champion in round one — the favorite is favored for a reason, and a second straight early knockout loss is a live outcome. A parlay needs both legs, and either one can sink it alone.
