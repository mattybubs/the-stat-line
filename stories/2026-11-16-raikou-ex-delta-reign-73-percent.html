Raikou ex is the week's biggest mover — up 73% to $132 as Delta Reign heats up

By Matt

Raikou ex from Delta Reign jumped 73% this week to $132 — the biggest gainer in Pokémon — while Yu-Gi-Oh! gained 16% on the month and Pokémon pulled back 8%.

The mover

The week's biggest gainer in Pokémon isn't a vintage chase or a 30th Anniversary grail. It's a brand-new card from the set that just hit shelves.

Raikou ex from Mega Evolution: Delta Reign (ME06) is up 73.0% this week to $132, the largest increase of any Pokémon card in TickerMint's market scanner for the week ending November 15. The move is based on the TCGplayer market price for the card, and it leads a Delta Reign board that is doing most of the hobby's talking right now: Aarune (139/103) is up 39.1% and Kecleon (117/103) is up 37.6% on the same weekly read.

The context matters. Delta Reign is the fresh 2026 print — the buy, at $188.12 for a booster box, against Mega Evolution's $299.85 sell. The set is a week into its life and already producing the week's three biggest gainers, with Raikou ex leading by a wide margin.

The other side of the board

Not everything is rising. The week's biggest drop is Espeon ex 108 — the 30th Celebration promo — down 52.1% to $40. The anniversary set's sealed product is still a second market (standard Elite Trainer Boxes cleared roughly $150-$170 in completed sales after the September 16 launch), but the promo singles are cooling fast.

The broader picture is mixed. Pokémon singles worth $2 or more are up a median 0.6% over 30 days and 6.3% over 90 days — 8,116 cards rose and 5,175 fell in the latest read. The high end of Delta Reign is holding its nerve: Mega Rayquaza ex, the set's chase, has a median of $315 across 21 PriceCharting sales from November 13-14, with a range of $253 to $385, and the special illustration rare has stayed between $296 and $310 for four days.

The month in the hobby

Zoom out a month and the story flips: Yu-Gi-Oh! gained 16% over the past month while Pokémon fell 8%, a 24-percentage-point gap in GempireCards' November market snapshot. Yu-Gi-Oh!'s index is up 47% over three months and 140% over the past year. Magic: The Gathering posted the only other monthly gain among the card categories, rising 1%.

Pokémon still leads on the longer charts — its index sits 84% above its level three months ago and 360% above a year ago — but the month belongs to Yu-Gi-Oh!, and the week belongs to a Raikou that nobody was talking about two weeks ago.
