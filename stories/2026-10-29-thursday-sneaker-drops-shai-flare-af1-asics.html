Thursday's Sneaker Drops: SHAI 'Flare,' Nike's College AF1 Pack, a 50-Pair ASICS Collab — Plus Hat Club's Spooky Fitteds

By Matt

Today's drop board: the Converse SHAI 001 'Flare' at $120, four Nike Air Force 1 Low college colorways at $125, a 50-pair SOSHIOTSUKI x ASICS SKYHAND OG at about $244, and Salehe Bembury's Spunge Lumina at $160 — with Hat Club's Spooky Season fitteds still in play for Halloween.

Thursday is a proper drop day — and if you collect sneakers or fitteds, this is the board to check before the weekend.

Converse SHAI 001 Lace Up "Flare" — $120

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander's signature line gets one more Converse chapter before the expected move to Nike: the SHAI 001 "Flare," an almost fully orange pair (Sun Glow/White, style code A23933C-832) that fits right in with the Thunder's colors. It drops today, October 29, for $120 via Nike SNKRS and select retailers. Note for collectors: the public pair keeps the Converse star and chevron on the heel, even though Shai has been spotted in a Nike-branded version.

Nike Air Force 1 Low college pack — $125 each

Four school colorways land today at $125 a pair: Texas (Desert Orange), Georgia (University Red), Kentucky (Game Royal) and Oregon (Apple Green). The Georgia pair ties into the wider Nike x Georgia x Outkast "Outkast U" collection that hit this week. All four are standard releases through Nike and select retailers.

SOSHIOTSUKI x ASICS SKYHAND OG — about $244, 50 pairs

The rarest thing on today's board: Japanese label SOSHIOTSUKI's take on the archival 1990s ASICS handball silhouette, in Oatmeal/Black with a cream suede upper, black stripes, gum outsole and an oversized Horween Chromexcel leather tongue made by artisans in Tokyo's Asakusa district. It's limited to just 50 pairs worldwide, priced at ¥38,500 (about $244), available through Kith Tokyo and Kith Manhattan with an October 29 release through SOSHIOTSUKI's online store. If you want this one, you already know the drill.

Salehe Bembury x Spunge Lumina "Cenote" — $160

After an early-access window on October 28, the Spunge Lumina "Cenote" gets its full launch today at $160.

For the hat collectors: Hat Club's Spooky Season fitteds

With Halloween two days out, Hat Club's Spooky Season 2026 59FIFTY collection — seven team hats in spooky-themed colorways with commemorative side patches, plus two Spooky pins — is the fitted drop to check at the Hat Club webstore. Hat Club's October run has also included the Ghouls, Glow My Gods and Pink Cattle Pack collections, all in the same 59FIFTY fitted silhouette.

And a reminder from yesterday's board: the adidas Originals by Wales Bonner FW26 collection — croc Karintha, green Gazelle, pony-hair BW Army pairs — also drops globally today, October 29, starting at $200. We covered the full lineup yesterday.
