ARC Raiders Turns Its Next Project Into a Memorial

By Matt

Soul's Offering goes live on October 27, 2026, as ARC Raiders' next Raider Project — a memorial built around a worn aviator helmet laid out beside lit candles.

ARC Raiders' next Raider Project is not another fight — it's a memorial. Soul's Offering goes live on October 27, 2026, turning the game's next event into a tribute to the Raiders who came before.

What Soul's Offering is

The artwork sets the tone: a worn aviator helmet laid out beside lit candles. It's a quiet, solemn image for a game built on tense extractions — a deliberate pause for remembrance rather than another escalation.

Why it stands out

Raider Projects are usually about new gear, new objectives, and new reasons to drop back in. Soul's Offering flips that script. By centering the fallen, Embark Studios is giving the community a shared moment of reflection — a memorial inside a shooter, where the tribute itself is the event.

When it goes live

Soul's Offering begins October 27, 2026. If you've been waiting for a reason to return to the Rust Belt, this one is worth showing up for.
