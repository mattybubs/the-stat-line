Hawks Demolish Hornets 128-81 in Charlotte — a 47-Point Statement

By Matt

Atlanta hung 128 on Charlotte in a 47-point rout — Jalen Johnson stuffed the stat sheet with 19 points, 8 assists and 7 rebounds as the unbeaten Hawks rolled to 2-0.

The Atlanta Hawks didn't just beat the Charlotte Hornets on Friday night — they buried them. Final score in Charlotte: Hawks 128, Hornets 81, a 47-point demolition that moved Atlanta to 2-0 and dropped Charlotte to 1-1.

Johnson does a bit of everything

Jalen Johnson led the Hawks with 19 points to go with 8 assists and 7 rebounds, and Aaron Wiggins matched him with 19 points of his own. It was that kind of night: Atlanta's lead kept growing, and nobody in a Hornets uniform could slow it down.

A wire-to-wire embarrassment

Charlotte never got a foothold. Naz Reid led the Hornets with 17 points, but the Hornets managed just 81 points total — and the 47-point margin is the kind of number that gets a game circled on the schedule the next time these two meet.

What it means

Two games, two wins for Atlanta — and the second one was a laugher. The Hawks are the early-season team nobody wants to see on the schedule right now, and they get a marquee test next: the Houston Rockets visit State Farm Arena on Saturday night.
