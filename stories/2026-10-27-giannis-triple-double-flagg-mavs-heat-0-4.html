Giannis Drops His First Triple-Double as a Heat — But Flagg and the Mavs Hand Miami an 0-4 Start

By Matt

Giannis Antetokounmpo posted 29 points, 14 rebounds and 11 assists in his first triple-double since joining Miami — and the Heat still lost, 113-109 to Dallas, as rookie Cooper Flagg took over the fourth quarter.

The result

Final: Dallas Mavericks 113, Miami Heat 109.

Giannis Antetokounmpo had his first triple-double since joining the Heat — 29 points, 14 rebounds and 11 assists — but Miami still fell to 0-4, the fifth 0-4 start in franchise history and the first since 2008-09.

Flagg takes the fourth

Dallas rookie Cooper Flagg scored 12 of his 29 points in the fourth quarter, added 11 assists, five steals and three blocks, and sealed it by blocking Andrew Wiggins' corner three with 31 seconds left and a two-point lead to protect.

Flagg, back from a shoulder strain, shot 12-of-23 from the field in 40 minutes. P.J. Washington added 20 points and Kyrie Irving scored 18 for Dallas.

The poster — and the free throws

Giannis' night had the highlight: a monster poster dunk over Daniel Gafford in the third quarter that had the whole league talking. But Miami missed 11 of 25 free throws, Giannis going 3-for-7, and blew a 90-84 lead early in the fourth.

Davion Mitchell scored 21 for Miami. Bam Adebayo continued his slow start with eight points on 3-of-10 shooting.

Why it matters

• Giannis' first triple-double in a Heat uniform still wasn't enough — Miami is winless through four games.

• Flagg's closing stretch — 12 fourth-quarter points plus the game-saving block — is the loudest statement of his young season.

• The Heat's 0-4 start is their first since 2008-09, and the free-throw line is already a season storyline.
