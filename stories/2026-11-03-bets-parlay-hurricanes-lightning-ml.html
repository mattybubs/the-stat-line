2-Leg Parlay (~+111): Hurricanes ML + Lightning ML — The Two Best Home/Road Favorites on the Board

By Matt

Two favorites that should win outright: Carolina, the NHL's best team at 12-2-2 on a 10-game win streak, and Tampa Bay at home against a back-to-back Detroit team missing its captain. Together at -192 and -260, they pay about +111.

The legs

Leg 1 — Carolina Hurricanes moneyline (-192, FanDuel). The Hurricanes are 12-2-2, the defending champions, winners of 10 straight, with a goaltender in Pyotr Kochetkov who is 10-0-0 to open the season. Nashville is 5-4-5 and starting Juuse Saros at 3-3-4. Carolina doesn't need to cover anything here — just win, which is what this team does.

Leg 2 — Tampa Bay Lightning moneyline (-260, FanDuel). Tampa is 8-3-2 at home against a Detroit team on the second night of a back-to-back, coming off a physical win at Florida on Monday, and missing captain Dylan Larkin (injured reserve/suspension). Tampa's scorers — Kucherov, Hagel, Guentzel, Point — are all available, and numberFire gives the Lightning a 69.1 percent win probability.

The reasoning

Each leg clears on its own: Carolina is the best team in hockey against a .500 team, and Tampa is a -260 home favorite against a tired, short-handed visitor. That's the parlay discipline — no leg that needs a miracle, no leg that only works if the other one hits. At -192 and -260, the two together pay about +111 — a little better than even money for two favorites that should both win.

The honest caveat: parlays are still parlays. One bad night — Saros standing on his head, or Vasilevskiy's shaky .877 save percentage turning into another soft night — and the whole ticket is gone even if the other leg cashes easily. That's why this stays a two-legger: two favorites, no lottery tickets.
