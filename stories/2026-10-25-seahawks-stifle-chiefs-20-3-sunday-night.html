Seahawks Stifle Chiefs 20-3 on Sunday Night

By Matt

Seattle held Kansas City to a single field goal in a 20-3 Sunday night win, with Jaxon Smith-Njigba going for 150 yards.

The Seattle Seahawks turned Sunday night into a defensive showcase, beating the Kansas City Chiefs 20-3 while holding them without a touchdown.

The game

Kansas City's only points came on a single field goal. Seattle led 13-3 in the fourth quarter before closing out the 20-3 win on Sunday Night Football.

JSN's night

Jaxon Smith-Njigba led Seattle with 9 catches for 150 yards and a touchdown on 12 targets. He entered the final minutes chasing 200 yards and finished 50 short, but his 150 still led all receivers on the night.
