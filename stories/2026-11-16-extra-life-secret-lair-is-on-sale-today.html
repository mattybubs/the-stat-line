Extra Life Secret Lair is on sale today

By Matt

Rise of the Polygons, Magic's Extra Life 2026 Secret Lair, is on sale November 16–30 at $39.99 non-foil and $49.99 foil — five low-poly cards, with half of every purchase going to Seattle Children's Hospital.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

The drop

Magic: The Gathering's annual Extra Life charity drop is live. Rise of the Polygons | Extra Life 2026 is on sale at MagicSecretLair.com from November 16 through November 30, closing at 1 a.m. PT on the 30th. The five-card set costs $39.99 non-foil and $49.99 foil — the same prices as the 2024 Extra Life drop.

Half of every purchase price, less third-party transaction fees, goes to Children's Miracle Network via Our Change Foundation, supporting Extra Life and Seattle Children's Hospital. On a $39.99 non-foil order, that's about $20 to the cause. This year's money is earmarked for mental and behavioral health care at Seattle Children's.

What's in the box

The drop is built like a lost N64 cartridge. All five pieces are illustrated by Brendan Sullivan in a late-1990s low-poly 3D video game style, and they tell a little story: the Young Pyromancer levels up through a treasure room and a legendary sword before the final boss, the Balefire Dragon.

Balefire Dragon: Mythic

Sword of Truth and Justice: Mythic

Big Score: Rare

Young Pyromancer: Rare

Elemental token (1/1 red): Token

The 2024 Extra Life drop went back to the 16-bit era. This year Wizards jumped a console generation forward, to blocky polygons and a charming sidekick with advice on how to beat that one especially tricky puzzle. The scenes were later revealed to be actual frames from a short film.

The charity behind it

Extra Life is the fundraising program where gamers raise money for Children's Miracle Network Hospitals, usually by streaming or hosting marathon game sessions and collecting donations. Wizards of the Coast has partnered with Extra Life since 2013, and at MagicCon: Atlanta this past weekend the company announced Magic players have now raised $10 million for Seattle Children's through the partnership.

Limited copies of Rise of the Polygons were sold early at the MagicCon: Atlanta merch booth from November 13 to 15 — at the convention, 100% of purchases went to Extra Life. The online sale runs at the standard 50% split.

Also on MTG Arena

The fictional video game from the drop crosses over into the real one: two bundles and a special emote are available in the MTG Arena store from November 16 to 30, including an Extra Life Secret Lair Sleeve Bundle priced at 3,000 gems with five sleeves based on the drop's art.
