Bucs rule out Mayfield plus three starters Thursday

By Matt

Tampa Bay is down its starting QB and three more starters for Thursday night in Dallas. The final injury report is in.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Final designations are in

Tampa Bay ruled out Baker Mayfield and three more starters for Thursday night's game against the Cowboys. All four absences went DNP all week, per Wednesday's final report.

What it means for Thursday

A backup quarterback on a short week, on the road, against a Dallas offense that has cashed 10 straight Overs. The Bucs were already thin; the final report made it official.

One piece of good news

Kicker Chase McLaughlin practiced in full and is off the report after Tuesday's groin/hip scare — so at least the kicking game is intact.
