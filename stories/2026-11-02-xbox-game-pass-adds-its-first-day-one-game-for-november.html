Xbox Game Pass adds its first day-one game for November

By Matt

Gravhounds entered Game Preview on November 2 at noon ET on Xbox Series X/S, PC and cloud, giving Game Pass its first day-one game of the month.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Xbox Game Pass has its first day-one game for November: Gravhounds, a co-op game that puts one to four players in charge of bio-engineered canine workers on hostile planets. It entered Game Preview on November 2 at noon ET.

The game

Gravhounds is built around a pack, not a lone hero. One to four players take control of bio-engineered canine workers and head out onto hostile planets — the premise is right there in the pitch: good dogs, hostile planets.

The co-op framing is the whole hook. Whether you are running it solo or with a full group of four, the game is about a crew of canine workers tackling dangerous worlds together.

When and where to play

Game: Gravhounds

Launch: November 2, at noon ET

Release stage: Game Preview

Platforms: Xbox Series X/S, PC, and cloud

Game Pass: Available day one

Game Preview is the key detail here: this is not a finished, final release dropping onto the service. It is arriving in preview form, which means Game Pass subscribers get in on day one while the game is still taking shape.

Why it matters for Game Pass

Day-one additions are the service's calling card, and Gravhounds is November's first. Subscribers on Xbox Series X/S, PC, and cloud can all jump in from the same launch moment — noon ET on November 2 — with no separate purchase needed beyond the subscription.

For a co-op game, that day-one, all-platforms launch matters more than usual: the whole point is playing together, and a shared launch window means everyone in the pack starts at the same time.
