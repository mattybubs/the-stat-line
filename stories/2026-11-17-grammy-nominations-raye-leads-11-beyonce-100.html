RAYE leads the Grammys with 11 nominations — and Beyoncé just hit 100

By Matt

RAYE leads the 69th Grammy nominations with 11, tying Beyoncé's single-year record for a woman — and Beyoncé herself became the first artist ever to reach 100 career nominations.

The morning

The nominees for the 69th Grammy Awards were revealed on Monday morning, and the list belongs to RAYE.

The British singer landed 11 nominations — the most of any artist this year — including nods for Record and Song of the Year for "Where Is My Husband!" and Album of the Year for "This Music May Contain Hope." Her 11 nominations tie Beyoncé's record for the most Grammy nominations earned by a woman in a single year.

ROSALÍA is next with nine, days after dominating the Latin Grammys with her album "Lux." Ella Langley, Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers each scored seven. Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas have six apiece, Olivia Dean has six, and Taylor Swift received five.

The other record in the room

Beyoncé didn't lead the nominations this year. She did something bigger: she became the first artist in Grammy history to reach 100 career nominations, extending her standing as the most nominated artist the awards have ever seen.

The milestone nod came for "Morning Dew (Donk)" in Best R&B Solo Performance. It is the latest line on a record she already owned outright — and the number now sits at triple digits.

The race taking shape

The nominations set up an Album of the Year showdown between a veteran and a new guard. Swift is in the field, and Bruno Mars — the only male artist nominated in the category, for his album "The Romantic" — is the other established name in a race led by RAYE and ROSALÍA.

One absence is already drawing debate: no R&B or rap albums were nominated for Album of the Year, a gap that has the internet arguing about what the Academy values in its biggest category.

The eligibility period covered recordings released from August 31, 2025, through August 28, 2026. Final-round voting, in which roughly 16,000 Recording Academy members choose the winners, runs December 10, 2026, through January 7, 2027. The winners will be announced at a ceremony in downtown Los Angeles on February 7, 2027.
