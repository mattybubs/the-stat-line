Connor McDavid Anytime Goal (+140): A Point in All 15 Games, Against a Goalie Question

By Matt

McDavid has a point in every game he's played this season — 15 for 15, with 11 multi-point games — and 9 goals on 42 shots. At +140 to score against New Jersey, you're betting on the best player in the world to do the thing he does most nights.

The game slip

Matchup: Edmonton Oilers (11-2-2) at New Jersey Devils (9-5-0)

Puck drop: Tuesday, November 3, 7:30 PM ET — ESPN+/Hulu/Disney+, Prudential Center, Newark

Line: Devils -154 | Oilers +128 | Total 6.5 (via FanDuel/Bleacher Nation); McDavid anytime goal +140

Oilers injury report: OUT — Devon Levi (goaltender, injured reserve), Mattias Ekholm (sidelined, nearing a return). Zach Hyman is on injured reserve with an estimated return around November 3 — his status is the one to check before puck drop, but it doesn't change this bet: McDavid's line runs through Draisaitl, who leads Edmonton with 12 goals.

Devils injury report: Anthony Mantha suffered an undisclosed injury October 30 against Vegas, left the game early, and had no update from head coach Sheldon Keefe beyond "we just don't know at this point." Connor Hellebuyck — the Devils' blockbuster acquisition from Winnipeg — is 2-0-0 in his first two Devils starts (1.50 GAA, .923 save percentage) but was scratched entirely one night after his debut with no official explanation, then listed as the expected starter for tonight. That uncertainty is real, and it's priced into this bet's caveat below.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. McDavid has a point in all 15 games this season — 39 points on 9 goals and a league-leading 30 assists, with 11 multi-point games. He has exceeded his points prop in 11 of 15 games with a set line.

2. He has scored in seven of 15 games, including multiple goals twice, and per PickDawgz he has scored at least one goal in five of Edmonton's last six games as a road underdog. Edmonton is a road underdog again tonight (+128).

3. Edmonton is 7-0-0 on the road this season. The Oilers' offense travels: McDavid and Draisaitl (28 points) are the two most dangerous players on the ice in this game, and New Jersey's goaltending situation — brilliant new starter, unexplained scratch, backup Jake Allen at 5-3-0 with a 3.11 GAA — is unsettled enough to attack.

The bet

Connor McDavid anytime goal (+140). This is a player-prop lead for a reason: you're not betting on Edmonton to win, or on a goalie you can't predict — you're betting on McDavid, who has scored in nearly half his games this season and has a point in every single one, to find the net once against a team whose crease is a question mark tonight.

The honest caveat: Hellebuyck, if he starts and plays like he did in his first two Devils games (one goal allowed to Dallas, two to Vegas), is exactly the kind of goalie who ruins a +140 anytime-goal ticket. And McDavid has not faced New Jersey yet this season. But +140 on the best player in the sport, in a game where his team is an underdog and he has scored in five of his last six as one, is a price worth taking.
