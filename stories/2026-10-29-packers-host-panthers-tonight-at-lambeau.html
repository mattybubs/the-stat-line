Packers Host Panthers Tonight at Lambeau

By Matt

Carolina's depleted secondary — starting corners on injured reserve, the fill-in ruled out — gives Jordan Love and Green Bay's passing game the opening it needs tonight at Lambeau.

The Carolina Panthers visit the Green Bay Packers at Lambeau Field tonight at 8:15 p.m. ET, and the matchup sets up in Green Bay's favor before a snap is played — because of who's missing from Carolina's secondary.

Game info

Matchup: Carolina Panthers at Green Bay Packers

Venue: Lambeau Field

Kickoff: Tonight, 8:15 p.m. ET

Carolina's secondary arrives depleted

Carolina's secondary is thin at exactly the wrong time. The Panthers' starting corners are on injured reserve, and the fill-in is ruled out for tonight's game. That leaves a patched-together back end walking into one of the league's toughest road environments on a short week.

The opening for Jordan Love

That is the story of this game for Green Bay. A depleted secondary gives Jordan Love the opening the Packers' passing game needs — a chance to attack downfield against a group that is missing its starters and its next man up. If Green Bay's passing game is going to get right in prime time, this is the matchup to do it against.
