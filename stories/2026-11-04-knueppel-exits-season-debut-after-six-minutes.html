Knueppel exits season debut after six minutes with leg soreness

By Matt

Kon Knueppel's season debut lasted less than six minutes against the Brooklyn Nets before he was ruled out with soreness in his left leg.

What happened

Charlotte Hornets guard Kon Knueppel lasted less than six minutes into his season debut against the Brooklyn Nets before being ruled out with soreness in his left leg.

That is the whole story of his first appearance of the season: a long wait, a brief return, and an early exit.

The wait before the debut

Knueppel had missed Charlotte's first eight games before finally getting back on the floor against Brooklyn.

He was originally sidelined by a left hamstring strain, according to The Rookie Wire.

What we know so far

• The opponent: the Brooklyn Nets

• How long he lasted: less than six minutes into his season debut

• The reason he left: soreness in his left leg

• What kept him out before: a left hamstring strain, which cost him Charlotte's first eight games

The team ruled him out, so the debut ended before it ever really got started. The frustrating part is the symmetry: the same left leg that kept him sidelined to open the season is the one that cut his return short.
