TSL Bets: Jaylen Warren OVER 61.5 rushing yards vs the Bengals

By Matt

The Steelers are off a bye, the Bengals have the worst run defense in football, and Jaylen Warren already ran for 127 on them once this season. The play: Warren OVER 61.5 rushing yards (-114) on Sunday Night Football.

The game slip

Pittsburgh Steelers (6-2) at Cincinnati Bengals (6-2)

Sunday, 8:20 PM ET — Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati — NBC

• Spread: Bengals -3.5

• Total: 49.5

• Moneyline: Steelers +155 / Bengals -178

• Odds source: BettorsInsider, November 14

Injury report — Steelers: TE Pat Freiermuth QUESTIONABLE (illness, added Saturday), DT Derrick Harmon questionable (foot).

Injury report — Bengals: S Kyle Dugger OUT (quadriceps), WR Andrei Iosivas doubtful, CB Jalen Davis doubtful, G Brian Parker II doubtful.

Storylines: The AFC North lead is on the line in primetime — both teams are 6-2. The Steelers come off a bye, rested and healthy at the skill spots. And the matchup is the whole bet: Warren against a Bengals run defense that has been the worst in football all season.

The bet

Jaylen Warren OVER 61.5 rushing yards (-114, FanDuel)

DraftKings lists the same bet at 63.5-64.5 — shop for the 61.5 at FanDuel if you can get it.

Why this prop

Warren is the most efficient runner in the league right now: 704 yards on 119 carries, 88.0 yards per game, 5.9 yards per carry — the best mark in the NFL at 5.92 per FantasyLabs. His last five rushing lines: 93, 101, 50, 101, 143. He's cleared 64.5 in five of his last six, has four 100-yard games, and has gone over 13.5 carries in five straight.

And he's already done it to this team. In Week 3, Warren ran for 127 yards on 17 carries against these same Bengals — plus 49 receiving yards — in a 30-27 Steelers win.

Cincinnati's run defense is the worst in the league by the numbers that matter: a league-worst 5.04 yards per carry allowed, 27th in EPA against the run. The Bengals are third in run-stuff rate and their defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence ranks 10th in run-stop win rate, so it's not a free 130 — but the volume is there, the matchup is there, and the Steelers are coming off a bye with a fresh lead back.

The honest caveats

Rico Dowdle is back in the Bengals' backfield, which could keep Cincinnati's offense on the field longer — and if Joe Burrow (2,386 passing yards, 23 touchdowns) gets hot early, Pittsburgh may have to throw more than run. Aaron Rodgers (1,871 yards, 13 touchdowns) has to keep the Steelers in the game for Warren to get his carries. But at 61.5, with the 63.5-64.5 available everywhere else, this is the number to play.
