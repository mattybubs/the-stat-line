GTA 6 has already banked $1.18 billion before launch — 12.1 million pre-orders and counting

By Matt

Sensor Tower estimates Grand Theft Auto VI has taken about 12.1 million digital pre-orders worth roughly $1.18 billion before a single copy reaches a shelf — and the game doesn't unlock until Thursday.

The number

Grand Theft Auto VI hasn't launched yet, and it has already taken in more money than most games make in their entire lives.

Analytics firm Sensor Tower estimates the game had about 12.1 million digital pre-orders across PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S by the morning of November 13, worth roughly $1.18 billion. The tracker has climbed fast: 7.71 million copies and about $751 million on November 3, past $1 billion and 10.31 million copies by November 11, then 12.1 million two days later.

PlayStation is doing most of the work — about 9.08 million copies and $880 million, roughly 75 percent of the total — with Xbox Series X|S at 3.06 million copies and about $296 million.

The caveats, because they matter

Every figure here is Sensor Tower's estimate, not an official count. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has confirmed a pre-order total. The estimate covers digital pre-orders only — boxed copies aren't counted — and a pre-order isn't a sale until launch, with players still able to cancel before Thursday.

Take-Two has said only that demand is running well ahead of its own plans. CEO Strauss Zelnick told CNBC's Squawk Box on November 10 that pre-orders were "vastly" beating expectations that were already high, and the company's November 9 results described pre-orders reaching "unprecedented levels" without giving a number. The company kept its full-year net bookings forecast at $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion.

And the $1.18 billion is gross consumer spending before platform fees — Take-Two's own share is smaller. By one estimate, the pre-order haul converts to roughly $826 million in net revenue to Take-Two, which would cover more than half of even the highest estimates of the game's development budget before a single launch-day sale.

Why the number is still staggering

Even as an estimate, this is a scale no game has approached before launch. Sony and Microsoft have confirmed GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game in the history of the PlayStation and Xbox storefronts.

The bar for launch week is just as outsized. Bank of America has attached a $1.9 billion gross-revenue benchmark for the first three days to satisfy investor expectations — roughly 33 million copies by its model. A G2A survey of just under 1,500 users, flagged by Fortune, found 29 percent had already requested time off work to play.

Launch week

Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the standard edition priced at $80. Pre-loading opened November 10. Rockstar shared new details about the game's dynamic weather system and new screenshots on Monday, two days before launch.

The streamers are treating Thursday like a holiday. Kai Cenat has confirmed a full playthrough aiming for 100 percent completion after a five-game GTA marathon that took roughly 130 hours, and IShowSpeed says he'll stream the game 12 hours a day to take his time with it.
