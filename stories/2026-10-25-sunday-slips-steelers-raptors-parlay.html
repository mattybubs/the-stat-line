Sunday's Slips: Vikings -6.5 vs. the Depleted Colts, Raptors +1.5 in Minnesota — Plus a 2-Leg Parlay (~+260)

By Matt

Updated slip: the Steelers leg was pulled — the Paris game was already underway when the outage-delayed post went live. Vikings -6.5 replaces it, Raptors +1.5 stays, and the parlay is fixed.

Updated 9:50 AM ET: the original version of this slip carried Steelers -3 in the Paris game. That game kicked off at 9:30 AM while the site was still down for credits, so the pick went live late — it has been pulled and replaced. Nothing here is posted after kickoff. The replacement leg is below.

Two games, two picks, one parlay. Skipped on purpose: Chiefs at Seahawks (Rashee Rice ruled out for Kansas City), 49ers at Falcons (Nick Bosa, Mike Evans and Deebo Samuel all out for San Francisco), Rams at Raiders (Puka Nacua questionable with a calf injury) and Ravens at Bengals (Lamar Jackson ruled out). No guessing on injury news — those four wait for another day.

GAME SLIP 1 — Indianapolis Colts (2-4) at Minnesota Vikings (5-0)

Kickoff: 1:00 PM ET, U.S. Bank Stadium, Minneapolis — CBS.

The lines: Vikings -6.5 (-115), Colts +6.5 (-105), total 41.5, moneyline Colts +240 / Vikings -295 (consensus: Sharp Football Analysis, updated Saturday 7:51 PM ET; Vegas Insider, October 24)

Injury report:

• Colts: OUT — CB Sauce Gardner (calf), TE Tyler Warren (groin), WR Anthony Gould (concussion), LB CJ Allen (ankle). P Rigoberto Sanchez (right foot) was removed from the report Saturday and is expected to play

• Vikings: remarkably healthy. RB Jordan Mason (thumb) was elevated from injured reserve Saturday and is expected to play; WR Justin Jefferson and WR Jordan Addison are expected to play; TE T.J. Hockenson cleared concussion protocol

Storylines:

1. The health gap is the whole bet: a 5-0 home team at nearly full strength against a 2-4 team missing its top corner, its top tight end, and two more starters. Warren led the Colts in targets, receptions and touchdowns — Daniel Jones loses his safety blanket against one of the league's best pass defences

2. Minnesota's offence doesn't need to be spectacular here, just functional: Jefferson and Addison against a secondary missing Gardner is the mismatch that decides whether this covers

3. The case against: 6.5 is nearly a touchdown, and Jonathan Taylor can shorten any game if the Colts feed him and keep Minnesota's offence on the sideline. A backdoor Colts touchdown late is how this one loses

PICK: Vikings -6.5 (-115). Honest caveat: laying nearly a touchdown means a 6-point Vikings win loses. The bet is the health gap and the home field, not a blowout call.

GAME SLIP 2 — Toronto Raptors (2-0) at Minnesota Timberwolves (2-0)

Tip: 7:00 PM ET, Target Center, Minneapolis — NBA TV

The lines: Timberwolves -1.5 (-108), Raptors +1.5 (-108), total 232.5 (FanDuel)

Injury report:

• Raptors: Nate Bittle is out — a depth piece, not a rotation swing

• Timberwolves: Donte DiVincenzo is out (Achilles) — a long-term absence, already priced in

Storylines:

1. Both teams are 2-0, but Toronto's start has been the louder one: 123.5 points per game on offense, just 97 allowed, with Scottie Barnes averaging 25.5 points and 10 rebounds, RJ Barrett 21.5, and Kawhi Leonard 19.5 with a team-high 3.5 steals

2. Minnesota won both meetings last season (128-126 in Toronto, 115-107 at home) — which is exactly why the Wolves are favoured, and exactly why the points have value

3. The trend backs the dog: the Timberwolves have failed to cover in 11 of their last 12 home games against Eastern Conference opponents after winning as underdogs

PICK: Raptors +1.5 (-108). Honest caveat: Anthony Edwards is averaging 30 a game and Minnesota is at home for its home opener. If Edwards goes off, the points won't save you — but catching points with the better defence is the right side of a coin-flip game.

THE PARLAY — 2 legs, ~+260

• Vikings -6.5 (-115)

• Raptors +1.5 (-108)

Two prices, both sourced above, no lottery ticket. Minnesota needs its health advantage to show up at home; Toronto needs its defence to travel to Minneapolis. If either leg scares you, play them straight — that's the whole point of keeping it to two.
