Phil Collins cried, Oprah surprised everyone, and Wu-Tang brought Staten Island to the Rock Hall

By Matt

The Rock & Roll Hall of Fame's Class of 2026 took over the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Saturday — Phil Collins, 75, weeping at a standing ovation on two canes; Oprah Winfrey's unannounced induction of Luther Vandross; and Wu-Tang Clan, all ten of them, closing the night out with the room on its feet.

The night

The Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony returned to Los Angeles on Saturday night, and the Peacock Theater got one of the most emotional nights in the Hall's history.

Phil Collins, 75, was the center of it. Inducted as a solo artist — he was already in the Hall as a member of Genesis, in 2010 — Collins took the stage slowly, on two canes, and cried at the ovation that greeted him. Travis Barker inducted him, praising his unprecedented dual stardom as a drummer and a hit-making pop singer in the 1980s, and Cynthia Erivo sang a slowed-down "In the Air Tonight" while Barker played the drum fill. Collins hasn't performed since 2022, and his daughter Lily Collins was in the crowd. His message to the room: you haven't heard the last of me.

The surprise

Oprah Winfrey's appearance was unannounced. She took the stage to induct Luther Vandross, who died in 2005, calling him "the maestro of the love song" — and noting, with relish, that he was once correctly named "the top artist that people make love to." Usher sang a medley of "Here and Now" and "So Amazing" in tribute.

The party

Wu-Tang Clan was inducted by Chris Rock, all ten current members on stage together, and turned the ceremony into a celebration — a medley of "C.R.E.A.M.," "Method Man," and Ol' Dirty Bastard's "Shimmy Shimmy Ya" that had the crowd on its feet. "We took Staten Island to the world," RZA said.

Queen Latifah and MC Lyte entered as Early Influence inductees, alongside Gram Parsons, Fela Kuti, and Celia Cruz — Megan Thee Stallion inducted Latifah and performed "U.N.I.T.Y." and "Ruffneck." U2 opened the night as surprise guests with a new single, "Silencio," performed with riot-shield dancers, then played "Pride (In the Name of Love)." Joy Division and New Order were inducted together, with Peter Hook performing alongside Deftones and Lauren Mayberry, and Rick Rubin was inducted with the Red Hot Chili Peppers dressed as him. Run-DMC and Aerosmith closed the show with "Walk This Way."

Not everyone made it: Oasis were no-shows (Noel Gallagher was gigging in London, Liam declined), Sade didn't attend (inducted by Andra Day, with Sienna Spiro singing "Smooth Operator"), and Iron Maiden were touring Australia.

The edited broadcast airs on ABC and Disney+ on December 9. The Class of 2026 spans 18 artists and figures — and Saturday night proved the Hall still knows how to put on a show.
