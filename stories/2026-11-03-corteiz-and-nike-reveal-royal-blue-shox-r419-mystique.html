Corteiz and Nike Reveal Royal Blue Shox R419 Mystique Capsule

By Matt

Corteiz and Nike's royal blue Shox R419 Mystique capsule — headlined by the collaborative sneaker and a matching apparel range — launches online Wednesday, November 4, at 7 PM London time.

Disclosure: this post contains affiliate links. I earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Corteiz and Nike have officially unveiled the CRTZ Nike Shox R419 Mystique alongside a full royal blue apparel collection. The collaborative capsule is built around the Shox R419 Mystique sneaker and a matching range of sportswear, all rendered in a bold royal blue aesthetic — and it launches online on Wednesday, November 4, at 7 PM London time.

The capsule

The collection is a head-to-toe proposition: the sneaker leads, and the apparel follows in the same royal blue palette. According to Hypebeast, the range spans heavyweight sweatsuits with coordinating hoodies, crewneck sweatshirts, and sweatpants, alongside seasonal beanies and technical long-sleeve performance tops.

Every piece carries prominent co-branding that pairs Nike's Swoosh with Corteiz's Alcatraz island motif — the London label's signature mark meeting Nike's heritage performance design language.

Shox R419 Mystique sneaker: Headlines the capsule

Technical tracksuit: Black detailing, "CRTZ SHOX" typographic hits, mesh detailing along the zipper path

Sweatsuits: Heavyweight, with coordinating hoodies, crewnecks, and sweatpants

Performance tops: Technical long-sleeve styles

Beanies: Seasonal additions to the range

The centerpiece tracksuit

The centerpiece of the apparel selection is a technical tracksuit constructed with contrasting black detailing, distinctive "CRTZ SHOX" typographic hits, and breathable mesh detailing along the zipper path. Inside, the design goes further: the interior reveals a custom futuristic graphic printed across the inner lining — the detail that gives the Mystique capsule its name.

Release details

Corteiz confirmed the launch on Instagram. The entire collaborative range arrives with worldwide shipping.

• Launch: Wednesday, November 4, at 7 PM London time

• Where: Online via r419.com

• Shipping: Worldwide

The capsule continues one of streetwear's most closely watched partnerships — Corteiz's London sensibility applied to one of Nike's most recognizable performance silhouettes, this time in a single uncompromising color.
