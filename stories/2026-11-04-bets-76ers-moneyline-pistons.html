76ers Moneyline (+112): The East's Best Team Is Somehow the Underdog in Detroit

By Matt

Philadelphia is 7-1, unbeaten on the road, and getting plus money against a Detroit team missing its starting center. With Embiid off the injury report, the 76ers at +112 is the value side of the night's marquee game.

The game slip

Matchup: Philadelphia 76ers (7-1) at Detroit Pistons (6-1)

Tip-off: Wednesday, November 4, 7:00 PM ET — ESPN, Little Caesars Arena, Detroit

Line: Pistons -2.5 | Total 218.5 (via Odyn's morning board); 76ers moneyline +112 (via FanDuel, per rg.org's morning prices)

76ers injury report: OUT — Dominick Barlow (back), Ariel Hukporti (Achilles, out for the season). Joel Embiid is NOT on the injury report. He has been managing plantar fasciitis in his right foot and played under a minutes restriction against Chicago on Monday (15 points, 8 rebounds in about 25 minutes), but the clean report is a real development — and the restriction could come off entirely if the game is tight.

Pistons injury report: OUT — Jalen Duren (right hamstring strain, targeting a return around November 9), Isaiah Joe (hip bruise, was targeting this game but ruled out). Detroit's starting center is out, and its bench shooting takes a hit with Joe sidelined.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. These are the East's two best records, and Philadelphia's only loss came against this opponent — in the first meeting on October 25 in Philadelphia, a game Embiid rested. This time Embiid is available, and Detroit's frontcourt of Paul Reed and John Collins has to deal with him without Duren.

2. Philadelphia is 7-1 after a 126-107 win over Chicago on Monday, when Tyrese Maxey had 26 points and 11 assists, Jaylen Brown had 25 and 11, and VJ Edgecombe added 21. Detroit is 6-1 after a 124-91 win in Charlotte on Monday, when Cade Cunningham scored 33 and Duncan Robinson added 24. Both teams are hot; only one is getting plus money.

3. Philadelphia has gone under the total in seven of eight games, which says something about how this team wins: defense, pace control, and enough scoring from four different 20-point threats to close games late. That profile travels, and the 76ers have not lost on the road yet.

The bet

Philadelphia 76ers moneyline (+112 at FanDuel). A 7-1 team that has not lost on the road, getting plus money against a team missing its starting center, with its own superstar back off the injury report — that is the kind of price that does not need a complicated case. The spread is only 2.5, so the moneyline is barely more expensive in probability terms than laying the points, and it pays plus money instead.

The honest caveats: Embiid's foot is still a management situation, not a solved problem — if the restriction stays tight, Philadelphia's paint edge shrinks. Detroit is 6-1 at home for a reason, Cunningham is playing at an All-NBA level, and the Pistons already beat this team once. If Embiid is limited to 25 minutes again, this becomes a coin flip, and +112 on a coin flip is still fine — it just isn't the steal it looks like if he plays 35.
