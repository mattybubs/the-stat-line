Zeus is back with T1 — the two-time world champion returns on a deal through 2029

By Matt

T1 officially announced the return of Choi 'Zeus' Woo-je on Tuesday, bringing the two-time world champion back to the organization he left two years ago — and reuniting most of the roster that won back-to-back Worlds titles.

The worst-kept secret of the League of Legends offseason is finally official. T1 announced on Tuesday that Choi "Zeus" Woo-je is returning to the organization, signing a three-year deal that runs through 2029 and putting the two-time world champion back in the top lane where he won back-to-back Summoner's Cups.

The announcement came at T1's "2027 T1 Announcement" event at the team's Basecamp in Gangnam, Seoul, where Zeus appeared on stage in front of fans to confirm the move himself. T1's own post said it plainly: "The God of Thunder returns! 'Zeus' is back with T1 through 2029."

The reunion two years in the making

Zeus came up through T1's academy and formed the core of the most decorated roster in modern League of Legends. Alongside Lee "Faker" Sang-hyeok, Mun "Oner" Hyeon-jun, Lee "Gumayusi" Min-hyeong and Ryu "Keria" Min-seok — the ZOFGK lineup — he won the World Championship in 2023 and again in 2024, the first back-to-back titles since SK Telecom T1's run a decade earlier.

Then it fell apart. Contract talks between Zeus and T1 broke down at the end of 2024, and he left for Hanwha Life Esports while Choi "Doran" Hyeon-jun went the other way. The split turned into a public dispute over how the negotiations were handled, and it only ended in July 2025, when T1 COO Josh Ahn publicly apologized to Zeus, his family and his agency.

The two years away were not wasted. At HLE, Zeus won the 2025 LCK Cup, the inaugural First Stand tournament and the 2026 Mid-Season Invitational — the first player to win First Stand, MSI, Worlds, the Asian Games and the Esports World Cup. The one thing missing was another Worlds title: HLE exited Worlds 2026 in the Swiss stage after losses to Gen.G, Invictus Gaming and eventual champions Bilibili Gaming.

How the deal came together

The move had been an open secret for a week. Zeus's contract with HLE expired on November 16, and the club officially said goodbye on November 19. Korean outlet Xportsnews first reported the T1 agreement, with Sheep Esports confirming that T1 executives Becker and Joe Marsh reached terms on November 18 — a straight three-year contract, not the two-plus-one structure earlier rumors had suggested.

Speaking at the announcement event, Zeus was candid about why he wanted a long deal wherever he landed. "After becoming a free agent, I had many thoughts," he said. "I felt it was time for a change. T1 presented a great value and vision, and I decided to return." He added that going through the transfer window is "mentally draining," and that he wanted a multi-year contract at his next team. His message to the fans was shorter: "I'm back to take T1's top-lane spot."

A new-look T1 for 2027

Zeus is the only change to the starting five, but the roster around him has shifted. The 2027 lineup is Zeus in the top lane, Oner in the jungle, Faker in mid, Kim "Peyz" Su-hwan at ADC and Keria on support — Peyz replacing Gumayusi, who is out after years as T1's bot-lane cornerstone. The coaching staff has been overhauled too: Kim "Roach" Kang-hee is the new head coach, with former pro jungler Eom "UmTi" Seong-hyeon and Lee "Easyhoon" Ji-hoon alongside him. T1 parted ways with head coach Kim "kkOma" Jeong-gyun and his staff on November 21.

Doran, the man Zeus replaces, has already landed: Dplus KIA signed him on November 20. He leaves T1 as a world champion himself, having helped the team to its third straight Worlds title in 2025.

The reunion rebuilds most of the core that dominated the last two years, with a new ADC and a new coaching staff around it. Zeus's first official match back in T1 colors comes when the 2027 LCK season begins — and the target is obvious. BLG hold the Summoner's Cup after beating Gen.G 3-1 in the Worlds 2026 final, and T1 just brought back the top laner who knows exactly what it takes to take it from them.
