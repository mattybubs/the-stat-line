Stamkos and Hoglander each score twice in Toronto

By Matt

A 4-4 third-period thriller at Scotiabank Arena, with Stamkos and Hoglander trading braces and McKenna's first career goal.

The game

Nashville and Toronto were tied 4-4 late in the third on Oct. 6 at Scotiabank Arena. Steven Stamkos and Nils Hoglander each scored twice in a back-and-forth night.

The rookie moment

Gavin McKenna scored his first career goal for Toronto, assisted by Auston Matthews — the same top pick who would follow it up with a multi-point night and an OT comeback win the next game.
