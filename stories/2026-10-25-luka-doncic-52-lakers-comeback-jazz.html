Luka Drops 52 as Lakers Storm Back from 18 Down in Utah

By Matt

Doncic poured in 52 points with 8 rebounds and 10 assists — his ninth career 50-point game — as Los Angeles erased an 18-point deficit to beat Utah 132-123.

Luka Doncic delivered his ninth career 50-point game on Sunday night — and it took a comeback to get there. Doncic scored 52 points with 8 rebounds and 10 assists as the Los Angeles Lakers erased an 18-point deficit and beat the Utah Jazz 132-123 at the Delta Center.

The line

Doncic shot 17-of-30 from the field, 8-of-18 from three and 10-of-11 from the free-throw line in 38 minutes. Austin Reaves added 29 points for Los Angeles, while Lauri Markkanen led Utah with 28. Jazz guard Keyonte George nearly answered with a triple-double of his own, finishing with 24 points, 10 assists and 9 rebounds.

The comeback

Utah blitzed Los Angeles early, leading 47-29 after the first quarter. The Lakers answered with a 45-24 second quarter to take a 74-71 halftime lead, then never trailed again on the way to the 132-123 win.

The history

Tonight was Doncic's ninth career 50-point game and his second as a Laker. His eighth came on March 12, 2026 against Chicago, when he scored 51 points in a 142-130 win — his first 50-point game since the February 2025 trade from Dallas.

Sources
