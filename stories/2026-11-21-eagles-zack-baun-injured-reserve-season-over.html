Eagles put All-Pro Zack Baun on IR — his season could be over

By Matt

The Eagles placed All-Pro linebacker Zack Baun on injured reserve Saturday, at least four weeks after the back injury he suffered in Week 9 — and with four games left after that, his season could be over.

Baun to IR

The Philadelphia Eagles placed All-Pro linebacker Zack Baun on injured reserve on Saturday, November 21.

Baun will miss at least the next four games, and ESPN's Adam Schefter reported that his season could be over. The Eagles also placed wide receiver Dontayvion Wicks on injured reserve in the same set of moves, and activated running back Will Shipley from IR.

It is the latest blow in a season that has gone sideways fast for Philadelphia. The Eagles are 2-7 and have lost seven straight games heading into Sunday's Week 11 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers — and now they will play the next month without the best player on their defense.

How it happened

Baun injured his back in Week 9 against the New York Giants, delivering a big hit on Giants quarterback J.J. McCarthy. He left the game and did not return.

He did not practice this week, and the Eagles had already ruled him out for Sunday's game against Pittsburgh before placing him on injured reserve on Saturday.

The IR stint keeps Baun out against Pittsburgh, Dallas, Arizona and Indianapolis. He would first be eligible to return against Seattle in Week 15, with four regular-season games remaining from that point — and whether he comes back at all will depend on his recovery.

What the Eagles lose

Baun has played eight games this season and recorded 65 tackles, eight for loss, two sacks, a fumble recovery and five pass defenses. He is the leader of the Eagles' defense — the All-Pro in the middle who sets the tone and makes the plays.

He earned that role the hard way. Baun re-signed with Philadelphia in March 2025 on a three-year, $51 million deal with $34 million guaranteed, after a breakout All-Pro season, and he has been one of the few constants on a team that has spent the year losing players and games in equal measure.

There is no replacing an All-Pro in the middle of a defense mid-season, and the Eagles will have to get by without theirs for at least the next month.

Wicks goes too

Wicks, in his first season with Philadelphia, has 19 catches for 284 yards and a touchdown. He missed the three games before the bye with an ankle injury, was limited in practice this week, and the Eagles added a foot injury to his designation on Friday.

Like Baun, he will miss at least the next four games and would first be eligible to return in Week 15 against Seattle.

What it means for Philadelphia

There is no way to dress this one up. The Eagles are 2-7, losers of seven straight, and they just lost their defensive leader for at least a month — maybe the season. The rest of the year is about finding out who can play, not about chasing anything.

Baun will be back. The question is whether it is this season or next, and what is left of the Eagles' year by the time he gets there.
