Bruins Face a Different Challenge as Crosby and Penguins Await

By Matt

Boston takes its 11-4-1 start on the road Thursday against Sidney Crosby and the Pittsburgh Penguins, with holding a lead away from home the test after letting a 2-0 advantage slip against Vegas.

Boston takes its 11-4-1 start on the road Thursday against Sidney Crosby and the Pittsburgh Penguins. It is a different kind of challenge from the one the Bruins have been passing at home: this time, the test is carrying a lead away from TD Garden.

The test: holding a lead on the road

The reason that test matters is fresh. Boston let a 2-0 advantage slip against Vegas, a reminder that a lead on the road does not hold itself. Against Pittsburgh, the Bruins will need to protect whatever advantage they build — not just find one.

Boston's start: 11-4-1

Opponent: Sidney Crosby and the Pittsburgh Penguins

Setting: On the road, Thursday

The challenge: Holding a lead away from home

The warning sign: A 2-0 advantage slipped against Vegas

Why Crosby changes the equation

Any trip to face Sidney Crosby and the Penguins is a measuring-stick game, and this one arrives with a specific question attached: can Boston's strong start travel? An 11-4-1 record says the Bruins know how to win. Thursday in Pittsburgh will say something more specific — whether they can close one out on someone else's ice.
