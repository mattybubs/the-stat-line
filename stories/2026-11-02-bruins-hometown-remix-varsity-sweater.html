Bruins Go Back to Brown: The Varsity Sweater Leads the NHL's Hometown Remix

By Matt

The NHL and Fanatics unveiled Hometown Remix for all 32 teams today, and Boston's entry is a stunner: an all-brown Varsity Sweater, the club's first since 1924, dressed up like a college hockey jersey mashed with a varsity jacket.

Brown is back in Boston, and it might be the best jersey the NHL has dropped in years.

The NHL and Fanatics unveiled Hometown Remix on Monday morning, a league-wide special edition uniform, apparel and headwear program that gives all 32 teams a look built around their own city. In the league's words, each design "uniquely and authentically celebrates the fans, fabric, and local culture" of its team. It is the NHL's answer to MLB's City Connect and the NBA's City Edition programs, the first league-wide special edition uniform series since the last Reverse Retro set in 2022-23, and the first 32-team program since Fanatics took over as the NHL's official uniform supplier in 2024. The collection was announced back in August, and now it is here: jerseys and apparel went on sale right away, and the new sweaters will show up in 261 games this season, 19 of them with both teams remixed. The Florida Panthers are even wearing their new pink set tonight against Detroit.

Every jersey in the set carries a few shared touches: a "Hometown Remix" wordmark inside the back collar, an HTR arrows logo on the hem loop, and a special design or motto inside the back hem, plus embossed and debossed texture work in the striping and crests that only really shows up close. But no two teams feel alike, and Boston's entry is the one everyone is talking about.

The Bruins preview: the Varsity Sweater

The Bruins are calling it the "Varsity Sweater," and the name tells you everything. It is an all-brown sweater, the club's first all-brown uniform since its inaugural season in 1924, dressed up like a college hockey jersey mashed up with a varsity jacket as a tribute to Boston's standing as a college town.

Here is what is on it:

• All brown, on purpose. Boston wore brown and gold from its debut in 1924-25 into the mid-1930s, the brown borrowed from founding owner Charles Adams' grocery store chain. More than a century later, Fanatics calls the Brownstone palette one that "honours tradition, culture, and community."

• "BRUINS" arched across the chest in gold collegiate block lettering above the player's number, with a front number for the first time since the 1940s.

• A lace-up collar, gold-and-white striping on the sleeves and around the waist, and a letterman-style "B" on a split brown-and-gold shield on each shoulder, with Ivy League prep stylings throughout.

• A gold helmet, with gold-and-cream striping carrying down onto the socks.

• The Freedom Trail on the inside hem. Flip it up and you will find a red brick pattern running along the inner lining with "BOSTON" worked into it.

The college connection is not just a look. The sweater takes inspiration from Boston's four prominent college hockey programs: Boston College, Boston University, Harvard, and Northeastern, the schools that play the annual Beanpot on TD Garden ice every February. "College hockey is a big part of Boston's identity, and the tradition and success of the programs in our city have helped make this one of the best hockey communities in the world," Bruins president Cam Neely said. "The Varsity Sweater is a great way to recognize that history while bringing back elements of the Bruins' original look."

When the Bruins wear it

The Varsity Sweater debuts Thursday, November 12, at home against Original Six rival Montreal, an event the club is dubbing "Homecoming." Every ticketed fan that night gets a themed T-shirt, and the Northeastern University Pep Band will be serenading the crowd all night. And because the Bruins are fully leaning into the bit, the club launched Causeway University as part of its season-long collegiate-themed campaign, a fictional prestigious institution built around grit, excellence, teamwork, and leadership. Whenever the Bruins wear the Varsity Sweaters, fans can complete Causeway University "syllabus assignments" through the Bruins + TD Garden App, with each completed assignment earning an entry to win an autographed Bruins Hometown Remix jersey.

The rest of the league is not holding back either

Boston leads the buzz, but the rest of the set is bold. Chicago went green, with the Blackhawks' crest in old-school chain-stitch embroidery and four six-pointed stars from the Chicago city flag debossed into the sleeve stripes. Buffalo leaned all the way into the Goathead, recoloured into modern blue and gold with debossed lightning bolts and stars for the "City of Light." Calgary's is a farewell letter to the Saddledome, with "1983" and "2027" stitched inside the hem. Carolina put a pig on the front of an NHL jersey. Picking a favorite is guaranteed to start arguments in every fan group chat, which is exactly the point.
