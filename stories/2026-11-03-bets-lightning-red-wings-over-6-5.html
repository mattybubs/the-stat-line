Lightning vs. Red Wings Over 6.5 (+110): Two Offenses, a Back-to-Back, and a Shaky Vezina Winner

By Matt

Tampa Bay averages 3.69 goals a game, Detroit 3.4 — and the Red Wings are on the second night of a back-to-back after beating Florida on Monday. The total is 6.5, and the over pays +110. Take the goals.

The game slip

Matchup: Detroit Red Wings (7-5-3) at Tampa Bay Lightning (8-3-2)

Puck drop: Tuesday, November 3, 7:00 PM ET — ESPN+, Benchmark International Arena, Tampa

Line: Lightning -260 | Red Wings +210 | Total 6.5, Over +110 / Under -134 (via FanDuel)

Red Wings injury report: OUT — Dylan Larkin (captain, injured reserve/suspension, expected back November 14), Marco Kasper (upper body), Mason Appleton (undisclosed), Albert Johansson (undisclosed, expected back November 5). Larkin being out is the biggest name on either injury list — but Detroit just beat Florida 5-3 on Monday without him, and this bet doesn't need Detroit to win. It needs goals, from either side.

Lightning injury report: OUT — Yanni Gourde (hip, injured reserve to December 2). Day-to-day — Erik Cernak (undisclosed), Zemgus Girgensons (lower body). Tampa's core scorers — Kucherov, Hagel, Guentzel, Point — are all available.

Storylines driving the bet:

1. The offenses are both live: Tampa Bay averages 3.69 goals per game, Detroit 3.4, and both clubs went 6-2-2 over their last 10. Tampa's last 10 averaged four goals for and 3.3 against; Detroit's averaged 3.4 for and 3.0 against. Add those up and 6.5 looks short.

2. Detroit is on the second night of a back-to-back after a physical 5-3 win at Florida on Monday night, with Daniil Tarasov (who beat his former team Monday) likely going again or John Gibson stepping in. Back-to-back legs mean looser defense late — exactly when totals get decided.

3. Andrei Vasilevskiy, Tampa's starter, is not having a Vezina season: 6-3-1 with a 3.00 goals-against average and a .877 save percentage, 48th in the league, and he gave up four goals on 33 shots (.879) in his last outing before a shootout loss. A shaky Vasilevskiy is the over's best friend.

The bet

Lightning vs. Red Wings Over 6.5 (+110). This is the game-total leg of tonight's rotation — no side, no star, just goals. Two teams combining for roughly seven goals a game, a back-to-back visitor, and a home goalie saving under 88 percent of shots. At plus money, the over is the value.

The honest caveat: Tampa has lost three of its last four, and a tight, low-event game is always possible when a favorite is slumping — and Detroit without Larkin can go quiet for stretches. But the total is 6.5, not 7.5, and both teams' recent form says seven goals is the baseline, not the ceiling.
